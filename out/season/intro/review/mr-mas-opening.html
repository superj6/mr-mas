MR. MAS

The full 30-second opening, built entirely in code: pixel-art picture on a 96 BPM grid, an original score with a jazz feel and 8-bit motifs, sound effects and a synthetic stock voice for Mas. Pick a score variation below; switching keeps your place in the video.

Defaults I chose

  • V1 is the primary mix. V2 to V4 share the same picture and hits.
  • Mas speaks with a stock synthetic voice (Kokoro, am_michael), trimmed to end by frame 91. No real voice is cloned or imitated.
  • RUMPT appears only as the silhouette in the roll call. His face waits for the show.
  • The roll call keeps Kram's soup, the Whale's breach and Rima's spotlight as traits from Ep1.
  • Alyi's card reads "EFFIGIES: 1" instead of "BUNKER: YES" until you rule on it.

Worth checking

  • All audio was checked by meters, spectrograms and onset timing only. Please audition the four mixes by ear.
  • The Nole card at 17.5 s is the loudest hit. Listen there for limiter pumping.
  • The Orb as the title's period can read busy at small sizes.
  • Mas's mid-turn head in the cold open is the weakest drawing if held.

A 4K version with the V1 mix is in the project at out/season/intro/intro-ep1-V1-4k.mp4.

What would raise quality further

A human voice for Mas. One performer doing a soft, deadpan cartoon read of the cold-open line would lift it more than any other change. ElevenLabs Voice Design (from a text description, never a clone) is the fallback if you have access.
Better instruments or live players. The score renders from free sample libraries. Licensed plugins (felt piano, chamber strings, session horns, real brushes) run through the same scripts, or the MIDI can go to a pianist, a bassist, a brushes drummer and a muted trumpet.
Video-model access (for example Veo, Sora, Runway or Kling) for fluid shots in the episodes. Footage would be converted to pixel or glyph in code so it matches. The intro doesn't need it.
Reference photos of the real public figures, to tighten the likeness in the pixel portraits. And period screenshots (1993 Mac, 2008 web) to pin the era palettes.
