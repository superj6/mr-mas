# Orbit dossier: EA, rationalist and AI-safety lore (plus the e/acc counter-lore)

> **What this is.** A research dossier for the orbit-and-lore pass. It collects the effective-altruism, rationalist and AI-safety lore (and its accelerationist mirror image) that a knowing AI audience will enjoy seeing nodded to. It answers the showrunner's note (2026-09-25): *"we should also try to incorporate various ai/ea lore where reasonable but once again only where natural"* and *"another one maybe is leopold."*
>
> **Menu, not quota.** Nothing here asks for a new scene. Every "use" line rides a beat, prop or set piece that already exists in the drafted season, or it costs 0–1 s of screen. Many episodes should carry none of it. Everything reaches the audience the way the season does: as something **Mas notices, reads, dodges or edits** (limited third person, [pov-clarification](../../bible/pov-clarification.md)).
>
> **Checked through:** 2026-09-25. **Pass:** orbit research, read-only on every existing file. **Does not edit** naming.md, the episodes, world-stakes or the reel. Name proposals are in [§9](#9-naming-proposals-for-namingmd-8), ready to paste.
>
> **Leopold Aschenbrenner has his own dossier:** [orbit-leopold.md](orbit-leopold.md) (written by a sibling pass, with primary sources). [§7](#7-leopold-aschenbrenner-the-situationist-the-lore-angle-only) here covers only how he connects to the EA and safety lore, and defers to that file for everything else.
>
> **Coverage limit.** This session's WebSearch budget was already spent, so the research ran on direct fetches of known URLs. X/Twitter, Reuters, Vox, The Atlantic, The Verge, Business Insider, The Economist and the Open Philanthropy grant page all refused the fetcher. Items that depend on those stay [K] and say so.
