## A. Timeline (1985 to 2019)

### Childhood and school
- **Apr 22, 1985: birth.** Sam Altman was born in Chicago and grew up in the St. Louis area. He was the eldest of four. His brother Jack later co-founded Hydrazine with him.
  - *Parody hook:* show baby Mas as a "Chosen One" in a Midwestern nursery.
- **About 1993, age 8: first computer.** He got a **Mac LC II** for his 8th birthday and taught himself to code and to take the hardware apart.
  - Wikipedia's main article says only "an Apple Macintosh". The LC II model comes from Jack Altman's page and secondary sources citing the 2016 New Yorker.
  - The LC II came out in March 1992 at $1,699, which fits the timing.
  - *Parody hook:* a small kid lit by a beige CRT in a dark bedroom. This mirrors the "computer in a dark room" cold open. A running gag could be that he has always worked in the dark.
- **Teen years: the internet as a lifeline.** He has said the internet and AOL chat rooms mattered to him while growing up gay in the Midwest. [UNVERIFIED as an exact quote]
  - *Parody hook:* at most a quick dial-up-modem sound effect. Keep it respectful.
- **About 2002, age 17: the school assembly speech.** This was at John Burroughs School in Ladue, Missouri; he graduated in 2003.
  - Some students boycotted a National Coming Out Day assembly. Altman wrote a speech overnight, gave it at assembly the next day, and got a standing ovation.
  - He also pushed for "Safe Space" placards in classrooms.
  - *Parody hook:* "The first room Mas ever turned." It is an origin moment for his persuasion powers. Keep it sincere and do not make the sexuality the joke.

### Stanford and Loopt
- **2003 to 2005: Stanford computer science.** He left after two years.
  - He later said he learned more from playing poker with classmates than from lectures. Poker taught him "how to notice patterns in people over time, how to make decisions with very imperfect information" (NYT, 2023).
  - A 2017 tweet said the same: "Learned a lot... from playing poker... the most valuable one is the importance of acting with imperfect information."
  - *Parody hook:* a dorm poker table where young Mas reads everyone's tells. The poker face can become a recurring "tell-less stare" gag.
- **2005, age 19: Loopt, first YC batch.** He co-founded Loopt (first called "Radiate") with Nick Sivo and Alok Deshpande.
  - Loopt was in YC's first batch (Summer 2005, Cambridge, Massachusetts), eight startups in all. Reddit's Steve Huffman and Alexis Ohanian were batchmates.
  - Series A: $5M from Sequoia and NEA. Series B: $8.25M (July 2007). Total raised was over $30M.
  - *Parody hook:* a "class photo" of the first YC batch with Reddit cameos, and a young Paul Graham figure handing out ramen.
- **Sept 2006: Boost Mobile launch.** Loopt launched on Boost Mobile with the tagline **"Where you at?"** Sprint followed in 2007 and Verizon in 2008.
  - *Parody hook:* a cheesy mid-2000s phone ad with Mas saying "Where you at?"
- **June 9, 2008: WWDC keynote with Steve Jobs.** At 23, Altman went on stage during Jobs' keynote (the iPhone 3G event) to demo Loopt for iPhone. The app went live July 11, 2008.
  - He wore **two layered, bright polo shirts, both collars up**. It is still a tech meme, and John Gruber mocked it the same day.
  - Colors are usually described as green plus pink or red. [exact colors UNVERIFIED]
  - Right after launch there was an SMS-spam flap: the app texted invites to users' contacts, and this was fixed by July 17, 2008.
  - *Parody hook:* this is the best visual of the era. Mas walks on in a double-popped collar next to a black-turtleneck "Jobs" figure. A later running gag could add one more collar for every jump in status.
- **Aug 2008: Paul Graham's "A Fundraising Survival Guide".** Verbatim: *"Sam Altman has it. You could parachute him into an island full of cannibals and come back in 5 years and he'd be the king. If you're Sam Altman, you don't have to be profitable to convey to investors that you'll succeed with or without them. (He wasn't, and he did.)"*
  - *Parody hook:* **an intro sequence built on this line.** Mas parachutes onto a cannibal island, then a hard cut shows him crowned on a throne of skulls, or of server racks.
- **Apr 2009: Paul Graham's "Five Founders" essay.** Graham ranks Sam alongside Steve Jobs, Larry Page and Sergey Brin, TJ Rodgers and Paul Buchheit. Verbatim: *"On questions of design, I ask 'What would Steve do?' but on questions of strategy or ambition I ask 'What would Sama do?'"*
  - Graham elsewhere, often quoted: *"Within about three minutes of meeting him, I remember thinking 'Ah, so this is what Bill Gates must have been like when he was 19.'"*
  - *Parody hook:* "WWSD?" wristbands.
- **July 2010:** Loopt reached 4M registered users.
- **Loopt-era turmoil.** Per the WSJ (Dec 2023, "Sam Altman's Knack for Dodging Bullets"), senior Loopt employees twice asked the board to replace Altman as CEO, citing chaos and side projects.
  - *Parody hook:* "Firing #1" in a running gag where every attempt to fire Mas fails. It sets up the Ep1 climax.
- **2011:** He became a part-time partner at YC.
- **March 2012: Loopt sold.** Green Dot, a prepaid debit card company, bought Loopt for **$43.4M**, of which $9.8M was set aside for employee retention.
  - *Parody hook:* the "future of location" sold to a prepaid gift-card company, played as an anticlimax.
- **Apr 2012: Hydrazine Capital.** Sam and Jack Altman started a $21M fund, most of it from **Peter Thiel**.
  - *Parody hook:* hydrazine is toxic rocket fuel. The fund's name is a character note.

### The Y Combinator era
- **Feb 21, 2014: YC president.** Paul Graham's post "Sam Altman for President" named Sam as YC president, starting with the next batch.
  - Graham called him *"one of those rare people who manage to be both fearsomely effective and yet fundamentally benevolent."*
  - Graham said the recruiting effort had started in 2012.
  - *Parody hook:* a coronation. Graham hands over a crown, or a bowl of ramen.
- **Fall 2014:** Altman taught Stanford CS183B, "How to Start a Startup". [UNVERIFIED]
- **Oct 24, 2014: Musk at MIT's AeroAstro symposium.** He said *"With artificial intelligence we are summoning the demon"* and made the pentagram-and-holy-water comparison.
  - *Parody hook:* Nole literally drawing a pentagram on a whiteboard.
- **Nov 2014: Reddit CEO for eight days.** Altman was Reddit's interim CEO for **8 days** after Yishan Wong left and before Ellen Pao took over.
  - In July 2015, in a Reddit thread titled "What's the best long con you ever pulled?", Wong spun a tongue-in-cheek tale. In it, the founders and Altman engineered Reddit's escape from Condé Nast.
  - The exact wording of his sign-off and any reply from Sam are [UNVERIFIED].
  - *Parody hook:* Mas's "8-day reign" gag, and the "it's all a long con" conspiracy board.
- **Feb 2015: Altman's blog post "Machine intelligence, part 1".** It includes *"Development of superhuman machine intelligence (SMI) is probably the greatest threat to the continued existence of humanity."* [exact date and line recalled, not re-fetched]
- **May 2015: the pitch email to Musk.** Altman emailed Musk proposing that *"it would be good for YC to start a Manhattan Project for AI,"* structured as a nonprofit but paying startup-level salaries. Musk replied: *"Probably worth a conversation."*
  - *Parody hook:* the smallest reply in history to the biggest idea.
- **June 2015: the doom quote.** At a tech conference Altman said: *"I think that AI will probably, most likely, sort of lead to the end of the world. But in the meantime, there will be great companies created with serious machine learning."*
  - *Parody hook:* **a candidate for the cold-open quote**, delivered in the dark room.
- **July 2015: Musk's 44th birthday in Napa.** At a 3-day party, by a firepit next to the pool, Larry Page called Musk a **"speciesist"** for favoring humans over digital life.
  - At the 2026 trial Musk said: "The reason OpenAI exists is because Larry Page called me a speciesist."
  - The two soon stopped speaking. Musk has said the fight over recruiting Ilya ended the friendship.
  - *Parody hook:* a Google-founder parody calls Nole "SPECIESIST!" and the sound effect is a record scratch.
- **July 2015, a few weeks later: the Rosewood dinner.** A private room at the Rosewood Sand Hill in Menlo Park, about 20 minutes from Google.
  - Present: Altman, Musk, Greg Brockman (who had left Stripe in May 2015), Ilya Sutskever and Dario Amodei (both at Google then), and Chris Clark (a Loopt alumnus), among others.
  - *Parody hook:* **a "heist crew assembles" scene.** Each character enters with a name-card flash. This fits the intro montage well.
- **Oct 2015: YC expands.** YC Research launched as a nonprofit lab funded with **$10M of Sam's money**; its work later included the UBI study. YC Continuity launched as a **$700M** growth fund.
- **Nov 22, 2015: Musk's $1B email.** Musk wrote that raising only $100M would sound hopeless and pushed for a **$1B** pledge, saying he would cover whatever others did not provide.
- **Dec 11, 2015: OpenAI announced.** It launched as a nonprofit (Wikipedia lists incorporation on Dec 8).
  - Co-chairs: Altman and Musk. Greg Brockman was CTO and Ilya Sutskever was research director.
  - Founding researchers included Andrej Karpathy, John Schulman, Wojciech Zaremba, Durk Kingma, Trevor Blackwell, Vicki Cheung and Pamela Vagata.
  - The $1B was pledged by Musk, Altman, Brockman, Reid Hoffman, Jessica Livingston, Peter Thiel, AWS, Infosys and YC Research.
  - By about 2021, only **$133M** had arrived. Musk gave about **$44–45M**; he has claimed $100M at other times.
  - The first "office" was Brockman's apartment.
  - Greg reportedly took the recruits on a Napa offsite and set a deadline for yes/no answers. [UNVERIFIED details]
  - *Parody hook:* "$1 BILLION!" flashes on screen, then a receipt prints out "…$133M."
- **Recruiting Ilya: the "toughest recruiting battle".** Musk called it "one of the toughest recruiting battles I've ever had." Ilya went back and forth between Demis Hassabis and Google on one side and Musk on the other.
  - Ilya was paid **$1.9M in 2016** (NYT, from tax filings) and said he had turned down offers worth multiples of that.
  - *Parody hook:* a tug-of-war over Alyi.
- **Jan 2016: "less open".** Ilya emailed that as they got closer to AI, it would make sense to be "less open... it's totally OK to not share the science." Musk replied **"Yup."** This became public in OpenAI's March 2024 blog post. [wording from memory]
  - *Parody hook:* the "Open" in OpenAI is quietly peeled off the sign.
- **Apr 2016:** OpenAI Gym launched. **Dec 2016:** Universe launched.
- **Aug 2016: first DGX-1.** Nvidia gave OpenAI its first DGX-1. There is a well-known photo of Jensen Huang presenting it, with Musk nearby and a signed message on the machine. [signature wording UNVERIFIED]
  - *Parody hook:* a leather-jacket GPU dealer hands over "the first box."
- **June 2016: safety paper.** "Concrete Problems in AI Safety" came out (Dario Amodei, Chris Olah and others, written at Google). Dario joined OpenAI later in 2016.
- **Sept 2016: YC Group and the UBI study.** Sam became president of the YC Group. The Oakland basic-income pilot started (about 100 families, $1,500 a month).
- **Oct 2016: New Yorker profile.** Tad Friend's "Sam Altman's Manifest Destiny" ran online; the print title was "Adding a Zero".
  - The prepper quote, verbatim: *"I have guns, gold, potassium iodide, antibiotics, batteries, water, gas masks from the Israeli Defense Force, and a big patch of land in Big Sur I can fly to."* He said this at a party of Silicon Valley people while listing apocalypse scenarios (a lab-made virus, AI attacking us, nuclear war).
  - His backup plan for a pandemic was to fly with **Peter Thiel** to Thiel's house in **New Zealand**. Altman later said he was joking.
  - Paul Graham: *"Sam is extremely good at becoming powerful."*
  - Thiel: *"Sam is not particularly religious, but he is culturally very Jewish—an optimist yet a survivalist, with a sense that things can always go deeply wrong..."*
  - Altman on neural nets: *"you have no idea what they're doing, and they can't tell you."*
  - Also attributed to the same article, not confirmed: an anecdote about "two tech billionaires" secretly funding scientists to break humanity out of the simulation, and a line about Altman owning several cars including McLarens. [both UNVERIFIED]
  - *Parody hook:* **a Big Sur bunker reveal.** Mas opens a closet: guns, gold bars, a gas mask, potassium iodide, and a helicopter out the window. This is a strong recurring set piece.
- **June 2017:** Karpathy left OpenAI to become Tesla's Director of AI.
- **July 2017:** Altman floated a California political platform ("The United Slate") and reportedly considered running for governor. [UNVERIFIED]
- **Aug 11, 2017: the Dendi match.** OpenAI's 1v1 bot beat pro player Dendi at The International 2017 in Seattle. Brockman said it learned from "two weeks of real time" of self-play.
  - The same day Musk tweeted: *"If you're not concerned about AI safety, you should be. Vastly more risk than North Korea,"* with a poster reading "In the end the machines will win."
  - *Parody hook:* an esports arena with the OpenAI Five bots as characters.
- **Late Aug 2017: the Model 3 meeting** (per Brockman's testimony, May 2026).
  - Musk **gave each co-founder a Tesla Model 3**, and **Ilya commissioned a painting of a Tesla** as a return gift.
  - When told he would not get control, Musk sat silently, then said **"I decline."** He reportedly circled the table, **grabbed the painting**, and stormed toward the exit before turning back with a parting demand.
  - Brockman called Musk's behavior "mean."
  - *Parody hook:* **a comedy gold scene.** Nole stomps out holding Alyi's Tesla painting.
- **Sept 20, 2017: the "Honest Thoughts" email.** Ilya and Greg wrote to Musk and Altman.
  - Verbatim: *"The goal of OpenAI is to make the future good and to avoid an AGI dictatorship... it is a bad idea to create a structure where you could become a dictator if you chose to."*
  - To Sam they asked **why the CEO title was so important to him**.
  - Musk replied within minutes: *"Guys, I've had enough. This is the final straw... I will no longer fund OpenAI... Discussions are over."*
  - Sam replied: ***"i remain enthusiastic about the non-profit structure!"*** (all lowercase)
  - *Parody hook:* **an email-thread-as-soap-opera scene.** Sam's cheerful lowercase reply lands in the middle of the explosion.
- **Late 2017: the control fight.** Musk wanted majority equity, initial board control and the CEO role, and held back funding while he pushed. **Reid Hoffman covered salaries and operations** in the gap. A for-profit entity was discussed.
- **Dec 2017: Altman's blog post "The Merge".** Quotes: *"The merge has already started, and we are a few years in."* *"We will be the first species ever to design our own descendants."* *"Double exponential functions get away from you fast."* [exact date recalled as Dec 7, 2017]
- **Jan 2018: "cash cow".** Musk proposed OpenAI "attach to Tesla as its cash cow" and forwarded a Karpathy note arguing Tesla was the only path. [Karpathy wording UNVERIFIED]
- **Feb 20, 2018: Musk leaves.** Musk held a goodbye all-hands. The official reason was a conflict of interest with Tesla's AI work, and most staff "didn't entirely buy the story" (Semafor). His words: OpenAI is on "a path of certain failure." OpenAI announced the departure publicly on Feb 21.
  - Later books say Musk called a researcher who challenged him a "jackass". [UNVERIFIED this session]
  - Context gags from the same months: the **Falcon Heavy / Starman Roadster** launch was Feb 6, 2018. The Boring Company sold its "Not a Flamethrower" starting Jan 2018. The **"funding secured"** tweet was Aug 7, 2018. Musk smoked on **Joe Rogan's** show in Sept 2018.
- **Apr 9, 2018: the OpenAI Charter.** It includes the clause that if a "value-aligned, safety-conscious project" gets close to AGI first, OpenAI will "stop competing with and start assisting" it. [paraphrase from memory]
  - *Parody hook:* a foreshadowing prop, the "assist clause" framed on the wall, for the late-season episodes.
- **June 2018: GPT-1** ("Improving Language Understanding by Generative Pre-Training"). OpenAI Five moved to 5v5.
- **Aug 2018: The International 2018 (Vancouver).** OpenAI Five lost to paiN Gaming and a Chinese all-star team.
  - The bots reportedly announced their own win probability in chat, e.g. "We estimate the probability of winning to be above 95%." [UNVERIFIED]
  - Training ran at about 180 years of games per day on 256 GPUs and 128,000 CPU cores.
- **Dec 26, 2018: Musk's "0%" email.** Musk emailed Sam, Greg and Ilya: *"Even raising several hundred million won't be enough. This needs billions per year immediately or forget it."* He put OpenAI's chance of being relevant against DeepMind and Google, without drastic change, at **0%**.
- **Feb 14, 2019: GPT-2 withheld.** GPT-2 was announced but held back as too dangerous to release in full. The famous sample text is about scientists finding **unicorns in the Andes**. [sample content from memory]
  - *Parody hook:* a Valentine's Day "too dangerous" press conference with a unicorn in the room.
- **Mar 8–11, 2019: YC exit and OpenAI LP.** Altman left the YC presidency.
  - The WaPo (Nov 2023) reported that Paul Graham flew from the UK to fire him.
  - Graham disputes that. His account is that Jessica Livingston told Sam to choose between YC and OpenAI, and Sam agreed to find a successor.
  - On **Mar 11** OpenAI announced **OpenAI LP**, a "capped-profit" company where first-round returns are capped at **100x**. Khosla Ventures and Reid Hoffman's foundation invested.
  - Sam became CEO and took **no equity**.
  - *Parody hook:* "Firing #2 (disputed)." A "100x cap" in giant font, followed by an eyebrow raise.
- **Apr 13, 2019:** OpenAI Five beat world champions **OG 2–0** in San Francisco. In the public Arena afterward it won **99.4%** of 42,729 games.
- **July 2019: Microsoft invests $1B.** OpenAI moved to Azure. Satya Nadella enters the story. [exact date recalled as July 22, 2019]
- **Oct 2019, just past scope:** Wojciech Zaremba's robotics team's Dactyl hand solved a Rubik's Cube.
- **Nov 2019: the office wedding.** Brockman married at the OpenAI office, with flowers arranged in the OpenAI logo. **Ilya officiated** and **a robot hand was the ring bearer** (MIT Technology Review, Feb 2020).
  - *Parody hook:* a quirky flashback gag. Keep the spouse offscreen and unnamed.

## B. Recent facts useful for framing flashbacks (after 2019, verified)

- **Musk v. Altman trial.** Jury selection began Apr 27, 2026. Musk sought **$134B**.
  - On **May 18, 2026** the jury took under 2 hours to find the claims barred by the statute of limitations, and Judge Yvonne Gonzalez Rogers dismissed the case.
  - Musk texted Brockman before trial: "By the end of this week, you and Sam will be the most hated" (not shown to the jury).
  - Musk's lawyer in closing: "OpenAI exists because Larry Page called me a speciesist."
  - *Parody hook:* use the trial as a **framing device**, so flashbacks are "testimony".
- **Ilya at OpenAI, 2022–2023.** At OpenAI's 2022 holiday party (California Academy of Sciences) he led chants of **"Feel the AGI!"**, and there was a Slack emoji for it. At a leadership retreat he **burned a wooden effigy** representing unaligned AI (Hao and Warzel, The Atlantic, Nov 2023).
  - In 2023 he told staff: **"We're definitely going to build a bunker before we release AGI,"** and added that getting in would be optional (Karen Hao, *Empire of AI*).
  - Feb 2022 tweet: "it may be that today's large neural networks are slightly conscious."
- **Thiel's warning, Nov 2023** (per Keach Hagey's *The Optimist*): at a dinner in Los Angeles, Thiel told Altman, *"You don't understand how Eliezer has programmed half the people in your company to believe in that stuff."*
- **New Yorker, Apr 2026.** A new profile by Ronan Farrow and Andrew Marantz. It contains contested allegations; details are excluded here.
- **Where people are now.**
  - Karpathy joined **Anthropic** (CNBC, May 19, 2026).
  - Schulman went to Anthropic in Aug 2024, then became chief scientist at Thinking Machines Lab in Feb 2025.
  - Ilya left OpenAI in May 2024, founded SSI in June 2024, and has been SSI's CEO since 2025.
  - Dario and Anthropic were in a **Pentagon dispute** in Feb–Mar 2026. Anthropic refused to lift bans on mass surveillance and autonomous weapons, was labeled a "supply-chain risk," and won an injunction on Mar 26, 2026.
