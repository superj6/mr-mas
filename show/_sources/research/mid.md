# Mr. Mas: research dossier for 2019–2023 (read-only pass)

**Conventions.** Times are Pacific (PT) unless marked otherwise. I checked items against news reports and Wikipedia and decoded some tweet timestamps from tweet IDs, which is marked "[ID-decoded]". [UNVERIFIED] means the search budget ran out before I could confirm it, or I only had secondary paraphrase. Each event ends with a "Parody hook:" line. Name suggestions follow the show's pattern of reversals and syllable swaps (Mas Manalt, Mario, Nole, Alyi). They are optional.

**Deliberately excluded:**
- Annie Altman's allegations and lawsuit (family matter, unproven).
- Anyone's marriages, partners or children, including Sam's marriage and Tasha McCauley's spouse.
- Greg Brockman's 2019 wedding at the OpenAI office. It is public and harmless, but personal, so it is left out unless the user opts in.
- Musk's family life.
- SBF's criminal case, beyond the public fact that FTX invested in Anthropic.
- Emmett Shear's resurfaced old tweets.
- Detailed 2024 claims about Sam's conduct ("psychological abuse" and similar). I suggest staying with the board's own public wording, "not consistently candid."
- Speculation about why Larry Summers left the board in 2025 (see the pointers section).

## 3. THE BLIP, day by day (Nov 16–29, 2023)

**Board at the time (6 members):** Sam Altman (CEO), Greg Brockman (Chairman/President), Ilya Sutskever (Chief Scientist), Adam D'Angelo (Quora CEO), Tasha McCauley (GeoSim Systems CEO, formerly RAND), Helen Toner (Georgetown CSET). **The four non-executive members, Ilya, Adam, Tasha and Helen, voted Sam out** and removed Greg as chair.

**Thu Nov 16**
- At the **APEC CEO Summit** (SF), Sam says: "Four times now in the history of OpenAI, the most recent time was just in the last couple weeks, I've gotten to be in the room when we sort of **push the veil of ignorance back and the frontier of discovery forward**."
- Evening: **Ilya texts Sam asking to talk at noon Friday.**
- **Mira Murati is told Thursday night** and agrees to become interim CEO.
- **Parody hook:** The ultimate "day before" line. It is my top pick for the intro's cold-open quote, delivered in the dark room with the Orb glowing. The line is grandiose and the firing comes the next day.

**Fri Nov 17**

- **12:19pm:** Ilya texts Greg asking for a quick call.
- **12:23pm:** On a Google Meet, Greg is told he is removed from the board but keeps his role, and that Sam is fired.

## 4. Persona tropes (as of 2019–2023)

Naming follows the reversal pattern. Parody names are optional suggestions.

- **Satya Nadella** ("Aytas"). **The Serene Landlord / Zen Kingmaker.** Soft-spoken, calm, always smiling, and three moves ahead. He owns the servers everyone lives in. Signature lines: "we made them dance," "We love you guys" (with a hearty laugh), and "below them, above them, around them." He hires Mas at midnight without raising his voice. He's a known cricket fan (usable as a sport metaphor). **Avoid accent humor**; make the comedy about his unflappable power.
- **Mira Murati** ("Arim"). **The Unflappable Understudy.** A Tesla Model X and Leap Motion alum who became CTO in 2022. Composed, diplomatic, the steady hand.
