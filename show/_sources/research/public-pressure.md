## 1. The answer in ten lines

1. **The public moved from curious to against, and the data center is where that shows.** In Aug 2025, Americans were split on a data center near home (about 43% for, 42% against). By Aug 2026, **75%** were against [V✓ Heatmap *(std ✓)*: fielded Aug 8–13, n = 2,045]. Pew, Gallup, NYT/Siena and Marist all found majorities against a nearby data center in 2026, and NBC found only 11% more likely to back a candidate who supports one.
2. **Who objects changes over the four years:**
   - 2022–23: creatives (ArtStation, the WGA and SAG-AFTRA strikes).
   - 2024: small safety pickets (PauseAI).
   - 2025: local land-use fights plus electricity bills.
   - 2026: bipartisan statehouses, governors, and hundreds marching on the labs.
3. **The fights are countable.** Data Center Watch's tallies of projects blocked or delayed:

   | Period | Blocked or delayed |
   |---|---|
   | 2023 → Mar 2025 | $64B |
   | One quarter of 2025 | $98B |
   | Q1 2026 | $130B |
   | Q2 2026 | $68B |

   Opposition groups number **843 across 49 states** [V✓].
4. **Local "no" rarely ends a project. It reroutes it.** Developers sue and settle (Saline Township, the Michigan GATESTAR site), move to county land with private water (Tucson → Pima County), or withdraw and keep the option to refile (Google in Indianapolis). The navigable obstacle is the workaround, and the workaround produces the next moratorium.
5. **Bills turned the fight into electoral politics.** Georgia's regulators flipped (Nov 2025). Both parties now run against data-center costs. RUMPT moved first: on Jan 12, 2026 he posted that Microsoft would make changes so Americans don't "pick up the tab," and that tech must "pay their own way" [H✓ *(std ✓)*]. The labs pledged within days: MACROSOFT "pay our way" (Jan 13), NopeAI "pay its own way" (Jan 20). RUMPT's Ratepayer Pledge (Mar 4) is already in the plan.
6. **In 2026 RUMPT is almost alone.** His own party's governor froze Texas permits (Sep 21). His own county paused data centers (Sep 24). He answers with insults and, on the record, **China**: "China could not be happier with this anti Data Center movement" (Aug 31) and "the only one that is happy about it is China" (Sep 14). That makes him a verified CHINA CARD player, if the card is approved.
7. **Democrats split too.** MOSWEN signs seven laws and attacks RUMPT (Sep 21). Virginia's governor bans by-right approvals but refuses a pause (Sep 18–23). A Democratic senator blocks the House's 417–3 ratepayer bill as too weak (Sep 17). SREDNAS wants a national moratorium.
8. **The lab protests now quote the labs.** The Sep 17 march chalked "Your CEO agrees that AI will kill everyone" outside NopeAI. The labs' own "pace" language has become the protesters' demand. It's a mirror for Mas, not a mob.
9. **Consumer and creative backlash forces retreats you can film:** ads pulled, AI-first memos walked back, replies closed, likeness guardrails added after an actor complains, AI performers barred from the Oscars. The joke is always the retreat, never the crowd.
10. **Guardrails decide what's usable.** Hunger strikes are OUT (X3). Stop AI is HOLD (violence adjacency, a trial of private individuals). Memphis/Southaven is BG (litigation). Election results are X11. Protesters are never named and never the butt.

## 3. The five patterns: how pressure gets navigated

Each pattern is a thriller move: pressure, then the powerful's workaround, then new pressure. The joke lands on the workaround.

| # | Pressure | Workaround (the navigable obstacle) | Record | Who's roasted |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P1 · THE VOTE → THE WORKAROUND** | A council or township votes no | The developer sues and settles, relocates, withdraws before the vote, or builds under another jurisdiction | Saline Twp no (Sep 11, 2025) → suit within days (reported Sep 19) → settled (Oct 21) → GATESTAR built → 19+ Michigan moratoria. Tucson 7–0 → county land → construction → county moratorium. Google withdraws "minutes" before the Indianapolis vote | NopeAI (GATESTAR), NOZAMA-linked developer, ELGOOG, MACROSOFT |
