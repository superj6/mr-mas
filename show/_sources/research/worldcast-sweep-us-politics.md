- Article text fetched directly (NBC, CNBC, Guardian, USA Today, Fortune, ABC, CA.gov, senate.gov, house.gov).

**Tags.**
- **[P]** Primary source: a transcript, a Truth Social post or a Federal Register entry. The quote is verbatim.
- **[V]** Confirmed from article text fetched this session.
- **[H]** Confirmed by headline and date only.
- **[K]** Widely reported, but I did not re-check it.
- **[SINGLE]** Only one outlet reported it.
- **[UNVERIFIED]** No source found.
