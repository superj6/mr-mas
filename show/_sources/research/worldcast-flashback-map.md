## 2. The flashback map

**Rules for the map:**
- 1–3 flashbacks per episode. Each one enters on a present-day trigger and exits on the present-day consequence it explains.
- Episode 1 runs at most 20s of flashback in total. The intro stays a trailer: every backstory shot in bars 3–8 is a promise paid later (see the 2F table).

**Rules that hold across every tier:**
1. **POV rim.** A 6px frame border in the narrator's accent color: Gerg #39FF88, Alyi #FF6A1A, Mario #1F3A93, Nole #E0301E, Mas cyan #3FE6FF, Nesnej #7CFF4F, Luap #FF7F2A, Tasya #5B6B8C. The model's POV (ep12) has all rims converging on cyan.
2. **Truth labels on screen.**
   - `(REPORTED)`: Tpool revolts.
   - `(DISPUTED)`: YC exit.
   - `HIS VERSION`: ep6 Nole.
   - `RECONSTRUCTED`: any [K] detail, such as the auction room or the Charter wording.
   - Unreliable versions get their own render style: KORG album art for Nole, six fingers for Mas.
3. **Caption case tells the age.**
   - 1993: ALL CAPS, hidden until ep12.
   - 2002: sentence case, the sincere speech.
   - 2003 onward: lowercase, because poker taught him.
   - The ep7 "I" is the first slip back.
4. **The cup that never ripples, in every era.** A juice box (1993), a poker-night beer, a water bottle on the WWDC stage, a crystal glass at the Woodrose. **There is no glass at the 2002 assembly**, before he had the poker face.
5. **The Orb's memory rule.** The iris replay can only show what happened after 2019. Earlier memories need another device. From ep9 onward the Orb starts replaying things unprompted, which foreshadows the model studying Mas.
6. **Mas never freezes**, even in memory, while other characters freeze into two-tone. The exception is ep9's assembly, where nobody freezes and nothing is a joke.
7. **Budget.**
   - ep1: at most 20s of flashback in total.
   - ep2–11: 60–150s each, and no single flashback over 60s except the Rashomon (≤90s).
   - Every flashback exits on a present-day object that matches its entry (napkin to napkin, box to box, receipt to check).
8. **Exclusions inside flashbacks:**
   - No family on screen (the Hydrazine co-founder, the wedding spouse, Dario's father).
   - No drawn weapons (the prepper "guns" is text only).
   - No physical staging of "hit me."
   - Cannibals are only ever hoodie founders.
   - No content from the 2023 memo or the Apr 2026 New Yorker allegations.
   - The Tpool and YC stories carry only their public, labeled versions.
