1. **The Trump equivalent is DLANOD J. PMURT, called "President PMURT."** Alternates are **PRUMT** and **THE PODIUM**. THE PODIUM stays as his prop and codename.
2. **Entry plan:**
   - Ep1: silhouette only. **EOJ NEDIB** is the Ep1 president.
   - Ep2: voice and hands only, with his face hidden.
   - **Ep3 button:** THE PODIUM turns around to reveal his face.
   - Ep4: his first name card, `RENAMED IT.`

   - The "power stays offscreen" rule is retired for the President. THE PENTAGON building-giant stays.
   - The Ep7 line "THE PODIUM orders agencies off CLOD" becomes PMURT's HTURT meteor.
