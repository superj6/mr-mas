   - He is fired from OpenAI's superalignment team (Apr 2024).
   - He publishes *Situational Awareness* in June 2024, headed "Dedicated to Ilya Sutskever." It says "AGI by 2027 is strikingly plausible" and that "by 27/28 we'll get some form of government AGI project" [V · situational-awareness.ai].

By Feb 2026 Claude is "the only AI model used in classified missions," and its access runs "through its interoperability with Palantir" [V·wiki].    - **Use:** in Ep3, MARIO signs PLANTAIR paperwork in the same six weeks that Mas signs his own defense paperwork (item 5). In Ep7, the red lines Mario paints run along PLANTAIR's pipes. Paperwork only.

   - **Use:** it rides Ep7's Big Game ad war and cross-references [public-pressure U9](public-pressure.md). The game whose ad war made Mas post "I laughed" also carries a doorbell that finds dogs, and the public hears the word *surveillance* less than three weeks before Mario paints `NO MASS DOMESTIC SURVEILLANCE`. *(Date correction, 2026-09-26: Mas's post was Feb 4, the day Anthropic released the campaign [H: TechCrunch, Entrepreneur, Feb 4]; the game and Ring's ad were Sun Feb 8.)* The pole that gets dropped carries a `WHY COMBINATOR S17` sticker: Mas's own school graduated it.

### 3c. How it maps onto public feeling about AI surveillance

Flock is the public's picture of "AI surveillance": a black box on a pole at the end of your street. Three verified moments trace the mood:
1. The protest sign "Thiel gets rich, we get watched." (Sep 2025) [V].
2. The lost-dog ad reaction (Feb 2026) [V·wiki].
3. A big-city police department walking away over "civil liberties" (Jul 2026) [V].

The Pentagon fight is the same argument at national scale.
