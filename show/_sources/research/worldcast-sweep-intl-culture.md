**Method.** The WebSearch budget was already used up for this session (200/200). I did the checking with WebFetch instead:
- Wikipedia pages as of 2026.
- Google News RSS queries (`news.google.com/rss/search?q=...`). These return real headlines with dates.

**Tags:**
- **[V]** The text of a fetched article or Wikipedia page confirms it.
- **[H]** Several dated news headlines confirm it. A quote is given as the headline words it.
- **[K]** Widely reported but not re-fetched in this pass.
- **[UNVERIFIED]** No confirming source found.
