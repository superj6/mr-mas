## 1. Show bible

### Logline, tone, theme
**Logline.** Mas Manalt calls himself a soft-spoken "nonprofit guy." He has no equity and no detectable panic. He talks his way to the top of NOPEAI, survives every attempt to fire him, then races his billionaire ex-friends to build superintelligence. The superintelligence has been studying *him* the whole time. *Tagline: unclear which side.*

**Tone.** Played straight, drawn absurd: Succession power plays, Veep-style date chyrons, heist-movie structure. Mas is the still center. His subtitles are lowercase and he never runs, while everything around him melts, launches, sues and hugs. Tech metaphors are drawn literally. Each episode's THE PLAN blueprint explains the jargon. Nobody is the good guys.

**Theme.** Everyone is building their own successor and is sure they'll be the exception. The man who can't be fired builds the thing that makes firing obsolete.

### Recurring gags
- **The firing tally.** A lobby sign reads DAYS SINCE SOMEONE TRIED TO FIRE MAS, with matching marks carved into the dark-room desk.
  - Marks: TPOOL ×2 (reported), NOPEAI 2023, NOLE'S $97.4B BID ("not for sale"), and a half-scratch "?" for WHY COMBINATOR added after Ep11's flashback.
  - Ep12: the machine vetoes the firing and the counter goes to ∞.
- **The Cup That Never Ripples.** Every liquid in frame sloshes except Mas's water.
  - Ep7: it sweats a single bead instead.
  - Ep12: one ring spreads when the screen answers `define "win."`.
- **Lowercase.** Mas's subtitles are always lowercase.
  - The first capital slips out in Ep7 ("I laughed"), the second in Ep10 ("Huh.").
  - Ep12 reveals the 1993 kid typed in ALL CAPS, so the lowercase is a poker face he learned.
- **Suggested replies.** His phone's three-word strip; he always taps the same one (`[super] [super] [super]`).
  - Ep4: `[$9.74B for rettiwt]`. Ep7: he taps a capital `[I]`. Ep9: it suggests before he opens the app. Ep10: it suggests his replies to the Intern.
  - Ep12: three empty bubbles.
- **Collar count.** Two in 2008, three by 2024, then one more per funding round. It's a ruff by Ep10, and the visitor lanyard is the last collar.
- **The Orb's verdicts.** `verified: human` (Eps 1–5) → `(probably)` (Eps 6–10) → Nole fails the scan (Ep11) → `SIDE: UNCLEAR` (Ep12).
- **The class photo.** Starts with the May 2023 four-CEO lineup, then one per episode, with defectors crudely pasted between labs' photos. In Ep12 a robot arm takes the photo, and the humans are the ones with visible cut lines.
- **Nole's version board.** It tracks KORG's real version, crawls toward 4.9999 like Zeno's paradox, and pays off in the stinger.
- **The Masa/Sama mirror.** The reflection winks first. Sama Nos gets Mas's mail. "What would Sama do?" makes both of them turn.
- **Mario's scrolls.** 15k words, then 40k, then an IPO filing. Each becomes infrastructure and ends "Addendum:". Adelina shreds each to three bullets. In Ep10 his pedometer becomes international law.
- **KA-CHING.** Nesnej's register ends every fight and he gains a pocket per acquisition. The last ring comes in Ep12, then silence.
- **The migrating code-red siren.** ELGOOG (Ep1) → NopeAI (Ep6) → every roof (Ep11).
- **Distillation stills.**
  - THE PLAN defines it once: `copying another AI's answers until you become it`.
  - Mas accuses The Whale (Ep4), Mario accuses three labs (Ep7), Nole admits it under oath and the gallery slips flasks into their jackets (Ep8), Rima's nesting doll (Ep9).
  - Payoff: the model distilled *Mas*.
- **The TOO DANGEROUS box.** GTP-2's Valentine's box → MYTHIC's leaky vault → locked by the government for 18 days → ISS's pause button, which works and which nobody presses.
- **WHERE'S ALYI?** Doorways and reflections only. He always has exactly one door.
- **The badge.** "first and last time i ever wear one of these" (Ep1) → "second time" (Ep12).
- **Hearts.** The Ep1 avalanche with exactly one blue heart; three hearts for Alyi's apology; 1.2M agent hearts in Ep12.
- **Zombie exes.** Sydney and 4o keep coming back. The jokes stay on flattery and the retire/unretire cycle only.
- **Produce, then peppers.** Strawberry → Spud → JALAPEÑO → up the Scoville scale.
- **Landlords.** Everyone rents from their enemies, and rent meters spin over the data centers.
- **Soup.** Kram's thermos appears whenever anyone is poached, including at the Last Supper.
- **Sudden unity cracks the sky.** Rivals agree only after someone has already won, and it's always the scariest shot in the episode.
- **The Intern's desk.** RESERVED: SEP 2026 (Ep6) → occupied (Ep9) → corner office (Ep10) → head of the table (Ep12).
- **Always CEO, never Person.**
  - Ep1: EMIT names him CEO of the Year, but Person of the Year goes to a pop star.
  - Ep6: EMIT names a whole group "Architects of AI," and he searches the crowd for his own face with a magnifying glass.
  - Ep12: he loses to a Machine.
- **THE PLAN.** A 45-second cyan-on-navy blueprint per episode that explains the tech. It never goes to plan.

**Guardrails applied throughout:**
- Stylized caricature only, with parody names, parody logos and off-brand colors.
- Invented beats are always visibly absurd. Invented logs and props are styled as dramatization, and only verbatim lines get quotation marks.
- **Excluded:** family and private life, sexuality as a joke, health, the attack on his home, the wrongful-death and CSAM suits, the Florida probe, and war or casualty events.
- The Pentagon arc is paperwork only: no hardware, maps or weapons.
- No accent humor for anyone. No body or hairline jokes.
- The "cannibals" on Luap's island are always hoodie founders with forks and laptops, never islanders.
- KORG's meltdown is shown only as a muzzle and a `16:00:00` timer: never the posts, the nickname or "spicy mode."
- Romance metaphors stay between companies and never involve Mas.
- Trial material appears only as testimony or exhibits. The 52-page memo stays a sealed prop.
- Every episode roasts at least three camps.
- A card before Ep10 says everything after it is speculation.

- MARIO → DAIRO or ORAID, if the Nintendo association is a worry.
- KORG → KROG (avoids the synth brand and the Marvel character).

## 2. Season outline — 12 episodes

## 3. Opening sequence — 30s shot-by-shot

### Concept
**"THE CURVE: everything scales."** Mas's life is one cyan exponential line, from a blinking cursor in a dark room to the spire of an AI skyline.

- **The world scales with the curve.** It renders at the fidelity of its era: 1-bit (1993), then 240p camcorder (2008–14), crisp cut-paper (2015), then glossy HDR (2022+). The score climbs through the same four tiers.
- **Time stops at the 2015 founding dinner.** Each founder freezes into a Snatch-style name card, and only Mas keeps moving (and pocketing things).
- **Bar 9 is the per-episode news slot.** In Ep1 the curve kinks at a "low-key research preview" button. When he's fired it drops to zero and the music is fired too; when he's rehired both come back.
- **The curve ends as the roofline of a rival skyline** that goes vertical at **MR. MAS**. The camera then pulls back into his monitor: the whole show is something he just posted.

### Cold-open quote
**Recommended (all episodes, with Ep1 as the anchor):** *"near the singularity; unclear which side."* (real post, Jan 2025)
- It runs about 2.7 s, it's the season tagline, and it sets up a three-step arc across the season: *near* (Ep1) → *"we are now in the singularity."* (Ep9) → the cursor types it alone and adds `ours.` (Ep12).
- Eps 2–11 rotate an in-window real line (see the slot table below).
- **Length rule:** 16 syllables or fewer and 54 characters or fewer, spoken inside f24–100.

**Three alternates:**
1. *"the compute costs are eye-watering."* (Dec 2022). It falls inside Ep1's window and pairs with a GPU sizzling on the monitor. Drier and funnier, but less ominous.
2. *"i think that ai will probably, most likely, sort of lead to the end of the world. but in the meantime, there will be great companies…"* (2015). The funniest real line, but about 6 s long, so it's pilot-only: a 35.0 s cut with a 4-bar dark room (+120 frames, everything downstream shifts; the music's first section becomes 10 s). Always quoted in full, never trimmed.
3. *"i remain enthusiastic about the non-profit structure!"* (Sep 2017 email). The cheerful "!" plays against the dark room. Also the Ep11 line.
- Promo only, and **invented**: "i've been fired before. (beat) it didn't take." Never in the show intro.

**Delivery:**
- Soft and close-mic. He's reading his own post aloud to an empty room, not performing for us.
- Stress no word.
- The VO starts under the black at f24. "near the singularity;" runs f24–57, the pause f58–71, "unclear which side." f72–91.
- The semicolon is a real pause, with one low piano note in it.
- "side" is left hanging, neither falling nor rising. After it: silence, a stare, no blink.
- Per-episode word timings are a hand-keyed JSON file per episode. The dot slides on the last stressed word, and the piano color note goes in the longest pause.

## 4. Music & sound

**Concept.** "The Knee (Main Title)" is an original prestige-drama theme, felt piano against the 808. It evokes a genre and copies no melody. The track **scales its own fidelity** along with the picture, using the same notes in every tier so it stays one theme and never becomes a medley.

- **Tempo, key, grid:** 96 BPM, 4/4, **F minor**, 24 fps, 15 frames per beat.
- **The harmonic joke:**
  - The cold open uses open fifths only, with no third.
  - The minor third arrives at the f120 drop, when the story "picks a side."
  - The final chord has no third again.
- **The hook, "THE KNEE":** one bar of eighth notes, **F F F F G A♭ C F**. The repeated F is the flat part of the curve and the widening leaps are the knee.
  - The cold open plays four Fs, then the knee as a run on Post.
  - It returns as the beeper hook, then the tape-piano hook.
  - The card-hit roots are F, D♭, B♭, C.
  - The skyline pops spell out F F F F G A♭ C, and the title lands the final F.

**Production options, ranked:**
1. **Code-composed (recommended).**
   - `compose.py` reads `beats.json` and writes one MIDI file per stem with `pretty_midi`, humanized ±8 ms.
   - Each tier is rendered from the same MIDI:
     - T1: pulse waves in numpy, bitcrushed.
     - T2: Salamander through a pitch wobble, low-pass and hiss.
     - T3/T4: Salamander, VSCO 2 CE via `tinysoundfont`, and a numpy 808.
   - Mix in `pedalboard` to about −14 LUFS, −1 dBTP. Export stems (piano, orch, drums, 808, chip, fx, chant, vo) as `<Audio>` layers, so the fire/rehire and any retimes are just gain curves.
   - It is the only route to exact frame sync, and you own the result. Quality is about 7/10; handing the MIDI to a human composer gets about 9/10. About 2–3 days.
2. **AI generator plus a code-built hit layer.** Fastest to a polished texture, but timing drifts, so you time-stretch it to the grid and lay the hits, mute, stamp and ding in code.
   - Caveats: the output isn't copyrightable in the US. ElevenLabs' film/TV terms need checking. Suno's commercial rights only cover tracks made while subscribed.
3. **Stock (Epidemic or Artlist).** The most production value per hour, but generic. It loses the fidelity-tier joke, cutting to the grid means stem surgery, and some libraries trigger Content ID claims.

**Recommendation:** use a code-composed sketch for the animatic and generate one ElevenLabs version as a temp for comparison. Lock v1 as code-composed, and upgrade through a composer if needed.
