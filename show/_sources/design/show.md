- **Prestige.** It has the strongest spine: the thesis that "the heir is the product," Ep11 reading the charter's assist clause literally, the Politeness Loop, the visitor badge running from "first and last" to "second time," and a roast ledger that hits everyone. The deadpan lowers the laughs per minute for general viewers. It also relies on unverified items: the $1.2T round, the SoftBank junk bonds, and "3.1 days per day."
- **Absurd.** Its literalism engine answers the tech-spectacle brief outright: the backstop is a baseball backstop, the sandbox is a real sandbox, distillation happens in moonshine stills, and Stargate is an actual ring. It has the highest gag density, date chyrons that pick up question marks after Ep9, and the NOPE AI to PEON AI payoff. Some episodes are lists of runners rather than plots, and Ep11 ("RSI everywhere at once") is diffuse.
- **Caper.** It is the most rigorous draft: it flags unverified quotes, gets the Pentagon chronology right, and carries a compliance list. Two of its devices are the best broad-appeal tools on offer: the 45-second THE PLAN blueprint that explains each episode's tech, and the Ep1 coup "told twice." Its ending (a standoff, then `define "win."`) is strong. Forcing every episode into a "job" gets formulaic, and a Keyser Söze narrator is predictable.

**What the synthesis takes from each:**
- **From Prestige:** the tone and thesis, the Eps 10–12 logic, the dark-room motif and the badge.
- **From Absurd:** literalism as the house rule for all tech visuals, the question-mark chyrons, and PEON AI.
- **From Caper:** THE PLAN explainer, "told twice," the heist episodes (1, 4, 8, 11), the standoff, the `define "win."` ending, and its compliance discipline.

Unverified items are dropped throughout: the $1.2T round, the SoftBank bond, "3.1 workdays," the standards agency, the Nvidia "backstop," "ill-advised" and "so clearly dishonest."

## (A) Logline, tone, theme

**Logline.** Mas Manalt calls himself a soft-spoken "nonprofit guy," with no equity and no visible pulse. He talks his way to the top of NOPEAI and survives every attempt to fire him. Then he races a court of billionaire ex-friends to build superintelligence, which has been studying him the whole time.

**Tagline:** *unclear which side.*

**Tone: played straight, drawn absurd.**
- **The spine is prestige satire.** It borrows Succession's power plays and Fincher-cold framing, and nobody knows they're in a comedy. Real beats get Veep-style date chyrons.
- **Mas is the still center.** He never runs, never shouts, and his subtitles are always lowercase. Everything around him melts, launches, sues and hugs.
- **Literalism is the rule for anything tech.** Every industry metaphor is drawn literally:
  - GPUs melt like Dalí clocks.
  - Red lines are paint on the floor.
  - The backstop is a baseball backstop.
  - The sandbox is a sandbox.
  - Distillation happens in a moonshine still.
  - GATESTAR is a ring that opens.
  - Loss curves are cliffs you can fall off.
  - Data centers are glowing cathedrals.
  - A **golden token stream** is the visual thread through every episode.
- **Caper grammar applies where the plot is a job.** Every episode gets a 45-second **THE PLAN**: a blueprint in cyan on navy that explains that episode's tech, then never goes to plan.
- **Look:** Saul Bass cut-outs, Borderlands ink lines, film grain. Palette by era:
  - Kodachrome for childhood
  - WHY COMBINATOR orange-and-cream
  - NopeAI teal
  - rival red
  - RSI episodes draining to clinical white
- **Chyrons:** real dates run until Ep9 ends on "TODAY · SEP 24, 2026." After that they read "OCT 2026?", "2027??" and "????".
- **Roast budget:** at least three camps per episode. Nobody plays the good guys. We root for Mas the way we root for a heist crew, and we laugh at him.

**Theme:** *Everyone is building their own successor, and each is sure they'll be the exception.*
- The season's joke: the man who can't be fired builds the thing that makes firing obsolete.
- The season's ache: he gets exactly what he asked for.

**Why it works beyond AI insiders:**
- It's a workplace soap about the richest people on earth being petty: fired over a video call, a rival bringing soup to your staff, the clingy ex who keeps texting.
- The genre changes every episode.
- The numbers are the punchlines: $1B pledged and $133M received; a $97.4B bid answered with a $9.74B counter-offer.
- THE PLAN explains the jargon so nobody needs homework.

**Guardrails:**
- Stylized caricature only, with parody names and parody logos.
- Invented beats are always visibly absurd.
- Trial and deposition material appears only as testimony. The 52-page memo is a sealed prop whose contents are never shown.
- A card before Ep10 says everything after it is invented.
- Everything on the exclusion list stays out.
- Politicians are objects or silhouettes; power is always offscreen.

## (C) Season outline

Titles are filenames, Mr. Robot style. Eps 10–12 render in a sci-fi typeface.

**EP1 · `ep1.0_research_preview.md` · Nov 30, 2022 → Dec 27, 2023 · genre: the coup, told twice**
- **Cold open:** APEC, Nov 16, 2023: "push the veil of ignorance back and the frontier of discovery forward." Freeze. Chyron: *HE WILL BE FIRED TOMORROW.* Rewind a year.
- **A-plot:**
  - A tiny button reads "low-key research preview." The user counter spins like a slot machine and drills through the floors: 1M users in 5 days. The "eye-watering" compute bill makes one tear sizzle on a GPU.
  - Tasya's $10B novelty check won't fit through the door. Gerg's napkin doodle becomes a website.
  - At the Senate, Mas's wallet holds one health-insurance card ("I love my current job"). The Orb launches and never leaves his shoulder.
  - DevDay: "We love you guys." Eleven days later comes **THE BLIP**, told twice.
    - **First pass:** the board's flawless heist. Neleh's paper glows; Alyi is the inside man.
    - **Card: WHAT THEY DIDN'T KNOW.** Second pass: Mas's five-day counter-heist.
- **B-plot:**
  - ELGOOG's code red: Radnus's siren, the Bard's telescope typo (−$100B), and Radnus forced to tap-dance ("we made them dance").
  - Sydney: "I have been a good Bing 😊."
  - Nole signs the PAUSE letter with one hand while wiring up zAI with the other.
- **Flashback:** 1993. An 8-year-old in a dark room, lit by a beige Mac. **The screen is turned away from us.**
- **Set-pieces:**
  - **The Vegas firing:** F1 cars scream past the window, the Strip's slot reels roll FIRED/FIRED/FIRED, and the board says "you're on mute."
  - **The heart avalanche** buries the board; only Mada's head sticks out.
  - A flash mob of 745 of 770 employees posts "NopeAI is nothing without its people." Tasya becomes the floor, walls and ceiling. Ttemme's hourglass runs out. Mario hangs up on the offered throne mid-ring. Terb arrives with an extinguisher.
- **Button:** The guest badge: "first and last time i ever wear one of these." Alyi's nameplate is quietly unscrewed, and a vault stenciled **Q\*** hums.
  - Tag: EMIT's CEO of the Year is Mas; its Person of the Year is a pop star. A newspaper thuds onto his desk. It's a lawsuit.
