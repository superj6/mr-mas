# MR. MAS: title-sequence director's pass

## Step 1: Scoring the three drafts

The scaling-law draft arrived cut off. Its concept header and sections 4–7 (name cards, music plan, episode slot, risks) were missing, so I scored it on the parts I could see.

| Criterion (1–10) | Prestige-montage | Scaling-law (partial) | Spectacle ("Player One") |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook in the first 5 s | 8 | 8 | **9** |
| Story recap clarity (childhood → YC → NopeAI → rivals) | **9** | 8 | 7 |
| Name-card comedy | 8 | 8 | **9** |
| Tech-as-spectacle | 7 | 9 | **10** |
| Pacing and readability in 30.0 s | 6 | 5 | 6 |
| Premium look | **9** | 8 | 7 |
| Buildability in Remotion by a coding agent | 6 | 5 | 6 |
| Music concept | **9** | 7 | 7 |
| **Total** | **62** | 58 | 61 |

- **Prestige-montage.** This has the best spine: one cyan "curve" thread, date stamps on every era, a monitor bookend, and the best harmonic joke of the three (no third, then minor, then no third again, which is "unclear which side" in chords). But the middle is newsprint and VHS rather than tech spectacle. It packs about 34 events into 30 s, the title holds for only 1.875 s, and the halftone-stills pipeline adds a whole extra art track.
- **Scaling-law.** This has the smartest meta-idea: the world renders at the fidelity of its era, so the technology itself tells the timeline. The 1-bit alert dialog with a greyed-out **Cancel** is the best single Mas gag in any draft. But it needs five art styles and three low-res sub-compositions, the cameos come every eighth note, and the tiered score (beeper, ringtone, ukulele, prestige, trailer) risks sounding like a medley.
- **Spectacle.** This has the best entrances and hook: a time-stopped dinner filmed as one continuous shot where only Mas moves, cards that each break their own template, and an Orb scan that reveals the dark room is a data hall. But the game HUD, XP bar and chiptune grammar pull it toward meme video rather than prestige TV. The dinner anachronisms blur the chronology, and the score's five genres fragment the theme.

## (A) Concept: "THE CURVE — everything scales"

Mas Manalt's life is drawn as one cyan exponential, and everything rises with it. The world renders at the fidelity of its era (1-bit monitor, then 240p camcorder, crisp cut-paper vector, then HDR bloom), and the theme climbs from a beeper to tape to a full orchestra with an 808 in step. The line runs flat in a dark room and climbs through childhood and YC. Through the nonprofit years it lies flat as the candlelit table runner, while time stops and each founder freezes into a poster card (only Mas keeps moving). It kinks at the "low-key research preview" button, drops to zero for exactly one beat when he is fired, and ends as the roofline of a skyline of rival AI towers that goes vertical at **MR. MAS**. Then the camera pulls back into his monitor: the whole show is something he just posted.

**Where each part comes from:**
- Prestige-montage: the structure, the curve thread, the harmony, the headlines, the skyline and the monitor bookend.
- Scaling-law: the fidelity tiers, the 1-bit Mas dialog card and the "low-key" button shockwave.
- Spectacle: the Orb scan, the time-stopped dinner shot, template-breaking cards and the set-piece entrances.
- New in this pass:
  - OPEN→NOPE is a real anagram, and Mas moves the N himself.
  - The disabled Cancel button comes back at the firing.
  - The music climbs through the same fidelity tiers as the picture.
  - The episode slot has a fixed grammar where the music gets "fired" and "rehired".

## (B) Cold open

**Recommended line (real, Jan 2025 post):** *"near the singularity; unclear which side."* It runs about 2.6 s, it is the season tagline, and it sets up the RSI finale. In Ep 9 it swaps to the real July 2026 line, *"we are now in the singularity."*

**Three alternates**

| # | Line | Source | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "i remain enthusiastic about the non-profit structure!" | Real: Sep 20, 2017 email, sent in the middle of the Nole blow-up | Ep 2 (Nole sues) or Ep 8 (trial) swap. The cheerful "!" plays against the dark room. |
| 2 | "agi has been achieved internally." *(the monitor edits it to: "obviously just memeing")* | Real: Sep 2023 Reddit comment and edit | Ep 11 swap (RSI). The self-edit is the gag. |
| 3 | "i've been fired before. *(beat)* it didn't take." | **Invented** | Best laugh for a general audience. Use for a promo or a season-2 open; it would spoil Ep 1. |

The 2015 line "AI will probably, most likely, sort of lead to the end of the world…" is the funniest real quote, but it runs about 4.5 s. Use it only in a 45 s extended cut, quoted in full rather than trimmed.

**Delivery**
- Soft and close-mic. He is reading his own post aloud to an empty room, not performing for us.
- Lowercase flatness: stress no word.
- The semicolon is a real pause of about 0.65 s (f56–71), and one piano note lives there.
- "side" is left open, neither falling nor rising. After it: silence, the stare, no blink.
- Casting: a human sound-alike performer, or a voice designed from a text description only. **Never clone the real voice.**
- Processing chain: high-pass at 90 Hz, gentle 2:1 compression, a small room reverb with about 0.3 s decay, light saturation.
- Run Rhubarb on the final take to get the mouth cues.

**Staging, f0–119**
- **f0–29:** Black. A cyan block cursor sits at 62% x / 46% y (the exact spot the piece ends on). It blinks on the beat: on for 8 frames, off for 7. The LCD subpixel grid is visible. A dolly-out starts at f15.
- **f30–59:** The reveal. The monitor shows a generic "Z" post composer, and under it a log chart: a flat cyan line with a blinking dot at the knee labelled `you are here`.
  - MAS sits in the left third, three-quarter view, grey hoodie, drawn only as cyan-lit planes.
  - The chrome **ORB** hovers at his shoulder with its iris half open. Three rack LEDs blink on eighth notes.
  - At f33 he says "near the singularity;" and it types in sync.
- **f60–89:** The pause, with the cursor blinking twice and a 2° head tilt. At f72–92 he says "unclear which side." The words glint mirrored in his catchlights. On "side" (f86–92) the dot slides up the curve and off the top of the monitor.
- **f90–104:** Stillness.
  - f94: his eyes snap to the lens (a 2-frame dart).
  - f97: the Orb's iris swivels to the lens.
  - f99–104: the Orb sweeps a thin scan fan across the room and through the lens. In its light, for 5 frames, the "dark room" is revealed as an **endless data-center cathedral**.
  - A toast appears: `COINWORLD ID ✓ verified: human (probably)`.
- **f105–119:**
  - f107: a micro-smile.
  - f112: he clicks **Post**. The line bursts into pastel tokenizer chips, ⟨near⟩⟨ the⟩⟨ singular⟩⟨ity⟩⟨;⟩…, that stream past the lens.
  - The chart line snaps vertical and flares white, and the camera rides it into the screen.
  - f118–119: pure white. At f120 the drop comes.

## (C) Shot-by-shot, 0.0–30.0 s

**Grid:** 24 fps, 96 BPM, 15 frames per beat, 60 frames per bar (2.5 s), 12 bars, 720 frames. `b(n)=(n−1)*15`, and bar.beat X.Y is beat 4(X−1)+Y.

**Build preface**
- **One timing source.** `src/beats.ts` holds `FPS, BPM, FPB=15, b(n), f(bar,beat,eighth)`, and it is exported to `beats.json`. Both Remotion and `music/compose.py` read it.
- **Composition tree:**
  ```
  <Intro ep={n}>
    ColdOpen b(1)=0 · Era1993 b(9)=120 · Era2008_14 b(13)=180 · Tableau b(16)=225
    EpisodeSlot b(33)=480 · Skyline b(37)=540..689 · TitleOverlay b(43)=630 · Bookend b(47)=690
  ```
- **Pre-rendered low-fidelity tiers.** `Era1993` is 512×342 at 6 fps and is placed at 3× with `image-rendering:pixelated`. `Era2008_14` is 320×240, then 640×360, at 12 fps, with smooth upscaling. Both are rendered once to video and placed with `<OffthreadVideo>`.
- **Two passes.**
  - Pass 1 is clean SVG and CSS.
  - Pass 2 loads pass 1 as `<Video>` and applies `@remotion/effects` keyed to frame ranges:
    - scanlines and chromatic aberration on f180–239;
    - halftone and paper on the card holds;
    - glow, light-leak and chromatic aberration on f480–689;
    - zoom-blur on f113–119 and f525–539;
    - CRT barrel and glow on the monitor in the bookend;
    - 5% grain, ±0.5 px gate weave and a vignette everywhere.
- **Performance.** Motion blur only on whips (f225–229, f525–531). Particles on one `<canvas>`. Glows, chrome, smoke and rack textures are pre-baked in Pillow.
- **Characters.** Mas uses one rig with age and outfit swaps. Gerg, Alyi, Mario, Adelina and Nole each get an action-and-freeze rig. Luap, the board members and the rivals are one-pose sprites with one moving part. That is about 15 designs.

## (E) Music

### Concept

The cue is **"The Knee (Main Title)"**: an original prestige-drama theme that pits felt piano against the 808, and that *scales its own fidelity*. It evokes a genre and borrows no melody.

- **Tempo and key:** 96 BPM, 4/4, **F minor**.
- **Harmonic joke:**
  - The cold open is thirdless (open fifths only).
  - The minor third arrives at the f120 drop, when the story "picks a side".
  - The final chord is thirdless again: "unclear which side."
- **Hook, "THE KNEE":** one bar of eighths, **F F F F G A♭ C F**. The repeated tonic is the flat part of the curve; the widening intervals are the knee. It appears in six forms:
  1. Stretched across the cold open, one note per cursor blink.
  2. As the 16th-note run on Post.
  3. As the beeper hook in the 1-bit bar.
  4. As the tape-piano hook.
  5. As the roots of the card hits (F, D♭, B♭, C).
  6. **Spelled by the skyline pops**, F F F F G A♭ C, landing its final F on the title.
- **Fidelity tiers (the music scales with the picture):**
  - Bar 3 is **1-bit**: pulse-wave beeper, square bass and noise hats over a real sub.
  - Bar 4 is **tape**: lo-fi boom-bap and a wobbly cassette piano.
  - Bars 5–8 are **full**: Salamander piano, strings, brass, timpani, 808.
  - Bars 9–12 are **max**: everything plus a choir pad, sub drops and risers.
  - It is the same notes throughout, so the cue stays one theme and never becomes a medley.

### Code-composed alternative (recommended; the only route to exact frame sync)

1. `music/compose.py` reads `beats.json` (48 kHz: 30,000 samples per beat, 1,440,000 total) and writes one `pretty_midi` file per stem. Humanize with ±8 ms timing and velocity curves.
2. Render each tier from the **same MIDI**:

   - **T2:** Salamander piano through a wow-and-flutter pitch LFO (±15 cents), a 6 kHz low-pass and hiss, over lo-fi kit hits from VCSL.
   - **T3:** Salamander, plus VSCO 2 CE strings, brass and timpani via `tinysoundfont` or sfizz, plus a numpy 808 (sine with a pitch envelope and saturation).

3. Tape spin-up and spin-down are variable-rate resamples; the chant and VO are human recordings.
4. Mix in `pedalboard`: a short room reverb on bars 1–2, a hall on the title, bus compression, and a limiter at −1 dBTP to about −14 LUFS.
5. Export stems (piano, orch, drums, 808, chip, fx, chant, vo) and bring them into Remotion as `<Audio>` layers, so the dropout and retimes are just gain curves.
6. Expect about 7/10 quality. Hand the MIDI to a human composer for 9/10.
