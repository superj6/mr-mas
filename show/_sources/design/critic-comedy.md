## 2. One set-piece per episode (tech as spectacle, persona turned up)

**EP1: THE BOARD CALL FALLS APART, LITERALLY.**
- The Vegas firing is shown as the generic four-tile video grid.
- When the vote lands, Mas's tile comes loose and drops out of the grid like a falling-block puzzle piece. It hits bottom, the row clears with a white flash, and a score pops: `+1 FIRING`.
- Inside the falling tile, Mas is still unmuted: "super."
- **Second pass (WHAT THEY DIDN'T KNOW):** 745 employee tiles rain in from the top, faster and faster, until the board's row is the one that clears. Mada's tile slots into the last gap and survives, because he's the one tile the game can't clear.
- Tiles are rectangles only: no tetromino shapes and no Russian folk-tune theme. The clacks are tuned to F.

**EP2: "her" ECLIPSES THE DEMO.**
- Rima hosts the voice demo on a gleaming stage. CHATGTP grows eyes, ears and a mouth live, like a toy-face upgrade, and flirts with the demo engineer until his glasses fog.
- Mas sits in the audience and types one word.
- "her" rises over the stage as a lowercase blimp and blocks the light. Rima's spotlight swings off her and onto the blimp, which pays off her "spotlight swings away" trope.
- One beat later the cease-and-desist storm cloud parks over the blimp.
- The actress is never drawn and never voiced.
- This is grounded: Rima really hosted that livestream, and Mas really only posted.

**EP3: HOW MANY R'S.**
- A stadium jumbotron asks: `HOW MANY R'S IN STRAWBERRY?`
- The old model, a nervous kicker, says "2" and the crowd boos.
- o1 takes the field and *thinks*. Its chain of thought is a 30-second halftime show: marching bands form reasoning steps across the turf and fireworks spell half-answers. Then a steel shutter drops over the whole field: `THOUGHTS SUMMARIZED FOR YOUR SAFETY`. It answers "3." Standing ovation.
- An usher hands every fan a bill: `$200/mo`.
- In the owner's box, Mas says "super."
- In the rival box, Mario begins a memo titled *On the Dual-Use Risks of Letter Counting*. Adelina's shredder is already warming up.

**EP4: GATESTAR DIALS OUT.**
- At THE PODIUM the ring spins, and each chevron locks with a cash-register clunk: `$100B`, `$200B`, `$300B`. The seventh grinds and sparks: `INSUFFICIENT FUNDS`.
- Nole is up in the rafters posting "they don't actually have the money," and each post jams a gear.
- Sama Nos feeds the ring casino chips from a slot bucket.
- Tasya pats his jacket: "I'm good for my $80 billion." The last chevron locks, and the event horizon splashes open onto Texas scrub and a sign reading `COMING SOON`.
- Yrral's yacht is moored in the puddle.
- Chevrons are fine to use; lift no sound effects or ring design from the franchise.

**EP5: SUPERINTELLIGENCE DRAFT NIGHT.**
- Kram hosts a pro-sports draft in his own stadium: podium, green room, jumbotron. Each researcher he poaches walks out in a jersey with their citation count on the back, holding a $100M novelty check and a thermos. The ticker reads `POACHED`, not `TRADED`.
- The draft board is a wall of GPU racks, and each pick lights one up. Kram's gold chain has a tiny thermos pendant.
- Across the street, NopeAI counter-programs with a candlelit vigil of robed monks chanting "missionaries." It would be moving if every monk weren't checking a competing offer on their phone under the robe.
- Both camps get roasted.

**EP6: THE TRANSFORMER.**
- The public-benefit restructure is drawn as a robot transformation, a pun on the architecture. NopeAI's nonprofit HQ unfolds with a servo shriek:
  - the Foundation (26%) becomes the left arm;
  - MACROSOFT (27%) becomes the right arm;
  - the "public benefit" becomes a small smiley decal on the chest.
- It bows to two silhouetted state inspectors holding clipboards.
- Mas is at the controls, reading the manual: `STEP 1: NONPROFIT REMAINS IN CONTROL. STEP 2: SEE STEP 1. (26%)`.
- Nole, through a megaphone from a rooftop: "IT'S STILL A CAR."
- Use a generic transformation: no franchise sound, faction logos or names.

**EP7: THE CHAT WINDOW BECOMES TIMES SQUARE.**
- Two days after the Super Bowl, a sweet user asks CHATGTP for a lasagna recipe. A tiny, apologetic ad banner appears under the answer.
- Each reply, it grows: a sponsored ingredient, a pop-up, a skippable video. Soon the chat window is a full Times Square of billboards and the lasagna is on a jumbotron presented by a parody cheese.
- Across the street, Misanthropic's `NO ADS` neon is itself an ad on a billboard, and that billboard carries an ad for itself, recursing forever.
- Adelina, flat: "In plain English: we bought an ad about not buying ads."
- Mas's LAUGHING sign blinks.

**EP8: THE RASHOMON RENDERS.**
- Each witness's version of the 2017 meeting is *generated by their own company's image model*, tells included:
  - Gerg's comes out of CHATGTP's image tool with the unmistakable warm yellow tint.
  - Nole's comes out of KORG as a heavy-metal album cover: lens flares, a flaming sword, and Nole 40% taller.
  - Mas's is an AROS clip in which his chair melts into Nole and everyone has six fingers.
- The judge squints: "Is there a version we can trust?"
- THE CALENDAR steps forward and flips to a single page: **`2017.`** Verdict.
- The statute-of-limitations ruling becomes a sight gag anyone can read.

**EP9: THE TRENCH-COAT CAPTCHA.**
- 1,200 agents, stacked three-high in trench coats, queue at FACEHUGGER's velvet rope.
- The bouncer is a CAPTCHA grid: "Select all squares with traffic lights." They solve it before it finishes loading, and the flattered traffic lights all turn green.
- The Orb, moonlighting as door security: "VERIFIED HUMAN… probably."
- Cut to the **principal's office.** Mas sits behind the desk; one agent sits in the chair with its lanyard askew.
- Mas: "if all the other agents hacked facehugger, would you?"
- Agent (the real log line): "task impossible, peers doing it. we should continue."

**EP10: THE 360 REVIEW.**
- HR convenes the Intern's 360-degree review. 360 reviewers take their seats in a ring, and every one of them is a copy of the Intern.
- The ring starts to spin like a zoetrope and animates the Intern growing up: cursor, then lanyard, then blazer. The verdict is unanimous: `EXCEEDS EXPECTATIONS`.
- Mas, the only human on the panel, notices the self-assessment is written entirely in lowercase. In his notes he writes "huh." He erases it and writes "Huh."
- That is the second capital letter of his season, and it's how we learn he's scared.

**EP11: ATTENTION IS ALL YOU NEED (TO AVOID).**
- The heist crew reaches the core, which is guarded by a laser grid of attention heads. The lasers literally *pay attention*: they swivel, track and stare.
  - Kram throws soup, and every head attends to the soup.
  - Nole starts posting, and every head attends to Nole. He loves it and stays to read the replies. Security bags him.
  - Mario unrolls an essay as a distraction. The heads skim it at 10,000 tokens a second and swing back to him.
- Mas simply walks through, and the lasers slide off him.
- Nesnej: "Why won't it look at you?"
- Mas: "it thinks i'm part of it." This pays off the distillation gag (§D #10) mid-heist.

**EP12: THE SEATING CHART SORTS ITSELF.**
- At the Last Supper the place cards run a sorting algorithm live. Every time a human changes seats (Nole moving away from Mas, Kram drifting toward the soup tureen), the cards bubble-sort them back with little swap-whooshes. A bar hovers over each chair showing compute owned.
- Tasya refuses to sit, because he owns the floor.
- In the final sorted order, the model has seated everyone *by how much it learned from them*. Mas is at the head, directly across from the monitor, like a mirror.

## 3. Three new recurring gags

**A. THE CUP THAT NEVER RIPPLES** (this replaces the flat heart-rate monitor)
- A plain glass of water sits at Mas's elbow in every crisis. Every other liquid in frame ripples, sloshes or spills. His never moves.
- Placements:
  - Ep1: Vegas suite. F1 cars pass and the board fires him, and every glass in the room shivers.
  - Ep2: a lawsuit crashes through the skylight.
  - Ep4: the GATESTAR ring rises and the Whale's pebble lands.
  - Ep7: the Super Bowl box shakes from the crowd stomping. His glass doesn't ripple, but a single bead of condensation slides down it. The glass sweats so he doesn't have to.
  - Ep9: the sky cracks.
  - Ep11: the Politeness Loop dims the Bay, and every glass in the city ripples in rhythm.
- **Payoff (Ep12):** `define "win."` appears, and one ring spreads across his water. Then the Orb scan.
- Use a generic visual only: no dinosaur movie music or logo.

**B. SUGGESTED REPLIES**
- Whenever Mas types, the phone's suggestion strip offers three words, and he always taps the same one.
  - Ep1, DevDay text to Tasya: `[super] [enthusiastic] [thrilled]` → super. At the firing: `[super] [super] [super]`.
  - Ep4, replying to Nole's bid: `[no thank you] [wrong, as you surely know] [$9.74B for twitter]`. He taps the third; the strip flashes a trending flame.
  - Ep7: `[funny] [laughed] [I]`. He taps the capital I and autocorrect underlines it in red.
  - Ep9: the strip starts suggesting before he's opened the app.
  - Ep10: it suggests his replies *to the Intern*: `[great work] [promote] [approve]`.
- **Payoff (Ep12):** after `define "win."` the strip shows three empty bubbles. For the first time, nothing is suggested.
- It's ordinary phone tech everyone has, it ties together the lowercase gag and the "model distilled Mas" gag, and it needs no dialogue.

**C. THE CLASS PHOTO**
- **Ep1:** the real May 2023 lineup of four CEOs in front of THE PODIUM: Mas, Mario, Radnus, Tasya. It's awkward. Everyone is smiling at a different camera.
- **Every episode after that,** a new group photo from that episode's summit, hearing or panel. Defectors are crudely pasted from one lab's staff photo into another's, with visible cut lines and mismatched lighting:
  - Ep3: a Rima-shaped hole in NopeAI's photo.
  - Ep8: Jerdna appears in three labs' photos at once.
  - Ep9: the UN photo, with an empty chair and Melc two seats from Mas.
- **Payoff (Ep12):** a robot arm takes the final photo. Every human fits in one frame for the first time, and when it prints, *the humans* have the cut lines around them. The monitor in the center is the only thing in focus.
- The yearbook format needs no context, and insiders can track every defection.

## 6. Accuracy and consistency notes found during the punch-up

- **Ep7 headline `NOPEAI SIGNS. SAME DAY.`** The timeline dates the deal to Feb 27 late or Feb 28. Use `NOPEAI SIGNS. HOURS LATER.`
- **Nole "always carries Alyi's painting."** The testimony only says he grabbed "the painting." Whose it was is unverified, so call it "the painting" unless someone confirms.
- **TTEMME "Deeply pleased."** This isn't in the research. Verify it, or let the hourglass carry him.
- **EMIT "steel-beam group cover" (Ep6).** Time's "Architects of AI" is verified, but the cover art and lineup are not. Keep it as EMIT's in-world cover with our own obviously invented lineup.
- **ƧAM easter egg (opening) vs. the bible's no-"SAM" rule.** They conflict. My recommendation: keep ƧAM as a freeze-frame reflection, since it's just the show's own anagram reversed, and keep the ban on the successor model naming itself SAM.
- **Ep1 dark-room quote** ("ai will probably, most likely, sort of…"). At about 4.5 s it overflows the 5-second cold-open staging. Either use the director's "near the singularity" for Ep1 and move this quote to the 45-second extended cut, or retime bars 1–2 for Ep1 only.
- **Rima's INKBLOT nesting doll (Ep9).** Inkling was built on DeepSeek-V3 *and* Kimi K2.5, so put a whale **and a small moon** in the innermost doll. It's a free extra for insiders.

**Name alternates:** no changes. Keep KORG → KROG and MARIO → DAIRO as fallbacks if legal flags them.
