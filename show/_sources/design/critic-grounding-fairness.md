# MR. MAS: grounding and fairness critique

**Verdict.** The package is mostly well grounded. All 12 intro quotes are real, and the big numbers match the research: $97.4B/$9.74B, −$589B, $14.3B, $6.5B, $1.5B, $1.25B a month, $965B/$852B, 745/770, 26%/27% and $133M. The main problems are:
- a few factual slips, mostly in the opening;
- three hard-rule risks: the Ynoj "engagement" gag, how the "cannibals" and The Whale are drawn, and the imagery around 4o;
- Mario gets no roast in Eps 1–4;
- the bible and the opening disagree in several places (the quote system, the withheld 1993 screen, the Orb and collar gags, name cards, filenames and date ranges).
