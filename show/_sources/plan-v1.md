# MR. MAS: Show Bible v1 and 30s Opening Sequence Plan

## Context
You want a high-quality, ~30s animated opening for **"Mr. Mas"**, a parody series about Sam Altman's rise. The broad plot follows real events. Filler scenes are invented and more dramatic. Every character gets a renamed version of their real name.

The season runs as follows:
- **Ep1:** ChatGPT through the firing and rehiring.
- **Throughout:** flashbacks fill in Mas's earlier life.
- **Eps 2–9:** catch up to today (Sep 2026).
- **Eps 10–12:** imagine what happens when the AI starts improving itself (the RSI endgame).

Your mid-session direction: stay grounded in real events, but **exaggerate each person's public persona** and **make the technology itself the visual spectacle**.

This document is v1 of the creative package for us to iterate on. It is not the final build.

**How it was made:**
1. Web-verified research built a timeline from 2015 to 2026-09-24. A critic pass spot-checked 41 claims.
2. Three competing show outlines and three competing 30s intros were drafted independently, scored, and merged.
3. Separate checks covered accuracy and fairness, comedy punch-up, and timing and whether each shot is buildable.

## 1. Show bible

**Logline.** Mas Manalt is a soft-spoken "nonprofit guy" with no equity and no detectable panic. He talks his way to the top of NOPEAI and survives every attempt to fire him. Then he races his billionaire ex-friends to build superintelligence, which has been studying *him* the whole time. **Tagline: *unclear which side.***

**Tone.** Played straight, drawn absurd: Succession-style power plays, Veep-style date chyrons, heist-movie structure.
- Mas is the still center. He never runs, and his subtitles are always lowercase. Everything around him melts, launches, sues and hugs.
- Tech metaphors are drawn literally. Each episode has a 45s "THE PLAN" blueprint that explains the jargon for general viewers.
- **Nobody is the good guys.** Every episode roasts at least three camps, Misanthropic included.

**Theme.** Everyone is building their own successor, and everyone is sure they'll be the exception. The man who can't be fired builds the thing that makes firing obsolete.

### Recurring gags (top 12)
1. **DAYS SINCE SOMEONE TRIED TO FIRE MAS.** Tally marks carved into the dark-room desk: Tpool ×2, NopeAI 2023, Nole's $97.4B bid. In Ep12 the machine vetoes the firing and the counter goes to ∞.
2. **The cup that never ripples.** Every liquid in frame sloshes except Mas's water. In Ep12 one ring finally spreads.
3. **Lowercase.** Mas's first capital letter slips out in Ep7 ("I laughed"). Ep12 reveals the 1993 kid typed in ALL CAPS.
4. **Collar count.** Two collars in 2008, a ruff by Ep10.
5. **The Masa/Sama mirror.** Sama Nos gets Mas's mail.
6. **Nole's version board.** It crawls toward 4.9999, Zeno's-paradox style.
7. **Mario's scrolls.** They grow from 15k words to 40k to an IPO filing, and each turns into infrastructure. Adelina shreds each one to three bullets.
8. **KA-CHING.** Nesnej's register ends every fight. It goes silent in Ep12.
9. **The migrating code-red siren.** It moves from ELGOOG (Ep1) to NopeAI (Ep6) to every roof (Ep11).
10. **Distillation stills.** Everyone accuses everyone of copying other AIs' answers. Payoff: the model distilled *Mas*.
11. **The guest badge.** "first and last time i ever wear one of these" (Ep1) → "second time" (Ep12).
12. **Everyone rents from their enemies.** Rent meters spin over every data center.

## 3. Opening sequence (30.0s shot by shot)

**Concept: "THE CURVE. Everything scales."**
- Mas's life is **one cyan exponential line**. It starts as a blinking cursor in a dark room and ends as the roofline of an AI skyline.
- **The world renders at the fidelity of its era.** 1-bit pixels (1993), then 240p camcorder (2008–14), then crisp cut-paper (2015), then glossy HDR (2022+). The music climbs through the same four tiers.
- **Time stops at the founding dinner.** Each founder freezes into a Snatch/Borderlands-style name card, and only Mas keeps moving (and pocketing things).
- **Bar 9 changes every episode.** In Ep1 the curve kinks at ChatGPT. When he's fired, the curve drops to zero and **the music gets fired too**; when he's rehired, both come back.
- **The show literally scales**, which is the tech-as-spectacle joke.

**Cold-open line (recommended):** *"near the singularity; unclear which side."* This is Sam's real January 2025 post, recast as Mas's.
- **Delivery:** soft and close-mic, like reading his own post to an empty room. The semicolon is a real pause holding one low piano note, and "side" is left hanging.
- **It fits the season:** it's the season tagline, and it evolves: *near* (Ep1) → "we are now in the singularity." (Ep9) → the cursor types it alone and adds `ours.` (Ep12).
- **Alternates:**
  - "the compute costs are eye-watering." Inside Ep1's 2022–23 window; drier.
  - "i think that ai will probably, most likely, sort of lead to the end of the world. but in the meantime, there will be great companies…" From 2015. The funniest option, but it needs a 35s pilot cut.
  - "i remain enthusiastic about the non-profit structure!" From a 2017 email.

## 4. Music and sound

**"The Knee (Main Title)."** An original prestige-drama theme: felt piano against a heavy 808. It is in the spirit of Succession, with Social Network dread in the cold open. **It evokes a style and copies no melody.**

- **Specs:** 96 BPM, F minor, locked to the 24 fps frame grid.
- **The hook motif, "the knee":** F F F F G A♭ C F. Four flat notes and then a rising leap: an exponential curve you can hear. It appears in every section.
- **The music scales too.** The same notes are re-orchestrated for each era:
  1. Cold open: piano and sub drone.
  2. 1993: 1-bit chiptune.
  3. 2008–14: cassette-warble piano and boom-bap drums.
  4. The dinner: full hybrid orchestra with an 808. Each name card lands on a timpani-and-brass hit, on the chords F minor, D♭, B♭ minor and C.
  5. Launch and skyline: adds a choir.
- **Signature moves:** the music is "fired" (it cuts to one dry piano note) and "rehired" (a slam). There's no third in the first or final chord.

**Production routes, ranked:**
1. **Recommended: code-composed.** I write MIDI on the exact beat grid (`pretty_midi`) and render it with a free sampled piano (Salamander) and orchestra samples (VSCO 2 CE). The 808 and synths are built in numpy, and the mix is done in `pedalboard`.
   - Stems stay separate, so the fire/rehire moment and any retiming are just volume curves.
   - It's the only route that is frame-exact, and you own it.
   - Quality is about 7/10. A human composer given the MIDI could take it to 9/10.
2. **AI generator** (ElevenLabs Music composition plan, Suno v6, Lyria 3.5), plus a hit layer built in code.
   - The most polished texture, fastest.
   - Timing drifts, so it needs time-stretching to the grid.
   - Licensing caveats: purely AI-generated music isn't copyrightable in the US, and TV/film terms vary by plan. Udio is currently unusable because downloads are disabled.
   - A paste-ready ElevenLabs composition plan with five timed sections is in the full doc.
3. **Stock** (Epidemic or Artlist). The most production value per hour, but generic. It loses the tier-scaling joke, cutting it to the grid means editing stems, and some tracks trigger YouTube Content ID claims.

**Sound effects:** about 35 synthesized in code, tuned to the key: scan, servo, bonk, collar pops, camera shutter, klaxon, rocket, stamp, neon buzz, plucks, ka-ching, ding. About 6 recorded: the voice line, the "feel the AGI" gang chant (4–6 friends), crowd "ohh", a mechanical keyboard, room tone, flame. No meme sounds and no real operating-system sounds.

**Voice for the cold-open line:** you or a friend performing Mas's cadence (soft, measured, earnest pauses), clearly as a cartoon. Record it on a phone in a closet and process it in code. The alternative is a synthetic voice designed from a text description. **Never clone the real person's voice**; several states now regulate that.

I author all art as code: SVG, CSS and WebGL inside Remotion. I can't generate images with a diffusion model. The machine is CPU-only. Honest feasibility:

| Ink outlines, cel shading, halftone (Borderlands) | **Excellent** | Name cards, freeze frames, comic energy |
| 1-bit and pixel art, terminal/CRT, UI parody | **Excellent** | Native territory: dither, scanlines, glitch |
| VHS / home-video / camcorder texture | **Very good** | Grain, chroma bleed, tracking lines, date stamps |
| Isometric skyline (SimCity / Silicon Valley) | **Very good** | Simple polygons, window grids, springy pop-ins |

## 6. Production pipeline (after approval)

**Stack:** Remotion 4.0.x. Code is React + TypeScript, and characters are layered SVG cut-out rigs.
- It works on the installed Node 18.19.1.
- It brings its own ffmpeg and headless Chrome, so nothing needs installing system-wide.
- The license is free for an individual.

**Audio:** a Python virtualenv with numpy, scipy, Spotify `pedalboard`, `pretty_midi` and `tinysoundfont`.
- Samples are free to use: the Salamander grand piano plus a few VSCO 2 CE string and brass patches.
- The machine has no GPU, about 9 GB of free RAM and about 30 GB of free disk. That rules out heavy 3D and big sample libraries.
- Heavy effects (grain, VHS, CRT, paper, light leaks) go in a second pass with `@remotion/effects`, or come from textures pre-rendered with Pillow.

**Project layout** (in `/home/jgon/project/art/mrmas/`)
```
docs/bible.md             ← full show bible + opening spec (copied from the scratchpad design docs)
docs/research/            ← timeline + craft research dossiers
src/timing.ts             ← single source of truth: FPS=24, BPM=96, bar(n)/beat(n) → frame; exports beats.json for audio
src/text-registry.ts      ← every on-screen text item with in/out frames → read-time lint
src/Root.tsx              ← compositions: StyleFrames, Animatic, Intro (720 frames @ 24fps = 30.0s)
src/scenes/*.tsx          ← ColdOpen, Era1993, Era2008_14, Tableau (dinner), EpisodeSlot, Skyline, Title, Bookend
src/characters/<Name>/    ← SVG rig per character (head/eyes/brows/mouth-set/arms), pose + expression props
src/fx/                   ← Grain, VHS, Dither1Bit, CRT, PaperHalftone, LightLeak, RenderFront wrappers
src/type/                 ← NameCard (freeze → pop → punch-in → hold → exit), Headline, Toast
public/audio/, public/textures/
audio/*.py                ← compose.py (MIDI from beats.json), sfx.py (procedural SFX), mix.py (stems + master)
```

**Iteration order.** Each step ends with a review from you before the next one starts.
1. **Style frames.** Render three stills for each of three looks: the recommended mix, flat Saul Bass, and prestige newsprint. The stills are the cold open, Nole's name card and the title card. I'll add a v0 Mas character sheet (front and three-quarter views, 4 expressions) and publish everything as a private side-by-side page.
2. **Animatic.** A 30s cut of timed placeholder layouts, a click track and a rough code-composed music sketch. We lock pacing and text density here, before any polish.
3. **Rigs.** Build Mas first, then the four founders, then the skyline bosses.
4. **Music.** Write the scratch score on the locked grid. The final route is your call (section 4).
5. **Full build.** Build shot by shot, then add the texture pass, the sound effects and the mix. Final render is 1080p at 24 fps. Ep1 is the reference version; per-episode variants only swap the slot sections.
6. **Polish.** Work through your notes, then do the final render.

**One-time setup (needs your approval when we start):**
- `npx create-video@latest` (blank template), which downloads Chrome Headless Shell.
- A Python venv plus `pip install` of the audio libraries.
- Downloading the samples, which are about 1–2 GB if trimmed to the needed instruments.

**How we check it:**
- `npx remotion studio` for live, frame-by-frame review.
