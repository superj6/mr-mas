# EP08 · `ep1.7_statute_of_limitations.pdf` · Beat sheet

**Tags:** **REAL** (date) = the event happened; see [facts.md](facts.md). **INVENTED** = obviously comedic filler. **Trial rule:** trial material appears only as testimony or exhibits. No private diaries are "pried open," and no physical staging of "hit me." Jurors are private people: nothing is ever staged on the jury box. The show never rules on the merits: THE PLAN lists what had to be shown, and nothing on screen ever ticks it. † marks a `[K]` line to re-verify.

**Quote rendering on screen:** real lines keep their wording, but real names inside them are swapped for registry names (precedent: "Dario is right" → "Mario is right", FINAL §7): Nvidia → INVIDIA, Anthropic → MISANTHROPIC, OpenAI → NOPEAI, Claude → CLOD, Jensen → NESNEJ, and so on. Mas's subtitles are always lowercased. [facts.md](facts.md) keeps the original wording.

**How it plays.**
- **Act One** is April on his dark-room monitor, and it ends at his desk the night before the trial, where he opens the complaint and it unfolds into THE PLAN.
- **Act Two** is the trial, played straight through in the courtroom: no V.O., and nothing cuts away from it. The rent meter is seen through the courtroom window, and Beijing gets a split screen only after he steps down.
- **Act Three** moves: the dark-room monitor, the lobby for THE FLIP, his phone on the way out of it, and the Évian lunch in person.

The only exit from his view is F8.2, Tasya's key, now a micro.
