## Logline
NOLE v. MANALT finally goes to trial. Every witness's memory of 2017 comes out rendered by their own company's image model, and the verdict is delivered, in under two hours, by a calendar. Outside the courtroom Nole becomes Mario's landlord, Misanthropic passes NopeAI, and President RUMPT keeps asking "Who?" as Mario walks through the Gold Oval again and again.

## Plots

### A-plot: NOLE v. MANALT (Apr 27 → May 18)
- **The morning it opens (Apr 27),** TASYA quietly removes one key from his ring: the AGI clause is gone from the MACROSOFT deal. That triggers the landlord's-key flashback.
- **Nole on the stand (Apr 28–30):** "I came up with the name." "In strict monetary terms, I contributed $38 million." "Generally AI companies distill other AI companies," and the gallery slips flasks into their jackets. "The biggest risk would be that AI kills us all," which triggers **THE RASHOMON RENDERS**.
- **Gerg** codes in the witness box while his diary is read into the record as exhibits. **NOVIHS**'s switchboard (May 8). **TASYA:** Nole "never expressed concern" (May 11).
- **Mas (May 12–13):** "Are you completely trustworthy?" He first says he believes so, then amends it to "yes." [V], and the *courtroom* buffers.
- **Verdict (May 18):** THE CALENDAR, in under two hours, on the statute of limitations. Nole: "calendar technicality."

### B-plot: THE FLIP (May 6 → Jun 16)
- **Landlord Nole:** Misanthropic rents COLOSSAL for $1.25B a month (May 6), and ELGOOG rents too ($920M a month, Jun 5).
- **JERDNA** defects mid-stride (May 19). **Misanthropic passes NopeAI:** $965B to $852B (May 28). The lighthouse outgrows the cathedral.
- **The SPACEZ IPO** reaches orbit (Jun 12), and Nole becomes the first trillionaire. SPACEZ buys POINTER (Jun 16).

### C-plot: MYTHIC AND THE MAN WHO CAN'T SEE MARIO (Apr 7 → Jun 30)
- **MYTHIC in the vault (Apr 7–10):** the bankers, with TNESSEB and LLEWOP, hide under the table.
- **"WHO?" (Apr 17):** Mario meets EISUS SELIW; RUMPT on the runway: "Who?" Then "disjointed" (May 1), and in Évian, "Going fine… Howard, right?" (Jun 17).
- **The government locks MYTHIC's box for 18 days (Jun 12–30).** EO 14409 prints as a `30-DAY FREE TRIAL` coupon (Jun 2).
- **Beijing (May 12–14):** Air Force One picks Nesnej up in Alaska; Nole flies too. THE COUNTERPART's banquet grows a seat.

### Runners
- **Goblins** (Apr 23), fight-club style. The encyclical (May 25) dwarfs every scroll. NEYEL hits snooze (May 7). Mario explains THE GAP (Jun 18).
- **The balance pairing:** SITNASED vs preemption (R vs R); RENEIW's bill-number hydra follows him onto the SF House ballot (D); and the XELA SEROB dodgeball, with both PACs' money flying.
- **Button:** a log line nobody reads (Jun 18); then THE TAILOR's JALAPEÑO (Jun 24), and Nesnej's register sweats.

## Themes
1. **Time is the only judge nobody can buy.** The calendar decides a case that every witness has rendered in their own favor.
2. **Memory is rendered by self-interest.** Four image models, four 2017s. The only thing that's the same every time is the painting walking out.
3. **Landlords win.** Nole rents to his enemy; Tasya keeps the keys; Nesnej sells everyone the shovels.
4. **Being unseen.** Mario is #1 by valuation and invisible to the President. Mas is on every stand and in every photo, and he still gets moved to the second seat.
