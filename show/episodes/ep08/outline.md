## Logline
NOLE v. MANALT finally goes to trial, and the night before, Mas opens the complaint: what Nole has to prove, in three steps, and the third won't unfold. Every witness's memory of 2017 comes out rendered by their own company's image model, and when Mas is asked if he's completely trustworthy, the courtroom buffers. The calendar opens the fold. Then the win costs him first place: the rival's lighthouse outgrows his cathedral in the same week the contractor at his front desk records the greeting that will replace her.

## The through-line (what a newcomer should be able to retell)
- Nole, the co-founder who left, takes Mas to trial for turning the nonprofit into a company. Nole has to prove a promise, a break, and a third thing that stays folded out of sight.
- Every witness's memory of 2017 comes out rendered in their own favor. Asked whether he is completely trustworthy, Mas says "yes," and the room buffers.
- The third thing was time: Nole had to sue in time, and he hadn't. The calendar decides it, and the rest of the evidence blows off the table.
- But the win costs him first place. Mario's lab passes NopeAI's $852B the same week DOT, at his front desk, records the greeting that will train her replacement.

## Themes
1. **Time is the only judge nobody can buy.** The calendar decides a case that every witness has rendered in their own favor, and the evidence never gets weighed.
2. **Memory is rendered by self-interest.** Four image models, four 2017s. The only thing that's the same every time is the painting walking out.
3. **Landlords win.** Nole rents to his enemy; Tasya takes back the one key he lent; the campus keeps its own power plant.
4. **Being unseen, being replaced.** Mario is #1 by valuation and invisible to the President. DOT records the cheer the machine will use, and Mas, who is on every stand, resets his face the way she resets hers.
