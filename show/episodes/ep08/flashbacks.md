**What we see**
1. **EXHIBIT A (10 s, 240p), the former separate demon flashback.** Oct 24, 2014, THE INSTITUTE (MIT) symposium lecture video. Nole: "With artificial intelligence we are summoning the demon." [V] A chalk pentagram on the whiteboard (`RECONSTRUCTED`), an overhead-projector fan whirr, and a **1 s callback** to EGAP's EP06 record scratch. His grievance is older than the company.
2. **Prologue (Aug 11, 2017).** The ATOD bot beats THE CHAMPION. Nole's tweet card: "Vastly more risk than North Korea." [V] It's text only, no imagery of the country.
3. **The Model 3 meeting (late Aug 2017), four ways:**

   | Witness | Render style | Tell |
   |---|---|---|
   | **GERG** | Pure **ASCII**, green on black (per the flashback map) | His own figure is the only one rendered at full resolution |
   | **NOLE** | **KORG**'s heavy-metal album cover: lens flares, a flaming *guitar* (swapped for FINAL's sword, so no weapons anywhere near a real person), Nole 40% taller | The Model 3s are drawn as chrome chariots |
   | **MAS** | An **AROS** clip: his chair melts into Nole's, and everyone has six fingers | His water glass is the only thing rendered correctly |
   | **ALYI** | **ISS has no product,** so his version is a blank white square with one door. `RENDER: PENDING (SAFETY FIRST)` [INVENTED] | He appears only as a reflection in the square's glossy surface |

   **Every version ends identically:** "I decline" [V]; the Tesla painting leaves the room under Nole's arm [V; call it "the Tesla painting," not Alyi's commission]; and on the way out, "When will you be departing OpenAI?" [V]
4. The judge squints: *Is there a version we can trust? [INVENTED]* THE CALENDAR flips to `2017.`
5. **Coda (Sep 20–21, 2017), a soap opera.** The "Honest Thoughts" email thread plays as organ-stinger melodrama. Nole: "Guys, I've had enough. This is the final straw." [V] It ends on Mas's lowercase reply the next day: "i remain enthusiastic about the non-profit structure!" [V; dated Sep 21, 2017 per WC-FB §0 #1]

**Why here.** The trial (Apr 27–May 18, 2026). Nole says "The biggest risk would be that AI kills us all" on the stand, and Mas reads the 2017 line aloud during his own testimony. The montage makes the verdict legible to anyone: the story everyone is fighting over happened in 2017, and the calendar says the window has closed.

**Transition**
- **In:** an **exhibit sticker** slaps onto the courtroom projector, and its light blooms into the render.
- **Out:** the sticker peels off, the projector clicks, and we're back in court. Exhibit → exhibit.

**Guardrails**
- "I thought he was going to hit me" is **not** used or staged.
- The judge's "beat your wife" exchange is excluded.
- No family.
- The optional "TOO MANY LARRYS" freeze includes EGAP and YRRAL only, **never THE OTHER YRRAL** (WC-CRIT D29). Default: cut it (see open questions).

## F8.2 · "THE LANDLORD'S KEY"
| Field | |
|---|---|
| **When** | Feb 4, 2014 → Jul 22, 2019 |
| **POV** | TASYA (rim #5B6B8C) |
| **Era tier** | **T2a 240p** (2014), turning to **T3 cut-paper** (2019) with each key click |
| **Runtime** | ~25 s |
| **Truth labels** | The deal date is `[K]`, so it gets no day-level date card (`2019` only). THE MATCHER's role [V] |

**What we see.** Feb 4, 2014: TASYA takes over as CEO [V] and is handed a key ring the size of a steering wheel. (Correction: the flashback map's "cloud CEO" is wrong; he ran Server & Tools 2011–14, then became CEO.) Each turn of a key in a lock jumps a year. In 2019 THE MATCHER walks Mas in [V: Sam credits Scott with selling him on the deal]. Tasya slides one key off the ring and hands it over. Its fob reads `AGI CLAUSE` in microtext.

**Why here.** On Apr 27, 2026 the amended deal removes the AGI clause, the same day the trial opens [V]. On May 11 Tasya testifies that Nole "never expressed concern" [V]. The landlord gave out exactly one key, and took it back the morning of the trial.

**Transition**
- **In:** in the present, TASYA slides the `AGI CLAUSE` key off his ring.
- **Out:** in 2019, a key turns in a lock; in the present, it unlocks the courthouse door. Key → key.
