**What we see**

2. **Prologue (Aug 11, 2017).** The ATOD bot beats THE CHAMPION. Nole's tweet card: "Vastly more risk than North Korea." [V] It's text only, no imagery of the country.
3. **The Model 3 meeting (late Aug 2017), four ways.** **Style moment (proposed) · drastic ×4, the season's range showcase.** Motivation: the plot is four companies' image models rendering one memory. **Equal polish:** all four are rendered with the same care, each keeps one small self-flattering tell, and each carries **exactly one artifact** typical of its medium, so no version reads as the lie at a real trial.

   | Witness | Medium | Its one artifact | Its tell |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | **GERG** | Pure **ASCII**, green on black (extra blocky; per the flashback map) | One mis-encoded glyph (`�`) where the painting's corner should be | His own figure is drawn in the densest characters |
   | **NOLE** | A glossy, **toy-like 3D album cover**, stylized and never near-photoreal: lens flares, a flaming *guitar* (never a sword or any weapon near a real person) | A lens flare where Mas's face should be | Nole stands 40% taller; the Model 3s are chrome chariots |

   The old MAS render (chairs melting, six fingers on everyone) is retired: it marked his version as the lie, the same credibility problem the court V.O. cut exists to avoid. The style-range pass decides the finish (its 8.B mannequins are its call).

   **Every version ends identically:** "I decline" [V]; the Tesla painting leaves the room under Nole's arm [V; call it "the Tesla painting," not Alyi's commission]; and on the way out, "When will you be departing OpenAI?" [V]

**Why here.** The trial (Apr 27–May 18, 2026). Nole says "The biggest risk would be that AI kills us all" on the stand, and Mas reads his 2017 reply aloud during his own testimony. The montage makes the verdict legible to anyone: the story everyone is fighting over happened in 2017, which is what THE PLAN's folded step 3 turns on when the calendar opens it.

**Transition**

**Guardrails**

- "I thought he was going to hit me" is **not** used or staged.

- The optional "TOO MANY LARRYS" freeze includes EGAP and YRRAL only, **never THE OTHER YRRAL** (WC-CRIT D29). Default: cut it (see open questions).

## F8.2 · "THE LANDLORD'S KEY" (micro since the second pass)
| Field | |
|---|---|
| **When** | 2019 (the 2014 ring is a single frame) |
| **POV** | TASYA (rim #5B6B8C) |
| **Era tier** | **T3 cut-paper** (2019); the 2014 frame in **T2a 240p** |
| **Runtime** | ~10 s |
| **Truth labels** | The deal date is `[K]`, so it gets no day-level date card (`2019` only). THE MATCHER's role [V] |

**What we see.** One 240p frame: 2014, TASYA takes over as CEO [V] and is handed a key ring the size of a steering wheel. (Correction: the flashback map's "cloud CEO" is wrong; he ran Server & Tools 2011–14, then became CEO.) A key turns, and it's 2019: THE MATCHER walks Mas in [V: Sam credits Scott with selling him on the deal]. Tasya slides one key off the ring and hands it over. Its fob reads `AGI CLAUSE`, and it fits one lock, on a door marked `MACROSOFT`: the one key that could lock the landlord out. Mas pockets it.

**Why here.** On Apr 27, 2026 the amended deal removes the AGI clause, the same day the trial opens [V]. On the courthouse steps his phone shows Tasya's ring gaining a key back, and Mas's pocket comes up empty; the micro answers why. On May 11 Tasya testifies that Nole "never expressed concern" [V], and the heavier ring jangles once. The landlord gave out exactly one key, and took it back the morning of the trial.

**Transition**
- **In:** in the present, Mas's hand comes out of his coat pocket empty.
- **Out:** in 2019, a key turns in a lock; in the present, the bailiff's key unlocks the courthouse door. Key → key.
