## 2. The lock rules

1. **Every line takes its length from its take** (`frames_24`). Nothing is lengthened for a window, a box or typing.
2. **A reply is chained to the line it answers:** it starts where that line ends, minus the cue sheet's overlap (`overlap_prev_frames`), plus the dialogue editor's marked gap (`gap_prev_s`: the scripted pauses, all ≤ 1 s: "good question." exactly 1 beat late, TASYA 1 beat after the D8 line), or plus the default 3–7 f (0.125–0.29 s). A line is chained when `lines.json` names the relation or the board put it ≤ 8 f after the previous line.
3. **Conversation cuts land on the turn:** a cut-on-the-turn shot starts at its first line minus the board's lead; a negative lead is the board's L-cut pre-lap, kept frame-accurate.
4. **A line-carrying shot inside a conversation block keeps the board's tail** after its last line (the listener's hold, a read floor, a rack), so its length follows the take.
5. **Everything else keeps the board's length:** set-pieces, inserts, cards, posts and holds are cut to their music and staged action. A take that overruns its shot grows it by exactly the overrun, never to the grid. A line may L-cut ≤ 8 f into a shot that continues the read.
6. **V.O.** ends ≥ 1 beat before its cut and the next spoken line (pov-and-framing §5.1).
7. **No padding.** No conversation block is closed back onto the beat grid; each set-piece's music is cut from its own first frame (§6).
