## What changed from v4

v4 had 45 lines and 176 words of dialogue in 4:11: a median of 4 words per line, and a longest conversation of 10 s. The showrunner said conversations never got to play out.

v5 rewrites the act so they do. It has 101 lines, about 836 words in the reel, and a median of 5 words per line. The board's call, Neleh and Mario's phone call, the all-hands, and the return terms now play as scenes. The dialogue guide changed to match ([tone-and-dialogue.md](../../../../bible/tone-and-dialogue.md): "let conversations play out", "Scene craft and film grammar").

## Process note (for a successor)

On 2026-09-26 a usage limit stalled this workflow (`mrmas-dialogue-v5`). Resuming it re-ran every step after the stalled one, including the Ep1–3 script critics and fixers, which had already finished earlier that day. Resume only caches the unbroken prefix of agent calls. Those re-run critics produced a useful extra round, "dialogue pass 5", saved in [show/production/reviews/2026-09-26-dialogue-pass-5-critiques.md](../../../../production/reviews/2026-09-26-dialogue-pass-5-critiques.md). A separate finishing pass applies it.

**Lesson:** before resuming a workflow whose later steps have side effects, check which steps would re-run, or split it into separate workflows.
