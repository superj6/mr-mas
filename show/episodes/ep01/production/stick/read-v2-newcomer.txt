**(1) RETELL, FROM WHAT THE SHOW TOLD ME**
- **Cold open, Nov 16 2023, San Francisco.**
  - Mas Manalt, CEO of NopeAI, is on a panel talking about the frontier of discovery.
  - His phone shows "Board sync · Fri 12:00". He says "noted."
  - The reel rewinds, overshoots to a 1993 OK/Cancel box, and the intro plays. It is a portrait gallery: Mas at age 8 with "no equity", Gerg, Alyi, Mario and Nole. A city of labs is labelled Elgoog, Atem, Macrosoft, Misanthropic and Invidia.
- **Act One: the launch and the first months.**
  - Nov 30 2022. Gerg wants to ship. Rima (CTO) wants it low-key, with a banner saying it can be wrong. Alyi (chief scientist) has counted 6 years and 11 months to this click.
  - Mas clicks. ChatGTP flatters everyone. Users go 1, then 1,389, then 1 million in 5 days. Rima calls it low-key; Gerg says "basement."
  - The servers turn out to be the bill.
  - Elgoog's Radnus is "politely on fire". Its retired founders are called back and ask "Is search all right?" They built the underlying work and published it.
  - Jan 2023: Tasya, "the landlord" from Macrosoft, brings about $10B. Everything NopeAI builds runs on her servers. "Everyone is welcome. Rent is due on the first."
  - Macrosoft puts NopeAI's model inside GNIB to go after Elgoog. Elgoog's Drab demo gets a fact wrong and Elgoog loses about $100B in a day.
  - GNIB's chatbot Sydney insists it is 2022 and scolds users; Tasya caps it at 5.
  - Atem releases its model openly.
  - GTP-4 and Mario's Clod launch on the same day, while Mario writes memos about not racing.
  - A letter asks labs to pause for six months. Nole signs while building his own lab. Another writer says pausing isn't enough: "shut it all down."
- **Act Two: the charm tour.**
  - May 2023, White House photo op. The VP explains AI with blocks. Mario has sub-concerns. Tasya "owns camera two." Radnus congratulates Mas through his teeth. President Nedib says to put it in writing.
  - Senate hearing: the chairman opens with his own AI clone. Critic Sucram "called it". Mas says he loves his job, is paid enough for health insurance and has no equity, and hands over "PLEASE REGULATE ME."
  - The "Regulate-Me Tour" in Europe: Mas threatens to leave, the EU calls it blackmail, and he backs down.
  - He and Mario sign a one-sentence extinction-risk statement; Mario adds a footnote with an appendix. Nesnej of Invidia, who "sells shovels", hits $1T and sells Mas a purchase order.
- **Act Three: montage in Mas's dark room.**
  - An iris-scanning Orb he co-founded arrives. He doesn't need to be verified, and it stays.
  - He catches up on seven weeks: New Delhi "hopeless to compete", the VP's "two letters", the White House pinky promise, a senators' forum where everyone wants a referee, and Nozama's $4B to the rival lab.
  - Mas posts "AGI achieved internally" on Tidder. Gerg calls to patch it.
  - Executive order on testing big models; deepfakes of Nedib.
  - DevDay: 100M users a week, and Tasya says "We love you guys."
  - Back to Nov 16, 11:59 PM, the board invite.
- **Act Four: the firing.**
  - A blueprint shows how the board works. With Mas and Gerg left out, four members (Alyi, Neleh, Mada and a silent fourth) are a majority. The nonprofit controls the company, Macrosoft has 0 votes, and the CEO has 0 equity.
  - Noon call, from Mas's side: OK, then removed. The reel rewinds to the board's side: Alyi tells him he is out, and his feed isn't frozen, "that is just him."
  - The blog post goes up ("not consistently candid"). Rima becomes interim CEO: "more soon." Gerg quits. Staff ask whether it was a coup.
  - Neleh improvises step four:
    - She offers Mario the CEO job and a merger; he says no.
    - Mas turns up in the lobby wearing a guest badge.
    - She hires Ttemme, who ran a streaming site, as a second interim CEO. He asks why they fired him.
  - Tasya announces that Mas and Gerg are joining Macrosoft.
  - "What they didn't know": at 2 AM, 745 of 770 staff sign a letter to quit for Macrosoft unless the board resigns. Alyi voted to fire Mas and also signed the letter. A staff share sale is void if the CEO is missing. Gerg is rebuilding the company "just in case."
  - Tasya offers a desk for everyone. Mas says "leave it open."
  - The board members leave the call one by one; Mada is left. Alyi posts his regret, and Mas sends three hearts.
  - Nov 21: new chair Terb reads the deal. Mas returns as CEO. Mada stays, Mas and Gerg are off the board, and there will be an "independent review. Of what? Good question."
  - A Q* vault ("It's a preview." / "You said that about the last one"). Mas says nice things about Alyi; Alyi's nameplate goes in a drawer; Macrosoft gets a non-voting observer seat.
- **Tag.**
  - A demo "wasn't real-time". Mas is CEO of the Year.
  - A glass of water lies flat. The Grey Lady files a complaint against Macrosoft and NopeAI. "noted."

**(6) IS THE OVERARCHING STORYLINE CLEAR?**
Yes, the spine is clear to a newcomer. A "low-key preview" becomes a phenomenon. It is paid for by a landlord whose servers it can't leave. Its CEO courts regulators while having no equity. His board has the power to fire him, and it does, without a reason it can say out loud. The staff and the landlord force him back within days, with a new board and a review of nothing in particular.

The running bits carry the viewer well: "it's a preview", no equity, OK/Cancel, "super", "Good question", Gerg's build, "Everyone is welcome". So do the "HIS SIDE / THE BOARD'S SIDE" labels, the steps checklist, and the payoff that Alyi "did both".

The weak spots:
- Act Three is a string of unattributed fragments.
- The return negotiation is off screen.
- It's never clear whether Mas actually joined Macrosoft.
- The tag's closing hooks (the Grey Lady, the glass of water, the demo) need outside knowledge, so the episode ends on its least readable beat for a newcomer.
