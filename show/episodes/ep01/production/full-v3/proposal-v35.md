**How to read a scene:**
- **Status:** KEPT (as in the v3.4 film), CHANGED (the film's scene with something added, moved or cut), or NEW (not in the film). Lengths are estimates.
- **Mode:**
  - EXTENDED CONVERSATION (plays out)

  - MONTAGE (time compressed)
  - FLASHBACK
  - SINGLE IMAGE / INSERT (a held picture)
  - DOCUMENT (a post, letter or page, typed or read)
- **Feeling:** what the viewer should feel, and what makes them feel it.
- **Understanding:**
  - **FULL:** understood now.
  - **PARTIAL:** sensed; what's missing, and where it's filled.
  - **WITHHELD:** not answered on purpose; why, and where, if ever.
- **Lines:** new and changed lines are spelled out; kept lines show their first words.
- **Out:** what causes the next scene, the sound that leads into it, and the object that carries the cut.
- **Pace:** how fast people answer each other.
- **Tags:** **[P]** primary source · **[V]** verified · **[H]** headlines · **[K]** known, not re-checked · **[INVENTED]** our dialogue or props.
- The small italic line under each scene gives the v3.4 beat ids.

## The story in one paragraph

Mas Manalt ships a chatbot his own team calls a preview, and in five days the world decides otherwise.

**What he wants isn't money.** He owns none of it, by a design he drew himself in 2019. He wants to be the one in the room when it happens, and to keep it growing. So every problem becomes a move: the bill becomes the landlord, the fear becomes his own ask to be regulated, and the rules become his stage.

**Act One is his rise and the race it starts.** Every rival answers him:
- Elgoog panics: the incumbent, forced to race.
- Macrosoft puts his model in its own clothes: Tasya wants to own the building.
- Atem goes open because it can't win closed: Kram.
- Misanthropic warns against a race and launches the same day: Mario, safety as the reason to build.
- Nole, scared of it from its first week, signs a pause with one hand and files his own company with the other: credit and control.
- Meanwhile the team watches its dream come true, and we see why they started: a 2018 night when a machine taught itself, and Alyi asked him what else you would build.

**Act Two.** The president wants promises in writing, and Mas's is half written.
- He takes it to the Senate, asks to be regulated, and shows he owns nothing.
- He won't run the agency, so he carries his rules to the world himself.
- The race's leaders sign one sentence warning of extinction, and the same day they all order more chips from Nesnej, who sells to everyone.

**Act Three is the peak.** The Orb (proof you're human), a fake president the Orb can tell from the real one, and a hundred million people a week.

**Act Four is the blow and the return.**
- The board he put on top removes him over a video call.
- He's surprised, and scrambles through a weekend with a still face while the world spins around him.
- Alyi, watching the company he built empty into the landlord's building, turns.
- The staff, on their own, say they'll leave, and Mas comes back in five days on what he'd arranged: the landlord, the money, Gerg.
- His count at the end is a lesson he first learned at TPOOL.
