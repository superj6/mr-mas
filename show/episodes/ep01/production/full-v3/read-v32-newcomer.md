## 1. Retell (10–15 sentences)

1. Mas Manalt is the quiet, lowercase-talking CEO of NopeAI. He has a dry inner voice and a floating eyeball-orb for company.
2. On Nov 30, 2022 he signs off on Gerg's "low-key research preview" chatbot. Rima wants it quiet, Alyi asks whether it will "wake up," and someone asks whether anyone told the rest of the board. Nobody really answers.
3. The bot flatters everyone and hits a million users in days. The servers melt, so Mas phones Tasya at Macrosoft: "we're going to need more servers." Tasya says, "I'll bring a pen."
4. Tasya becomes the landlord. He brings a giant cheque, puts a NopeAI key on his key ring and gives Mas free servers, with "rent due on the first."
5. Macrosoft puts Mas's model in its search engine. Rival Elgoog panics, calls its founders back and flubs its own demo, losing $100B in a day. Macrosoft's version, "Sydney," goes rogue and gets put on a leash.
6. Mario, who used to work at NopeAI, runs a safety-first rival, and critics call for a pause, then for "shut it all down." Mas answers by writing "PLEASE REGULATE ME."
7. He takes that to the White House and the Senate, where he asks for a licensing agency, says he owns no equity and turns down the job of running it. Then he tours the world, and the chip seller gets rich.
8. By autumn 2023 he's the face of AI. He posts "AGI has been achieved internally" as a joke, runs DevDay (100M users a week) and has to pause sign-ups because demand is too high.
9. One of his own board members, Neleh, publishes a paper calling the launch "frantic corner-cutting." Then a Friday board invite arrives.
10. Nov 17, in a Las Vegas suite: four board members (Alyi, Neleh, Mada and "the Quiet Vote") fire him on a video call and remove him from the meeting. Their post says he was "not consistently candid." Rima becomes interim CEO, and Gerg quits.
11. Mas posts a gracious goodbye. The internet floods him with hearts, investors push to bring him back, and he walks into HQ wearing a GUEST badge.
12. The board offers the job to Mario, who says no, then replaces Rima with a streamer, Ttemme. Meanwhile Macrosoft announces that Mas and Gerg will join it.
13. At 2 AM Mas calls Gerg: "read me the letter." 745 of 770 staff will quit unless the board resigns, and Alyi, who voted to fire him, signed it too. Mas tells Gerg to "keep building," and he keeps Macrosoft's offer open.
14. The board collapses. Mas accepts a board invite and comes back as CEO with a new chair in a firefighter's helmet, and he insists Gerg comes back too.
15. The tag: Elgoog's new rubber-duck demo, a CEO of the Year cover and a newspaper's copyright lawsuit, which Mas meets with "noted.", the same word as the cold open.

## 2. The three direct questions

### 2a. What does Mas want, and what does he *do* to get it?

**What he wants:** to keep NopeAI shipping and growing, on his terms, and to stay the one holding the pen. Once he's fired, he wants back in, and he wants Gerg back with him.

**What he does,** as a chain:

- **So Mas** presses the button on a "preview" nobody fully agreed to (2:07). He dodges Rima's question with "it's a preview" and says nothing when she asks whether the board was told (2:00).
- **Because of that,** a million people show up and the bill explodes, **so Mas** calls Tasya himself (3:26, "we're going to need more servers").
- **Because of that,** Macrosoft owns the servers and holds a NopeAI key. Mas wears the collar and jokes about it ("and the rent?"). His model powers Macrosoft's search, and Elgoog stumbles.
- **Because** the pause and shut-down calls grow louder (6:05–6:17), **Mas** writes "PLEASE REGULATE ME" (6:22). At the Senate he asks for the licensing agency himself and slides the signed page across the table (8:24–8:30). He turns down the job of running it and says he has no equity.
- **Because of that,** he's welcomed everywhere: the world tour, the extinction statement, DevDay. He keeps pushing (DevDay's "build your own," pausing sign-ups under demand) while his own board member writes that the launch was corner-cutting (11:01).
- **Because of that,** the board fires him (12:22). **So Mas** posts a gracious exit (12:42) and walks into HQ with a guest badge and a selfie (15:19). He calls Gerg and asks to hear the staff letter (16:39–16:49). He tells Gerg "keep building" (17:36) and keeps Macrosoft's door open as leverage (17:51, "leave it open."). He sends Alyi three hearts (18:25) and accepts the board's invite (18:55).
- **Because of that,** he's back (19:07), and he gets "gerg comes back too" into the deal (19:24).

That is a real spine now. Most links are cause and effect, and in Acts One and Four Mas is usually the one making the move.

**What's still thin:** in Act Two Mas has **no inner voice at all**. The SRT has zero VO lines across 6:28–9:41, and Act Three has two. So the key question about the regulate-me tour goes unanswered: does he *mean* "please regulate me," or is it a move? One VO line there would turn the tour from a sequence of events into a strategy (something like "if they're writing rules, i'd like to hold the pen").

### 2b. Does it feel like his rise to power, or a random sequence of events?

- **Act One** (0:59–6:28) mostly feels like his rise now. Launch, the bill, *his* call to the landlord, the collar, his model in someone else's search engine, the rival stumbling, the critics, his "PLEASE." Each scene hands off to the next.
- **Act Two** (6:28–9:41) reads as a rise, but a told one. It's scenes of rooms welcoming him: the White House photo, the Senate, the tour poster, the rooftop. Because he says nothing inside, it plays like a highlight reel rather than a climb. The act-out (the stock chart becoming a waterline in a glass, 9:33–9:41) doesn't say "rise" to me. It says "drowning," and I don't know whose head that is.
- **Act Three** (9:43–12:00) is the **most random-feeling stretch left**. It's mostly Mas at his desk watching things on a monitor: Macrosoft welcomes Atem, the orb arrives, the VP's "two letters" clip, the pinky promise, the senators raising hands, Tidder, Neleh's paper, the executive order and deepfakes, DevDay, the sign-up pause. Several of these are fun, but only two push the story:
  - Neleh's paper, which plants the board.
  - DevDay and the sign-up pause, which show peak power.

  The rest is a channel-surf.
- **Act Four** is a clear, gripping story. It isn't a rise, though. It's a fall and a comeback, and Mas has a hand in the comeback now.

**Net:** it now reads as "the rise, the reckoning, the return," with the rise front-loaded into Act One. Act Three still drifts, and it is where the rise should peak.

### 2c. What still confuses me, or comes out of nowhere?

Short list (details in sections 3 and 4):
1. **The glass and waterline** at 9:33–9:41 (Act Two out). Whose head is it, and why water?
2. **"CO-FOUNDER" on the Coinworld box** at 9:55. Why does Mas co-found an eyeball-scanning company? It's never explained.
3. **The Act Three TV montage** at 10:13–10:28: the VP on "AI," the pinky promise, the raised hands. It reads as filler.
4. **Why Mas posts "AGI has been achieved internally"** at 10:29. There's no trigger.
5. **Neleh's paper** at 11:01 is the key plant, but Mas never reacts to it.
6. **Why the board dumps Rima for Ttemme**, and what Ttemme learned from the paper he read at 15:39–15:56.
7. **"Down here."** at 18:50. Who says it, and what does it mean?
8. **The 406 / 407 / 406 count** at 16:24–16:33 is still a riddle.
9. **Who the long-haired woman is** in the over-the-shoulder board shots at 13:25 and 15:27.
10. **Alyi as a blue ghost head** in some shots and a plain human face in others.
11. **The Q\* safe** at 20:08 and **the rubber duck** at 20:37. Neither is set up.

Structurally this is a different film from the one I saw yesterday. Mas has a spine now:
- He makes the Macrosoft call.
- He writes the "please regulate me."
- He asks for the agency.
- He walks into the building.
- He calls Gerg.
- He says "keep building."
- He keeps the door open.
- He accepts the invite.
- He brings Gerg back.

The board's case is also planted: Rima's question at 2:00 and Neleh's paper at 11:01.

What's left is mostly **Act Three** and **Mas's silence in the middle**. Act Two has no inner voice at all, and Act Three is Mas watching screens. The rise needs one or two moments where we hear him *want* it. And the paper that gets him fired needs one beat of him reading it and deciding to ignore it.
