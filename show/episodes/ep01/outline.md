## Logline
A soft-spoken "nonprofit guy" with no equity presses a tiny button labeled *low-key research preview* and launches the fastest-growing product anyone has seen. Every government on earth asks him to regulate himself, and he says yes to all of them. Then his own board clicks the one button nobody has ever managed to click on him, **Cancel**, and it works for five days.

## Plots

### A-plot: THE BUTTON AND THE BLIP
- CHATGTP launches as a "research preview". The user odometer drills through NopeAI's floors, and the compute bill makes Mas's eyes water, literally.
- NopeAI goes from lab to landlord's favorite tenant: MACROSOFT's ~$10B check, Gerg's napkin demo, DevDay's "We love you guys."
- **THE BLIP, told twice.** Pass one is the board's heist, a flawless plan that fires Mas over a video call in Vegas. The card `WHAT THEY DIDN'T KNOW` starts pass two: the same five days from the other side. Hearts, a 745-of-770 letter, a midnight job offer from the landlord, and a guest badge. He is back in five days.

### B-plot: THE RACE STARTS
Everyone reacts to the button.
- ELGOOG sounds a code red and loses ~$100B to a typo.
- GNIB's SYDNEY gets clingy.
- CLOD 1 launches the same day as GTP-4.
- Kram's model leaks, and he calls it open source.
- Nole signs the PAUSE letter with one hand and founds zAI with the other.
- Nesnej's register rings at $1T.
- Mario warns about race dynamics while banking NOZAMA's and ELGOOG's checks.

### C-plot: THE REGULATE-ME TOUR
The governments meet the chatbot, and both parties get roasted.
- EOJ NEDIB and SIRRAH hold the first class photo, collect a pinky-promise scroll and sign EO 14110 in fountain-pen ink while deepfake NEDIBs heckle.
- LAHTNEMULB's cloned voice opens the Senate hearing and reads better than he does.
- REMUHCS's forum ends with every hand raised and no bill.
- NEYEL and NOTERB hold the "cease operating" / "blackmail" tour stop. Bletchley has KING LARCHES on a video screen.
- **DLANOD J. RUMPT is a silhouette only**: a phone glow across the water at OGAL-A-RAM, one repost of a voice-cloned anchor, and a single HTURT hailstone in the cold-open city.

## Themes
- **Cancel.** The 1993 dialog's Cancel button is greyed out. In Nov 2023 the board finally clicks it, and one beat later it greys out again. The man who can't be fired is the pilot's premise, and it's also the season's.
- **"Research preview" as deniability.** A launch that doesn't count if it fails. Every institution in the episode does the same thing: pinky promises, forums with no bills, a pause letter signed by someone building a lab.
- **You can't take anything from a man who owns nothing.** "No equity" is his shield at the Senate and his punchline at the Blip.
- **Everyone signs the warning, then buys the GPUs.** The extinction statement and INVIDIA's $1T land on the same day.

## THE PLAN (45s blueprint)
**`HOW TO FIRE A CEO WHO OWNS NOTHING.`**
- The blueprint shows the nonprofit board on top, the capped-profit company underneath, the landlord outside the fence with no vote, and a CEO box labeled `EQUITY: 0`.
- The board's four steps: noon video call, blog post, interim CEO, and Step 4, which is blank.
- It explains "nonprofit control", "capped profit" and "not consistently candid" for general viewers, then fails live.
