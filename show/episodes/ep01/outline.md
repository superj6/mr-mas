## Logline
A soft-spoken nonprofit CEO with no equity presses a tiny button labelled *research preview* and launches the fastest-growing product anyone has seen. Every government on earth asks him to help write the rules, and he says yes to all of them. Then the one body that can remove him, his own board, clicks the one button nobody has ever managed to click on him, **Cancel**, and it works for five days.

## Through-line (what a newcomer must be able to retell)
- **Who he is and what he wants:** Mas runs NopeAI, a nonprofit AI lab, and owns none of it. He wants to keep shipping, and to be the one everyone asks.
- **What happens:** his chatbot becomes the fastest-growing product ever; the landlord (MACROSOFT) pays in; the rivals panic; he spends a year charming every government into letting him help write the rules.
- **The turn:** his board fires him over a video call. Within five days the staff, the investors and the landlord bring him back, the board is gone, and nobody has ever looked harder to fire.
- **Every load-bearing fact is set up inside the show** (the season revision's newcomer passes): who he is (the APEC tent card), who Gerg, Rima, Alyi, Mario and Nole are to him (plates on their first lines: co-founder, CTO, chief scientist, the one who left to build the careful rival, the early funder building his own), which rival Radnus and Kram run, that the landlord is MACROSOFT and pays (the check and Tasya's card), that NopeAI is a nonprofit (the door the check jams in), that the man at the White House is the president (his card), that he owns no equity (the Senate wallet), and what "AGI" means (the first reply under his TIDDER post). What the board can do is withheld until THE PLAN tells it, once. See [open-questions](open-questions.md#setups-planted-for-act-four).

## Plots

### A-plot: THE BUTTON AND THE BLIP
- CHATGTP launches as a "research preview". The user odometer drills through NopeAI's floors, and the compute bill makes Mas's eyes water, literally.
- NopeAI goes from lab to the landlord's favourite tenant: MACROSOFT's ~$10B check, Gerg's napkin demo (which ships Mario's memo too), and DevDay, where the launch-night odometer comes back up through the stage at 100 million a week and Tasya says "We love you guys."
- **THE BLIP, told twice.** First his side: THE PLAN, the noon call in Vegas, the falling tile. Then the board's side, blind: their steps, the phones, the offer to the rival, the landlord's door. The card `WHAT THEY DIDN'T KNOW` starts his side again: the hearts, the 745-of-770 letter, the check void without him, Gerg rebuilding. He is back in five days.

### B-plot: THE RACE STARTS
Everyone reacts to the button.
- ELGOOG sounds a code red (on his phone) and loses ~$100B to a telescope fact.
- GNIB's SYDNEY, the landlord's chatbot, gets clingy.
- CLOD 1 launches the same day as GTP-4, while Mario drafts a memo on race dynamics.
- Kram's model leaks, and he calls it open source.
- Nole signs the PAUSE letter with one hand and solders a GPU with the other.
- Nesnej's register rings at $1T.
- Mario warns about race dynamics while answering NOZAMA's call.

### C-plot: THE REGULATE-ME TOUR
The governments meet the chatbot, and both parties get roasted.
- SIRRAH and EOJ NEDIB hold the first class photo; NEDIB collects a pinky-promise scroll and signs EO 14110 in fountain-pen ink while deepfake NEDIBs heckle.
- LAHTNEMULB's cloned voice opens the Senate hearing and reads better than he does. **Mas promises the systems do "tasks, not jobs" while the clone, in the better chair, reads the chairman's next card for him** (W1.3). He shows he owns nothing, and asks to be regulated.
- REMUHCS's forum ends with every hand raised and no bill.
- The London stop: "cease operating", NOTERB's "blackmail" stamp, "no plans to leave". (Bletchley is cut: KING LARCHES first appears in Ep6.)
- **DLANOD J. RUMPT is a silhouette only**: a phone glow across the water at OGAL-A-RAM, one repost of an AI-altered anchor (a clip Mas is already watching on his phone at the bullpen window), and two HTURT hailstones.

## Themes
- **Cancel.** The 1993 dialog's Cancel button is greyed out. In Nov 2023 the board finally clicks it, and one beat later it greys out again. The man who can't be fired is the pilot's premise, and it's also the season's.
- **"Research preview" as deniability.** A launch that doesn't count if it fails. Every institution in the episode does the same thing: pinky promises, forums with no bills, a pause letter signed by someone building a lab.
- **You can't take anything from a man who owns nothing.** "No equity" is his shield at the Senate and the question at the heart of THE PLAN.
- **Everyone signs the warning, then buys the GPUs.** The extinction statement and INVIDIA's $1T land on the same day.
- **Tasks, not jobs.** His promise to the Senate, made while a machine does the chairman's job beside him. Ep8 takes it back.

## How the firing lands (no spoiler before Act Four)
The showrunner's rule for the pilot: hint at the firing, never spoil it before the end. It is a season rule ([pacing-model L9](../../format/pacing-model.md#24-no-announced-climaxes-l9)): no flash-forward chyron, countdown or card states the outcome.
- **The clock** is a date, not a verdict. At APEC the frozen room leaves two things in colour, Mas and his phone, which shows `Board sync · Fri 12:00` with four unnamed attendee circles (a doorway, a glowing page, a spinner, a black square). "noted." He accepts without looking.
- **The act-outs turn on hints, never a count, and each has a different out:** Alyi's reflection reading the doom headline behind him, played dry, then his own pen lifting off `PLEASE` (Mar 29; THREAT on the pen, never on Alyi, so it implies no motive); the sky's crack running on in his glass until it crosses his reflection (May 30; the register bell's decay, no sting); the invite's reminder and the rail rolling to `NOV 17` (Nov 16; THE CLOCK's last step, black, and the crane truck pre-lapping Vegas).
- **Zero-read eggs** for viewers who know the week: two tally marks with room for a third, and NELEH's glowing paper in the monitor's corner.
- **The payoff:** THE PLAN's four blueprint figures are the four circles, then the call grid, then the falling tile. A first-time viewer gets a surprise; a rewatch shows it was on screen from the cold open.
