## F1.1 · `1993` · *the screen, part 1 of 4*
- **Date card:** `1993` **only**. Per critic §E42, the computer came "at eight" [V Wikipedia], not necessarily on his birthday, and possibly not until early 1994. Don't print `APR 22, 1993`.
- **We see (4 s):** a paper-white field and a 1-bit alert dialog floating alone in it: `OK`, with `Cancel` greyed out in a 50% dither. A plain single-rule frame, no bomb or stop icon, no computer, desk or hand. The kid himself first appears in the intro's bar 3 about 12 s later, so it plays as a reveal, not a repeat.
- **Why here:** the cold open has just shown him accept a Friday-noon board invite without looking; nothing says what the meeting is for (the no-spoiler rule, [pacing-model L9](../../format/pacing-model.md#24-no-announced-climaxes-l9)). This shot sets up the button the board will finally click at that noon: the one thing that has never worked on him is Cancel. First-time viewers read a greyed-out button; the rewatch reads it as the clock.
- **Transition in:** the Orb's `rewinding…`; the APEC room scrubs backward and steps down its light ramps to paper white.
- **Transition out:** the Orb's toast `rewinding… too far`, then SMASH TO the intro. After the intro and the title card, the beige `research preview` button sits in the exact screen position the 1993 `OK` held, a callback across the intro (sc 5).
- **Tier spec (T1):**
  - Pure `#000/#FFF`, 512×342, 6 fps, Bayer dither, 3:2 pillarbox, Silkscreen type.
  - Sound: MM-06's three square-wave chip notes on F, uneven in rhythm and stepping in pitch: never even beeps and never a held tone (X3).
- **Escalation thread** (the 1993 screen): **Ep1, the dialog alone** → Ep4, the other 1993 (Nesnej's SYNNED) → Ep7, the CAPS LOCK light → Ep12, `HOW DO I WIN?`
- **Guardrails:** no family and no bedroom detail. The kid never visibly types.

## F1.2 · 2005–08 · TPOOL · *the first two firings*
- **We see (≈ 3.5 s, inside 26A):** the Orb's iris steps onto tally mark 1, and a render front sweeps out of the mark and turns the frame to sixteen colours with GIF-era dither: a frosted-glass boardroom door with two shadows behind it, leaning together. No whisper and no POV rim: `(REPORTED)` material belongs to the show, never to his account.
- **Rail:** `2005–08 · TPOOL, HIS FIRST COMPANY · (REPORTED)`, typed on as the front sweeps in and cleared before it sweeps back into the wood grain.
- **Why here:** that night he carves mark 3 with the pen from MACROSOFT's check ("i don't keep score."), and the Orb counts the marks. We learn only that this has happened before. Per [timeline/flashback-map.md](../../timeline/flashback-map.md), the details are held for Ep12's tally montage (F12.3), always in their public, labelled `(REPORTED)` version.
- **Transition out:** the front sweeps back into the wood grain; the Orb's count resumes on 2, then 3, and stops on his thumb.
- **Truth labels:**
  - Wikipedia: "The board of Loopt attempted, twice, to remove Altman… but Altman's supporters defended him." [V]
  - WSJ's version is that senior staff asked the board [K].
  - Both say twice, so the rail reads `(REPORTED)`. "His first company" is for the facts owner to confirm.
- **Sound:** the dark room's felt thins to its pedal under F1.2, so `(REPORTED)` material carries no Mas motif; a frosted office's room tone crossfades in and out on the fronts.
- **Guardrails:** silhouettes only. No co-founders drawn. No detail of any allegation, and nothing beyond the public "twice."
