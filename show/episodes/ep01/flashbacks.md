## F1.1 · `1993` · MAS (age 8) · *the screen, part 1 of 4*
- **Date card:** `1993` **only**. Per critic §E42, the computer came "at eight" [V Wikipedia], not necessarily on his birthday, and possibly not until early 1994. Don't print `APR 22, 1993` on this card.
- **We see (4s):**
  - A small kid, lit only by the back glow of a beige computer with no logo. The model is unconfirmed, so it stays generic.
  - **The screen faces away from us.** It isn't revealed until Ep12.
  - A 1-bit alert dialog floats in the dark: `OK`, with `Cancel` greyed out.
  - A juice box on the desk doesn't ripple.
  - No captions. The kid's caption case (ALL CAPS) stays hidden until Ep12.
- **Why here:** The cold open has just told us he'll be fired tomorrow. This shot sets up the button the board will finally click at noon on Nov 17: the one thing that has never worked on him is Cancel.
- **Transition in:** The APEC freeze → **the curve rewinds** to its first pixel → `downgrading… HDR → 1-bit`.
- **Transition out:** The Orb's toast reads `rewinding… too far`. The curve snaps forward to `NOV 30, 2022`, and the 1-bit **OK** button match-cuts into the beige *low-key research preview* button (beat 6).
- **Tier spec (T1):**
  - Pure `#000/#FFF`, 512×342, 6 fps, Bayer dither, 3:2 pillarbox, Silkscreen type.
  - Sound: the square-wave beeper plays the knee motif (F F F F G A♭ C F).
- **Escalation thread** (the 1993 screen): **Ep1, back of the screen** → Ep4, the other 1993 (Nesnej's SYNNED) → Ep7, the CAPS LOCK light → Ep12, `HOW DO I WIN?`
- **Guardrails:** no family and no bedroom detail beyond the desk. The kid never visibly types.

## F1.2 · 2005–08 · TPOOL · *the first two firings*
- **We see (6s, silent):**
  - A 240p cutaway: a boardroom door and two shadows behind frosted glass.
  - A whispered "twice." [INVENTED whisper]
  - Chyron: `(REPORTED)`.
  - Cut back to the dark room. Mas carves **tally marks 1 and 2** into the desk beside a fresh **mark 3**.
- **Why here:** At noon on Nov 17, 2023 his video-call tile drops out of the grid. We learn only that this has happened before. Only the fact that it repeated is shown. Per [timeline/flashback-map.md](../../timeline/flashback-map.md), the details are held for Ep12's tally montage (F12.3), always in their public, labeled `(REPORTED)` version.
- **Transition in:** **a VHS tracking wipe,** triggered by his tile going grey.
- **Transition out:** The tracking lines settle into the grain of the desk, where the knife is already carving mark 3.
- **Truth labels:**
  - Wikipedia: "The board of Loopt attempted, twice, to remove Altman… but Altman's supporters defended him." [V]
  - WSJ's version is that senior staff asked the board [K].
  - Both say twice, so the card reads `(REPORTED)`.
- **Tier spec (T2a):** 320×240, 12 fps, scanlines, chroma bleed, VT323 OSD. There's no music hit, just tape hiss.
- **Guardrails:** silhouettes only. No co-founders drawn. No detail of any allegation, and nothing beyond the public "twice."
