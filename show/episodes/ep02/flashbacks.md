## F2.3 · FEB 20, 2018 · NOLE, then MAS · *the goodbye all-hands*

**Trigger (present).** The Email Séance, Mar 5, 2024. The ghosts of 2016–18 emails argue with present-day Nole. The last ghost won't speak.

**We see.**
- The candle snuffs, and its smoke draws a cut-paper all-hands.
- Nole addresses staff. His stated reason for leaving is the conflict with ALSET's own AI work.
- Staff "didn't entirely buy the story" [V Semafor]. Semafor [V] also reports that he believed NopeAI had "fallen fatally behind" ELGOOG [V]; show that as a caption paraphrase, not a spoken quote.
- Gerg and Alyi sit in the second row. Mas stands at the back, his crystal glass unrippled.
- The POV rim switches from Nole's red to Mas's cyan as Nole walks out, so the last image is Mas watching the door close.

**Why here.** He sues on Feb 29, 2024. His grievance isn't only the company; it's the email he never sent at his own exit. This is chapter 1 of Nole's grievance, deliberately told out of order: ep2 is the exit, ep6 the pledge, ep8 the 2014 demon and 2017.

**Transition in.** The séance candle snuffs, and the smoke carries the cut-paper transition.

**Transition out.** A 2s exit tag, per the canonical map. **DIRE's checkbook relights the candle**: he covered salaries after Nole left [V, gaps §3 "Late 2017… Reid Hoffman covers salaries"]. Present-day Nole snatches it to keep arguing.

**Tier (T3).** Crisp vector with paper grain. Frozen moments drop to navy `#14213D` and cream. Anton / Permanent Marker. A séance organ under the hybrid orchestra.

**Guardrails.**
- "path of certain failure" is [K]: never in quotes.
- The "jackass" anecdote is [UNVERIFIED]: excluded.
- **Keep all 2017 material out.** Honest Thoughts, the Model 3s and the painting belong to ep8.
- No physical staging of anger.

## F2.2 · 2022 · ALYI · *the bonfire*

**Trigger (present).** WHERE'S ALYI?, May 14–17, 2024. He has left; his door's IOU has fluttered away. Mas opens the TPOOL app and asks "where you at?" about Alyi [INVENTED].

**We see.**
- The pin lands on `LAST SEEN: 2022 · BONFIRE`.
- At a leadership offsite, a commissioned wooden effigy of "unaligned" AI burns [V, via The Atlantic in mid.md].
- The holiday-party chant builds, "FEEL THE AGI! FEEL THE AGI!" [V], with GPUs humming in harmony.
- Alyi is seen only as a silhouette against the flame, in the doorway of the room.

**Why here.** It explains the exit. He leaves on May 14 and founds ISS on Jun 19. The high priest who burned an effigy of an unaligned god now wants a lab with one goal, one product and one door.

**Transition in.** The pin's ping ripple turns into firelight.

**Transition out.** The fire's glow shrinks to the brass plate on his empty office door.

**Tier (T4).** Glossy HDR, lit only by flame. JetBrains Mono. Choir and glass shimmer.
- **Device note:** T4's usual entry is the Orb's iris replay, but the Orb didn't witness 2022. The map uses the pin device instead. That's consistent with rule 5, since the Orb's memory only covers events after 2019 that it saw.

**Guardrails.** Mysticism is his rhetorical style, not a belief system to mock. No clinical framing.
