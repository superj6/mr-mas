## F2.3 · FEB 20, 2018 · NOLE, then MAS · *the goodbye all-hands*

**Trigger (present).** The Email Séance, Mar 5, 2024. The last ghost, `DRAFT · NEVER SENT · Re: Why I'm leaving`, has no body and won't speak. Mas: "…anyone?"

**We see.**
- The candle nearest Nole snuffs, and its smoke repaints the boardroom into 2018: a small, crowded all-hands. `RAIL: FEB 20, 2018` [V].
- Nole at the front, mid-speech; the chamber tier carries it, and we don't hear the words. Gerg in the second row, typing even then. Alyi only in the window glass, in profile. Mas at the back with a crystal glass.
- The staff nod, all together, on the beat. A 2-TONE freeze in which only Mas moves (a sip). Nole leaves by a ladder and a ceiling hatch; the rim turns from red to cyan; the last image is Mas watching the hatch slide shut.
- **No captions (draft 4).** His stated reason (a potential future conflict with ALSET's AI work, [K]†) and Semafor's "didn't entirely buy the story" [V] stay in [facts.md](facts.md), off screen: the unison nod and the one moving sip say the room didn't buy it, as picture.

**Why here.** He sues on Feb 29, 2024, and his grievance isn't only the company; it's the email he never sent at his own exit, and the man at the back who didn't move. This is chapter 1 of Nole's grievance, told out of order on purpose: Ep2 is the exit, Ep6 the pledge, Ep8 the 2014 demon and 2017.

**Transition in.** The candle's smoke.

**Transition out.** The smoke thins back into 2024 on the dead candle. Nole strikes a match and relights it himself, holds it up to Mas's face, "You sat at the back.", and gets the flashback's sip for an answer. "See you in court." (Draft 3's DIRE exit tag is cut: an opaque insider insert with its own plate.)

**Sound.** The séance organ crossfades into the cut-paper chamber tier on the smoke, and back.

**Guardrails.**
- "path of certain failure" is [K]: never in quotes.
- The "jackass" anecdote is [UNVERIFIED]: excluded.
- **All 2017 material stays out.** Honest Thoughts, the Model 3s and the painting belong to Ep8.
- No physical staging of anger.

## F2.2 · 2022 · ALYI · *the bonfire*

**Trigger (present).** Alyi's empty office, the night of May 17, 2024. The chair went to BUKAJ; the IOU is in Mas's inventory. No V.O. (draft 4 cut "i wasn't looking for him."): his thumb is already scrolling to the dead TPOOL app (`LAST UPDATED 2012`), and he types "where u at?" [INVENTED], the string from the intro's 2008 keynote. Every pin reads `LAST SEEN: 2012` except one: `LAST SEEN: 2022 · BONFIRE`.

**We see.**
- At a leadership offsite, a wooden effigy of a paperclip robot stenciled `UNALIGNED` burns [V, The Atlantic via mid.md].
- Alyi stands in a lodge doorway, a silhouette against the flame, half cut off by the frame.
- A cut on the phrase to the holiday party: silhouettes against palette-cycled string lights; the chant builds from one voice to all, "FEEL THE AGI! FEEL THE AGI!" [V]; the GPUs hum in harmony.
- [GLYPH-MASKED · 12 FRAMES] at the peak: Alyi's eyes become scrolling token streams.

**Why here.** It explains the exit. He leaves on May 14 and founds ISS on Jun 19. The man who burned an effigy of an unaligned god now wants a lab with one goal, one product and one door.

**Transition in.** The pin's ping ripple widens, turns orange and becomes firelight.

**Transition out.** The fire's glow shrinks to one point of light, which is the pin pulsing on his phone. His thumb covers it, and he puts the phone away. Then receipt paper noses under the office door: the bridge's exit papers arrive from the departed man's room. (No hold on his face after a real item.)

**Device note.** The Orb didn't witness 2022, so the entry is the pin, not the Orb's iris replay (the Orb's memory covers only events after 2019 that it saw).

**Guardrails.** Mysticism is his rhetorical style, not a belief system to mock, and never clinical. A generic tech cathedral: no liturgical or religious iconography (X10).

## F2.1 · SEP 2006 → JUN 9, 2008 · MAS · *the Tpool era*

**Trigger (present).** WWDC, Jun 10, 2024. ELPPA builds CHATGTP into its phones (company to company; X2). **Mas watches the stream on his phone** at the back of the lobby (WC-crit §E47; his attendance in person is [UNVERIFIED]). A GPS breadcrumb, in colours older than the phone, draws itself across the stream's stage.

**We see.**
1. **A 2006 TSOOB phone ad** (≈ 9 s). Young Mas holds up a flip phone and grins, a GPS pin bobbing over his head. He doesn't speak (draft 4 cut his echo line, so "where you at" is told once, by the real end card): the ad's end card slams in, `"WHERE YOU AT?"` [V].
2. **Jun 9, 2008** (2 s, a match on the pin). The intro's stage: he walks on in **two stacked polos, both collars popped** [V], and takes the clicker from **THE SLEEVE**, a faceless turtleneck sleeve. The tiled crowd applauds. (Draft 3's giant screen, `TPOOL` and `where u at?`, and the `APP LIVE JUL 11` egg are cut: the intro shows that frame every week.)
3. **A match cut:** the clicker in his 2008 hand becomes his 2024 phone in the same grip.

**Why here.** Sixteen years later, the company whose stage he once borrowed builds his chatbot into its phones, and he isn't on the stage. The walled garden follows: CHATGTP is walked in wearing `GUEST`, raises its hand and waits before it speaks; the gate shuts and locks with Mas outside; V.O. *they ask first in there.* (No collar pops: Ep1 popped the third, and collars follow money.) The Tpool revolt details are **not** told here: the canonical map holds them for Ep12's tally montage (F12.3). The parked 3 s `(REPORTED)` insert stays parked ([open-questions](open-questions.md) #9).

**Deliberate withholding.** TPOOL is opened in sc 15, about three and a half minutes before this flashback explains it; the intro's `where u at?` primes it, and the show answers the question it raised.

**Transition in.** The breadcrumb crosses the boards he once stood on.

**Transition out.** The match cut back to his phone; the phone's picture then opens outward into the garden.

**Sound.** The cassette-piano and boom-bap tier over the quartet's held pedal (LEVERAGE runs sc 18–20 as one performance). The quartet turns into the garden-party arrangement after the match cut.

**Guardrails.**
- **THE SLEEVE:** no face, no thin or frail cues, no reference to his health or death (WC-crit §D39). A sleeve and a clicker, nothing more.
- Collar colours are design only, never on-screen text.
- The Tpool co-founders are not drawn. No revolt detail here (held for F12.3).
- The Jul 2008 SMS-invite spam flap is available (early.md) but unused.
