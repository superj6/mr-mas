## Logline
Nole sues, so NopeAI summons his old emails in a candlelit séance. Mas types one word ("her") and steals the spotlight from his own demo. His safety team walks out one resignation at a time, his exit papers turn out to be longer than the Bay Bridge, and his chief mystic disappears into a white room with one door. Across the country a faceless candidate inflates a balloon that says SO SCARY.

## Plots

### A-plot: GHOSTS AND VOICES
- **The Email Séance.** Nole's lawsuit arrives as 90% exclamation points (Feb 29). NopeAI answers by publishing his old emails (Mar 5). They rise by candlelight as ghosts and argue with present-day Nole. The last ghost is his own 2018 goodbye.
- **"her" eclipses the demo.** Rima hosts the omni-voice launch (May 13) and CHATGTP grows a face live. Mas, in the audience, types one lowercase word, and a blimp spelling it drifts over the stage and steals her spotlight. A cease-and-desist storm cloud parks over it a week later (May 20). The actress is never drawn or voiced.

### B-plot: WHERE'S ALYI?
- Alyi is seen only in doorways and reflections. He leaves on May 14. The `IOU: 20% COMPUTE` taped to his door flutters off, and EKIEL's resignation dominoes fall onto Mas's desk.
- The exit NDA unrolls across the Bay Bridge: "i did not know this was happening."
- THE FORECASTER walks away from his equity rather than sign.
- NELEH's podcast reveals the board learned about CHATGTP from RETTIWT.
- Jun 19: Alyi steps into ISS's white room and its one door shuts.

### C-plot: FEAR (the faceless candidate) and the rule-writers
- **DLANOD J. RUMPT: voice and hands only, face hidden.** "most dangerous thing… so scary" (Feb 2). A "superpower" that's "alarming" on a podcast (Jun 13). The platform promises to repeal NEDIB's order (Jul 8). He falsely says SIRRAH "A.I.'d" her crowd, and the Orb fact-checks it wordlessly (Aug 11). "I accept!" over AI fan images (Aug 18). A few days later he's afraid of AI again (Aug 22).
- **Balance: the Democrats and the regulators.**
  - REMUHCS's bipartisan roadmap: `9 FORUMS · $32B · 0 BILLS`.
  - ISOLEP calls RENEIW's bill "ill-informed", and RENEIW's hydra grows its first head.
  - NEYEL's RULEBOOK passes 523–46.
  - TRAWETS roasts "Mas, Tasya and Radnus" (rendered; the original wording is in [facts](facts.md)).
- **The landlord and the walled garden.** THE HUMANIST moves into MACROSOFT's basement. ELPPA marries CHATGTP at WWDC, and Mas watches on his phone. Nole builds a Faraday cage for iPhones.

## Themes
- **Whose voice is it?** The episode is about voices borrowed, cloned, silenced and summoned: a voice that sounds like someone who said no, NDAs that buy silence, dead emails speaking at a séance, and a candidate who is all voice and no face.
- **Consent after the fact.** "i did not know this was happening" is the house style for the whole industry.
- **Safety leaves by the side door.** The safety leads resign while "shiny products" take the stage.
- **Fear as a campaign prop.** RUMPT is afraid of AI and posts AI fakes in the same week.

## THE PLAN (45s blueprint)
**`OMNI: ONE MODEL THAT HEARS, SEES AND TALKS.`**
- The blueprint shows an ear, an eye and a mouth feeding **one** box. The old version was a three-box relay (speech-to-text → model → text-to-speech).
- The launch plan:
  1. Answer as fast as a person does.
  2. Launch the day before ELGOOG's big show.
  3. Post one word.
  4. `PICK A VOICE.` It never goes to plan: the storm cloud.

## Tech-as-spectacle set-pieces
1. **The Email Séance.** The emails rise as candlelit ghosts made of quoted-reply chevrons (`>>>`). Each argues with present-day Nole, and the candle smoke carries the 2018 flashback.
2. **"her" eclipses the demo.** CHATGTP grows eyes, ears and a mouth live. The lowercase blimp blocks the stage lights, and Rima's spotlight swings onto it. A cease-and-desist storm cloud parks overhead.
3. **The AROS mammoth.** A photoreal woolly mammoth crosses the boardroom. An extra grows a sixth finger and a chair melts. YRREP's soundstage cranes freeze mid-lift.
4. **The NDA across the Bay Bridge.** It unrolls like a pharmacy receipt, lane by lane, and traffic reads it.
5. **The white room.** ISS is a plain-text sign ("one goal and one product") and one door. It shuts.
6. **The Orb's wordless fact-check.** It scans SIRRAH's crowd and returns `verified: human (all of them)`.
