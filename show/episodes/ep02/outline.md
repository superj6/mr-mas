## Through-line
- Back in charge after the firing, Mas wants to look untouchable. He answers Nole's lawsuit by summoning Nole's own emails, and he steals his own launch with one word, "her."
- The people who worried about safety leave one by one. Alyi, the one person Mas could never read, steps through a single white door and doesn't come back. Mas keeps the unpaid IOU.

## Logline
Nole, who funded the company and left it, sues, so NopeAI holds a candlelit séance for his old emails. Mas types one word, "her," and a blimp steals the spotlight from his own demo. Then the safety team walks out one resignation at a time, the exit papers turn out to be longer than the Bay Bridge, and his chief scientist disappears into a white room with one door. Across the country, a faceless candidate inflates a balloon that says SO SCARY.

## The engine: who leaves, and whose voice (split from Ep3)
Per [SEASON-NOTES note 10](../../reel/SEASON-NOTES.md#10-eps-2-and-3-run-on-the-same-engine), **Ep2 owns the departures, and Alyi is the loss.** Every thread here is someone leaving or someone's voice being borrowed, summoned or silenced:
- Nole left in 2018 and sues from outside (the séance, F2.3).
- Alyi leaves: the empty seat, the search, the one door.
- EKIEL leaves (the safety team's plate comes off the door) and turns up at the rival's lighthouse.
- THE FORECASTER refuses to sign the silence and walks off the bridge.
- The voice that sounded like someone who objected is paused.
- Mas himself is shut outside a garden his own product was walked into.

**Ep3 doesn't repeat it.** Ep3's A-plot is the price and the hidden thinking (the strawberry, the shutter, `$157B`), and its departures stay light. Ep2 carries no price beat: the valuation ticker stays dormant in the intro, and no collar pops.

## Plots
