**How to read this draft**
- **Quotation marks mean real.** Every quoted line carries its tag at the end ([P] [V] [H], or [K]† = re-verify before lock, shown on screen only with `(REPORTED)` or `RECONSTRUCTED` in the rail). **Unquoted dialogue, signs and props are invented** ([INVENTED]); the tag is written out only where an invented line could be mistaken for the record.
- **Mode letters** open each scene and mark switches inside it: **I** in the moment · **S** spectacle · **M** macro · **F** flashback · **P** THE PLAN. A DELIVERY (a real event landing in a room as a prop) is I and M at once and is split 50/50 in the notes. Timed non-dialogue elements carry bars and beats (96 BPM: 1 beat = 0.625 s, 1 bar = 2.5 s).
- **Style tags:** [BASE] unless marked. [2-TONE FREEZE] for cards and frozen memories · [EARLY-WEB16] for 2006–08 · [LEDGER] ≤ 6 frames for money · [TERMINAL] only for THE ORB's own POV · [GLYPH-MASKED] for dread · [BLUEPRINT] for THE PLAN.

PORTRAIT windows: Mas left, the other right, always (a split screen puts Mas's pane on the left too). `CARD:` is a 1-bar 2-TONE freeze; `PLATE:` is a no-freeze name strip read during the line.
- **Scene numbers are locked from draft 1**, so the table-read notes still point at the right scene. A cut scene stays as OMITTED.
- **⏱ stamps are the page timer** (pacing-model rates, calibrated on draft 1), not a separate design clock. Draft 1's design clock ran about 1:20 ahead of its pages; this draft has one clock.
