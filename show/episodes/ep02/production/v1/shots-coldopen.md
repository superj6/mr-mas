The lock, its sounds and the score are untouched; the subtitles and the mix don't read shot boundaries. A re-lock that moves the cup's mark relative to 1.07's end needs `J08` in `sc-1.ts` updated (it is 4: 1.07 is 131 f with the cup at its k127). [GUIDE: the lock's cut; reason the review's #7, P3]
- **The step-out has three in-between drawings** (k8-28) before the walk; the plan's "It steps onto the carpet" now lands on the lock's second step sound (k29), and the fifth leg is on the last in-between's two frames, as the review asked.
- **1.09 and 1.11 use a new set:** the lobby straight down its length (the master is side-on and can't look down the axis), painted in the master's own lit materials.
- **The hand truck has a cause:** the courier's kick (1.09) and the tip-back under its own load (1.11). The plan says "It rolls down the axis to his feet" and "The complaint tips off the hand truck and hits the floor"; the courier is a leg and a boot only, pixel, no face (P8 forbids people from the video model, not ours).
- **The flyer's photo has nobody in it** (1.11, 1.13). The art pass's flyer ECU draws a figure in the doorway; the plan says "its photo a doorway", and the Act Three rule is no image of Alyi in any surface. The empty doorway is the question.
- **The flyer covers the complaint's caption.** The review suggested landing it "across the caption, not YOU PROMISED"; at room scale the flyer must be about as wide as the cover for its headline to read, so any placement leaves some caption beside it or a fragment of it. [GR, firm]

- **The take holds** from the foot's step to the cut (1.02 k148-174): the hold makes the preview stop as its subject leaves it [J].
