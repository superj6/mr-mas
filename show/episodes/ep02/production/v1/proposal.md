## The story in one paragraph

Back in charge after the firing, **Mas turns the comeback into reach.** Nole, who funded the company and left it, sues; Mas answers with Nole's own emails, published by his own click, and the séance that follows shows what Nole wanted (the credit for founding it, and control of it) and what scared him: in 2016 a rival's machine played a Go move nobody wrote. Mas takes his seat back, tells the firing story once on the friendliest podcast there is, puts his launch on the Monday before Elgoog's Tuesday, and closes a deal that puts his chatbot in ELPPA's new phones, for no money either way. Then, after the stream, he steals his own demo's coverage with one word, "her".

**What he can't arrange is the people.** The next afternoon his chief scientist posts that he's leaving, warmly, and Mas answers warmly in public. The safety team's co-lead walks out in his own words, the team's plate comes off the team's own door, an old IOU falls off Alyi's door into Mas's pocket, and the exit papers turn out to be longer than the Bay Bridge. He answers each blow with a move of his own: legal told to find everyone who signed, an apology, voice 5 paused by his own thumb, a seat on the committee that checks his own company, and his own post from the crowd on the day ELPPA announces his chatbot from its stage. Through it all he tries to reach Alyi, the one person he wants back, and in a flashback we finally see what Alyi wanted: to feel it, and to steer it, with his own finger on Publish and his own hand on the flame. On Jun 19 Alyi posts that he's starting a company of his own. Mas goes to its one door in person, posts the old `WHERE IS ALYI?` flyer through the slot, sees him at work for one beat, and doesn't knock.

**The episode's engine** (kept from 5.4): *who leaves, and whose voice is it?* The suit, the dead emails speaking, "her", the paused voice, the NDAs that buy silence, a candidate who is all words and no face. Ep3 owns the price; Ep2 owns the departures.
