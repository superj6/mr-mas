## The story in one paragraph

Back in charge after the firing, **Mas turns the comeback into reach.** Nole, who funded the company and left it, sues; Mas answers with Nole's own emails, published by his own click, and the séance that follows shows what Nole wanted (the credit for founding it, and control of it) and what scared him: in 2016 a rival's machine played a Go move nobody wrote. Mas takes his seat back, tells the firing story once on the friendliest podcast there is, and puts his launch on the Monday before Elgoog's Tuesday. Then he steals his own demo with one word, "her".

**What he can't arrange is the people.** The morning after the demo, his chief scientist is gone. The safety team leaves one post at a time, an old IOU falls off a door into his hands, and the exit papers turn out to be longer than the Bay Bridge. He answers each blow with a public move: an apology in lowercase, a seat on the committee that checks his own company, and ChatGTP in every ELPPA phone, while he watches from the crowd at the edge of a garden he built the road into. Through it all he looks for Alyi, the one person he wants back, and in a flashback we finally see what Alyi wanted: to feel it, to fix it, and to hold the company to a promise. On Jun 19 Mas carries that promise to a white room with one door. It comes back under the door, unchanged.

**The episode's engine** (kept from 5.4): *who leaves, and whose voice is it?* The suit, the dead emails speaking, "her", the paused voice, the NDAs that buy silence, a candidate who is all voice and no face. Ep3 owns the price; Ep2 owns the departures.

## The spine: the chain test per act

Each act retold as cause and effect (LEARNINGS W1; calibration §9). Real events arrive as results of his moves or as obstacles to them.

**Cold open.** A rival lab's product walks out of a screen; then Nole's lawsuit comes through the door. *(The obstacle; no move needed.)*

**Act One.**
1. So Mas answers the suit in public: he clicks Publish on Nole's own emails (the court will read them eventually; everyone reads them tonight).
2. Because of that, Nole crashes the "séance" to make his case: he paid for it, and it was supposed to be open.
3. So Mas runs the room with questions, and the emails answer Nole: "less open", Yup; fold it into his car company; 0% against MINDDEEP without billions.
4. Because Mas asks "why zero?", Nole and Gerg show what scared him: a 2016 machine that learned a move nobody wrote. Mas privately concedes the bill was real.
5. Because the emails are out, Nole is left with "You kept them." and a promise to see him in court.
6. Three days later the review is back. Mas stands until he's told to sit, and takes his board seat again.
7. So he accepts one long interview, to tell the firing story once.
8. Because he tells it on XEL's show, the mic and the questions grow, and "i love alyi" goes on the record.
9. Meanwhile his landlord, Tasya, hires its own AI chief downstairs: a spare, and Mas is "A tenant."

**Act Two.**
1. So, at home, Mas watches a candidate put a price on the fear, sees himself in a TV lineup beside his landlord (one of them pays rent), and swipes Europe's new rules away.
2. He sets his launch on the Monday, because Elgoog has Tuesday.
3. Because it's his Monday, he asks backstage "is it ready?", and grants Rima a stage that's hers.
4. THE PLAN shows what the launch is: one model that hears, sees and talks, out today, free.
5. Because the product flirts, the light slides off Rima, and Mas types one word, "her".
6. Because of the post, the blimp takes the rest of her light, and the stream asks `is it safe?`.
7. Because the demo is over, Rima hands him the clicker; and the seat reserved for his chief scientist is empty. May 14: Alyi leaves.

**Act Three.**
1. So the staff tape flyers, and Mas tells them to leave them up.
2. Because Alyi's gone, Mas searches the floor (reflections only), gives the chair to its new owner, and watches the safety lead walk out, saying compute was short.
3. Because the safety team's IOU falls off Alyi's door, Mas picks it up.
4. So he goes to Alyi's office and uses the dead app from his first company to ask "where u at?"
5. Because the pin lands on a 2023 bonfire, we see what Alyi wanted: the chant, the post, the pledge on his door, the fire.
6. So Mas decides on his offer: he'll bring Alyi his own promise.
7. Because the exit papers arrive from under Alyi's door, the press calls, and a researcher refuses to sign them, Mas posts the apology.
8. Because "her" went out, a famous voice objects, and voice 5 goes grey. Alyi's pin updates.

**Act Four.**
1. So Mas takes a seat on the committee that checks his own company's safety; across the bay, the safety lead takes a lanyard at Misanthropic.
2. Because it's the same day, a former board member's podcast plays in his own boardroom, and the chair turns it off.
3. So Mas goes to ELPPA's keynote and watches his chatbot announced for every phone, from the crowd.
4. Because Gerg calls, we see where he stood in 2008.
5. Because the phone deal is real, the chatbot walks into the garden as a guest, and the gate shuts with Mas outside.
6. Because of the deal, Nole cages his staff's phones, and Mas calls him. Nobody answers. Next day Nole drops the suit, for now.
7. So Mas takes the IOU to the white room Alyi just founded. It comes back. He keeps it.

**Tag.** The suit comes back in federal court; his other company's Orb proves a crowd was real; the candidate who was scared of AI ties the balloon back to the podium.
