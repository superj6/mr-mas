## The story in one paragraph

Back in charge after the firing, **Mas turns the comeback into reach.** Nole, who funded the company and left it, sues; Mas answers with Nole's own emails, published by his own click, and the séance that follows shows what Nole wanted (the credit for founding it, and control of it) and what scared him: in 2016 a rival's machine played a Go move nobody wrote. Mas takes his seat back, tells the firing story once on the friendliest podcast there is, puts his launch on the Monday before Elgoog's Tuesday, and closes a deal that puts his chatbot in every ELPPA phone, for no money either way. Then, after the stream, he steals his own demo's coverage with one word, "her".

**What he can't arrange is the people.** The morning after the demo, his chief scientist posts that he's leaving, warmly, and Mas answers warmly in public. The safety team leaves one post at a time, an old IOU falls off a door into his hands, and the exit papers turn out to be longer than the Bay Bridge. He answers each blow with a public move: an apology in lowercase, voice 5 paused by his own thumb, a seat on the committee that checks his own company, and ChatGTP in every ELPPA phone, while he watches from the crowd at the edge of a garden he built the road into. Through it all he looks for Alyi, the one person he wants back, and in a flashback we finally see what Alyi wanted: to feel it, to steer it, and the promise his team was made, on his door. On Jun 19 Mas carries that promise to a white room with one door. It comes back through the slot, unchanged.

**The episode's engine** (kept from 5.4): *who leaves, and whose voice is it?* The suit, the dead emails speaking, "her", the paused voice, the NDAs that buy silence, a candidate who is all words and no face. Ep3 owns the price; Ep2 owns the departures.

## The spine: the chain test per act

Each act retold as cause and effect (LEARNINGS W1; calibration §9). Real events arrive as results of his moves or as obstacles to them.

**Cold open.** NopeAI's own new product walks out of a screen; then Nole's lawsuit thuds up the front steps and through the door. *(The obstacle; no move needed.)*

**Act One.**
1. So Mas answers the suit in public: he clicks Publish on Nole's own emails (the court will read them eventually; everyone reads them tonight).
2. Because of that, Nole crashes the "séance" to make his case: he paid for it, and it was supposed to be open.
3. So Mas runs the room with questions, and the emails answer Nole: "less open", Yup; fold it into his car company; 0% against MINDDEEP without billions a year.
4. Because Mas asks "why zero?", Nole and Gerg show what scared him: a 2016 machine that learned a move nobody wrote. Mas privately concedes the bill.
5. Because the emails are out, Nole is left with "You kept them." and a promise to see him in court.
6. Three days later the review is back. Mas stands until he's told to sit, and takes his board seat again.
7. So he accepts one long interview, to tell the firing story once.
8. Because he tells it on XEL's show, "chaotic and shameful" and "i love alyi" go on the record, and the mic grows with every question.
9. Because the landlord plays that interview the next morning, Tasya hires a spare AI chief downstairs, and Mas is "A tenant."

**Act Two.**
1. So, in his dark room, Mas lets a candidate's fear balloon stay on his ceiling when he could swipe it away, swipes Europe's new rules away instead, and sees himself in a lineup beside his landlord: the next model runs on rented racks.
2. He sets his launch on the Monday, because Elgoog has Tuesday.
3. The same day he closes the phone deal on a call, no money either way, and ELPPA's June keynote goes into his calendar.
4. Because it's his Monday, he asks backstage "is it ready?", and grants Rima a stage that's hers.
5. THE PLAN shows what the launch is: one model that hears, sees and talks, out today, free.
6. Because the product flirts, the light slides off Rima, and she closes the stream in the half-dark.
7. Because the stream is over, Mas types one word, "her", and the blimp takes the coverage.
8. Because the house is emptying, Rima leaves the clicker on a road case and he picks it up; the seat reserved for his chief scientist is empty. Next morning Alyi's own post says he's leaving, and Mas answers it in public, warmly.

**Act Three.**
1. So the staff tape flyers; Mas tells them to leave them up, and tapes one himself.
2. Because Alyi's gone, Mas searches the floor (reflections only), and at Alyi's locked door an old IOU flutters into his hands.
3. So he gives the chair to its new owner, and watches the safety co-lead walk out on his own posts.
4. So he goes to Alyi's office and uses the dead app from his first company to ask "where u at?"
5. Because the pin lands on a 2022 party, we see what Alyi wanted: the chant, the post, the team's promise on his door, the fire.
6. So Mas decides what to bring him: the promise, by hand.
7. Because the exit papers run out of the building and across the bridge, and the press calls, Mas scrambles and posts the apology at dawn.
8. Because "her" went out, letters arrive; Mas pauses voice 5 with his own thumb.

**Act Four.**
1. So Mas takes a seat on the committee that checks his own company's safety, the same morning a former board member's account plays on his boardroom TV; across the bay, the safety lead takes a lanyard at Misanthropic.
2. Because of the deal he closed in May, Mas goes to ELPPA's keynote and watches his chatbot announced for every phone, from the crowd.
3. Because the stream catches him in that crowd, Gerg calls, and we see where he stood in 2008.
4. Because the phone deal is real, the chatbot walks into the garden as a guest, and the gate shuts with Mas outside.
5. Because of the deal, Nole cages visitors' phones; a pocket dial from Mas rings in the cage. Next day, with the dismissal hearing a day away, Nole drops the suit, for now.
6. A week later the old app updates: Alyi's pin has moved.
7. So Mas takes the IOU to the white room Alyi just founded. It comes back. He keeps it.

**Tag.** The suit comes back in federal court; his other company's Orb proves a rival's crowd was real; the candidate who was scared of AI ties the balloon back to the podium, and Mas looks up at the empty spot on his ceiling.
