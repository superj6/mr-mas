## F6.1 · THE WOODROSE, part 3 of 5: "who paid, and who named it" (`NOLE'S VERSION`)
| Field | |
|---|---|
| **When** | Jul 2015 (Napa, then the Woodrose) → Nov 22, 2015 (email) → Dec 11, 2015 (founding) |
| **POV** | NOLE (rim #E0301E). **Unreliable** |
| **Era tier** | **T3 cut-paper**, but rendered in Nole's KORG metal-album-cover style (lens flares, chrome, Nole 40% taller). This is the unreliable-version render rule |
| **Runtime** | ~45 s |
| **Truth label** | Chyron `NOLE'S VERSION` throughout. The pentagram riff is not used here (it moved to EP08) |

**What we see**
1. **Napa firepit (Jul 2015).** EGAP calls Nole "SPECIESIST!" [V], and a record scratch cuts the soundtrack. A MINDDEEP stock certificate (Nole was an early investor [V]) blows off his lap into the fire.
2. **Nole speeds to THE WOODROSE.** In *his* version he sits at the head of the table and hangs the `OPEN` neon himself. That sign pays off in EP08: "I came up with the name."
3. **Mas in `NOLE'S VERSION`** is drawn 40% smaller, and he still never freezes (rule 6). He pockets the CTRL key, exactly as in the intro.
4. **Nov 22, 2015.** An email pushes for a `$1 BILLION` pledge [V]. The on-screen check reads `$1,000,000,000`; the receipt that prints under it reads `RECEIVED: $133M (BY ~2021)` [V]. His own line item reads `$44M` (court records), with a sticky note in his handwriting: `"$38 million in strict monetary terms"`. [V; that phrasing is his 2026 testimony, used here as a margin note].
5. **Reveal added:** the money, and the fear of ELGOOG.

**Why here.**
- The Money-Go-Round's check grows every lap, while his 2015 check shrinks every time it's retold.
- His Nov 1, 2025 post, "stole a non-profit" [K], is the trigger.
- On Nov 18, INIMEG 3 ships and the code-red siren flies to NopeAI (Dec 1). His 2015 fear of ELGOOG comes true, just not the way he meant.

**Transition**
- **In:** his Nov 1 post falls as a hailstone (rail `NOV 1`, Act Two's first). The record scratch (in his red-and-chrome style) rewinds the frame.
- **Out:** the receipt curls, and in the present it becomes the carousel's check. Exit object matches entry: receipt → check.

**Guardrails.** No family. The Napa party is shown only as a firepit, no birthday framing, no guests beyond EGAP. `NOLE'S VERSION` stays on screen the whole time.

## F6.2 · "THE FIRST BOX"
| Field | |
|---|---|
| **When** | Aug 15, 2016 |
| **POV** | NESNEJ (rim #7CFF4F) |
| **Era tier** | **T3 cut-paper** |
| **Runtime** | ~25 s |
| **Truth label** | The box inscription is **illegible scrawl** (its wording is [UNVERIFIED]). The delivery date is from [H] headlines |

**What we see.** Nesnej hand-carries a 1-XGD (the first DGX-1) into a tiny office. Nole is there, beside a whiteboard. The inscription on the lid is a squiggle. The office is so small the box fills a third of it.

**Why here.** On Oct 14, 2025, as the carousel passes SPACEZ, Nesnej hand-delivers a new box to Nole at SPACEZ "after nine-year hiatus" [H]. It's the same walk and the same box, ten times smaller and a hundred times the price. It explains the carousel: Nesnej has been walking boxes to both sides of this fight since before there was a fight.

**Transition**
- **In:** the Oct 2025 shipping label's date stamp flips `2025` → `2016`.
- **Out:** box tape rips across the frame back to 2025, and the new, smaller box sits in Nole's hand. Box → box. Its shipping label peels off and unrolls into THE PLAN's blueprint.

**THE PLAN's trigger (season revision).** F6.2 used to land 10 s after THE PLAN, a double stop (SEASON-NOTES, smaller notes; L6). Now it *is* THE PLAN's door: IELIM's ring (Oct 10) passes on the lap before the Oct 14 delivery so the dates run in order, and the flashback's exit flows straight into the blueprint. A trigger that flows into THE PLAN plays as one stop, as Ep11's Charter does. In the second pass the delivery is itself a lap of the ride, so the flashback starts from Mas's seat.

## The Orb's iris (3 s): Act Three's opening (second pass)
| Field | |
|---|---|
| **When** | Nov 25 → Sep 30, 2025, on the Orb's own replay counter |
| **POV** | THE ORB |
| **Era tier** | **T4 glossy HDR** |
| **Device** | **First use of the iris replay** (it can only show events after 2019, per flashback rule 5) |

**What we see.** Act Three opens close on the Orb. Inside its iris the replay counter rewinds `NOV 25 → SEP 30` through a blur of the faces it has scanned, every one of them Mas; the iris opens onto CAMEO CITY on Sep 30. Later in the scene it stamps the real Mas `human (probably)`.

**Why here.** Two jobs in 3 s. It is the device the drama critic asked for: Act Two ends in late November and Act Three starts on Sep 30, and the rewind happens inside the Orb, so the show's rail never runs backward. And it motivates the first `(probably)`: the Orb has now seen more Mases than it can vouch for. (The first pass's version replayed the Orb's own 2019 → 2021 first scan mid-CAMEO CITY; that 3 s is spent here instead.)
