# EP06 · `ep1.5_backstop.xlsx` · Beat sheet

**Tags:** **REAL** (date) means the event happened; see [facts.md](facts.md). The staging is a cartoon unless noted. **INVENTED** means filler, kept obviously comedic. Mas's subtitles are lowercase. † marks a `[K]` line to re-verify; on screen it carries `(REPORTED)` until it's upgraded.

**Quote rendering on screen:** real lines keep their wording, but real names inside them are swapped for registry names (precedent: "Dario is right" → "Mario is right", FINAL §7): Nvidia → INVIDIA, Anthropic → MISANTHROPIC, OpenAI → NOPEAI, Claude → CLOD, Sora → AROS, Jensen → NESNEJ, and so on. Mas's subtitles are always lowercased. [facts.md](facts.md) keeps the original wording.

**Shape (second pass).** Each act is a chapter with its own clock: Act One is the money (Sep 4 → Oct 30), Act Two the structure and the fear (Oct → Nov 25), Act Three the fakes and the race (Sep 30 → Dec). **The show's rail never runs backward.** Act Two carries no rail until `NOV 1` (FEAR-MONGERING's feed and THE TRANSFORMER's `LIVE` tag need none), and Act Three opens inside the Orb's iris, which rewinds on its own counter to `SEP 30`. The ride starts right after the round table, so Act One's news passes as scenery on its laps, seen from Mas's seat.
