## ACT 2: "THE TRANSFORMER"
11. **REAL (Oct 28), set-piece: THE TRANSFORMER.**

    - Nole, from a rooftop megaphone: *IT'S STILL A CAR. [INVENTED]*
    - On the same livestream: $1.4T and 30GW of commitments, and an "automated AI research intern by Sept 2026" [V]. BUKAJ's countdown clock appears. In a window, a desk gets a plate: `RESERVED: SEP 2026`. "We may totally fail at this goal." [V]
12. **FLASHBACK F6.3 lands (≤10 s).** The robot's head pops off, and a `100x CAP` hat from Mar 11, 2019 flutters out, then a bigger hat drops on.
    - **Motivates:** every restructuring is a hat swap.

16. **REAL (Nov 6).** Nole's $1T pay package [V]. His check laps the carousel in the opposite direction, and nobody notices.
17. **REAL (Oct 14–22), "FEAR-MONGERING."**
