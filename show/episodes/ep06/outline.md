## Logline
One check goes around and around, from Nesnej to Mas to Yrral, growing every lap and lighting a new data-center cathedral each time. President RUMPT hosts a "HOW MUCH WILL YOU INVEST?" game show, NopeAI transforms into a robot (with a smiley decal for "public benefit"), and the CFO says the one word that makes the whole carousel wobble: **backstop**.

## Plots

### A-plot: THE MONEY-GO-ROUND (Sep 10 → Nov 24)
- ELCARO's $300B, INVIDIA's $100B letter (10GW), ASIL's 6GW, CRAB DOOM's 10GW, GNUSMAS/KS wafers, NOZAMA's $38B. SAMA NOS sells his whole INVIDIA stake to throw it in. NopeAI's secondary sale reaches $500B, and INVIDIA reaches $5T.
- A second, smaller check circles TASYA → MARIO → NESNEJ ($15B, Nov 24). Misanthropic's circular money is in frame too.
- **THE TRANSFORMER (Oct 28):** NopeAI HQ unfolds into a robot. The Foundation (26%) is one arm and MACROSOFT (~27%) the other; the chest has a smiley decal for "public benefit." The same livestream promises $1.4T of commitments and an **automated research intern by Sep 2026**, so a desk labeled `RESERVED: SEP 2026` appears.
- "i'll find you a buyer. enough." [K†]
- **BACKSTOP (Nov 5–6):** HARAS says the word, and a baseball backstop rises behind HQ with taxpayers in the bleachers. SKCAS says "no federal bailout." NERRAW holds the bleacher tickets. THE SHORT bets against the shovels.

### B-plot: "HIGH-IQ GROUP" (RUMPT at peak love)
- **THE ROUND TABLE game show (Sep 4):** CEOs buzz in with pledge numbers. Kram's buzzer catches the hot mic. Nole's chair is empty.
- **DEPARTMENT OF RAW (Sep 5):** the label gun turns DEFENSE into WAR. ETEP HTESGEH debuts with his door-sized stamp (paperwork only).
- **Windsor (Sep 17):** KING LARCHES hands everyone a printed copy of his speech. **Same day:** Beijing tells firms to stop buying Invidia's China chip, so the crate bounces between two tollbooths: `15% OF ZERO`.
- **KORG sold to agencies for 42¢ (Sep 25).** **Busan (Oct 30).** **"One Federal Standard" (Nov 18).** Nole returns at THE CROWN PRINCE's dinner (Nov 18) carrying the re-keyed golden key.
- **CUT DIAL to 25% (Dec 8).** **The "SI" seed (Dec 11):** the label gun hovers over `ARTIFICIAL`, then holsters: "that's okay with me." The giant marker goes to THE SENIOR ADVISOR.
- **Balance:** NIVAG MOSWEN signs SB 53 (Sep 29) wearing a `2028` button. LUHCOH signs RAISE (Dec 19). Both sign anyway, a week after the preemption EO.

### C-plot: FEAR, FINES AND FAKES (Misanthropic, Alyi, AROS)
- Mario pays a **$1.5B library fine** (Sep 5). Then he discloses that state hackers used CLOD (Nov 13–14): *We warned you it'd be dangerous. It was ours. [INVENTED]*
- **FEAR-MONGERING (Oct):** KCAJ's lantern throws a creature's shadow on the wall, and SKCAS stamps it. DIRE and SKCAS fight over it (the PayPal-mafia feud), and Mario answers with a rebuttal scroll. THE LETTER-WRITER's superintelligence statement gathers signers from both ends, NONNAB included (seeding THE HORSESHOE).
- **Alyi's deposition** (Oct 1; released Nov 4) is answered in one-word koans.
- **CAMEO CITY (Sep 30):** AROS 2 floods SF with deepfake Mases. The Orb: `VERIFIED HUMAN… probably.` YLLIT debuts and ARTFA-GAS pickets.
- **CODE RED (Nov 18 → Dec 1):** Gemini 3 ships, and the siren lifts off ELGOOG's roof and flies across the Bay to NopeAI's. Radnus smiles.

## Themes
1. **Money that goes in a circle looks like growth.** Every lap lights a cathedral, and nobody asks where the check started.
2. **Who holds the backstop?** Taxpayers, the landlord, the chip seller, or the man with the carousel ticket. Everyone says "not us."
3. **Restructuring is a hat swap.** The 100x cap comes off and a bigger hat drops on (F6.3). The transformer is still a car.
4. **Everyone owns a version of the founding.** Nole's HIS VERSION flashback turns the pledge into a receipt.
5. **Identity dilution.** Deepfake Mases flood the city, and the Orb's certainty drops a notch for the first time.
