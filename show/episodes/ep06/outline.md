## Logline
One check goes around and around, from Nesnej to Mas to Yrral, growing every lap and lighting a new data-center cathedral each time, and Mas rides it; the check is stamped `COMMITTED`, and nobody can find the ink to stamp it `PAID`. President RUMPT hosts a "HOW MUCH WILL YOU INVEST?" game show, NopeAI transforms into a robot with a smiley decal for "public benefit" (and for a blink we see what the smile is stuck to), and the CFO says the one word that makes the whole carousel wobble: **backstop**.

## Through-line (what a newcomer must be able to retell)
- **Want:** money on a scale nobody has ever raised.
- **How:** one check goes round and round between NopeAI, its chip supplier and its cloud landlords, lighting a data center every lap.
- **Where it wobbles:**
  - his CFO says the government might "backstop" it
  - a small town's "no" gets sued into a "yes"
  - across the water, each government hangs a weight on the other's toll gate, then they balance it
- **The question it leaves:** who's holding the bag? THE PLAN's answer is a taxpayer holding the ink pad; the landlord's is one clink of keys, muffled in his fist.

**Set up once, inside the action:**
- **A commitment is not a payment:** THE PLAN, kept to that one idea, entered straight out of F6.2's box. One check stamped `COMMITTED` laps INVIDIA → NopeAI → ELCARO → INVIDIA, and each lap lights a cathedral. The picture says "a city's worth of power", because Ep4's PLAN already told it. **The break does story work:** the `PAID` stamp comes down dry, and the ink pad is in a tiny taxpayer's hands in the margin, which BACKSTOP pays off.
- **The toll gate:** Windsor's `15% OF ZERO` tollbooths, seen on a lap of the ride, so Busan's magnet and weight have an arm to hang on.
- **The RESERVED desk and the clock:** the automated research intern "by Sept 2026" [V]. Ep3's split-flap on Mas's wall finally finds a date (`INTERN: SEP 2026 · RESEARCHER: MAR 2028`), and DOT's cuff screws a `RESERVED: SEP 2026` plate onto an empty desk. One clock, one desk, both paid in Ep9 and Ep10.
- **"Backstop" means the taxpayer catches it:** a baseball backstop with the taxpayers in its bleachers, holding tickets. The tickets come back in Ep10's public-comment queue.
- **The creature:** KCAJ's lantern shadow, stamped `FEAR-MONGERING`, thrown across HQ's facade on the pull-back, is the shape the smiley is stuck to at THE TRANSFORMER.
- **The AGI switch:** wired to one key on TASYA's ring, so Ep8's missing key reads without a line.

## Themes
1. **Money that goes in a circle looks like growth.** Every lap lights a cathedral, and nobody asks where the check started. A commitment is not a payment.
2. **Who holds the backstop?** Taxpayers, the landlord, the chip seller, or the man with the carousel ticket. Everyone says "not us," and nobody asked the bleachers.
3. **Restructuring is a hat swap.** The 100x cap comes off and a bigger hat drops on (F6.3). The transformer is still a car.
4. **A smile on something with too many eyes.** The public-benefit decal is stuck to the thing the czar calls fear-mongering.
5. **Everyone owns a version of the founding.** `NOLE'S VERSION` turns the pledge into a receipt.
6. **Identity dilution.** Deepfake Mases flood the city, and the Orb's certainty drops a notch for the first time.
