## Delivery note for the line
"i'll find you a buyer…" is polite and helpful, almost warm. Then comes the pause, with the low piano and the D♭ colour note in it, and "enough." lands as quietly as the first half, with no bite. The joke is that he doesn't raise his voice even when shutting someone down. The `you are here` marker slides on "enough."
