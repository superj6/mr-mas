# EP07 · `ep1.6_supply_chain_risk.pdf` · Beat sheet

**Tags:** **REAL** (date) = the event happened; see [facts.md](facts.md). **INVENTED** = obviously comedic filler. The Pentagon arc is **paperwork only**: no hardware, maps or weapons, and "autonomous weapons" appears only as words painted in a policy line. † marks a `[K]` line to re-verify.

**Quote rendering on screen:** real lines keep their wording, but real names inside them are swapped for registry names (precedent: "Dario is right" → "Mario is right", FINAL §7): Nvidia → INVIDIA, Anthropic → MISANTHROPIC, OpenAI → NOPEAI, Claude → CLOD, Jensen → NESNEJ, and so on. Mas's subtitles are always lowercased, except the one capital "I" in #7. [facts.md](facts.md) keeps the original wording.

**How it plays.** The quarter reaches Mas in three kinds of room.
- **Two news runs on his dark-room monitor**, glass in frame, each under one continuous cue that thins under the real lines: Act One's (#1–#7), which ends on the post he types, and the Pentagon week (#13–#16), in whose second half a pen starts scratching just off his shoulder.
- **His office for the ad week** (#8–#10): he ships the ads himself, retires 4o himself, and Misanthropic's `NO ADS` neon glows across the water.
- **In person for everything else:** Valentine's Day (#11), Delhi (#12), THE HUG (#17), his office window (#18), the funeral (#21), the COUNCIL's window (#22), his phone in the car (#23) and the call (#24).

The rail rolls like the odometer. The only exit from his view is F7.1, Mario's 2019.
