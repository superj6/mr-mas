## Logline
Mario paints two red lines on the Pentagon's floor and refuses to erase them. President RUMPT answers with an ALL-CAPS meteor through CLOD's roof. Mas, who has watched the whole standoff from his dark room with a pen scratching just off his shoulder, walks into the story and hugs his rival in bullet time while his third arm signs the same kind of contract. Then the public does what his board did in 2023: it clicks Cancel, and his users counter ticks backward for the first time.

## The through-line (what a newcomer should be able to retell)
- MARIO, the rival who left Mas's company to found Misanthropic, refuses two uses of his model by the Pentagon, and he's banned and stamped a "supply chain risk."
- Mas backs him in public and hugs him, and his own company signs a Pentagon deal hours later.
- The public answers with the season's button: it clicks Cancel on him, and it works.
- The cost shows in one letter first: the first time all season that Mas types a capital "I."

## Plots

### A-plot: RED LINES (Feb 23 → Mar 26)
- **Feb 24:** the ultimatum arrives at the lighthouse on a hand truck, and THE PLAN unfolds out of its top sheet: the two red lines, and the "supply chain risk" label the Pentagon is threatening.
- **Feb 26:** Mario paints the two lines on the Pentagon's floor and refuses. LIME LEAHCIM arrives by lime scooter. On Mas's side of the monitor, from here on, a pen scratches just out of frame.
- **Feb 27:** THE HTURT METEOR, the post that orders every agency to "IMMEDIATELY CEASE." A six-month hourglass. A senator's condemnation prints as a till receipt that won't stop. HTESGEH: "This decision is final."
- **Feb 27 → 28: THE HUG.** Mas walks into the feed he's been watching and embraces Mario in bullet time. His third arm, the pen we've been hearing, signs NopeAI's own classified-network deal "with safeguards," hours later. Mario's third arm deposits his own 2025 Pentagon check.
- **Feb 28 → Mar 2: CANCEL.** Hundreds of unlit arrows click Cancel on CHATGTP, in Ep1's dialog, and the users odometer's last digit clicks back one.
- **Mar 5:** the stamp shows its face on the lighthouse. **Mar 26:** the injunction, the hoodies, and a video call where nobody mentions the contract.

### B-plot: THE FIRST CAPITAL "I" (Feb 4 → Feb 13)
- **Feb 4:** Misanthropic's campaign mocks ads in AI. Mas posts "they are funny, and I laughed.": his **first capital "I"** of the season, with a 1993 CAPS LOCK flash. Then, back in lowercase: "it is a dark path."
- **Feb 8, the Big Game:** the ad plays to the stadium, and his glass sweats one bead. The next spot lights a suburb's doorbells into a camera grid.
- **Feb 9:** Mas ships ads into CHATGTP himself, and a lasagna recipe grows into Times Square. Across the water, Misanthropic's NO ADS neon is itself an ad.
- **Feb 12–13:** PRIVATE LAST's $20M squares off with FOLLOWING THE PAST, each checkbook's memo line saying what it wants, and Mas retires ZOMBIE 4o again with his own switch.

### C-plot: THE LOBSTER (Jan 27 → Feb 14)
- Misanthropic's trademark lawyers chase a crustacean from CLODBOT to MOLTBOT (Jan 27). Three days later RETEP renames it CLAWOPEN anyway (Jan 30).
- RETEP is hired on Valentine's Day (Feb 14), which triggers the Valentine's-box flashback, and the lobster gets a nonprofit shell. Mas takes what the rival's lawyers chased off. (BOOKMOLT, the unionizing agents and Kram's aquarium go to the ticker.)

### Runners
- **THE GAP (Feb 19, Delhi):** Mas and Mario raise fists instead of holding hands, leaving a fist-shaped hole in THE HOST's chain. They stand close and never touch, which the hug pays off a week later.
- **Distillation (Feb 23):** Mario accuses THE WHALE, THE MOON and a third lab.

## Themes
1. **Lines on floors.** Red lines, cut lines, the lines under stamps. Every principle is a stripe of paint, and someone always brings a roller.
2. **Hugging your rival while signing his deal.** Solidarity and opportunism happen in the same frame.
3. **Principles as branding.** An ad about not having ads; hoodies about being blacklisted; a vault labelled TOO DANGEROUS that always opens.
4. **Everyone labels everyone.** Supply chain risk, distiller, woke, "leftwing nut jobs," authoritarian.
5. **The button changes hands.** In 2023 a board clicked Cancel on him. In 2026 the public does, and it works.
6. **First crack in the mask.** One capital "I."

## How it's told
- **POV.** Limited third person through Mas. The quarter reaches him three ways:
  - **Two news runs on his dark-room monitor,** glass in frame: Act One's (Jan 12 → Feb 4), which ends on the post he types, and the Pentagon week (Feb 23 → 27). The standoff is a feed he watches, so the hug is him walking into a story he's been watching (SEASON-NOTES note 1). From Feb 26 a pen scratches just off his shoulder: he's never only a spectator.
  - **His office for the ad week** (Feb 9 → 13): he ships the ads and retires 4o himself, and the NO ADS neon glows across the water.
  - **In person for the rest:** Valentine's Day, Delhi, the hug, his office window for the stamp, the funeral, the COUNCIL's window, his phone in the car, and the call.
- **Mas share (estimate):** about 75–80%. The only exit is F7.1 (Mario's 2019, about 55 s, 5%).
- **V.O. (for the reel editor):** five lines. Keep 7.04 ("i read the title."), 7.17 ("so we resorted."), 7.20 ("i'll miss the honesty.", which now finishes ZOMBIE 4o's cut-off compliment), 7.26 ("we just don't touch.", which plants the hug) and 7.41, trimmed to *i stood outside by choice. better light.* Cut 7.12, 7.15, 7.25 (the shell rhyme is left to the picture), 7.28 ("mine had fine print." would tell the third arm before bullet time shows it), 7.33, 7.34 and 7.40 (the funeral's laugh is SELBEEP's line).
- **Dialogue.** THE HUG has no lines at all; the third arm says it. ADELINA's "In plain English" rests (SEASON-NOTES note 9). Mario spends `Addendum:` once, on the second red line. The funeral gets one invented line, and it lands on Mas.
- **Music.** Each monitor run is one continuous cue that thins under the real posts and cards. The pen's scratch sits in the Pentagon cue's rhythm and stops a bar before the hug. The Big Game box plays under the stadium's bed. THE HUG is scored as drama, never as the joke, and the Cancel clicks land under room tone, with no stinger.
