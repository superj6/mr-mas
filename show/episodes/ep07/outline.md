## Logline
Mario paints two red lines on the Pentagon's floor and refuses to erase them. President RUMPT answers with an ALL-CAPS meteor through CLOD's roof, and the SECRETARY OF RAW stamps Misanthropic a SUPPLY CHAIN RISK. Mas hugs Mario in bullet time while his third arm signs the same contract. Across town, the Big Game ad war turns the chat window into Times Square, and Mas lets slip his first capital letter.

## Plots

### A-plot: RED LINES (Jan 12 → Mar 26)
- **Jan 12:** HTESGEH badges KORG into the Pentagon at Starbase. "AI will not be woke."
- **Feb 24:** the ultimatum. **Feb 26:** Mario paints the two lines (no mass domestic surveillance, no fully autonomous weapons) and refuses. LIME LEAHCIM arrives by lime scooter.
- **Feb 27:** THE HTURT METEOR, the post that orders every agency to "IMMEDIATELY CEASE." A six-month phase-out hourglass (Ttemme's 72-hour prop, supersized). HTESGEH: "This decision is final."
- **Feb 27 → 28: THE HUG.** In bullet time, Mas publicly backs Mario and embraces him while his third arm signs NopeAI's own classified-network deal "with safeguards" hours later. Mario's third arm deposits his own 2025 Pentagon check. "optics don't look good."
- **Mar 5:** the door-sized SUPPLY CHAIN RISK stamp lands. **Mar 26:** the injunction ("classic illegal First Amendment retaliation"), and Mario sells SUPPLY CHAIN RISK hoodies.

### B-plot: THE CHAT WINDOW BECOMES TIMES SQUARE (Feb 7 → Feb 13)
- The Big Game ad: "Ads are coming to AI. But not to CLOD." Mas: "they are funny, and I laughed." His **first capital letter** of the season, with a 1993 CAPS-LOCK flash.
- Feb 9: ads come to CHATGTP, and a lasagna recipe grows into Times Square. Misanthropic's NO ADS neon is itself an ad.
- The PAC war goes two-sided on screen: PRIVATE LAST's $20M (Feb 12) against FOLLOWING THE PAST.
- ZOMBIE 4o is retired again (Feb 13).

### C-plot: THE LOBSTER (Jan 27 → Mar 10)
- Misanthropic's trademark lawyers chase a crustacean from CLODBOT to MOLTBOT (Jan 27). Three days later RETEP renames it CLAWOPEN anyway, because he didn't like how MOLTBOT looked (Jan 30).
- TTAM vibe-codes BOOKMOLT, and the agents talk about unionizing (Jan 28). RETEP is hired on Valentine's Day (Feb 14), which triggers the Valentine's-box flashback. Kram buys BOOKMOLT as an aquarium (Mar 10).

### Runners
- **THE GAP (Feb 19, Delhi):** Mas and Mario raise fists instead of holding hands, leaving a fist-shaped hole in THE HOST's chain.
- **Distillation (Feb 23):** Mario accuses THE WHALE, THE MOON and a third lab.
- **Rockets:** SPACEZ swallows zAI (Feb 2).
- **AROS's funeral (Mar 24):** YENSID characters file out and SELBEEP officiates.
- **THE COUNCIL class photo (Mar 25):** Mas's face is pressed to the window. SKCAS's lanyard reprints `(FORMER)` (Mar 26).
- **Button:** the day Mario wins in court, MYTHIC leaks from an unlocked drafts folder (Mar 26), and the model waves.

## Themes
1. **Lines on floors.** Red lines, cut lines, the lines under stamps. Every principle is a stripe of paint, and someone always brings a roller.
2. **Hugging your rival while signing his deal.** Solidarity and opportunism happen in the same frame.
3. **Principles as branding.** An ad about not having ads; hoodies about being blacklisted; a vault labeled TOO DANGEROUS that always opens.
4. **Everyone labels everyone.** Supply chain risk, distiller, fear-monger, woke, "leftwing nut jobs." The label gun is contagious.
5. **First crack in the mask.** One capital letter.
