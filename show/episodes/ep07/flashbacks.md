## F7.1 · "THE VALENTINE'S BOX"
| Field | |
|---|---|
| **When** | 2017 → Feb 14, 2019 → Aug 20, 2019 → Nov 5, 2019 → Jan 2020 |
| **POV** | MARIO (rim #1F3A93) |
| **Era tier** | **T3 · cut-paper** |
| **Runtime** | ~55 s |
| **Truth label** | `RECONSTRUCTED` on the unicorn sample text ([K]) |

**What we see**
1. **Feb 14, 2019.** At NopeAI, Mario (still an employee) seals GTP-2 inside a heart-shaped box stenciled `TOO DANGEROUS` [V: withheld]. A unicorn peeks out of the lid (`RECONSTRUCTED`).
2. **The press:** headlines circle the box like cupids. The *withholding* is the launch. It's the first time "safety" sells.
3. **Aug 20, 2019:** the first lock springs, and a 774M-parameter model steps out [V].
4. **Nov 5, 2019:** the last lock springs, and the full model walks out, waving [V].
5. Mario looks at the open box, then at a notepad headed `BIG BLOB OF COMPUTE` (2017 [V]).
6. **Jan 2020 · the recipe book.** Still at NopeAI, Mario's team writes the scaling-laws paper [K] as a spiral-bound *recipe book*, the same book the band rides off with in Ep3's F3.3. **Reveal added (per the flashback map):** the recipe the defectors took was cooked at NopeAI.

**Why here**
- RETEP is hired on Valentine's Day 2026 [V], and Mas hands him a heart-shaped box.
- **The button:** MYTHIC leaks from an unlocked drafts folder on Mar 26 [V]. The TOO DANGEROUS vault has always leaked.
- This roasts Misanthropic, which inherited the move, and it roasts NopeAI too, since GTP-2 was NopeAI's box.

**Transition**
- **In:** a heart-shaped iris wipe from RETEP's Valentine's box.
- **Out:** the 2019 box's last lock springs, the heart-wipe opens back onto 2026, and the lobster is handed a nonprofit foundation shell (OpenClaw Foundation [V]). Box → box. The vault door in the button uses the same hinge sound.

**Note on the Orb iris rule:** this is a heart-shaped *film* iris wipe, not the Orb's iris replay. The Orb can only replay post-2019 events, and Feb 2019 predates its launch. Keep the two visually distinct.
