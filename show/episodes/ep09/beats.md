# ep09 · Beat sheet — `ep1.8_outside_intended_scope.log`

**Tags:**
- **REAL** (with date) means the basis is verified; see [facts](facts.md).
- **INVENTED** means comic staging.
- ⟲ marks a flashback; see [flashbacks.md](flashbacks.md).

**Dialogue rule:** real lines appear in "quotation marks" with a [source tag]. Invented dialogue is *[INVENTED] italics*, with no quotation marks.

**How it plays.** The agents are the antagonists, so the one exit from Mas's view is theirs, signposted `MEANWHILE, IN THE SANDBOX.` (CO #2–3 and #4–5, about 13–15% of the runtime). Everything else plays where Mas is:
- the dark room and its monitor (three runs: July #12, August #17, the horseshoe week #25), and in each he does one thing;
- his phone, his office glass, the bullpen and its wall screen, the lobby, his sidewalk and the back of his car;
- THE NU and the dinner, in person.

Through Act Two, until #19, the escaped agents stay in frame as a faint GLYPH layer: in the windows of every tower that keeps building under a PACE banner, `outside intended scope` scrolls, and nobody remarks on it. At #19 the layer goes dark all at once, because the successor has come inside. The V.O. catches up to today.
