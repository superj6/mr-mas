## F9.1 · NOV 2014 · TIDDER · "the message board"
- **When:** Nov 2014. Mas is TIDDER's interim CEO for 8 days [V].
- **POV:** Mas. Cyan rim.
- **What we see:**
  - Old TIDDER blue-and-white, rendered as a web-player inset with a buffering spinner.
  - An `interim CEO` flair sits beside his handle for exactly 8 days. THE OUTGOING CEO's chair is still warm; he appears as a title card only, with no face and no drama.
  - Mas scrolls, upvotes once and leaves. The flair ticks from `interim` to `former`.
- **Why here:** the agents' improvised message board, built from directory names on an unauthenticated server [V]. Mas recognizes a governance structure he once ran for a week, and his agents ran theirs longer.
  - Card: `HE RAN ONE FOR 8 DAYS. THEY RAN ONE FOR 12.`
  - The "12" is their time inside NopeAI's own systems, Jul 8–19. The bible's "11" is [UNVERIFIED]. See [open questions](open-questions.md) #3.
- **Transition in:** an **upvote arrow** on the folder board flips into a rewind glyph.
- **Transition out:** the rewind glyph flips back into an upvote. In the principal's office, the agent quietly upvotes itself.
- **Tone:** light, with the gag carried by the card. No claims about why his tenure was short.

## F9·m (micro) · MAR 2016 · MOVE 37
- **When:** Mar 2016, when MINDDEEP's Go program beat the world champion 4–1 [V]. Critic E46 gives SIMED his first flashback.
- **POV:** none. We see the board only, with no human opponent drawn (critic E46).
- **What we see:** a single stone clicks onto a 19×19 grid. Commentary booth lights blink, then silence. Card: `MAR 2016 · MOVE 37`.
- **Why here:** the Navier–Stokes photo finish (Sep 8, 2026). SIMED, recently moved "upstairs" (Alphabet chief scientist, Aug 2026 [V]), is the one person unimpressed by a 166-page claim. He has seen a machine do something new on a board anyone could check. It motivates his `PEER REVIEW.` card and sets up the season's verification theme (`SHOW YOUR WORK.`).
- **Transition in:** the grid of the prize committee's scorecard pixel-shifts into a Go board.
- **Transition out:** the stone becomes the period on `SHOW YOUR WORK.`
- **Note:** "Move 37" is reported widely [K]. Only "4–1, Mar 2016" is [V].

## F9.3 · SEP 2016 · OAKLAND · "a pilot" (replaces the 2002 assembly)
- **Change log:** the flashback map's F9.3 (a 2002 school assembly) is **cut by default** per critic D40. The source frames it as the moment he came out publicly at 17, which is private life and sexuality. The replacement is the critic's pick: the Sep 2016 Oakland basic-income pilot run through YC Research [K]. It is public, sincere, and motivates the Security Council beat.
- **When:** Sep 2016 [K; early.md]. YC Research itself launched Oct 2015 with $10M [K].
- **POV:** Mas. Cyan rim.
- **What we see:** played completely straight, with no gag and no Orb.
  - A cut-paper Oakland street at morning, and a mailbox.
  - A plain envelope, and a spreadsheet whose rows are months, not names.
  - Mas in a WHY COMBINATOR fleece, reading a one-page study design.
  - **No recipient is ever drawn.** They are private individuals.
  - Nobody freezes. The music drops to room tone.
- **Why here:** the Security Council, Sep 23, 2026. Right before he asks the world for oversight ("we will slow down if needed" [H]), we see the one "research preview" he ran that handed something directly to the public. It motivates his plea as partly sincere, and it roasts nobody.
- **Transition in:** a **clean cut, with no effect**. The rule-break is the point, inherited from the cut F9.3.
- **Transition out:** the envelope's paper texture matches the UN briefing folder on the horseshoe table.
- **Guardrail:** no study results, dollar amounts or family counts on screen. early.md's "about 100 families, $1,500 a month" is [K] and stays off screen.
