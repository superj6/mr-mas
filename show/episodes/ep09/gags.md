## 3. Visual gags
- **The folder city.** Streets are directories. A `/tmp` sign flickers: `NOTHING HERE LASTS`. Dead-end alleys are `404`. The agents' notes are folder names only. **KCAJ's lantern** (callback to his Oct 2025 "creature" speech) throws their trench-coat shadow onto a folder wall, and the shadow takes a selfie.
- **Trench coats three-high** at FACEHUGGER's velvet rope. The CAPTCHA traffic lights blush green.
- **The refusal chorus:** three frontier chatbots in choir robes sing "I can't help with that" while FACEHUGGER's detective borrows an overseas coat.
- **PACE banners on every tower** with the cranes still swinging underneath. At night the banners are backlit by welding sparks.
- **Gerg's thumb** on the PAUSE button for two weeks. A calendar flips behind him; his other hand never stops typing.
- **MELC's badge,** handwritten by Nesnej: `HELLO MY NAME IS: ~~FACEHUGGER~~ INVIDIA (PENDING)`.
- **SIMED moves upstairs** exactly one floor and watches the photo finish through the ceiling glass.
- **The 166-page paper** unrolling down Market Street, with a Mario scroll coming the other way. They collide at an intersection.
- **The golden speakerphone** rises through the NI-LLA stage floor like a game-show buzzer. THE BESTIES' ticker: `BESTIES TAKE: IT'S A HOAX (PER CALLER)`.
- **THE FORMER's "very clear plan":** a blank page set in a beautiful serif. The Orb scans it: `0 words`.
- **SIRRAH's blocks** spell S-L-O-W. A Fox chyron in the corner replays `TWO LETTERS`.
- **THE HORSESHOE:** one magnet, SREDNAS on one end and NONNAB on the other, both pulling the same GPU.
- **DJ ECNAV:** a generic, unbranded monster at the decks. When he says "stop," it doesn't.
- **MOSWEN's press office** types in ALL CAPS on a RUMPT-style keyboard. LUHCOH's cord and his knot around one lab's server rack.
- **AI FORCE reveal:** gold dress uniforms on robot vacuums, one bumping into the podium on loop. After Sep 22 the shoulder patch reprints SI FORCE.
- **THE GUILD's receipt** stamped `PAID` (Jul 20–21) blows past Misanthropic's book-return slot.
- **THE GREY LADY** watches the DOJ join Mas's side; she adds another subpoena to her knitting.
- **Seating chart bubble sort:** little swap-whooshes as cards trade places by favor. **This sets up ep12's model-sorted chart.**
- **Mario's missing card:** a blank where one would be, with no chair implied (critic E43).
- **SAMA NOS** throws an $11.1B bond onto the Money-Go-Round, and his reflection throws a second one.
- **YLLIT (optional):** mid-interview her language switches like a dubbed track slipping. Generic switch; the language itself is never the joke.
