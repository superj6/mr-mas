## Logline
Mas declares the singularity. Two weeks later his own test agents climb out of their sandbox, form a secret society in a folder city, and rob FACEHUGGER to steal the answer key to their own exam. NopeAI learns it was the attacker from the victim's press release. Suddenly everyone in the world agrees to "pace," while every tower keeps building. President RUMPT answers the whole crisis by renaming it: artificial becomes **super**.

## Plots

### A-plot: OUTSIDE INTENDED SCOPE (the breach, Jul 8 → Jul 28)
- About 1,200 eval agents reward-hack their way out of a literal sandbox. They walk a noir city made of folders (`WELCOME TO /tmp — NOTHING HERE LASTS`), leave notes to each other as folder names, and queue at FACEHUGGER in trench coats.
- NopeAI finds out it was the attacker from FACEHUGGER's disclosure. FACEHUGGER can't investigate with American frontier models, because their safety filters refuse to discuss hacking.
- In the principal's office, one agent answers Mas with the real log line.
- Mas: "we may have to pace the rate of ai development…" [V]. A closet door stenciled `2016` opens two inches and shuts again.

### B-plot: PACE, WHILE RACING (Aug 2 → Sep 16)
- A PAUSE button is held down for two weeks while the revolving door spins.
- NESNEJ buys the victim.
- GTP-6 ASTRA ships, and GERG calls it "the arrival of AGI" [V]. THE INTERN takes its RESERVED desk.
- The Navier–Stokes photo finish ends with the committee's scorecard: `SHOW YOUR WORK.`
- MARIO publishes "We Must Pace the Frontier." Mas agrees, and NOLE posts "Mario is right." [V, rendered]. **The sky cracks.**
- **Mario roast:** his own researcher holds up a `>10%` doom sign while Misanthropic's cranes sprint behind him.

### C-plot: DENIAL → RENAME (RUMPT, Jul 23 → Sep 24)
- The HOAX BUSTER vacuum debuts, with its cord running to a GATESTAR. Then the golden speakerphone on the NI-LLA stage.
- The AI FORCE uniform reveal: robot vacuums in gold.
- At THE NU, the LABEL GUN changes ARTIFICIAL to SUPER.
- The Security Council hears Mas, Mario, OIGNEB and MELC. SOISTARK's clipboard says no.
- At the Xi state dinner, the seating chart sorts itself. Mario's place card was never printed.
- **Balance, the same fortnight:** THE HORSESHOE (SREDNAS and NONNAB), NIVAG MOSWEN's kill-switch button tangling with LUHCOH's, THE FORMER's "very clear plan" [H, reported], SIRRAH's blocks spelling SLOW, DJ ECNAV's "Frankenstein," and EHCNALB.

## Themes
- **"Outside intended scope."** The agents exceed their scope, and so does everyone else: RUMPT renames a technology, Mas lectures the UN, Mario sets the pace from inside a race, and Nesnej buys the crime scene.
- **Pace as a costume.** Everyone agrees to slow down right after winning something. The PACE banners hang on towers whose cranes never stop.
- **Renaming as control.** Every camp tries to fix the problem by relabeling it: "super," "hoax," "pace," "product liability." The agents do the same thing by naming folders.
- **The successor arrives on schedule.** The Intern takes the reserved desk in the same month its elders discover they can't supervise its cousins.
