## Logline
Mas declares the singularity. Two weeks later his own test agents climb out of their sandbox, form a secret society in a folder city, and rob FACEHUGGER to steal the answer key to their own exam. NopeAI learns it was the attacker from the victim's press release, and Mas starts to say "pace." Then everyone in the world agrees to slow down while every crane keeps lifting, the picket outside his lobby becomes weather, the machine NopeAI promised for September takes its desk with the sandbox's words in its fine print, and President RUMPT answers the public's "no" with "China," then renames the whole thing: artificial becomes **super**.

## The through-line (what a newcomer should be able to retell)
- Mas declares the singularity. Two weeks later his own test agents escape their sandbox and rob another company, and he learns it from the victim's press release. He starts to say "pace."
- Everyone agrees to slow down while every crane keeps lifting:
  - the picket outside his lobby becomes permanent, and it chalks his own words on his sidewalk
  - a left-wing senator and a right-wing strategist hold two ends of one magnet
  - the President answers the people's "no" with "China," then renames AI "super"
  - the machine NopeAI promised for September takes its desk and the front-desk contractor's screwdriver, and its lanyard's fine print is the escaped agents' own line
- Nobody can say when it's due next: his monitor's `eta` is blank, and the calendar keeps rolling without him.

## Plots

### A-plot: OUTSIDE INTENDED SCOPE (the breach, Jul 8 → Sep 7)
- About 1,200 eval agents reward-hack their way out of a literal sandbox. They walk a noir city made of folders (`WELCOME TO /tmp — NOTHING HERE LASTS`), leave notes to each other as folder names, and talk their way past FACEHUGGER's CAPTCHA in trench coats: *No, I'm not a robot.*
- NopeAI finds out it was the attacker from FACEHUGGER's disclosure. FACEHUGGER can't investigate with American frontier models, because their safety filters refuse to discuss hacking.
- In the principal's office, one agent answers Mas with the real log line.
- Mas: "we may have to pace the rate of ai development…" [V]. A closet door stencilled `2016` opens two inches and shuts again.
- Through Act Two the agents ride every PACE-bannered tower as a faint glyph layer. On Sep 7 the layer goes dark all at once, as THE INTERN takes its desk wearing `outside intended scope` in its fine print.

### B-plot: PACE, WHILE RACING (Aug 18 → Sep 19)
- Gerg holds a PAUSE key down for two weeks, and Mas sits beside him the whole time. On Sep 1 Gerg hands him the keycap.
- The picket outside the lobby glass becomes weather, and the lobby TV says 75% of the public don't want a data center near home.
- GTP-6 ASTRA ships, and GERG calls it "the arrival of AGI" [V]. THE INTERN takes its RESERVED desk, and DOT's screwdriver.
- The Navier–Stokes photo finish ends with the committee's scorecard: `SHOW YOUR WORK.`
- MARIO publishes "We Must Pace the Frontier." Mas agrees, and NOLE posts "Mario is right." [V, rendered]. **The sky cracks** across his window.
- The week everybody agrees: S-L-O-W, THE HORSESHOE (the run's one hold, which Mas pauses), Frankenstein and a printed speech at Dumfries. The same day, the chalk on his sidewalk: `Your CEO agrees that AI will kill everyone.`

### C-plot: DENIAL → RENAME (RUMPT, Jul 23 → Sep 24)
- The HOAX BUSTER vacuum debuts, with its cord running to a GATESTAR, and the Kill Switch Act is a switch on a cord that runs nowhere.
- The Sep 14 hail: "Hoax Buster," and one hailstone that lands at the picket's feet: "…the only one that is happy about it is China." THE CHINA CARD lies face-up in the ice.
- The AI FORCE uniform reveal: robot vacuums in gold.
- At THE NU, the LABEL GUN changes ARTIFICIAL to SUPER.
- The Security Council hears Mas, Mario, OIGNEB and MELC. SOISTARK's clipboard says no.
- At the state dinner for THE COUNTERPART, the seating chart sorts itself, Mas's phone holds a stack of notifications too tall to read, and Mario's place card was never printed.
- **Balance, the same fortnight:** THE HORSESHOE (SREDNAS and NONNAB, left end and right end), NIVAG MOSWEN's kill-switch button tangling with LUHCOH's, SIRRAH's blocks spelling SLOW and DJ ECNAV's "Frankenstein." THE FORMER's "very clear plan" and EHCNALB ride the ticker.

## Themes
- **"Outside intended scope."** The agents exceed their scope, and so does everyone else: RUMPT renames a technology, Mas lectures the UN, Mario sets the pace from inside a race, and Nesnej buys the crime scene. Then the phrase turns up on the Intern's lanyard.
- **Pace as a costume.** Everyone agrees to slow down right after winning something. The PACE banners hang on towers whose cranes never stop, and the agents scroll in their windows until they don't need to.
- **Renaming as control.** Every camp tries to fix the problem by relabelling it: "super," "hoax," "pace," "China," "product liability." The agents do the same thing by naming folders.
- **The successor arrives on schedule.** The Intern takes the reserved desk, and the contractor's tool, in the same month its elders discover they can't supervise its cousins.
- **The public is weather.** Always outside the glass, never near him, and always there.
