**Tags:**
- **REAL** (with a date) marks only the scaffolding, meaning the anchor event or quote. See [facts](facts.md).
- **INVENTED** is comic staging, which covers nearly every beat.
- **ECHO** is a real quote re-used in an invented scene. It never appears on a dated card.
- ⟲ marks a flashback. See [flashbacks.md](flashbacks.md).

**Dialogue rule:** real lines appear in "quotation marks" with a [source tag]. Invented dialogue appears as *[INVENTED] italics* with no quotation marks.

**On screen, the calendar never hedges, and it dates only what's real.** Ep9's rail rolls on into this episode and stays undated. A date appears only on a real thing: NOSNHOJ's quote carries its own attribution (`SEP 24 · TO THE PRESS`), and YELWAH's clock from Ep9 reads `DOCUMENTS DUE OCT 1` as it hits zero. Invented beats ride the rail undated. No `?` dates, no `(SCHEDULED)` tag, no speculation card.

**V.O.:** three lines. Two are claims the picture undercuts (#2, #11); the last is a caught denial (#28). "Huh." is written, not spoken.

**Sound through-line** ([flow §3](../../bible/flow-and-continuity.md#3-sound-a-continuous-bed)): Act Two is one bed from the card to the dealer. The table's cue carries #16–19 without a stop; the read (#20) re-voices it in the score's own piano and strings; the strings stop on the dealer's card snap into J4 (the episode's one designed stop), and the table cue re-enters under KA-CHING.
