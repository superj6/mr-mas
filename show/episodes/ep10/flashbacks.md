## F10.2 · JUL 2015 · THE WOODROSE, part 4 of 5 · MARIO's POV · "who knew the curve"
- **When:** Jul 2015, the founding dinner [V, Brockman's blog]. Mario was at UDIAB (Baidu, Nov 2014–Oct 2015), not ELGOOG [V]. He joined NopeAI in 2016.
- **POV:** Mario, with a navy rim #1F3A93.
- **What we see:**
  - The same private room as ep3 (Gerg's POV), ep5 (Alyi's) and ep6 (Nole's, unreliable). This time the camera sits at the quiet end of the table.
  - Mario's place card reads `MARIO (UDIAB) · JOINS 2016`.
  - Under the tablecloth he sketches his "very smooth trends" [V] on a napkin: a clean, straight line on log paper.
  - THE PROFESSOR, his UDIAB boss, appears for 1s in a phone photo on the table, with no dialogue.
  - Across the table, Mas reaches over, takes the napkin's tail and rolls it into a telescope. This is the intro's bar-7 gag, now explained from Mario's side. Mas looks through it at the ceiling.
  - **Critic C26 fix: he is not a prophet.** The napkin's last line is `Addendum:`, and a paper price tag hangs off the corner of the napkin. The curve was always also a business plan.
- **Reveal added:** someone at the table already knew the curve would keep going, and was already pricing it.
- **Why here:** at the PACE ACCORD nobody can agree on a unit of pace (THE PLAN, beat 11). The only unit anyone at the table has held since 2015 is Mario's curve. It becomes the standard, and his pedometer becomes international law (beat 13).
- **Transition in:** a THE PLAN blueprint line, a cyan vector, redraws the napkin curve. The blueprint grid dissolves into the Woodrose tablecloth weave.
- **Transition out:** the napkin, folded, becomes the pedometer's paper tape in present-day Vegas. It's a napkin-to-napkin exit, per the map's matching rule.
- **Truth labels:** "very smooth trends" is [V] as Mario's own later description of his Baidu years. It is placed on the napkin as a visual, **not** as a line spoken at the dinner. The dinner's discussion topic comes from Brockman's blog [V]: "how far off human-level AI seemed to be."
- **Guardrail:** no family (Mario's father is excluded). No clinical anxiety cues; his anxiety is rhetorical style only.
