## 3. Visual gags
- **The ballroom summit:** folding tables among paint cans. RUMPT's permit sign from ep9 (`BUILDING CODE: HIM.`) now has a sticky note: `+ CZAR OFFICE (TBD)`.
- **The hologram keynote:** Mas's DevDay hologram gets a standing ovation while the real Mas perches on a paint bucket. It's a lighter echo of ep6's deepfake city.
- **The High I.Q. exam:** a Scantron with one bubble per question, all pre-filled in lowercase.
- **The CZAR lanyard** reprinting itself onto the Intern, name by name: `SIRRAH → SKCAS → (FORMER) → ONLY HIGH I.Q. → THE INTERN`.
- **YELWAH vs the paper:** his subpoena lasso stretches around a document stack taller than the Senate office building. The top page reads `table of contents (1 of 40)`.
- **360 REVIEW zoetrope:** the ring of reviewers spins into an animation of the Intern growing up.
- **The PLAN's floating units:** FLOPs, gigawatts, tokens/sec, GPUs and "vibes," each grabbed by a different delegation like balloons.
- **Mario's pedometer as law:** every delegation has to walk at his step rate. Nole speed-walks in place; the Whale floats.
- **No-limit PACE:** GPUs as chips. NORCAM's two deepfakes in two extra seats. The Pope's encyclical as the tablecloth, too long for the table, so it trails out the door. PRAK's seeing-stone. THE COUNTERPART's opposite table, a mirrored podium that grows a seat per hand.
- **The rocket pen:** Nole's side letter is signed at escape velocity.
- **THE WHALE open-sources the table:** the felt becomes a public repo, every hand visible. Everyone's cards turn face-up at once.
- **THE RUMPT PACE:** the giant marker arrives by GATESTAR crane. The EO RECEIPT's tip line reads `CUT: 15% (ASKED 20)`.
- **ECNAV's afterparty:** a record labeled PACE is scratched into RACE. The monster from ep9 is at the decks again, and wears a visitor lanyard.
- **The Intern dealing:** the camera slowly pans from MAON's empty hands to a cursor blinking where the dealer's face should be.
- **CLOD in a visitor lanyard,** holding a clipboard with nothing on it.
- **The power strip:** MOSWEN's and LUHCOH's cords, and the federal switch, all in one strip, plugged into a GATESTAR socket.
- **The Grand Prix:** OIGNEB's safety car with a speed-limit sign bolted on. When he checks the mirror, every lab floors it. THE HUMANIST rides shotgun.
- **The IPO bell:** two ropes, two hands (Mas and Mario), one bell. HARAS in a catcher's mask. The crack runs down the wall into the closet.
