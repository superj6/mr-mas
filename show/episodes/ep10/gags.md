## 3. Visual gags
- **The ballroom summit:** folding tables among paint cans. RUMPT's permit sign from ep9 (`BUILDING CODE: HIM.`) now has a sticky note: `+ CZAR OFFICE (TBD)`.
- **The hologram keynote:** Mas's DevDay hologram gets a standing ovation while the real Mas perches on a paint bucket. It's a lighter echo of ep6's deepfake city.
- **The High I.Q. exam:** a Scantron with one bubble per question, all pre-filled in lowercase.
- **The CZAR lanyard** reprinting itself onto the Intern, name by name: `SIRRAH → SKCAS → (FORMER) → ONLY HIGH I.Q. → THE INTERN`.
- **YELWAH vs the paper:** his subpoena lasso stretches around a document stack taller than the Senate office building. The top page reads `table of contents (1 of 40)`.
- **360 REVIEW zoetrope:** the ring of reviewers spins into an animation of the Intern growing up.
- **The PLAN's floating units:** FLOPs, gigawatts, tokens/sec, GPUs and "vibes," each grabbed by a different delegation like balloons.
- **Mario's pedometer as law:** every delegation has to walk at his step rate. Mario takes one step and the whole room lurches with him; Nole speed-walks in place; the Whale floats. No line explains it.
- **No-limit PACE:** GPUs as chips. NORCAM's two deepfakes in two extra seats. The Pope's encyclical as the tablecloth, too long for the table, so it trails out the door. PRAK's seeing-stone. THE COUNTERPART's opposite table, a mirrored podium that grows a seat per hand.
- **The rocket pen:** Nole's side letter is signed at escape velocity.
- **THE WHALE open-sources its own seat:** the felt under its hand becomes a public repo and its cards turn face-up. Everyone else holds theirs closer.
- **PUBLIC COMMENT:** a `3:00` timer at a ballroom mic, a queue of tiles out the door holding a BACKSTOP ticket, a `PAUSE` sign and a fence's `NO`, and the same rubber stamp from every delegation: `PAY OUR WAY.` The REC light on the ballroom monitor blinks once, and nobody looks.
- **THE RUMPT PACE:** the giant marker arrives by GATESTAR crane. The EO RECEIPT's tip line reads `CUT: 15% (ASKED 20)`.
- **ECNAV's afterparty:** a record labeled PACE is scratched into RACE. The monster from ep9 is at the decks again, and wears a visitor lanyard.
- **The Intern dealing:** after the first hand the dealer is only ever hands. The read's push rests on them and tilts up to a caret blinking where the dealer's face should be; at the bar, MAON's hands are empty.
- **The location pin:** `THE INTERN · IN WASHINGTON` on Mas's phone as he lands in Vegas, styled like ALYI's pins in ep2.
- **CLOD in a visitor lanyard,** holding a clipboard with nothing on it.
- **The power strip:** three cords labelled `FEDERAL` · `CALIFORNIA` · `NEW YORK`, all in one strip, plugged into the ballroom's plain wall outlet. The chandeliers dim one step as the survey pops up.
- **THE HORSESHOE in the queue:** two politicians from opposite ends sharing one magnet, which keeps pulling toward the nearest steel.
- **THE BINDER:** a black ring binder on the Intern's shelf, `Situational Awareness` on the spine, one tab at `IV. The Project`, beside THE FORECASTER's scenario and REZEILE's hardcover. The Intern never touches it.
- **The IPO bell:** one rope, two hands (Mas and Mario), one bell. The rope is the Intern's last dealt card. HARAS in a catcher's mask. The crack runs down the wall into the closet.
- **The closet:** the Intern's rack in a generic gas mask, where the doom file was kept, with a juice box beside it. This rhymes with the 1993 cup, which never ripples.
