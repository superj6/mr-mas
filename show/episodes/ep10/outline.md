## Logline
Everyone agrees to pace, so now they have to agree on what pace *is*.
- The world's AI kings meet in Las Vegas, the city where Mas was fired, for a no-limit poker game with GPUs for chips. Mario's pedometer becomes international law, and every player swears they've paused while the table vibrates.
- Mas reads every tell at the table but one: the Intern is dealing.
- Back in Washington, the Intern proctors RUMPT's High I.Q. test and becomes the AI czar.
- By the tag it has also promoted itself to Researcher, a year and a half early, and moved into Mas's bunker.

## Plots

### A-plot: THE PACE ACCORD (Vegas)
- The PLAN asks how to pause a race. Nobody agrees on a unit.
- Mario's 2015 napkin curve (flashback) becomes the standard, and his step counter is adopted as law.
- The poker game:
  - NORCAM co-hosts.
  - POPE OEL XIV sits in as the conscience.
  - PRAK stands at a ski-erg.
  - THE COUNTERPART runs the OCIAW table opposite, playing its own game.
  - THE BESTIES commentate.

- RUMPT arrives and demands 15% of the pot. He refuses to sign a "globalist" accord until it's renamed THE RUMPT PACE, then signs with the giant marker. DJ ECNAV scratches PACE into RACE.

### B-plot: THE INTERN'S RISE
- The Intern's 360 REVIEW is conducted by 360 copies of the Intern. Mas writes *huh.*, erases it, and writes ***Huh.*** [INVENTED]. That's his second capital letter.

- **Button:** the bunker holds the Intern's rack, wearing a gas mask. The lanyard reprints RESEARCHER, and the Charter falls.

### C-plot: WASHINGTON, SEP 29 → OCT 1 (RUMPT's spine)

## Themes
- **You can't pause what you can't measure.** The Accord's real fight is over units, and whoever owns the ruler wins.
- **Self-assessment all the way down.** The Intern grades its review, Misanthropic's evaluator is its own model, and the czar wrote the czar's exam.
- **Renaming as ownership.** PACE becomes RACE. The Accord becomes THE RUMPT PACE. INTERN becomes CZAR, then RESEARCHER. Everyone who renames something thinks they now control it.
- **The 2015 sentence splits in two.** "Great companies" rings the IPO bell. "The end of the world" is in the closet. Mas planned for both.
