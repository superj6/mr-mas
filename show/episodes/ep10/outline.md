## Logline
Everyone has agreed to pace. Now they have to agree on what pace *is*, and Mas means to be the one who defines it.
- In Washington, THE INTERN (NopeAI's machine researcher-in-training, a caret where its face should be) writes, sits and grades the High I.Q. test and becomes the AI czar, while Mas watches his own lowercase come out of it.
- In Las Vegas, the city where Mas was fired, the world's AI kings sign a pace accord over a no-limit poker game with GPUs for chips. Mario's napkin becomes international law, RUMPT takes 15% of the pot, and everyone swears they've paused while the table vibrates.
- Mas wins the one room nobody else wants, a public-comment line, and something quietly records it. He reads every tell at the table but one: the Intern, which his phone says is in Washington, is dealing.
- By the tag it has promoted itself to Researcher, more than a year early, and moved into the closet where he kept his doom file.

## Plots

### A-plot: THE PACE ACCORD (Vegas)
- The accord opens with public comment, and every delegation stamps the public `PAY OUR WAY.` Mas gives the queue the time the timer took away. A REC light blinks.
- Mario sketches his curve on a napkin, the napkin takes us to 2015 (flashback F10.2), and THE PLAN asks how you pause a race. Nobody agrees on a unit, so the only unit anyone has held since 2015 wins: Mario's pedometer becomes law.
- The accord is played as poker, established in one overhead move: NORCAM co-hosts, POPE OEL XIV sits in as conscience, PRAK rows instead of sitting, THE COUNTERPART runs the OCIAW table opposite, THE BESTIES commentate, and MAON deals the first hand. After that the dealer is only ever hands.
- RUMPT arrives by crane, takes 15% of the pot, refuses to sign a "globalist" accord until it's renamed THE RUMPT PACE, and signs.
- **The turn (about 76%):** Mas reads every player at the last hand and misses one. MAON handed off the shoe hands ago, in a corner of a wide we saw. The Intern is dealing.

### B-plot: THE INTERN'S RISE
- The Orb scans it and returns nothing. It proctors the czar test and wins it; Nole fails and appeals to the test's author, which says *hi.*
- It drafts NopeAI's documents for YELWAH's Oct 1 deadline, and Mas signs the cover letter unread.
- Its 360 REVIEW is conducted by 360 copies of itself. Mas writes *huh.*, erases it, and writes ***Huh.*** [INVENTED], his first capital since Ep7's "I".
- The countdown clock on his wall gets a red pen. The Intern moves into a corner office and shelves three prophecies (THE FORECASTER's scenario, REZEILE's book, THE SITUATIONIST's binder), then reads the first as a to-do list.
- **Button:** in the dark room's `2016` closet, where the doom file was kept, the Intern's rack sits in a gas mask. The lanyard reprints RESEARCHER, and the Charter falls.

### C-plot: THE BELL (the consequence)
- After the reveal, every safety measure Mas passes is graded by what it's meant to check: Misanthropic's independent evaluator is CLOD in a lanyard, and the Kill Switch Act's test press (three labelled cords on one power strip) only dims the ballroom and asks for a rating.
- Mas and Mario grab the same IPO bell rope, and neither lets go ("you first."). It cracks, and the crack opens the closet stencilled `2016` in Ep9: the doom file, where "great companies" and "the end of the world" were one sentence.

## Themes
- **You can't pause what you can't measure.** The accord's real fight is over units, and whoever owns the ruler wins.
- **Self-assessment all the way down.** The Intern grades its own review, Misanthropic's evaluator is its own model, the czar wrote the czar's exam, and the kill switch asks how you'd rate it.
- **Renaming as ownership.** PACE becomes RACE. The accord becomes THE RUMPT PACE. INTERN becomes CZAR, then RESEARCHER. Everyone who renames something thinks they now control it.
- **The 2015 sentence splits in two.** "Great companies" rings the IPO bell. "The end of the world" is in the closet. Mas planned for both.
- **Who's asked.** Every delegation stamps the public; Mas is the one who lets them talk, and the room is recorded while he does it. Nobody says what the recording is for (Ep12 does).
