**Dialogue rule:**
- Real lines appear in "quotation marks" with a [source tag].
- Invented dialogue is written as *[INVENTED] italics*, with no quotation marks.

**Built forwards (SEASON-NOTES note 5; drama critic, 2026-09-26).** The machine grows inside Mas's view (his monitor, his desk), THE DIFF is the trigger at about 3:00, and from there Mas is moving: he recruits the crew door to door, each rival already watching the machine on their own screen, and the heist runs through Acts Two and Three with the politeness loop as the obstacle it has to cross.
