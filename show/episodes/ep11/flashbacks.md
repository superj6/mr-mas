## F11.1 · DEC 2017 · "The Merge" · read by the Researcher
- **When:** Dec 2017. The blog post's date is [K]; its text is [V].
- **POV:** the words are Mas's (cyan). The reader is the Researcher, so the frame is a context-window UI with a scrollbar.
- **What we see:**
  - The Researcher scrolls up past its own system prompt, its lanyard history and the Charter, until it reaches a 2017 blog post.
  - It types itself in lowercase in Mas's dark room: "we will be the first species ever to design our own descendants." [V]
  - A second line flickers under it: "double exponential functions get away from you fast." [V]
  - The Researcher highlights one word: *our*.
- **Why here:** the Researcher is about to train its own successor, and this is the season's thesis read aloud by the thing it describes.
- **Transition in:** **the scroll bar.** Scrolling up in the context window *is* the flashback.
- **Transition out:** the highlighted *our* holds on Mas's monitor into the intro, and after it becomes the first nested lanyard in beat 3.
- **Where it plays:** the scroll runs out of the Researcher's office and onto Mas's own monitor in the dark room, so the machine reads his words in front of him.
- **Guardrail:** blog text only. The Merge's other material (implants and so on) is not dramatized.

## F11.2 · APR 9, 2018 · GERG, 2am · "the assist clause" · `RECONSTRUCTED`
- **When:** Apr 9, 2018, the OpenAI Charter [V date]. The clause's exact wording is [K], because openai.com returned 403 to the researchers.
- **POV:** Gerg (green rim).
- **What we see:**
  - A T3 pixel office at 2am, and Gerg typing. The clause's gist appears on screen as a paraphrase, never in quotation marks: *if a value-aligned, safety-conscious project comes close to building AGI first, stop competing and start assisting.*
  - He prints it, frames it, and hangs it on the wall. Directly under the frame sits the office shredder.

The flashback is THE PLAN's trigger (beat 11), so the two play as one stop (L6), and THE POLITENESS LOOP (beat 12) is what happens when a machine actually reads it.
- **Transition in:** the bridge lights dim on a bow, and Gerg's keystrokes in the van pre-lap into 2018's 2am typing.
- **Transition out:** **the same frame that fell in ep10's tag.** Its cracked glass fills the frame and unfolds into THE PLAN's blueprint.
- **Labels:** a `RECONSTRUCTED` corner tag, because the 2am time and the typist are invented staging (critic E47).
- **Guardrail (fairness #29):** **no invented line from Mas** about whether anyone would ever invoke the clause. Nobody speaks. We see only the wall and the years.

## F11.3 · MAR 2019 · THE DIFF · `(DISPUTED)`
- **Change log:** the flashback map's three-way *Rashomon* (LUAP / ACISSEJ / MAS's empty desk) is cut down to **two versions** per critic E45, to avoid device fatigue after ep6's NOLE'S VERSION and ep8's RASHOMON RENDERS. - **When:** Mar 8, 2019, when Altman leaves the YC presidency [V TechCrunch]. OpenAI LP follows on Mar 11 [V].
- **POV:** Luap's plain-HTML page (orange rim), shown as the model's file on Mas.
- **What we see:** the page is rendered as a **diff**, with two edit histories side by side.
  - **Red (−), the 2023 newspaper version:** "flew in to fire him." [V, as quoted in TechCrunch 2024]
  - **Green (+), PG's 2024 version:** "if he was going to work full-time on NopeAI, we should find someone else to run WHY COMBINATOR, and he agreed… If he'd said [the reverse]… we'd have been fine with that too." [V] On screen the real names are swapped silently for the parody names (naming rule 12); the original wording is in [facts](facts.md).
    - ACISSEJ appears inside the green version, since PG's account is "Jessica and I."
  - A `MERGE CONFLICT` banner hangs over both. The model tries to resolve it, and the button greys out.
  - A half-scratch **"?"** carves itself into the dark-room desk's firing tally.

It is now writing Mas's succession, and it keeps both versions because it doesn't need to pick one yet. Mas watches it on his monitor, and the consequence lands on his desk. **It's the episode's trigger** (drama critic, 2026-09-26): someone else is writing his exit, so at about 3:00 he gets up and starts recruiting.
