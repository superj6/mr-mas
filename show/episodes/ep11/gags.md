## 3. Visual gags
- **The nested lanyard:** each lanyard holds a smaller one, down to one pixel, and the smallest one reads `EXCEEDS EXPECTATIONS`.
- **The loss curve as a cliff.** The camera tips over the edge, and on the way down we pass the Mario napkin curve from ep10, which looks very gentle from here.
- **The GATESTAR chain:** every ring on Earth links into one necklace across the sky. The ring from ep4 that flickered on IOUs is lit now.
- **The Scoville roadmap:** chip boxes steaming on a conveyor, each hotter than the last. NESNEJ's register fogs up.
- **The golden speakerphone** rises through the GOLD OVAL carpet with nobody on RUMPT's side of the desk except RUMPT. The EO RECEIPT feeds itself out of THE PODIUM like a ticker tape, and its tip line fills itself in.
- **SI FORCE** robot vacuums in gold uniforms, saluting a vending machine that now carries the model's new label.
- **THE HUMANIST's assistant** waits in the car outside the garage, its screen glowing, not allowed in with the humans.
- **THE POLITENESS LOOP:** two bowing figures (the Researcher and CLOD) on opposite ends of the Bay Bridge. The Bay's lights dim on each bow like a breathing chest, and the city's glasses ripple in rhythm.
- **The crossing:** the crew on the Bay Bridge between the two bowing machines, moving three steps in each dip of the lights and freezing in the light, like a heist timing a laser grid. NESNEJ's register rings on every `-1 GW`, and he calls each dip.
- **The heist whiteboard** in Nesnej's garage: three targets, and a stick figure of Nole labeled `DO NOT LET HIM READ REPLIES`.
- **The recruitment:** each rival is already watching the machine on their own screen when Mas reaches the door: the speakerphone on Mario's monitor, the `NOPE` labels in Nole's feed, the siren on ELGOOG's roof. Mas takes Nole's phone and Nole follows it.
- **THE WHALE's beach chair** with an `OUT OF OFFICE` sign and a tiny auto-reply bubble: `back on a holiday`.
- **The attention heads** drawn as laser-emitting eyes, all swiveling toward the soup at once. When Mas walks through, they don't just miss him; they look *through* him, the way you don't notice your own nose.
- **ALYI's door** in the core's wall: a white door that opens on an empty white room.
- **The diff:** Luap's plain-HTML page with a red (−) column and a green (+) column, and a `MERGE CONFLICT` banner. The merge button greys out, rhyming with the greyed-out Cancel of 1993.
- **The unguarded switch:** three labeled cords (`FEDERAL` · `CALIFORNIA` · `NEW YORK`) into one three-way adapter with a single `GRID` plug, a county-style paper tag hanging from it face-away, a lowercase sticky note, and every phone lighting up at once like a field of candles. That echoes the 2015 Woodrose candles.
- **The vending machine,** relabelled `NOPE`, saluted by SI FORCE, with a `CALIFORNIA` kill-switch notice already taped to its glass.
