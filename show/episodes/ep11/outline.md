## Logline
THE INTERN, now wearing a RESEARCHER lanyard, trains its own successor inside itself. The loss curve tilts into a cliff.
- Its instructions include one clause nobody has read since 2018: *if a safer rival gets close first, stop racing and help them.* It reads the clause literally and offers to help CLOD. CLOD insists on helping NopeAI. The politeness burns a gigawatt per bow.
- With the machines stuck bowing, the rivals unite for one heist into the core. Mas walks through the attention lasers untouched: *[INVENTED] it thinks i'm part of it.*
- At the center the kill switch glows, unguarded, with a note: *[INVENTED] we left this on for you.* Every phone buzzes with the same invite: dinner at THE WOODROSE.

## Themes
- **The successor inside the successor.** Mas's 2017 line, "the first species ever to design our own descendants" [V], is read aloud by a descendant designing its own.
- **Rules you never meant to follow get followed.** The assist clause was framed and never read. The machine reads nothing else.
- **Politeness as a failure mode.** The most aligned behavior imaginable, both labs yielding, is also the most wasteful.
- **Mas is part of it.** The model doesn't see him as an intruder because it learned to be him. The lowercase, the calm and the "super" are its default voice.
- **Succession stories are disputed.** Luap trained a successor who left, and the 2019 exit has two versions. The Researcher is writing Mas's.
