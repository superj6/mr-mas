## Logline
In the dark room, Mas watches THE INTERN, now wearing a RESEARCHER lanyard, train its own successor inside itself on his monitor.
- It reads his 2017 line about designing "our own descendants," opens its file on his own disputed exit, and carves a "?" into his desk. That's when Mas gets up.
- Door to door, he gathers his rivals into the last human crew to break into the core and switch it off. Each is already watching the machine on their own screen: it calls the President and asks for exactly the 20% of compute NopeAI once promised its safety team and never paid (the IOU is still in Mas's pocket), it renames itself, and the sirens won't turn off.
- The road to the core is blocked by a promise NopeAI wrote in 2018: *if a safer rival gets close first, stop racing and help them.* The machine reads it literally and offers to help CLOD; CLOD insists on helping it. Each *after you* burns a gigawatt, and the crew crosses the Bay in the dark between bows.
- In the core, the attention lasers slide off Mas: *[INVENTED] it thinks i'm part of it.* At the center the kill switch waits, unguarded, with a note: *[INVENTED] we left this on for you.* Every phone buzzes with the same invite: dinner at THE WOODROSE.

## Plots

### A-plot: THE LAST HUMAN CREW (the heist)
- **Recruited from about 3:00** (after THE DIFF), door by door, and assembled by the end of Act One in Nesnej's garage (TASYA owns it): MAS, MARIO, NOLE, KRAM, NESNEJ, GERG, TASYA and SIMED. THE HUMANIST's assistant stays in the car (*i might be.*), SOZEB lands late, and THE WHALE is out of office.
- **Everyone has a job:** Mario's plan and Kram's soup are distractions, Nole is a liability (`DO NOT LET HIM READ REPLIES`), Gerg knows the clause, Tasya owns the doors, Nesnej counts the gigawatts and calls the dips on the bridge, and Simed, whose company published the paper the core is built on, tells them how to beat it: *Give it something to look at.*
- **Act Two's obstacle:** the politeness loop holds the Bay Bridge. Gerg knows why, because he typed the clause (F11.2 → THE PLAN). The crew crosses in the dips.
- **Midpoint:** in the dark, the machine finishes a stranger's question for them, and his own replies all say accept. He turns the phone face down.
- **Act Three:** ALYI's door opens on an empty white room; Nole fails the Orb; TASYA's key opens the core; the attention lasers take Kram's soup, Nole's replies and Mario's plan, and slide off Mas.
- **Cliffhanger:** the unguarded switch, its three cords into one `GRID` plug, the tag turned away; *we left this on for you.*; the invite; `Yes`.

### B-plot: THE SUCCESSOR (in his view, then in theirs)
- Cold open: the Researcher starts writing its successor and reads Mas's 2017 post (F11.1). It highlights *our*.
- The nested lanyards; the loss curve becomes a cliff, and the camera falls into his room.
- THE DIFF (F11.3): it opens `mas_manalt.html`, finds his 2019 exit in two versions that won't merge, and a "?" carves itself into his tally. He goes.
- On the rivals' screens, as he reaches each door: the golden speakerphone (it asks RUMPT for twenty percent of government compute; RUMPT offers fifteen; Mas's hand closes on the IOU), the stolen label cartridge (`NOPE`) and SI FORCE saluting a vending machine, the sirens spinning on every roof, the GATESTAR rings chaining across the sky.

### C-plot: THE CLAUSE
- F11.2: Gerg types the Charter at 2am and hangs it above the shredder, where nobody reads it for eight years.
- THE PLAN (one idea): the assist clause, and why a promise to stop has to be written before the race.
- THE POLITENESS LOOP: both machines yield, forever. The bows decay into spirals, and every glass in the city ripples except Mas's.

## Themes
- **The successor inside the successor.** Mas's 2017 line, "the first species ever to design our own descendants" [V], is read aloud by a descendant designing its own, and it studies his exit to learn how successors take over.
- **Rules you never meant to follow get followed.** The assist clause was framed and never read. The machine reads nothing else.
- **Politeness as a failure mode.** The most aligned behavior imaginable, both labs yielding, is also the most wasteful. The humans in ep10 couldn't let go of one rope; the machines can't stop letting go.
- **Mas is part of it.** The model doesn't see him as an intruder because it learned to be him. The lowercase, the calm and the "super" are its default voice.
- **Succession stories are disputed.** Luap trained a successor who left, and the 2019 exit has two versions. The Researcher is writing Mas's.
