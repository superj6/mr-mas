## F4.2 · APR 5, 1993 · NESNEJ · *the screen, part 2 of 4*
- **Date card:** **`MEANWHILE · 17 DAYS BEFORE HE TURNED 8`** (WC-crit §E42). This is true whenever the computer actually arrived. Fallback: `THAT SAME SPRING`. Don't use "17 DAYS EARLIER": it implies the computer came on Apr 22, which is unverified.
- **Trigger (present):** Jan 27, 2025. DOT's "It's free." glow carries the cut into THE WHALE's pebble, which topples NESNEJ's gold statue: −17%, about $589B. The KA-CHING goes silent. Nesnej doesn't flinch.
- **We see:**
  1. The gold statue **shatters into 1-bit pixels**, and the pixels settle into a 1-bit booth at a **SYNNED on Berryessa Road in East San Jose**.
  2. INVIDIA is founded at the table with "$40,000 in the bank" [V]. A napkin shows the figure.
  3. A detail: Nesnej knows this booth. He worked at SYNNED from 1978 to 1983, starting at 15, as "dishwasher, busboy, and waiter" [V]. The busboy tray is on the next table.
  4. **1997 insert (3s):** the unofficial motto on a 1-bit sticky note, "Our company is thirty days from going out of business." [V]
  5. The register is an old 1-bit till. It goes *clunk* with **no bell**, because it's empty in 1993.
- **Why here:** he has been here before. The man who lost more value in one day than anyone in history once had $40,000 and thirty days. It is also the **same 1993 as the kid**: the chip company is born two and a half weeks before the boy turns eight.
- **Transition in:** the statue shatters into 1-bit pixels, the same tier as F1.1.
- **Transition out:** the pixels re-form into the gold statue in the present. The register rings **once**, the first KA-CHING since the pebble.
- **Tier (T1):** pure `#000/#FFF`, 512×342, 6 fps, Bayer dither, Silkscreen type. The square-wave beeper plays the knee motif, and the register "clunk" is a low square blip.
- **Escalation thread (the 1993 screen):** Ep1 (the back of the screen) → **Ep4 (the other 1993)** → Ep7 (the CAPS LOCK light) → Ep12 (`HOW DO I WIN?`). From this episode on, **intro bar 3's screen rotates 10°**; see [intro-slot.md](intro-slot.md).
- **Guardrails:** no accent humor. Keep the Denny's-to-diner reversal (SYNNED) as a place name only. No family.

## F4.1 · 2003–05 · MAS at DROFNATS · *the tells*
- **Trigger (present):** Feb 10, 2025, high noon on Market Street. Nole's $97.4B bid for the nonprofit. The suggested-replies strip is already open on Mas's phone.
- **We see:**
  1. A 240p dorm room with a poker table, pizza boxes and a camcorder OSD.
  2. Over every player's head, their **tells float as stat bars**: `BLUFF ▰▰▰▱`, `TILT ▰▰▰▰`, `WANTS IT ▰▰▰▰▰`.
  3. Over young Mas's head: **nothing.** An empty bar.
  4. His poker-night beer doesn't ripple.
  5. Caption, in lowercase (lowercase starts in 2003, because poker taught it): poker taught him "how to notice patterns in people over time, how to make decisions with very imperfect information" [K]† (NYT 2023). A 2017 post says the same: "the importance of acting with imperfect information" [K].
- **Why here:** before Mas answers the bid, we see **Nole's tell** as a stat bar over the 2025 Nole: `POST-BUTTON TWITCH ▰▰▰▰▰`. Mas has read him since 2015. The counter-offer (one decimal place over) is a poker raise.
- **No V.O.** The caption and the empty bar say it (the reel's 4.27 line is cut: it sat on the real caption and restated the picture). The two-hander after the tumbleweed turns the read around: Nole reads *him*.
- **Transition in:** a **poker chip spins** off the 2025 bid, and the camera follows it into 2004.
- **Transition out:** the chip lands on Market Street in 2025 as **Nole's bid token**. The tumbleweed rolls.
- **Tier (T2a):** 320×240 at 12 fps, scanlines, chroma bleed, VT323 OSD `▶ PLAY 2004`. A warbling cassette piano.
- **Seasonal payoff:** Ep12's F12.1 finally puts the camera in Mas's chair at THE WOODROSE, where every guest's tell floats and his own reflection shows nothing. Ep10's MAON poker bot shows a tell over Mas's head: `THINKING… 20s`.
- **Guardrails:** no gambling-addiction framing and no drinking gag. The beer is just the cup that doesn't ripple.

## F4.3 · AUG 2008 + APR 2009 · LUAP · *the first sycophant*
- **Trigger (present, second pass):** Apr 25, 2025. The sycophancy update ships, and CHATGTP's reply to Mas opens with a footnote, `[1] (2008)`, underlined like a hyperlink. He clicks it. *Why the move (drama critic):* F4.3 used to sit inside the pause, between the flattery and "roll it back.", and delayed the button. As the trigger it plays first; the audience sees the 2009 beat too long, and then watches him do it again in 2025, with nothing between the pause and the rollback.
- **We see:**
  1. **The blue hyperlink click.** A plain-HTML page loads in a Flash-era web-player inset, complete with a buffering spinner.
  2. **Aug 2008, "A Fundraising Survival Guide":** "You could parachute him into an island full of cannibals and come back in 5 years and he'd be the king." [V]
     - Staging: a **one-palm cartoon island**, or a startup campus, populated only by **hoodie founders with forks and laptops**. Never islanders, never a private estate.
     - Mas parachutes in and is crowned on a **throne of server racks**, echoing intro bar 4.2.
  3. **Apr 2009, "Five Founders":** "What would Sama do?" [V]
     - **Both Mas and SAMA NOS turn**, in the present-day mirror, at the name.
     - Plus: "a few people with such force of will that they're going to get whatever they want." [V] Optional: "the doctrine of the elect applies to startups" [V].
  4. **The 2009 beat too long.** Young Mas reads the essay about himself and pauses for exactly as long as he pauses at CHATGTP in 2025.
- **Why here:** the chatbot isn't the first thing to flatter him. It learned from the essays (the footnote says so without a line). The rollback button is the first time he says no to flattery, and even then he waits.
- **D7 answers it:** back in 2025, the reply finishes ("We couldn't do this without you, Mr. Manalt."), and he pauses exactly as long as he did in 2009. In that pause his reflection in the black monitor smiles one pixel wider than his face.
- **Transition in:** the underlined footnote in the model's reply. Click.
- **Transition out:** the **browser back arrow** returns him to the reply, which finishes loading. Its arrow shape comes back as the rollback: "roll it back." [INVENTED]
- **V.O.:** reel 4.42 ("i barely remember reading it.") just after the 2009 pause, which plays in silence; the line is caught by how long he held it.
- **Tier (T2b):** a web-player inset with plain Verdana-like HTML, blue underlined links, WHY COMBINATOR orange `#FF7F2A` on cream, and a dial-up handshake (0.5s maximum).
- **Guardrails:**
  - The cannibals are always hoodie founders (crit-fair #25, bible).
  - No Epstein-adjacent imagery. The island is cartoon or a campus, per WC-crit §D30.
  - The "Bill Gates at 19" line is **[UNVERIFIED]** (WC-fb §0.11) and **not used**.
