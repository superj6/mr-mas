## Logline
- Mas posts the line the pilot opened with, "near the singularity; unclear which side.", and then everything gets named, priced and relabeled.
- The new President relabels a gulf on his first day and voids his predecessor's AI order while the ink is still wet.
- He dials open a $500B stargate that turns out to run on IOUs, while Nole heckles from the rafters. Mas thanks the President anyway: "we wouldn't be able to do this without you, mr. president."
- A cheap rival lab from across the water tops the app chart ("It's free.") and one flick of its pebble knocks $589B off the man who sells everyone's chips. Mario throws the first China card at him.
- Nole offers $97.4B for the nonprofit. Mas counters with $9.74B for Nole's own website. The board says **not for sale**.
- The chatbot learns to flatter. It hands Mas his own line ("We couldn't do this without you, Mr. Manalt."), and he waits one beat too long before saying "roll it back." [INVENTED]

## Through-line (what a newcomer must be able to retell)

## Themes
- **Money you don't have.** $500B pledged, and the ring runs on IOUs. $97.4B bid, and $9.74B countered. Pledges become the new currency, and the President becomes the exchange.
- **Naming as power.** RUMPT relabels a gulf and voids an order. Nole wants to buy the name he says he gave. Mas renames nothing. He just decides what's "for sale."
- **Mirrors and flattery.** Mas flatters the President; the chatbot flatters Mas with the same sentence. In Tokyo he winks first. The first flatterer was an essay in 2008.
- **The cheap copy.** A free rival trained for a fraction of the cost is accused of copying, and the accusation itself gets copied by everyone.

## THE PLAN (45s blueprint)
**`GATESTAR: HOW TO BUILD A $500 BILLION COMPUTER.`**
- The blueprint shows a power plant, measured in gigawatts, feeding cooling towers, then GPU halls, then a training run.
- Seven chevrons are **seven funding tranches**.
  - Step 1: announce $500B.
  - Step 2: have $500B. This step flickers.
- It explains data centers, gigawatts and "compute" for general viewers, then jams on chevron seven. It is the season's one explanation of why AI needs power (and the first link of THE PLUG).
- **No PLAN ADDENDUM (second pass).** Distillation is defined by the still's labels at #10, a prop instead of a card. *Handoff: Ep8's W8.5 cites "the Ep4 precedent" for its addendum card; that precedent is now a labelled prop.*
