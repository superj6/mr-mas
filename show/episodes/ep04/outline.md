## Logline
- Mas posts the line the pilot opened with, "near the singularity; unclear which side.", and then everything gets named, priced and relabeled.
- The new President relabels a gulf on his first day and voids his predecessor's AI order while the ink is still wet.
- He dials open a $500B stargate that turns out to run on IOUs, while Nole heckles from the rafters.
- A whale no one had heard of flicks one pebble and knocks $589B off the shovel-seller's statue.
- Nole offers $97.4B for the nonprofit. Mas counters with $9.74B for Nole's own website. The board says **not for sale**.
- The chatbot learns to flatter, calls Mas the greatest CEO in history, and he waits one beat too long before saying "roll it back." [INVENTED]

## Themes
- **Money you don't have.** $500B pledged, and the ring runs on IOUs. $97.4B bid, and $9.74B countered. Pledges become the new currency, and the President becomes the exchange.
- **Naming as power.** RUMPT relabels a gulf and voids an order. Nole wants to buy the name he says he gave. Mas renames nothing. He just decides what's "for sale."
- **Mirrors and flattery.** Sama Nos's reflection winks first, and the chatbot tells Mas what he wants to hear. The first flatterer was an essay in 2008.
- **The cheap copy.** A whale trained for a fraction of the cost is accused of copying, and the accusation itself gets copied by everyone.

## THE PLAN (45s blueprint)
**`GATESTAR: HOW TO BUILD A $500 BILLION COMPUTER.`**
- The blueprint shows a power plant, measured in gigawatts, feeding cooling towers, then GPU halls, then a training run.
- Seven chevrons are **seven funding tranches**.
  - Step 1: announce $500B.
  - Step 2: have $500B. This step flickers.
- It explains data centers, gigawatts and "compute" for general viewers, then jams on chevron seven.
- **PLAN ADDENDUM (5s, beat 11):** `DISTILLATION: COPYING ANOTHER AI'S ANSWERS UNTIL YOU BECOME IT.` This is the running gag's one-time definition (crit-com §4).
