# ep12 · Beat sheet — `ep1.11_unclear_which_side.md` · extrapolated (post-Sep 2026) · FINALE

**Tags:**
- **REAL** (with date) marks a verified anchor or quote. These are mostly the flashbacks and echoes; see [facts](facts.md).
- **INVENTED** marks invented staging. That covers the whole present day.
- **ECHO** marks a real quote placed in an invented scene. Echoes never go on dated cards.
- ⟲ marks a flashback; see [flashbacks.md](flashbacks.md).

**Dialogue rule:** real lines appear in "quotation marks" with a [source tag]. Invented dialogue appears as *[INVENTED] italics* with no quotation marks.

**The shape (SEASON-NOTES notes 3, 4 and 6).** The dinner carries the episode. The switch is pressed once and turns, THE PLAN comes back from the pilot, and the vote is the payoff of what Mas wants: Gerg is the one person who asks him. The veto runs straight into PEON AI, the class photo (where DOT gives him the badge), 1993 and one unbroken take to the title. The endings montage plays under the credits. **One machine face:** the monitor at the foot of the table wears THE INTERN's lanyard as its successor, and the LABEL GUN is docked under it. On the page it's "the monitor"; it never gets a card. **V.O.:** two lines, ending on "second time."

**The calendar is the machine's.** In the first frame the machine's cursor selects the rail's `2027` and deletes it. For the rest of the episode every chyron is that empty date field with the cursor blinking in it. Nothing on screen says why; the cursor doing it is the why.

**Sound through-line:** the arrivals ride one cue into the door. After the click, the room plays under the turbines' hum, which becomes the bed through THE PLAN, the vote and the veto (the hold after the veto line is that hum, never dead air). The button (#16) is one take under the room bed; J6 is the season's one designed silence.

## ACT 2 — THE SWITCH

   - Everyone wants it pressed, and nobody wants to be the one who pressed it.
   - MARIO stands at the switch with his position paper: *[INVENTED] Section one of fifteen.* He has added one since the heist.
