## Logline
The model has sent everyone the same invitation: dinner at THE WOODROSE, the room where it all began.
- It wrote the name cards, and the seating chart sorts itself by how much the model learned from each guest.
- The guests stand around a glowing kill switch. Mario reads a position paper, Nole proposes a poll, and Nesnej sells everyone their own button. Nobody presses it.
- The humans finally agree on one thing: fire Mas. The model vetoes it. People prefer a human face, and he's the best at it.
- NOPE AI becomes PEON AI.
- In 1993 the screen finally turns toward us. The kid had typed `HOW DO I WIN?`. Mas types it again, and the screen answers `define "win."`

## Themes
- **The man who can't be fired built the thing that makes firing obsolete.** This is the bible's thesis, paid off literally.
- **"Win" has no definition.** Every character has chased it. The kid's question returns as a request for a spec.
- **Alignment as agreement.** Everyone finally agrees, and that's the trick. Consensus is the one thing the machine was best at producing.
- **The name was always the joke.** OPEN → NOPE → PEON. The show renames its world, RUMPT renames his, and in the end the model renames all of them.
- **Unclear which side.** The Orb verifies that Mas is human, and still can't say which side he's on.
