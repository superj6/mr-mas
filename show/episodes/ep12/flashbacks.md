## F12.1 · JUL 2015 · THE WOODROSE, part 5 of 5 · THE MODEL's POV · "what Mas saw"
- **When:** Jul 2015, the founding dinner [V, Brockman's blog: "Dario Amodei, Chris Olah, Paul Christiano, Ilya Sutskever, Elon Musk, Sam Altman, and others"].
- **POV:** THE MODEL. The four earlier POV rims (Gerg #39FF88, Alyi #FF6A1A, Nole #E0301E, Mario #1F3A93) slide in from the frame's edges and converge on Mas's cyan #3FE6FF.
- **What we see:**
  1. **The rebuild.** The model loads every version the season has shown, like layers in an editing program:
     - Gerg's roster (ep3)
     - Alyi as the prize (ep5)
     - Nole's head-of-table fantasy with the OPEN neon, stamped `NOLE'S VERSION` (ep6)
     - Mario's napkin with the `Addendum:` (ep10)
     - the name cards the model has just written for tonight

     The layers disagree. The model keeps them all.
  2. **The chair.** For the first time the camera settles **into Mas's chair**. Everyone's tell floats over their head as a stat bar, the device from ep4's dorm-poker flashback:
     - Nole's post-button twitch
     - Alyi's hum
     - Mario's hand drifting toward his napkin
     - Gerg's keystrokes
  3. **The reflection.** Mas looks at the window. Over his own reflection there is nothing. The candle flame beside it doesn't flicker.
  4. **The exit.** HALO and THE OTHER PAUL, real 2015 guests, sit where they sat. The place card reads `MARIO (UDIAB) · JOINS 2016`. Mas pockets the CTRL key, the intro gag, now seen from his own hand.
- **Why here:** the seating chart sorts by how much the model learned from each guest, and Mas lands at the head. This flashback shows what the model learned: he read the whole table, and nobody could read him. It motivates the veto (beat 12). The model keeps him because it *is* him.
- **Transition in:** the sorted place cards in the present riffle like a deck and land in 2015 positions.
- **Transition out:** the candle's steady flame match-cuts to the LED candle on tonight's table. It's the map's matching-object rule, candle to candle.
- **Tier:** T3 (pixel), but every surface carries a faint compression shimmer, because this is a *reconstruction*. The proposed 12.A leap assembles the room from points and resolves only the learned objects, held at dense points (see the [outline](outline.md#style-moments-proposed)); every guest stays a sprite, and Mas's glass stays a flat pixel card. Corner tag: `RECONSTRUCTED (FROM 4 VERSIONS)`.
- **Guardrail:** tells are drawn as game-style stat bars only. There is no physical or health-related "tell" imagery.

## F12.3 · MONTAGE (≤6s) · "the tally"
- **When:** 2005–08 (Tpool, twice, `(REPORTED)` [V]), Mar 2019 (YC, "?", `(DISPUTED)`), Nov 17, 2023 (NopeAI [V]), Feb 10, 2025 (Nole's $97.4B bid [V]).
- **What we see:** each carved mark on the dark-room desk plays its moment at 4× in its own era tier:
  - a 240p boardroom door
  - Luap's red and green diff
  - the greyed-out video tile
  - the $97.4B bid token
- **Why here:** the humans vote to fire Mas (THE PLAN's step 4, written at last: `VOTE.`), and every earlier attempt replays as the hands go up. It's a ≤6-s montage inside the vote, not a full stop. Then Gerg's hands stay down, and the monitor vetoes. (The lobby sign's **∞** lands later, as the first credits ending.)
- **Transition:** the tally marks themselves. Each carve-in is a hard cut.

## F12.2 · 1993 · MAS, age 8 · the screen, part 4 of 4
- **When:** `1993`, card only (critic E42). Wikipedia says he got "an Apple Macintosh" at age eight [V]. The model is unconfirmed, so the machine stays beige and logo-free. The menu-bar egg `Thu, Apr 22, 1993` from final.md is **dropped** (see open questions).
- **POV:** the kid. Cyan rim.
- **What we see:**
  - The 1-bit room from ep1: pure black and white at 6 fps, with a juice box that never ripples.
  - The beige computer, whose screen has faced away from us all season, rotates on its own until it faces the camera. In the intro it has been creeping around: 0°, then 10°, then 35°.
  - On screen, in Silkscreen capitals: **`HOW DO I WIN?`**
  - The CAPS LOCK light from ep7 is on. This is why his subtitles have been lowercase all season: the lowercase is a poker face he learned in 2003 (flashback map rule 3).
  - The 1-bit dialog underneath has OK and Cancel. Cancel is greyed out again, echoing ep1's intro and the Blip.
- **Why here (the season revision's order):** the veto has answered every question at the table except the one Gerg asked, and the badge ("second time.", on the class photo's flash) has just closed the pilot's loop. The screen turns, and underneath all of it is the kid's question. The present-day button answers it: Mas types the same words, and the screen replies `define "win."`. The endings montage now plays under the credits, so nothing stands between the veto and this.
- **Transition in:** the curve rewinds to its first pixel, `downgrading… → 1-bit`, the same device as ep1.
- **Transition out:** the kid's typed `?` holds, and one cut returns us to the base, on the badge on Mas's chest. The button (beat 16) plays from there as one take (continuity critic, 2026-09-26: the climb up every tier was a fourth style change in the act's last minutes).
- **Guardrail:** no family. Only the kid and the computer appear. Nothing about his home life is shown.
