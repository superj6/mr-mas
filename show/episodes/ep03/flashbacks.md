## F3.1 · JUL 2015 · THE WOODROSE, part 1 of 5 · GERG's POV · *who was at the table*

**Trigger (present).** Sep 25, 2024: the revolving door has taken Rima and two others, and the class photo has a Rima-shaped hole. Gerg sees it through his laptop's camera on the beach.

**We see:**
1. **The private room at THE WOODROSE**, the intro's `mdinner` set. The camera travels the place cards, which carry the whole roster, per Brockman's own blog [V]: `MAS` · `NOLE` · `ALYI` · **`MARIO (UDIAB) · JOINS 2016`** · `HALO` · `THE OTHER PAUL` · `AND OTHERS`. HALO and THE OTHER PAUL are unlit figures named by their cards alone (no plates).
2. **Gerg says yes** "before dessert" [INVENTED staging; he became the founding CTO, V], as Young Gerg raises a hand without looking up from his laptop. Mas's crystal glass doesn't ripple.
3. *Cut in Draft 3.1:* Gerg's "developer infrastructure…" blog line and Mas's "keep in touch" caption. Two real texts back to back slowed the entry; both stay in [facts.md](facts.md) (row 20, table B).

**The dimming (present over past).** As Gerg talks, the chairs of everyone who has since left drop into the 2-TONE freeze, one at a time:
- Nole: "Loudly."
- Mario and HALO, together, over a faint tour-bus horn (the tease for F3.3)
- THE OTHER PAUL: a lanyard marked `SAFETY INSTITUTE · 2024` [V] on his chair, and "A *government* lanyard."
- Alyi: no topper, a doorway-shaped shadow. Ep2 owns his loss.

Last, the camera finds Gerg's own chair, empty. Mas, the only thing in colour, sips and never freezes.

**Why here.** The founding table is emptying in real time, and this part answers only its own question: who was there. The later parts are Ep5 (who they wanted), Ep6 (who paid, and who named it), Ep10 (who knew the curve) and Ep12 (what Mas saw).

**Transition in.** Keycaps pop off Gerg's beach laptop, and each becomes a candle flame on the Woodrose table.

**Transition out.** The sea breeze blows the last candle out, and its smoke becomes the heat shimmer over his laptop on the sand: "Mine's fine. It's on sabbatical."

**Tier (T3, pixel).** The base engine in the 2015 palette. Frozen memories drop to navy `#14213D` and cream. Music: the ERA TIER chamber cue, the knee motif on brushes, a timpani note on each arrival, the dimming under one sustained chord.

**Guardrails.** Nothing from private conversations beyond the blog's public roster. No spouse. Mario's place card uses the corrected employer (WC-fb §0.2). No Mas V.O. inside Gerg's memory.

## F3.2 · MAR–APR 2012 · MAS · *the last price tag*

**Trigger (present).** Oct 2, 2024: $6.6B at $157B. The spire's odometer, the ticker he tapped like a stuck gauge in August, has just slammed home, and it ticks up one more dollar. His V.O. says "2012 was a good year.", and the odometer takes him there.

**We see:**
1. The odometer runs **backwards** at speed and burns its gears, landing on `$43,400,000` in EARLY-WEB16. *(Draft 3's extra-blocky steps are withdrawn: extra blocky is THE WHALE's medium, and its first use is Ep4's pebble, 4.A.)*
2. **EXT. A PARKING LOT** (the only set). TPOOL's sign is swapped for **TOD NEERG**'s, a prepaid-card company: $43.4M, with $9.8M set aside for retention [V]. Sad confetti and a kazoo, which answer the V.O.; one gift card flutters down face up. LEDGER, 6 frames, on the figure. Rail: `MAR 2012 · TPOOL SOLD FOR $43.4M` [V].
3. **ENIZARDYH CAPITAL** (alt ROCKET FUEL CAPITAL), a fund named after rocket fuel: about $21M, mostly **LEIHT's** check [V]. LEIHT hands it over in the same lot; the check is a loading spinner with the hazard-diamond logo printed on it. Plate: `LEIHT · BACKS HIS FUND` (what he is to Mas; he returns in Ep6 and Ep10). *(The `APR 2012 · MOSTLY HIS CHECK` rail is cut: the plate and the check carry it.)*

**Why here.** *The last time Mas's company had a price, it bought gift cards* (the map's framing, not a quote). The episode's valuation jump plays against a sale most viewers never knew about. Aloud, on the way out: "that one had confetti."

**Transition in.** "2012 was a good year." The odometer shudders and runs backwards.

**Transition out.** The spinner's smoke re-forms as the present-day `$157,000,000,001` ticker in BASE, and it ticks up by one.

**Tier.** EARLY-WEB16: sixteen colours, a GIF-era checker dither, the cyan rim. Music: the odometer's whirr reversing like a rewound tape into the cassette tier's piano, and one sad kazoo for the confetti.

**Guardrails.** LEIHT is the check and the spinner only: no fund office, no second chair (the Hydrazine co-founder is family, guardrails §1), and no Epstein-adjacent imagery (no islands, jets or estates; WC-crit §D30). No New Zealand or bunker material; the prepper list belongs to Ep9–10. No token or pawn on the check (orbit-and-lore §2.1: it's plated by name, and SIMED's chess pawn plays in this episode's sc 16–17).

## F3.3 *(moved from F7.2)* · DEC 2020 → JAN 2021 · MARIO and ADELINA · *the exodus*

**Trigger (present).** Oct 11, 2024. *Machines of Loving Grace* publishes and finishes the bridge. Without a cut, the camera travels back along the planks to the first one, where LUNCHMAS stands on his own August footprints, and under them eleven fainter, older pairs. The camera follows the eleven.

**We see:**
1. **The plank lifts like a page,** and a pop-up book opens: cut-paper hills on hinges. The NopeAI **tour bus** crosses them, hand-lettered `NOPE AI` in a generic band script.
2. **The band breakup.** The bus splits across the middle with a paper tear. The front half keeps driving. The rear half rolls off the other way with **eleven** researchers aboard [V] (not "about 14"; WC-fb §0.9), Mario and ADELINA among them, HALO in the back seat, ADELINA holding the keys. It has no engine and rolls downhill anyway. On the back seat: the **scaling-laws recipe book** [K, Jan 2020]. Someone paints `MISANTHROPIC` over the old name.
3. **The sign.** `DIRECTIONAL DIFFERENCES`, taped to the rear window as an invented prop with no quotation marks (the phrase is reporters' summary, not anyone's statement).
4. **The 2022 pull-tab.** A fuel pump folds up out of the hillside, stickered **`PREVIOUS OWNER: THE OTHER MAS`**, a nod to FTX's $500M (Apr 2022) [V]. The bus fills up without stopping. ADELINA nearly peels the sticker off, and leaves it.

**Why here.** LUNCHMAS thinks he was the first across; he wasn't. It tells a newcomer that Misanthropic was founded by people who left NopeAI, which explains why Mario's and HALO's chairs dimmed in F3.1, and it puts Mario's origin in Ep3, since he's a MAJOR character from Ep1 (critic §E41).

**Transition in.** The eleven pairs run to the first plank's edge, which lifts like a page.

**Transition out.** The pop-up book folds shut, and its spine becomes the bridge's first plank in the present, with LUNCHMAS standing on it: "So I wasn't the first one across." / "You're the first one who read it."

**Style moment (proposed) · style-range 3.D ★ · drastic medium change (tier 2; the band retracts) · paper pop-up book.** *Motivation:* Misanthropic's founders tell their own origin, in their handmade house style (style-range's BRANDS family: paper for its people's words). The episode's one full-frame leap. Filler: 2.5D hinged paper planes; final: scanned card stock.

**Music.** The knee motif on a road-trip guitar, a sighing violin at the split; Mario's ink-blue rim.

**This is the episode's one exit** from Mas's POV, with the writing room before it (≈ 9% of story time): no V.O. inside it, Adelina's "Manalt's fit on a phone." keeps him in mind, and the next shot back is his glass.

**Ep7 callback (in reserve).** Style-range keeps a 2-s return, the book folding shut under the SUPPLY CHAIN RISK stamp (7.E), in reserve.

**Guardrails.**
- No *Sound of Music* or annexation escape: it's WWII-adjacent and would cast NopeAI as the regime (critic §D38).
- Dario's father (health, death) is excluded. The Amodei siblings stay in the professional frame only.
- THE OTHER MAS appears as a sticker only. No prison or trial imagery.
