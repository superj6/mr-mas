## F3.1 · JUL 2015 · THE WOODROSE, part 1 of 5 · GERG's POV · *who was at the table*

**Trigger (present).** On Aug 5–6, 2024, Gerg starts a beach "sabbatical" (still coding) on the same afternoon LUNCHMAS leaves for MISANTHROPIC. Rima quits seven weeks later. Gerg narrates the memory from the beach.

**We see:**
1. **May 2015: Gerg leaves EPIRTS.** His line: "developer infrastructure wasn't the problem that I wanted to work on for the rest of my life." [V] Mas, in lowercase: "we've been thinking about spinning up an AI lab through WHY COMBINATOR. we should keep in touch." [V] (original: "…through YC…")
2. **July: the private room at THE WOODROSE.** Per Brockman's own blog [V], the roster is Mas, Nole, Alyi, Mario (place card **`MARIO (UDIAB) · JOINS 2016`**), HALO, THE OTHER PAUL, "and others."
3. **The question on the table.** They discussed "the state of the field, how far off human-level AI seemed to be, what you might need to get there." [V]
4. **Gerg says yes** (staging [INVENTED]; he became the founding CTO [V]). Mas's crystal glass doesn't ripple.

**The dimming (present over past).** As Gerg talks, the chairs of everyone who has since left dim one at a time: Nole (2018), Mario and HALO (2020–21), THE OTHER PAUL (whose chair holds a lanyard marked `SAFETY INSTITUTE · 2024` [K], an egg that pays off in Ep5), and Alyi (2024). A faint tour-bus horn plays under Mario's and HALO's chairs, which is the tease for F3.3. Last, the camera finds Gerg's own chair: empty.

**Why here.** The founding table is emptying in real time. This is part 1 of the five-part Woodrose escalation: who was there. The later parts are ep5 (who they wanted), ep6 (who paid, and who named it), ep10 (who knew the curve) and ep12 (what Mas saw).

**Transition in.** Keycaps pop off Gerg's beach laptop, and each becomes a candle flame on the Woodrose table.

**Transition out.** The sea breeze blows the last candle out, and its smoke becomes the heat shimmer over his laptop on the sand.

**Tier (T3).** Crisp vector and paper grain. Frozen memories drop to navy `#14213D` and cream. Timpani land on each arrival.

**Guardrails.** Nothing from private conversations beyond the blog's public roster. No spouse. Mario's place card uses the corrected employer (WC-fb §0.2).

## F3.2 · MAR–APR 2012 · MAS · *the last price tag*

**Trigger (present).** Oct 2, 2024: $6.6B at $157B. Talk of going for-profit is in the air, and the NopeAI spire's odometer spins up to `$157,000,000,000`.

**We see:**
1. The odometer runs **backwards** at speed and burns its gears, landing on `$43,400,000`.
2. TPOOL is sold to **TOD NEERG**, a prepaid-card company, for $43.4M, with $9.8M set aside for retention [V]. The "future of location" is sold for gift-card money. Sad confetti falls, and one gift card flutters into frame.
3. Next is **ENIZARDYH CAPITAL** (alt ROCKET FUEL CAPITAL), a fund named after toxic rocket fuel: about $21M, mostly **LEIHT's** check [V]. LEIHT hands it over behind a loading spinner.
4. **The co-founder is offscreen.** He's family, so he stays excluded. The fund's second chair is turned away.

**Why here.** *The last time Mas's company had a price, it bought gift cards* (the map's framing, not a quote). The episode's valuation jump plays against a sale most viewers never knew about.

**Transition in.** The odometer runs backwards.

**Transition out.** The burnt gears smoke, and the smoke re-forms as the present-day `$157B` ticker, which ticks up by one.

**Tier (T2a).** 320×240 at 12 fps with scanlines and a VT323 OSD reading `▶ PLAY MAR 2012`. Tape hiss, a sad kazoo sting and confetti.

**Guardrails.** Keep LEIHT to the check and the spinner; no Epstein-adjacent imagery (no islands, jets or estates; WC-crit §D30). Don't use the New Zealand or bunker material here; the prepper list belongs to ep9–10.

## F3.3 *(moved from F7.2)* · DEC 2020 → JAN 2021 · MARIO and ADELINA · *the exodus*

**Trigger (present).** Oct 11, 2024. *Machines of Loving Grace* publishes, and the bridge is finished. LUNCHMAS's August footprints are already on the planks: he crossed it when it was still draft pages (beat 3). The camera follows the footprints back.

**We see:**
1. **Pop-up-book hills unfold.** The NopeAI **tour bus** is crossing them, painted with a band logo.
2. **The band breakup.** The bus splits down the middle. The front half keeps driving. The rear half rolls off in the other direction carrying **eleven** researchers [V] (not "about 14"; WC-fb §0.9), Mario and ADELINA among them, with HALO in a back seat. On the dashboard sits the **scaling-laws recipe book** [K, Jan 2020].
3. **The press release.** A band-breakup statement cites "directional differences." [V]
4. **2022 insert (fuel stop).** The rear-half bus refuels at a pump stickered **`PREVIOUS OWNER: THE OTHER MAS`**, a nod to FTX's $500M (Apr 2022) [V]. Keep it light, with no prison or trial imagery.

**Why here.** The trigger is LUNCHMAS's Aug 2024 walk to Misanthropic, the critic's chosen trigger. He wasn't the first to cross. The flashback also explains why Mario's and HALO's chairs dimmed in F3.1, and it puts Mario's defection origin in Ep3, since Mario is a MAJOR character from Ep1 (critic §E41 on back-loading).

**Transition in.** Footprints on the bridge planks lead into the cut-paper hills, which unfold like a pop-up book.

**Transition out.** The pop-up book folds shut. Its spine becomes the bridge's first plank in the present, with LUNCHMAS standing on it.

**Tier (T3, pop-up variant).** Cut-paper hills on hinges. POV rim in Mario's ink blue. The knee motif plays on a road-trip guitar, then a sighing violin at the split.

**Ep7 callback.** Ep7 keeps only 2s: the hills fold shut under the SUPPLY CHAIN RISK stamp.

**Guardrails.**
- No *Sound of Music* or annexation escape. It is WWII-adjacent and would cast NopeAI as the regime (critic §D38).
- Dario's father (health/death) is excluded. Keep the Amodei siblings to the professional frame only.
- THE OTHER MAS appears as a sticker only.
