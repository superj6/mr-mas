## Visual gags
- **The corkboard** runs from Coit Tower to a taco truck, and one string ends at Mas's actual garden.
- **Keycaps in the sand**: Gerg's beach towel is a keyboard.
- **The draft-page footbridge**, stamped `DRAFT — DO NOT PUBLISH` on every plank. LUNCHMAS tiptoes.
- **The dimming chairs**, each with its place card:
  - `NOLE`
  - `MARIO (UDIAB) · JOINS 2016`
  - `HALO`
  - `THE OTHER PAUL`, with a lanyard reading `SAFETY INSTITUTE · 2024`
  - `ALYI`
- **The stadium ushers** hand out `$200/mo` bills. They return in SHIPMAS door 1.
- **The shutter**, a steel garage door labeled `THOUGHTS SUMMARIZED FOR YOUR SAFETY`, comes down over the whole field.
- **The revolving door** spins so fast it powers the lobby lights, which flicker every time someone quits.
- **The Rima-shaped hole** in the staff photo, with a tiny cut-out Rima tucked into MACHINES THINKING's photo (foreshadowing Ep4).
- **The iris-scan pledge**: investors line up at the Orb to swear off rivals. The Orb reads each one as `verified: human (committed)`.
- **The odometer** runs backwards so fast its gears catch fire.
- **ENIZARDYH CAPITAL**'s logo is a rocket-fuel hazard diamond.
- **THE BRIDGE**: a scroll unrolled down Market Street and across the Golden Gate. Its last paragraph is a `NOW HIRING` sign.
- **The tour bus** splits on the pop-up hills, and the rear half's band name is hand-lettered over the old one.
- **A PREVIOUS OWNER sticker** on the gas pump.
- **THE PODIUM wheeled up the hill**, still facing away, with the Ep2 `SO SCARY` balloon slack on its string.
- **The EGOD sign**: a dog-shaped neon, flickering. It's DOGE reversed; don't explain it.
- **SKCAS's stacked hats**, AI on the bottom and CRYPTO on top. He keeps swapping them.
- **THE BESTIES' four-mic table** floats past the window like a blimp, with a `BESTIES TAKE:` ticker.
- **The advent calendar**: Mas in a Santa hat, each door pricier than the last.
  - Door 3: a mammoth pulling a sleigh.
  - Door 5: IRIS scoots over on ELPPA's phone.
  - Door 12: the benchmark bar skewers the ceiling, Mario's scroll and SUCRAM's stamp like a kebab.
- **The reflection**: SAMA NOS appears only in THE PODIUM's gold panel, and the reflection winks first.
- **The coin slot**: `$1,000,000`, then *clink*, then the podium turns.
- **The bite**: the desk strawberry has one bite missing in the final frame, and a whale-shaped shadow passes under the ice.
