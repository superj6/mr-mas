## Logline
Mas posts a strawberry, and the whole internet builds a conspiracy board while NopeAI teaches a machine to think before it speaks, then hides the thinking. Behind the fruit, the founding table empties chair by chair: a sabbatical, a defection over a bridge made of Mario's essay, and three executives gone in one afternoon. The company gets a $157B price tag, the first since Mas's last company sold for gift-card money. On the hill, the podium that has been facing away all year finally turns around when a $1M check drops into its slot.

## Themes
- **Thinking out loud versus thinking in private.** The machine learns to reason, and the company learns to hide the reasoning. Mas has always done both.
- **The empty table.** Everyone who sat at THE WOODROSE is leaving, and each thinks they're leaving to build the safe version.
- **The price tag.** $157B for a nonprofit's subsidiary, and $43.4M the last time. You can't sell what you don't own, but you can price it.
- **Coins in the slot.** Access costs $1M, and the podium turns toward whoever pays.

## THE PLAN (45s blueprint)
**`WHY IT CAN'T COUNT THE R'S (AND HOW IT LEARNED TO THINK).`**
- **Tokens:** the word `strawberry` splits into chunks, `str | aw | berry`. The model sees chunks, not letters.
- **Chain of thought:** o1 does scratch-paper reasoning before it answers. More thinking gives better answers, and you pay per thought.
- **Hidden reasoning:** the scratch paper is kept behind a shutter. You get a summary.
- **Step 4:** `SHOW THE THINKING.` It never goes to plan: the shutter drops.

## Tech-as-spectacle set-pieces
1. **The corkboard.** A red-string conspiracy board spans the city; the strawberry seeds are Morse code.
2. **HOW MANY R'S?** A stadium, a nervous kicker, a halftime show of reasoning steps, and the steel shutter.
3. **The revolving door.** It spins so fast it becomes a turbine that powers the lobby lights.
4. **The bridge.** Mario's essay unrolls down Market Street and over the Golden Gate.
5. **SHIPMAS.** A 12-door advent calendar. Door 12's o3 bar breaks the ceiling and pierces Mario's scroll and SUCRAM's stamp.
6. **The podium turns,** with a coin clink.
