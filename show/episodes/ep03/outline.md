## Through-line
Mas wants a number. The only price NopeAI has is an old tender figure on the lobby ticker, `$86B`, and he taps it like a stuck gauge. To move it, he teases a model that thinks before it answers, with a photo of a strawberry. While everyone decodes the fruit, the machine's thinking goes behind a shutter, his own essay can't put a number on superintelligence, and the founding table empties chair by chair. The ticker lands at $157B, a long way from the gift-card money his last company sold for. On the hill, the new President's podium, which has faced away all year, finally turns around, and it isn't his coin that turns it. The man who wanted a number is told to say one.

## Plots

- **The stuck gauge (Aug 5).** In the lobby he taps the ticker, `$86,000,000,000 (TENDER)`. It doesn't move.

## Themes
- **Wanting a number.** A price, a date, a count of R's: he wants all three, gets a summary, a "few thousand days" and "the nonprofit.", and finally meets a man who deals only in numbers.
- **Thinking out loud versus thinking in private.** The machine learns to reason, and the company learns to hide the reasoning. Mas has always done both; the stadium's camera tries to read him and gives up.
- **The price tag.** $157B for a nonprofit's subsidiary, and $43.4M the last time. You can't sell what you don't own, but you can price it.
- **The revolving door.** The nonprofit's door comes off; an industrial one goes in. People out, money in, through the same door, and the door powers everything.
- **The empty table.** Everyone who sat at THE WOODROSE is leaving, and each thinks they're leaving to build the safe version. Gerg keeps coming back.
- **Coins in the slot.** Everyone lines up; the podium turns for the biggest number.

## THE PLAN (45 s blueprint)
**`WHY IT CAN'T COUNT THE R'S (AND HOW IT LEARNED TO THINK).`** It enters through the jumbotron Mas is watching; MAON walks onto it and voices it, in three short lines. It's the one telling of the idea.
- **Tokens:** `strawberry` splits into `st | raw | berry`; `letters seen: 0`. "It reads chunks. Not letters."
- **Chain of thought:** a reasoning model does scratch work first. More thinking gives better answers, and you pay per thought (`$ PER THOUGHT`, which pays off at door 1). "This one thinks first."
- **The shutter:** it hangs over the scratch paper with no label. A Chekhov's shutter: the stadium names it.
- **Step 4:** `SHOW THE THINKING.` It never goes to plan: the blank shutter slams on o1's raised pencil and the clang tears the blueprint.
