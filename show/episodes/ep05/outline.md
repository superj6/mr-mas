## Logline
Mas wins the one room that could slow him down, a Senate hearing where "beat China" ends the talk of pre-approval, while an hourglass nobody explains starts running. Then Kram crosses the street with a $100M check "for your best one" and a thermos of soup, and throws a **Superintelligence Draft Night** with $100M jerseys; Mas counter-programs with a candlelit vigil of monks chanting "missionaries," every one of them checking a rival offer under the robe. At night he finally meets the contractor who badges him in every morning and grades his chatbot. Then GTP-5 launches with a chart shorter than its own number, the router faints, users revolt, and at a dinner he says the word "bubble" himself while the cranes keep lifting.

## Themes
1. **Everyone has a price, and everyone claims a mission.** The monks and the mercenaries are the same people in different outfits.
2. **The cheapest draft wins.** Luap bought a whole batch of founders for less than one of Kram's jerseys, and at the Woodrose the prize took the offer worth less. Price and value have come unglued.
3. **Who gets drafted.** The graders get jerseys; the contractor who grades his chatbot at night gets the soup.
4. **Renaming as value creation.** RUMPT relabels "artificial" as "genius," Kram relabels open as closed, and nothing changes shape underneath.
5. **Every deal has a skim.** The CUT DIAL, the 15% tollbooth, the $1 contracts: somebody takes a coin every time a crate passes.
