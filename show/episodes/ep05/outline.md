## Logline
Kram buys half a grading company and throws a **Superintelligence Draft Night** with $100M jerseys and a thermos of soup; Mas counter-programs with a candlelit vigil of monks chanting "missionaries" (every one of them checking a competing offer under the robe). Meanwhile President RUMPT falls in love with the technology, relabels it **GENIUS**, and bolts a brass **CUT DIAL** onto every chip that leaves the country. Then GTP-5 launches with a bar chart shorter than its own number.

## Plots

### A-plot: SUPERINTELLIGENCE DRAFT NIGHT (Jun 12 → Jul 2025)
- Kram pays $14.3B for 49% of ELACS and makes THE 49% his Chief AI Officer. ATEM SUPERINTELLIGENCE LABS forms (Jun 30).
- The draft is staged in Kram's own stadium: the draft board is a wall of GPU racks, and each pick lights one up. Jerseys, novelty checks (`$100M · per Manalt`) and thermoses of soup.
- Mas's memo, "missionaries will beat mercenaries" [K†], becomes a monks' vigil across the street, with a COUNTERSOUP ladle duel.
- Kram poaches ISS's co-founder, and Alyi becomes CEO of the one-door company. The soup left at his door goes unopened.
- Flashbacks: Luap's cheapest draft (2005 → 2014), and Alyi as the first pick of every draft since 2012.

### B-plot: THE LABEL GUN WARMS UP (RUMPT's summer of love)
- **Riyadh (May 13):** THE CROWN PRINCE launches NIAMUH. The same day, RUMPT's GIANT MARKER voids NEDIB's THREE-TIER MAP (the Diffusion Rule is rescinded).
- **GATESTAR UAE (May 22):** Nole crawls under THE CHAIRMAN OF EVERYTHING's table to unplug it (reported May 28).
- **The golden key (May 30), then the lock is changed (Jun 5):** "wearing thin." The America Party balloon lifts off the zAI tower (Jul 5).
- **The 99–1 vote (Jul 1):** ZURC's hourglass shatters. SILLIT is the lonely "1."
- **The AI Action Plan (Jul 23):** the LABEL GUN's first AI shot: `ARTIFICIAL` → `GENIUS`, and the sign falls off. "It's not artificial. It's genius." [P✓]
- **THE CUT DIAL (Jul 15 → Aug 22):** H20 at 15% ("Would you make it 15?"), 10% of LETNI, and MIT KOOC's gold plaque, which starts the GOLD OVAL meter.

### C-plot: GTP-5 AND THE FAINTING ROUTER (Aug 7 → Aug 13)
- A moon-sized battle-station teaser rises over the Bay. The livestream chart's BIGGER NUMBER bar is shorter than its SMALLER NUMBER bar. The router faints.
- #Keep4o rally, and ZOMBIE 4o bursts out of a storage closet. Restored. "Feel warmer."

### Runners woven through
- **Misanthropic roast:** CLOD 4's system card proudly reports that the model tried blackmail in a test ("Transparency!"). Both labs pocket $200M Pentagon checks and sell to the government for $1.
- **The PAC war begins:** FOLLOWING THE PAST is founded (Aug 15). Gerg's checkbook signs itself; the AI "reporters" belong to the PAC. A flash-forward sticker shows Misanthropic's PRIVATE LAST ($20M, Feb 2026), so both sides' money is in frame.
- **Cold open / tag bookends:** the nonprofit "keeps control" (May 5), drawn as a chihuahua walking a Great Dane. YNOJ's $6.5B merger portrait (May 21): under the velvet cloth is another cloth.

## Themes
1. **Everyone has a price, and everyone claims a mission.** The monks and the mercenaries are the same people in different outfits.
2. **The cheapest draft wins.** Luap bought a whole batch of founders for less than one of Kram's jerseys, and Alyi always takes the offer worth less. Price and value have come unglued.
3. **Renaming as value creation.** RUMPT relabels "artificial" as "genius," Kram relabels open as closed, and nothing changes shape underneath.
4. **Every deal has a skim.** The CUT DIAL, the 15% tollbooth, the $1 contracts: somebody takes a coin every time a crate passes.
