## ACT 2: "SUPERINTELLIGENCE DRAFT NIGHT"
10. **REAL (Jun 12–30), set-piece.** Kram's stadium draft.
    - The draft board is a wall of GPU racks, and each pick lights a rack. Picks walk out in jerseys with their citation counts on the back, holding novelty checks (`$100M · per Manalt`) and thermoses of soup [K]. The ticker reads `POACHED`, not `TRADED`.
    - THE PACKAGE DEAL arrives shrink-wrapped with its own cluster.
    - JERDNA's jersey changes three times mid-walk, and he leaves unsigned.
11. **REAL (Jun 17).** On a podcast, Mas says ATEM offered "$100 million signing bonuses" and that "none of our best people" took them [K†] (paraphrase around the two quoted fragments). The host is never shown (guardrail: family).
12. **REAL (Jun 2025, memo).** Across the street, NopeAI's counter-programming: a candlelit vigil of robed monks chanting "missionaries will beat mercenaries" [K†]. Under every robe glows a phone showing a competing offer. COUNTERSOUP meets Kram mid-street for a ladle duel, thermos against thermos [K].
13. **FLASHBACK F5.1 lands.** Kram slides Mas a $100M check "for your best monk." Luap's plain-HTML overlay types over it: `cost of my first draft: $200k`. Cut to 2005 → 2014 (see [flashbacks.md](flashbacks.md)).
    - **Motivates:** Mas really believes he can't be outbid. He was drafted cheap and became priceless.
    - **Exit:** the ramen crown's bowl dissolves back into Kram's thermos.
14. **REAL (Jul 2025).** Kram poaches ISS's co-founder, and Alyi becomes CEO. A thermos sits outside ISS's single white door. The door does not open.
15. **FLASHBACK F5.2 lands.** Alyi's face appears only as a reflection in a polished GPU heatsink. The fans spin up and we enter 2012 → 2015: the bedroom GPUs, the auction, the Woodrose from Alyi's seat.
    - **Motivates:** why he turns Kram down. He has been the first pick of every draft since 2012, and he always takes the offer worth less.
    - **Exit:** the fans spin down and the door shuts.
16. **THE PLAN (45 s): "RLHF, or why half a grading company costs $14.3B."**
    - The blueprint is an infinite exam hall. Human graders stamp a model's answers 👍/👎, and the stamps pour into a funnel labeled `TASTE`. A model trained on the stamps learns to give answers that earn stamps. *(Setup for the sycophancy/"flattery" thread.)*
    - **It never goes to plan:** mid-diagram, the graders are drafted too. Jerseys drop over the exam hall.
17. **REAL (Jun 3 → Jul 1).** The Senate.
    - ZURC carries a `10-YEAR` hourglass that shrinks to `5` in the NRUBKCALB deal, then shatters on a `99–1` scoreboard. SILLIT sits alone under the `1`.
    - ENEERG (Jun 3) opposes a provision she had already voted for; her card flips both ways.
    - A hairline crack appears in the sky: sudden unity, small dose.
18. **REAL (Jun 3).** DRAWOH KCINTUL's eraser rubs `SAFETY` off THE SAFETY INSTITUTE's sign, which now reads `THE CENTER (FORMERLY SAFETY)`. THE OTHER PAUL stands under it with his lanyard (micro payoff).
