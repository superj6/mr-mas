## F5.1 · "THE CHEAPEST DRAFT"
| Field | |
|---|---|
| **When** | Mar 11 / summer 2005 → Feb 21, 2014 |
| **POV** | LUAP (rim #FF7F2A) |
| **Era tier** | **T2b · Flash-era web**: web-player inset, buffering spinner, plain Verdana HTML, blue underlined links, orange #FF7F2A on cream |
| **Runtime** | ~40 s |
| **Truth label** | none needed; all shown lines are [V] PG/YC text |

**What we see**
1. **Mar 11, 2005.** Two founders walk home from dinner in Harvard Square. LUAP's HTML narration types itself over them: the idea for WHY COMBINATOR. ACISSEJ is framed strictly as co-founder, with no reference to their marriage.
2. **The price tag.** $200k of the founders' own money, seen "as a combination of an educational expense and a charitable donation." [V] The Summer Founders Program appears as a blue link: "an experimental replacement for the conventional summer job." [V]
3. **The first batch (summer 2005).** Teen-looking Mas with TPOOL, the TIDDER kids, and a pallet of ramen. Mas's juice-box-era cup has become a paper cup of water. It doesn't ripple.
4. **The coronation (Feb 21, 2014).** A ramen-bowl crown. LUAP's post types on: "fearsomely effective and yet fundamentally benevolent" [V] and "It took me over a year." [V]

**Why here.** Kram's $100M check, the first offer before Draft Night, is worth more than LUAP's whole first batch, which cost $200k. Mas's "missionaries" certainty isn't only rhetoric: he was the missionary draft, bought cheap and grown in place.

**Transition**
- **In (Jun 12, 2025):** Kram stands at the sidewalk table (the black hole has eaten his chair) with a $100M novelty check and a thermos: *For your best one.* The date in the check's fine print (`2025`) turns into a blue hyperlink, and a click loads the 2005 web-player inset with a buffering spinner.
- **Out:** the ramen crown's bowl dissolves into Kram's soup thermos, now in Mas's hands. Exit object matches entry: bowl → thermos. Mas slides the check back and keeps the soup; Kram: *Keep the soup.* That's Act One's out, and the thermos becomes the episode's travelling prop.
- **V.O.:** none (second pass). The reel's 5.22 line (*they drafted me for ramen. you can't outbid ramen.*) explained the flashback it followed, so it's cut; the soup in his hands says it. The reel's 5.21 line inside the flashback was already cut.

**Guardrails.** ACISSEJ appears as a co-founder only (no spouse framing). No family members in the batch photo. The "Bill Gates at 19" line is [UNVERIFIED] and must not appear (flashback map §0 #11).

## F5.2 · THE WOODROSE, part 2 of 5 ("who they wanted")
| Field | |
|---|---|
| **When** | Jul 2015 (the Woodrose), with a one-frame 2016 insert (the pay stub) |
| **POV** | ALYI (rim #FF6A1A). He is seen only in reflections, per his character rule. |
| **Era tier** | **T3 cut-paper** (frozen guests drop to navy #14213D and cream) |
| **Runtime** | ~20 s (was ~55 s) |
| **Truth labels** | none needed after the trim (the auction and its `RECONSTRUCTED` UDIAB paddle are cut) |

**What we see**
1. **Jul 2015 · THE WOODROSE, from Alyi's seat.** Every chair at the table leans toward him.
2. Nole and an ELGOOG envoy each pull an end of his napkin ("one of the toughest recruiting battles I've ever had" [V], Nole).
3. The napkin tears. One frame: his 2016 pay stub flutters down, `$1.9M` [V], a fraction of the offers he "turned down" [V].
4. **Reveal:** at the founding dinner, Alyi was the prize, and he took the offer worth less.

**Why here.** In July 2025 Kram poaches ISS's co-founder, and Alyi becomes CEO. This flashback explains the thermos at his door going unopened: the most wanted man at the founding dinner picked the seat that paid less. It also recasts the Woodrose: EP03 showed *who* was at the table (Gerg). EP05 shows *who they wanted*. Ep12's F12.1 cites exactly this layer ("Alyi as the prize").

**Why the trim (insider critic, 2026-09-26).** The old F5.2 opened on the 2012 bedroom GPUs and the auction, which made F5.1's "the cheap draft wins" point a second time about three minutes later, and it ran four locations in about 55 s. The Woodrose is the only part the season's thread needs, and it makes a different point ("who they wanted"). Cut: the bedroom (THE BEDROOM GENIUS), the auction (NOTNIH, the UDIAB paddle), and the 3-s Kram insert (the MINDDEEP miss, NUCEL).

**Transition**
- **In:** the thermos sits outside ISS's single white door. Alyi's face appears only as a reflection in the door's polished kickplate; the thermos's steam fogs it, and the fog clears onto 2015.
- **Out:** the napkin tears, the steam clears, and in 2025 ISS's single door closes on the unopened thermos. Exit object matches entry: kickplate reflection → door. The door's white face becomes the white of Mas's monitor, which opens the fold.
- **No V.O.** It's an exit from his POV (told from Alyi's seat), so the reel's 5.26 line stays cut.

**Guardrails.** The Woodrose roster follows Brockman's own account [V]; Mario's place card reads `MARIO (UDIAB) · JOINS 2016` (flashback map §0 #2). No family on screen.
