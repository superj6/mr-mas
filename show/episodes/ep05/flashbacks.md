## F5.1 · "THE CHEAPEST DRAFT"
| Field | |
|---|---|
| **When** | Mar 11 / summer 2005 → Feb 21, 2014 |
| **POV** | LUAP (rim #FF7F2A) |
| **Era tier** | **T2b · Flash-era web**: web-player inset, buffering spinner, plain Verdana HTML, blue underlined links, orange #FF7F2A on cream |
| **Runtime** | ~40 s |
| **Truth label** | none needed; all shown lines are [V] PG/YC text |

**What we see**
1. **Mar 11, 2005.** Two founders walk home from dinner in Harvard Square. LUAP's HTML narration types itself over them: the idea for WHY COMBINATOR. ACISSEJ is framed strictly as co-founder, with no reference to their marriage.
2. **The price tag.** $200k of the founders' own money, seen "as a combination of an educational expense and a charitable donation." [V] The Summer Founders Program appears as a blue link: "an experimental replacement for the conventional summer job." [V]
3. **The first batch (summer 2005).** Teen-looking Mas with TPOOL, the TIDDER kids, and a pallet of ramen. Mas's juice-box-era cup has become a paper cup of water. It doesn't ripple.
4. **The coronation (Feb 21, 2014).** A ramen-bowl crown. LUAP's post types on: "fearsomely effective and yet fundamentally benevolent" [V] and "It took me over a year." [V]

**Why here.** Kram's Draft Night pays $100M for a single researcher (per Manalt), while LUAP's whole first batch cost $200k. Mas's "missionaries" certainty isn't only rhetoric: he was the missionary draft, bought cheap and grown in place. It also sets up Alyi (F5.2): the season's most valuable people were always the ones taking less.

**Transition**
- **In:** Kram slides the $100M novelty check across the street. The date in its fine print (`2025`) turns into a blue hyperlink, and a click loads the 2005 web-player inset with a buffering spinner.
- **Out:** the ramen crown's bowl dissolves into Kram's soup thermos in the present. Exit object matches entry: bowl → thermos.

**Guardrails.** ACISSEJ appears as a co-founder only (no spouse framing). No family members in the batch photo. The "Bill Gates at 19" line is [UNVERIFIED] and must not appear (flashback map §0 #11).

## F5.2 · "THE FIRST DRAFT" + THE WOODROSE, part 2 of 5 ("who they wanted")
| Field | |
|---|---|
| **When** | 2012 → Dec 2012 (auction) → Dec 2013 (Kram insert) → Jul 2015 (Woodrose) |
| **POV** | ALYI (rim #FF6A1A). He is seen only in reflections, per his character rule. |
| **Era tier** | **T2b Flash-era web** (2012–13), crossfading to **T3 cut-paper** (2015 Woodrose; frozen guests drop to navy #14213D and cream) |
| **Runtime** | ~55 s (Kram insert 3 s) |
| **Truth labels** | `RECONSTRUCTED` on the auction room and the UDIAB paddle (both [K]) |

**What we see**
1. **2012 · THE BEDROOM GENIUS.** Two gaming cards whir in a childhood bedroom: AlexNet "trained on 2 Nvidia GTX 580 in Krizhevsky's bedroom at his parents' house" [V]. No parents on screen. Alyi appears as a reflection in the second card's fan shroud.
2. **Dec 2012 · the auction** (`RECONSTRUCTED`). NOTNIH's hotel-room auction, where paddles rise. Per **critic #47**, one paddle reads **UDIAB** (Baidu's bid, [K]), tying Mario's future employer to Alyi's first draft. ELGOOG wins: DNNresearch sells for $44M (Mar 2013) [V].
3. **Dec 2013 · 3 s KRAM insert.** Under a `MOVE FAST AND BREAK THINGS` banner (the internal motto "until 2014" [V]), Kram reaches for a MINDDEEP flag [K] and misses. He hires NUCEL instead (FAIR founded Dec 9, 2013 [V]). This is his first costume.
4. **Jul 2015 · THE WOODROSE, from Alyi's seat.** Every chair at the table leans toward him. Nole and an ELGOOG envoy each pull an end of his napkin ("one of the toughest recruiting battles I've ever had" [V], Nole). Later, the 2016 pay stub reads `$1.9M` [V], a fraction of the offers he "turned down" [V].
5. **Reveal:** at the founding dinner, Alyi was the prize.

**Why here.** In July 2025 Kram poaches ISS's co-founder, and Alyi becomes CEO. This flashback explains his non-reaction to the thermos at his door: he has been the first pick in every draft since 2012, and he always takes the offer worth less. It also recasts the Woodrose: EP03 showed *who* was at the table (Gerg). EP05 shows *who they wanted*.

**Transition**
- **In:** Alyi's reflection on a polished GPU heatsink. The fans spin up to enter.
- **Out:** the 2015 napkin tug-of-war tears, the fans spin down, and in 2025 ISS's single door closes on the unopened thermos. Exit object matches entry: heatsink reflection → door reflection.

**Guardrails.** No family on screen (the bedroom is shown without its owners). The auction staging is `RECONSTRUCTED`. The Woodrose roster follows Brockman's own account [V]; Mario's place card reads `MARIO (UDIAB) · JOINS 2016` (flashback map §0 #2).
