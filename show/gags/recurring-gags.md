## 10. Gag rules

1. **Every setup pays off.** Add a row to §8 the moment a gag has no payoff.
2. **Two layers.** Every gag has a broad version anyone gets and an insider layer for freeze-framers (the Q\* sticky note, the `EXPLOITGYM_ANSWERS` folder).
3. **Real beats keep their tags.** An escalation that quotes the record follows [guardrails §3–4](../bible/guardrails.md#3-fact-handling-tags). Invented escalations must be visibly absurd.
4. **No gag crosses an exclusion**, however good (for example: no black eye on NOLE's golden key, no Butler date on the endorsement, no yacht at OGAL-A-RAM).
5. **Balance.** A gag that roasts one party's officials has a mirror in the same episode (B01, B02, B16, B17 are the built-in mirrors).
6. **Props carry the joke**, not bodies ([props](../world/props.md#prop-rules)).
7. **Budget.** A recurring gag's in-episode appearance should run 5 seconds or less unless it's that episode's set piece. Intro-slot gags follow the intro's text-density cap (about 12 new characters of text per episode).
