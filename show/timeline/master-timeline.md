# MR. MAS: Master Timeline (verified) · 1985 → 2026-09-25

The single chronological fact spine for the writers' room. Everything here is merged and deduplicated from the research dossiers and the worldcast sweeps. The corrections in `worldcast-critic.md` have been applied, and they override the files they correct.

- **What this file is:** real, dated events. Each has a source tag and the episode where it lands.
- **What it is not:** a script. There is **no invented dialogue here.** Every line in quotation marks is a verified or tagged real quote. Invented lines belong in episode files, marked `[INVENTED]`.
- **Related files:** [flashback map](flashback-map.md) · [naming registry](../bible/naming.md) · [guardrails](../bible/guardrails.md) · per-episode `facts.md` (for example [ep01 facts](../episodes/ep01/facts.md))
- **Status:** current to **Thu 2026-09-25 (TODAY)**. Items dated after today are listed in [Scheduled after today](#scheduled-after-today-ep10-anchors).
