## 3. Gaps and proposals

**The gap:** the story of how NopeAI got from a research lab to CHATGTP, 2018–2022, is missing. The map covers the people and the money (the founding, Nole's exit, the capped profit, the landlord, the exodus). It doesn't cover the models that made the race: GTP-1 → 3, Codex and the teaching of manners.

That matters for the season's engine. Viewers should see that scaling worked, which is why everyone raced.

Proposed:
1. **Ep4 (compute, GATESTAR) · NEW F4·GTP, "the staircase" (about 12 s, Mas).** GTP-1 (2018, one reader), GTP-2 (2019, the box), GTP-3 (2020, a waitlist around the block), each a step up a stair made of server racks. Macrosoft's exclusive licence (Sep 2020) is a key on the landlord's ring. It motivates the $500B ring: every step needed ten times the compute.
2. **Ep4 (the sycophancy button) · NEW F4·RLHF, "teaching it manners" (about 8 s).** Jan 2022: rows of raters pressing 👍 on the politest answer. The model learns to please. The flattery in Ep1 ("Brilliant! You're clearly a visionary.") and the Ep4 update that calls Mas the greatest CEO come from the same place.
3. **Ep4 or Ep6 · NEW F·Codex, "the pair-programmer" (about 6 s).** 2021: the model writes code inside Macrosoft's editor, the landlord's first product built on the tenant. It sets up CLOD Code and the vibe-coding boom (Ep4), and THE TRANSFORMER (Ep6).
4. **Ep3 (Elgoog's code red goes to NopeAI's roof later) · MOVED EARLIER, the transformer paper (about 5 s).** Jun 2017: ELGOOG's researchers publish "attention is all you need", and it becomes everyone's engine. This pays off Ep1's "It is ours. We published it." Ep11's heist can still use the lasers.
5. **Optional, Ep3 or Ep6 (the image models, CAMEO CITY):** the first image model (2021) and the second's waitlist (2022), as a 3 s micro.

**Budget check:**
- Ep4 is at about 80 s against a 150 s cap, so the staircase (12 s) and the manners beat (8 s) fit.
- Ep3 is at about 95 s, so the transformer micro (5 s) fits.
- Ep1 goes from about 0 to about 19 s (THE WOODROSE and TPOOL), inside its ≤20 s budget.
