## 1. The hero sets

### THE DARK ROOM
*Mas's room. The show starts and ends here every episode.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Real counterpart** | None (invented). |
| **First ep / recurs** | 1 (intro cold open) · every episode's intro and bookend; in-episode for Mas's private beats |
| **Look** | Black (#05070A), lit only by a monitor (cyan #3FE6FF). A post composer over a log chart with a `you are here` dot. Three rack LEDs blink on eighth notes. THE ORB hovers at his shoulder. For 5 frames the Orb's scan reveals the "room" is an **endless data-center cathedral**. |
| **Set dressing** | The **firing tally** carved into the desk. A **coat hook** that gains one popped collar per episode. His **water glass**. A **closet door stenciled `2016`** (from Ep9). |
| **Season states** | The `you are here` dot moves up the curve one notch per episode: past the knee by Ep9, at the top edge in Ep10, off the top with `you are ↑` in Ep11, and in Ep12 the axes rescale. **Ep12:** the chair is empty, and the cursor types the line alone and adds `ours.` Mas walks in one beat late with a coffee. |
| **Rules** | It's never his home. It stays abstract, with no private space implied (guardrail X1). |

## 3. The skyline and the company towers

### THE SKYLINE
*An isometric dusk panorama. The intro's bars 10–11 and every episode's establishing shots.*

- **Look:** a dusk gradient (#0E6E7E → #C13C8E → navy), with windows in #FFD27A. Each tower pops with its boss on the roof. Then all the rooftops ignite into **one cyan line**: flat across the incumbents, steeper at the exes, vertical up NopeAI's spire.
- **Per-episode skyline changes** live in the [intro](../intro/) slot table. Highlights: the courthouse between zAI and NopeAI (Ep2), the whale breaching and INVIDIA sinking 17% (Ep4), a battle station rising (Ep5, a generic design), GATESTARs multiplying (Ep6), an AROS tombstone (Ep7), gargoyles becoming goblins (Ep8), the UN dome and PACE banners (Ep9), and in Ep12 a skyline that redraws itself every frame.
- **THE PODIUM on the hill:** a small gold podium on a far-left hill, **from Ep3**, outside the tower sequence. Ep3: a dark silhouette with a coin-slot glint and one coin clink. Ep4: lit gold, wired to GATESTAR. Ep5: `GENIUS` label. Ep6: a receipt curl. Ep7: an HTURT meteor streaks to the lighthouse. Ep8: THE COUNTERPART's mirrored podium appears across the water. Ep9: it relabels the UN dome `AI` → `SI`. Ep12: its plaque reads `USER`.
- **The Bay:** the frozen bay with a whale-shaped shadow gliding under it (Ep3 button). The Bay Bridge, where the exit NDA unrolls (Ep2). In Ep11 the Bay dims in rhythm with THE POLITENESS LOOP.

## 11. Location rules

1. **Real public places stay real; private venues, clubs and brands get parody names** ([naming](../bible/naming.md)).
2. **No private homes**, for anyone. No residence, bedroom or family space for any real adult. The 1993 room is an abstract kid-and-computer set.
3. **No island, private-jet or yacht-arrival imagery** near SUMMERS, SETAG, DIRE, LEIHT, NOLE or RUMPT (X8). Air Force One appears only as a state aircraft, exterior and in transit; no cabin or party imagery.
4. **Military sites are paperwork sets.** No bases, hardware, maps or operations (X6).
5. **Summit placement follows verified attendance.**
6. **Generic, never branded interiors**: keynote stages, studios and UIs are original designs.
7. **Every flashback set has an entry and exit object** that matches a present-day object (napkin to napkin, box to box, receipt to check).
