## Prop rules

1. **Props carry the caricature.** When a joke is about a person, it goes through their prop, not their body.
2. **Generic designs for anything franchise-shaped** (ring, robot, battle station, vacuum, fight club, ripple glass).
3. **Real numbers on props carry tags.** Invented numbers are visibly absurd (`15% OF ZERO`, `∞`).
4. **Weapons are never drawn.** The chainsaw cuts paper, the stamp is paperwork, and the prepper list is black-barred.
5. **No Epstein-adjacent props** (islands, files, jets, yacht arrivals) near SUMMERS, SETAG, DIRE, LEIHT, NOLE or RUMPT.
6. **Health-imagery ban.** No heart monitors, pills, IV lines on people, or hospital props. RIEK/YDNA's AI drip is a policy metaphor on a pole beside the PM and is **never connected to a person**.
7. **Parity.** When an administration gets a prop, the other side gets one of similar weight.
8. **Every prop that escalates has a payoff**, logged in [recurring gags](../gags/recurring-gags.md).
