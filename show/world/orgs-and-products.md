## 1. Parody-mark rules

1. **The wordmark is the parody name** set in a show font. Never the real logotype.
2. **No real logo shapes or trade dress.** No bitten fruit, no bird, no smile-arrow, no four-color letter, no knot or blossom, no real icon silhouettes. When a real mark is iconic, the parody replaces it with the **story gag** (a lighthouse of essays, a whale water tower, a key ring).
3. **Off-brand colors.** Every parody color is shifted away from the real brand (for example MISANTHROPIC is brick #B8573A, not the real terracotta; WHY COMBINATOR is #FF7F2A on #F3EEDC, not the real orange).
4. **Generic designs for anything franchise-shaped**: the ring, the transforming robot, the battle station, the ghost vacuum, the fight-club speech.
5. **No real UI.** Chat windows, post composers and dashboards are original designs.
6. **Model numbers stay** (H20, H200, o1, o3). They're specs, drawn on generic crates or badges.
