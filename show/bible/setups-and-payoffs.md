## 1. Why Mas was fired

**The record:**
- The board said only that he was "not consistently candid" and wouldn't elaborate.
- **Mar 2024:** an outside review found "a breakdown in trust", said his conduct didn't mandate removal, and he rejoined the board.
- **May 2024:** Toner's podcast gave her account. The board learned about ChatGPT on Twitter, among other claims.
- **Later:** a sealed memo surfaced in litigation.

Every specific reason is contested. The guardrails keep the show from asserting one, and the memo's contents stay out.

**So Ep1 withholds the why by design,** and the told-twice structure plays that withholding as the shock. What's weak is that the withholding isn't felt as a question the show will answer. Right now the plants don't connect.

| In Ep1 now | Status |
|---|---|
| Rima on launch night: "Did anyone tell the rest of the board?" (Gerg deflects; Mas's silent face) | HINTED: the launch without the board |
| Neleh's paper, p.30: "frantic corner-cutting" (his held face) | HINTED: a board member publicly critical |
| The post: "not consistently candid" | EXPLAINED: the stated reason, verbatim |
| Rima: "We'll share more soon." / "Will we?" / "We'll say we will." · Ttemme: "I need to know why you fired him." and the blank page · "an independent review" / "of what?" / "Good question." | HINTED: the running joke that nobody will say |

**Where it's answered:**
- **Ep2:** the Mar 8 review (planned, split rail).
- **Ep2 (at risk):** Neleh's podcast, "learned about CHATGTP from RETTIWT". It's planned in `characters/neleh.md`, but **the Ep2 outline has Neleh as "eggs only"**, so the payoff to Rima's launch-night question is currently lost. **GAP.**
- **Ep8:** the trial shows the memo exists; its contents stay out.

**Proposed:**
1. **Ep1: make the question felt, not answered.** On the board's side, the desk Neleh preps at holds two things, with no line about them:
   - a printout of Mas's launch-night post (the Dec 2022 RETTIWT post), and
   - her paper open to p.30.

   A viewer who remembers Rima's question connects them; one who doesn't just sees a board member's evidence file. It's her documented view, shown as her prop, not the show's verdict.
2. **Ep2: restore Neleh's podcast** as the payoff. It's the first time anyone on the board says what they thought, and it lands on Rima's Ep1 question.
3. **Keep** the review's split rail in Ep2, and the sealed memo out.
