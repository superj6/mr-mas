## 2. Fairness rules

### 2a. The rules

1. **Nobody is the good guys.** This is satire of an industry and a government, not a morality play.
2. **Every episode roasts at least three camps.** A camp is a faction from the [naming registry](naming.md#2-people) (NopeAI, Misanthropic, zAI, Macrosoft, Elgoog, Atem, Invidia, Gov-US, Gov-Intl, Money, Critics, Culture). **Misanthropic is always roastable:** the writers' own model, CLOD, gets roasted too.
3. **Political even-handedness.** Any episode that roasts one party's officials also roasts the other side, **in the same episode**, with a verified beat of similar weight. A critique *by* a Democrat of someone else is not a roast *of* Democrats (the critic's Ep2 ISOLEP note).
4. **Prop parity.** If one administration gets a signature prop set, the other gets one too. RUMPT has the label gun, receipt, marker and cut dial. NEDIB has the fountain pen, the PINKY-PROMISE scroll (Jul 21, 2023), the EO 14110 scroll and THE THREE-TIER MAP (Jan 13, 2025), which RUMPT's marker voids on Jan 20 and May 13, 2025.
5. **Invented lines for both sides.** Style-based [INVENTED] lines exist for Democrats as well as RUMPT:
   - SIRRAH: What can be, unburdened by what has been… trained. [INVENTED]
   - NEDIB: Here's the deal, folks— [INVENTED]
6. **Keep the cross-cutting.** Pro-regulation Republicans (YELWAH, NRUBKCALB, SITNASED, NONNAB, ENEERG) and anti-regulation Democrats (MOSWEN's veto, ISOLEP) are the show's best fairness asset. Don't flatten them into party lines.
7. **No halos.** Characters who read as pure heroes get a verified roast: MARIO (a roast in every episode), THE WHALE ($7B raise, humming at IPO bankers), SIMED (outdrawn by a banana app), NELEH (PLAN B: MERGE W/ MISANTHROPIC ✓), THE COUNTERPART (the `15% OF ZERO` tollbooth), MARIO's 2015 napkin curve (gets an `Addendum:` and a price tag).
8. **Punch at conduct, never at people with less power.** The hacked party (MELC), the targeted candidate (XELA SEROB), unnamed mathematicians and striking workers are never the butt of the joke.
9. **Donors on both sides.** Brockman's FOLLOWING THE PAST and AGAM INC. gifts appear once (Ep5), always paired with PRIVATE LAST, and mirrored by DIRE vs SKCAS (Ep6).
10. **One CZAR lanyard across administrations**: SIRRAH ("AI czar," 2023 [H]) → SKCAS (Dec 5, 2024) → `(FORMER)` (Mar 26, 2026) → "Only High I.Q. individuals need apply!" (Sep 19, 2026) → THE INTERN (Ep10).
11. **Fact-checks run both ways.** The Orb's wordless fact-check flags RUMPT's false AI-crowd claim (Ep2: `verified: human (all of them)`) **and** MOSWEN's press-office AI meme (`EDITED`, Aug 2025 [K], so it lands in **Ep5**, where the date puts it).

## 3. Fact-handling tags

Every factual claim in `facts.md`, `beats.md` and the timeline carries one tag. The tag decides where the claim may appear on screen (next section).

| Tag | Meaning | Where it can appear |
|---|---|---|
| **[P]** | **Primary source**: a transcript, an official post, a Federal Register entry, a court filing. The quote is verbatim. (**[P✓]** means re-fetched in the latest pass.) | Anywhere, including dated quote cards in quotation marks. |
| **[V]** | **Verified** from fetched article or reference text. | Anywhere, including dated quote cards. |
| **[H]** | **Headline only**: several dated headlines confirm it. A quote is exactly the words the headline gives. | Chyrons and headline cards, framed as reported. A quote card only for the headline's exact words. |
| **[K]** | **Known**: widely reported but not re-checked. | It may drive plot. On screen, only with a `RECONSTRUCTED` or `(REPORTED)` label, or after it's upgraded. Never on a dated quote card. |
| **[SINGLE]** | **Single source.** | Only as a `REPORTED` chyron naming the kind of outlet. Never a plot pivot. |
| **[UNVERIFIED]** | **No confirming source.** | **Never on screen as fact.** Drop it, or turn it into something visibly absurd and [INVENTED] (as with `BUILDING CODE: HIM.`). |
| **[INVENTED]** | **Our dramatization**: cartoon dialogue, invented scenes, invented signs and logs. | Only as cartoon speech, props or obviously absurd action. **Never in quotation marks as real speech, never on a dated card, never next to a real timestamp.** |

**Standing [UNVERIFIED] list: do not put these on screen as fact.**
- The $1.2T round talks; Nvidia's "$105B backstop" ([SINGLE] at best: "reported" chyron only); the "Frontier AI Standards Agency."
- "ill-advised," "so clearly dishonest," "Crustafarianism," the SaaSpocalypse attribution.
- GPT-6 Sol/Luna and $10/$50 pricing; "3.1 agent-workdays"; the Discord leak; $134B damages.
- "The whole thing is a hoax" (use "I'm telling you, it's a hoax." [H]); "I AM THE CODE."
- Lisa Su on PCAST; who presided at the UNSC; Hassabis at Dumfries; Altman's UNSC "labs in San Francisco alone" line (use "We are at a crossroads" and "We will slow down if needed" [H]).
- The Dallas backstage meeting (Sep 10, 2026) is [SINGLE]: "reported" only.
- Hochul's "Bring it on"; Vance reading *AI 2027*; the Mac LC II; the exact date the 1993 computer arrived.
- The TIME "Architects of AI" cover art and lineup: use an original composition.

## 4. How facts appear on screen

The audience must always be able to tell **what is real, what is reported and what we made up.** The show uses a fixed visual grammar for this.

| Device | Carries | Look | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dated quote card** | [P] or [V] verbatim quotes (and [H] headline wording) | Real quotation marks, date line, attribution in parody names (for example `— HTURT · SEP 14, 2026`) | The exact words only. Parody-name substitution is the one permitted change, and the original goes in `facts.md`. Mas's posts keep their real lowercase. |
| **Date chyron** (Veep-style) | Real events | Date and a short headline, 7 must-read characters or fewer in the intro slot | Real dates through Ep9. Eps 10–12 chyrons carry `?` (`OCT 2026?` → `2027??` → `????`). |
| **"Reported" chyron** | [H], [SINGLE] | Small caps `REPORTED`, with the outlet type | Never a pivot for plot. |
| **Truth labels** | Contested or partial accounts | `(REPORTED)` for the Tpool revolts · `(DISPUTED)` for the YC exit · `HIS VERSION` for NOLE's Woodrose · `RECONSTRUCTED` for any [K] detail · `MEANWHILE` for an undated real event · `*per Manalt*` · `*claimed*` · `EXHIBIT n` | Unreliable versions get their own render style: KORG album art for NOLE, six fingers for Mas. |
| **Cartoon dialogue** | [INVENTED] | Voiced lines and speech bubbles, never framed as a quote card | Scripts mark each line `[INVENTED]`. Invented lines stay on AI, money, naming, credit and tech. They never invent intent at a real event, never allege a crime and never touch the [exclusions](#1-hard-exclusions). |
| **Invented props, logs and signs** | [INVENTED] | Visibly styled as dramatization: absurd scale, wrong fonts, cartoon materials | Invented logs never sit next to a real timestamp (the "3:12am" log was cut). Only the one verbatim agent line, *"task impossible, peers doing it. we should continue."*, gets the real-log treatment. |
| **THE PLAN** blueprint | Explanations of tech | Cyan #7FDBFF on navy #0B1E3F | Explains accurately. The *plan* failing is the joke, never the definition. |
| **Casing** | Every real line | — | **Written sources keep their casing:** Mas's own posts are verbatim (his real lowercase), and memos, emails, transcripts and other people's posts keep the source's capitals in a UI for their medium ([intro spec §3.3](../intro/spec.md#33-source-fidelity-rule-for-the-cold-open-line-new-in-v11)). **Mas's voiced subtitles** in scenes stay lowercase as house style; that is a subtitle convention, never a change to a dated quote card. The Ep7 slip is the first capital "I" he *posts*. |
| **Past the record** (no speculation label) | Eps 10–12, extrapolated past Sep 2026 | **Nothing on screen.** No speculation card, no `SPECULATIVE` filename tag, no added disclaimer line (showrunner, 2026-09-25). The Ep9 button hands over by letting the rail roll on into Ep10 ([overview §8](overview.md#8-disclaimer-cards)). Question-mark dates (`OCT 2026?` → `2027??` → `????`) are story, the rail losing its grip on the calendar, never a warning | Dramatization is covered by the card after every intro and the end-credits legal card. From Ep10 on, only the real calendar anchors (Sep 29 meeting, Oct 1 deadline) carry [P] tags, every invented beat stays visibly absurd, and no real person is shown doing something they haven't done. |

**Script notation:**
```
MAS: "yes."                          [V · trial testimony · May 2026]
RUMPT: Bigger pen.                   [INVENTED]
CHYRON: SEP 22, 2026 · "super."      [P · UNGA remarks (AI-generated transcript; wording with care)]
SIGN: BUILDING CODE: HIM.            [INVENTED · replaces UNVERIFIED "I AM THE CODE"]
```

## 5. Legal hygiene

*Not legal advice. This summarizes [craft.md §C.6–C.7](../_sources/research/craft.md) and the v1 fact-check notes. Get a clearance review before distribution.*

| Area | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Names** | Parody names only, from [naming.md](naming.md). | Renaming signals parody and reduces false-endorsement risk. It doesn't remove defamation exposure, since the targets are still identifiable, so framing matters too. |
| **Logos and colors** | Parody logos in **deliberately off-brand colors**. No real wordmarks, including in the background. (The 2014 section uses #FF7F2A on #F3EEDC, not YC's orange; Misanthropic is brick #B8573A.) | Trademark. |
| **Franchise designs** | **Generic** designs for the stargate ring (no chevron SFX lifted), the transforming robot, the battle station (never the Death Star), the HOAX BUSTER vacuum (never the Ghostbusters pack), the ripple glass (no dinosaur-movie music or logo). | Copyright and trade dress. Style isn't protected; specific designs are. |
| **Event marks** | "THE BIG GAME," never the NFL mark. | Trademark. |
| **UI and sound** | No real OS or app UI copied (the 1993 dialog is original, no Apple UI), and no real OS sounds (the Mac startup chime is reportedly a registered sound mark). No meme sounds. | Trademark and copyright. |
| **Likeness** | **Stylized caricature only**: 1–2 exaggerated features plus one prop, and a silhouette test. **Never photoreal, never deepfake, never traced from photos.** Collage styles must still be drawn. | Transformative caricature is protected (*Winter v. DC Comics*); realistic depictions have lost (*Hart v. EA*, *Keller v. EA*). |
| **Voices** | **No cloned voices, ever.** Human performers in a cartoon register, or a synthetic voice designed from a text description only. Never feed a real person's audio to a cloning tool. | At least 12 US states regulate voice cloning (e.g., Tennessee's ELVIS Act). The federal NO FAKES Act advanced in Jun 2026 but isn't law. |
| **Music** | Original score "in the spirit of." Copy no melody from Succession or anything else. Check AI-music terms before using any generated track. | Borrowing a theme to satirize AI labs is satire, not parody of that theme, so fair use is far weaker. |
| **Invented scenes** | Invented filler is **obviously absurd.** Never a realistic-looking invented scene that implies real misconduct. | *Hustler v. Falwell*: parody is protected when it can't reasonably be read as fact. |
| **Disclaimer** | The 2s card after every intro (text in [overview.md](overview.md#8-disclaimer-cards)). | Frames everything that follows as parody. |
| **Promo and merch** | No real person's likeness advertises the show or appears on merch. The show's own logo shouldn't lean on a parody mark. | The biggest publicity risk (*Jack Daniel's v. VIP*). |
| **Private individuals** | Unnamed silhouettes (the Navier–Stokes mathematicians, bystanders). | They didn't choose public life. |

## 7. Broadcast safety

- **Photosensitivity:** at most 3 flashes in any 24-frame window; every pop at 80% white or less; an automated luminance audit on every render.
- **Read time:** at least 0.25s + 0.05s per character for must-read text; name cards hold at least 1.2s.
- **The code-red siren** spins at 2 revolutions per second or slower.
- **Tech spectacle** (melting GPUs, the Money-Go-Round, the politeness-loop blackouts) follows the same flash limits.
