See `production/QUEUE-season-revision.md`, "Showrunner note on adjacent figures and AI/EA lore".

**The rule for every item in this file.** Include it only where it's natural. It is never a checklist. Every beat does story work and can name its job:
- **an obstacle**: it's in someone's way;
- **a mirror of Mas**;
- **a consequence** someone has to live with; or
- **a laugh that lands without a footnote.**

A beat that can't name its job doesn't go in. Inside-baseball either reads cold for a general viewer or stays in the background as an egg, and is labelled as an egg.

**Precedence:** [guardrails](guardrails.md), then the POV contract ([pov-clarification](pov-clarification.md), [pov-and-framing](pov-and-framing.md)), then [tone-and-dialogue](tone-and-dialogue.md), then [world-stakes](world-stakes.md), then this file. Each episode's `beats.md` still decides what happens.

**How to read the references:**
- `#n` is a beat in the episode's `beats.md`, and `sc n` is a script scene (Eps 1–3).
- `Wn.n` is a world-stakes insertion, and `PPn.n` a public-pressure one.
- `On.n` is an id in this menu. Ids are kept from the placement map wherever the slot is the same, so the two files cross-reference. Cut ids are kept in [§4](#4-on-file-not-used) so nobody reuses them by accident.
- **Tags** follow [guardrails §3](guardrails.md#3-fact-handling-tags). Two local tags:
  - **[H✓]** is [H] (headline only) re-seen in this pass. It is still [H] for on-screen use.
  - **[V·wiki]** means the fact was seen only through Wikipedia. This doc reads it strictly, as [H], the way the security dossier does; that **overrides** the EA dossier's looser "treat as [V]". A [V·wiki] fact never goes on a dated card or rail until its primary source is opened before lock. If the primary can't be opened and a beat truly needs it, the file owner may carry it as a `(REPORTED)` chyron; no beat in this menu needs that.
- **Coverage limit:** the web-search budget was spent before these passes. NYT, WSJ, FT, CNBC, Reuters and X refused the fetcher in at least one pass. This revision re-checked headlines through Google News listings and opened a few pages directly. [§6](#6-hand-off-checklist) lists what to re-verify.

## 1. Budget

### 1.1 The rule

- **Season: 20 at most.** The earlier 12–20 range stays as a guide for a menu, not a target. This menu proposes **11 core and no optionals**, plus one swap.
- **Per episode: 0–2.** An episode may carry 3 only with a reason stated here. None does.
- **What counts.** A zero-second egg still counts as a beat: it costs a board artist's attention and a viewer's. An icon or element **added** to an existing prop counts. A **swap** that replaces an existing element one for one doesn't add a beat, and it's listed separately.
- **Seconds are paid in the same episode, and never twice.** A trim another insertion has already spent can't pay again: W7.1 has already cut Ep7 #10 (20 → 17 s), and W9.1 and W9.2 have already spent Ep9 #13's trim (30 → 22 s).
- **Where an orbit beat shares a scene with a world-stakes insertion,** it replaces that insertion's element or yields to it:
  - O9.1 replaces W9.2's apology slip, and falls if W9.2 isn't taken.
  - O11.1 needs #6 to keep "government compute" (see W11.3 in [§6](#6-hand-off-checklist)).
  - O6.4 shares Ep6 #11 with W6.4, and O11.3 shares Ep11 #11 with W11.1. Both orbit beats cost 0 s.
- **Kept separate from the other caps.** Nothing here uses DOT (about 5–6 beats a season) or plays THE CHINA CARD (capped at about 3). THE SITUATIONIST's essay is a verified China citer, and it still gets no card.
