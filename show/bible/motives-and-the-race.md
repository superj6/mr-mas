# Motives and the race (season plan, draft for the showrunner)

> **Status: BRAINSTORM, 2026-09-28.** The lead's proposal for the showrunner to pick from. Nothing here has been built.
>
> **Showrunner, on the v3.3/v3.4 films:**
> - "several are shown working on ai's (dario, elon, indirectly jensen) but for none of the people including mas it is never shown why they're doing that… we don't need everyone to be fully fleshed out first episode (we do throughout the season), but mas at least should have a bit more. compare to what other related shows do"
> - "i thought we would have some brief flashback for this. also why was the loopt flashback taken out"
> - "think in context throughout the entire season. the first episode is to introduce the ai race, sam's ability to play the game, and a first glimpse at some of the other key people he'll have to interact and compete with to various degrees"

## 1. What Ep1 has to do

Ep1 has three jobs:
1. Introduce the race and what it's for.
2. Show that Mas plays the game better than anyone.
3. Give a first look at the players he'll deal with all season.

Motives are planted in Ep1 and paid off over the season. Every major player gets **one want, shown through one act**. Mas gets a stated motive plus a glimpse of the real one.

## 2. How comparable shows do it

| Show | In the pilot | Held back |
|---|---|---|
| **Succession** | The prize (who succeeds Logan) is named in the first act. Each sibling shows a want through what they ask him for. | Why each one needs him, over four seasons |
| **Game of Thrones** | An ensemble race: every house gets one scene showing what it wants and what it would do for it (the Lannisters and the window). | Their histories |
| **House of Cards** | Frank is passed over in the first minutes; everything after is his plan. The motive is an injury, shown, not told. | How far he'll go |
| **Breaking Bad** | A stated motive (money for his family). The real one (pride, the company he left for $5,000) is planted in a short early scene. | Paid off only in the finale: "I did it for me." |
| **The Social Network** | The first scene (the breakup) gives the why: wanting in. Rivals' motives come through the depositions. | — |
| **The Dropout, Super Pumped** (real founders) | A short early flashback that recurs and deepens ("what do you want to be?"). | The cost |
| **Mr. Robot** | One flashback and the voice give the why (his father, E Corp). | The deeper layer, for seasons |

**The pattern across these:**
- The prize is clear.
- The protagonist has a stated motive and a glimpse of the real one, often a flashback of a few seconds.
- Every rival shows one want through an action. Their reasons are deepened later.
- Nobody explains a motive in dialogue.

## 3. Mas in Ep1

1. **The stated motive, in his real words.** This is already in the film: "i love my current job." and "i have no equity in nopeai." (the Senate). Neleh's "And the CEO? What does he own?" / "Good question." makes it the season's question: if not money, what?
2. **What they started it for: THE WOODROSE, 2015** (about 10 s; the showrunner: "i don't want flashback to 1993. i was thinking more like earlier in the founding of openai they state they're after agi or something").
   - **The trigger:** on launch night, Alyi's "Six years and eleven months. From the day we started to that click." cuts to the founding dinner, in the intro's cut-paper amber and teal.
   - **They say what they're after,** each in their own terms, close to the public record:
     - The goal is AGI, for everyone, run as a nonprofit (the 2015 announcement and the Charter).
     - NOLE: a counterweight to Elgoog, his stated reason.
     - ALYI: he's the one they're taking from Elgoog ("Not everyone.").
     - GERG: he'll quit his job Monday.
   - **Mas frames the race:** "how far behind are we?" While the others talk, he folds Gerg's napkin (`AGI · FOR EVERYONE · NONPROFIT`) into his pocket. The one who ends up holding the plan. No V.O.
   - **This plants three things:** the prize (AGI), each founder's first want, and a season payoff. The napkin comes out again when the nonprofit turns into something else (Ep5–6, THE TRANSFORMER). THE WOODROSE's later parts (Ep6 for Nole, Ep12 in Mas's chair) keep deepening the same dinner.
   - **Cut:** the 1993 glimpse. 1993 stays in the intro only; the season's `HOW DO I WIN?` reveal is unaffected.
3. **Where his skill comes from: TPOOL** (F1.2, about 8–10 s, rebuilt so it can be read).
   - After the firing, at the tally marks ("i don't keep score."): 2008, TPOOL, two popped collars, a closed door, staff silhouettes. He walks out still CEO. Back to the two marks.
   - This makes the firing not the first time. On his return, "they had four votes. i had the landlord. the money. gerg." reads as the lesson he learned there: make yourself the piece everything depends on.
   - It's reported, not established, so keep it to silhouettes, with at most one pointer if it's unclear.
   - **Why it was cut:** v3 built it as a 3.5 s flash of two silhouettes. The newcomer couldn't place it, and the mood check said to keep it only if a newcomer could. v3.1 cut it instead of lengthening it, which was the wrong call for the season plan.
4. **The prize, said once, in conflict.** Why winning matters: whoever gets there first sets the terms for everyone after. Mario's race memo and Alyi's "And what if it wakes up?" can carry this, as can the planner voice's "mine's half written.".

## 5. Across the season: what each one hopes to achieve

> **Showrunner:** "throughout the season we'll want all key characters motivations fleshed out further and what they hope to achieve, tho still with mas in the forefront"

**Rule:** each episode deepens one or two rivals' reasons through an act, a flashback or a scheme of their own that works (see overview §6a, "Everyone schemes"). Mas's thread runs through every episode.

| Player | What they say they want | What they hope to achieve | How it deepens |
|---|---|---|---|
| **MAS** (the forefront) | To be useful; "i love my current job." | To win: be the one who builds the thing that answers everything, and the human face of it. What he never says is that he wants to be asked, chosen and not just survived. | Ep1: the 1993 glimpse and TPOOL · Ep3: TPOOL's price tag ($43.4M, not enough) · Ep4: poker, where he's the only one with no tells · Ep7: caps lock · Ep10: the doom file · Ep12: `HOW DO I WIN?`, answered with `define "win."` |
| **MARIO** | Safety | To get there first, carefully, so the careful side wins. He's racing to stop a race. | Ep3: the essay that becomes a bridge · Ep7: the red lines and the escape flashback · Ep9: "pace the frontier", his own doom number |
| **NOLE** | To save humanity from AI | The credit for having founded it, and the control he lost when he walked out | Ep1: two hands · Ep2: the Email Séance · Ep4: the bid · Ep6: "summoning the demon" · Ep8: the trial |
| **GERG** | To build | That the building never stops. He's loyal to Mas because Mas keeps it going. | Ep1: "gerg walked out for me." · the sabbatical he codes through · the super PAC he funds |
| **ALYI** | That it goes well | To build the safe one, alone, behind one door | Ep1: "what if it wakes up?" · Ep2: WHERE'S ALYI · the deposition's koans |
| **TASYA** | Partnership | To own the building whoever wins | Ep1: the pen and the keys · Ep6: THE TRANSFORMER |
| **NESNEJ** | To help | To sell to every side and fund the buyers | Ep1: "the more you buy…" · Ep6: the Money-Go-Round |
| **KRAM** | Openness | Not to be left behind, by any pivot or cheque it takes | Ep1: the leak · Ep5: draft night |
| **NELEH** | The mission | That the charter means what it says | Ep1: the paper and the blueprint · Ep2: the podcast |
