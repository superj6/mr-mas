## 1. Naming scheme rules

| # | Rule | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Reversal or anagram is the default.** The target must stay identifiable. Parody depends on the audience knowing who's being mocked, so a name has to read within about two seconds. | MAS MANALT and RUMPT (anagrams), NOLE, NESNEJ, YRRAL, LUAP, DLANOD (reversals), GERG MOCKBRAN |
| 2 | **Companies get the same treatment, and the best ones carry a joke.** | OPEN → **NOPE** (a true anagram; Mas moves the N himself in the intro), MISANTHROPIC, FACEHUGGER, MACROSOFT, ELGOOG |
| 3 | **Products get letter swaps, reversals or translations.** | CHATGTP, GTP-n, CLOD, KORG, AROS, LE CHIEN → EBIV |
| 4 | **First-name reversals are reserved for the tech kings and RUMPT.** Supporting politicians use a reversed surname, or a title when a name collides. | MARIO, NOLE, RUMPT · but ZURC, YELWAH, SKCAS |
| 5 | **Non-Western leaders, and anyone whose reversal is unpronounceable, offensive or colliding, get a title name.** | THE COUNTERPART, THE HOST, THE CROWN PRINCE, THE CHAIRMAN OF EVERYTHING, THE INTERVIEWER, THE FORMER, THE 49%, THE SPAC KING |
| 6 | **Chinese lab founders are never drawn.** Their companies appear only as mascots or props. | THE WHALE (PEEKDEEP), THE MOON (TOHSNOOM), THE WORKAROUND (IEWAUH), NEWQ |
| 7 | **Real public places stay real. Brands, private clubs, private venues and companies get parody names.** | Paris, Davos, Riyadh, Beijing, Bletchley Park, Air Force One stay real · Rosewood → THE WOODROSE, Mar-a-Lago → OGAL-A-RAM |
| 8 | **Government bodies keep plain generic names** ("the DOJ," "the Senate," "Commerce") **unless there's a gag name.** | DEPARTMENT OF RAW, THE COUNCIL, THE CENTER (FORMERLY SAFETY), THE NU |
| 9 | **RUMPT is the only character who renames things in-world.** The show renaming him first is the thematic lock: his name card reads `RENAMED IT.`, a mirror of NOLE's `NAMED IT.` | Gulf, WAR, Lake America, AI → SI/"super" |
| 10 | **No hidden real first names.** Never spell a real person's actual name on screen in any orientation. | Rejected: the mirrored ƧAM egg; a successor model that names itself "SAM" |
| 11 | **No accidental slurs, protected traits or family.** A name that reads as an ethnic term, an insult or a family reference is banned (see [§7](#7-names-deliberately-not-used)). | SEMIT, REMRATS, GNAW, "coconut tree" |
| 12 | **Substitution inside real quotes is silent on screen and logged off screen.** A verified quote that contains a real name shows the parody name ("Mario is right" for "Dario is right"). The original wording goes in that episode's `facts.md`. Nothing else in a real quote may change. | "What the hell is Invidia?" · "Dario (Misanthropic!)" → "Mario (Misanthropic!)" |
| 13 | **Character file slug** = the canonical parody name, lowercased and hyphenated, leading "THE" kept. Symbols are spelled out, and a paired entry uses its primary name. Products share one file. | `characters/mas-manalt.md`, `characters/dlanod-j-rumpt.md`, `characters/the-orb.md`, `characters/the-49-percent.md`, `characters/the-manifesto-and-neb.md`, `characters/pope-oel-xiv.md`, `characters/products-as-characters.md` |

## 2. People

**Tier key.** **MAJOR**: carries A-plots. **RECURRING**: returns across episodes. **CAMEO**: one or two beats. **CHORUS**: a group that comments. **BG**: background plate, no lines.

**Faction key.** NopeAI · Misanthropic · zAI/SpaceZ · Macrosoft · Elgoog · Atem · Invidia (chips generally) · Gov-US · Gov-Intl (including Chinese and other international labs) · Money (VCs, funders, landlords, and Big Tech that isn't a lab) · Critics/Academia · Culture/Media · Agents/Products.

**Episode key.** `fb` = the appearance is in a flashback. `intro` = seen in the 30s opening. `(spec)` = Eps 10–12, where all plot is speculative.
