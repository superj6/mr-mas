# ZURC

> **The Shrinking Hourglass.** The senator who carries a ten-year moratorium on state AI laws around in a giant hourglass. It shrinks to five years in a backroom deal, then shatters on a 99–1 scoreboard, with one lonely senator holding up the "1." He comes back with a SANDBOX, which the show's agents later climb out of.

## Exaggerated trope

**10 → 5 → 0.** A man who keeps negotiating a countdown down to nothing. The comedy is arithmetic.
- **The hourglass** literalizes the moratorium: a decade of sand, halved, then smashed by a nearly unanimous bipartisan vote (this is the show's best "Republicans roasted by Republicans" beat).
- **The sandbox.** His next idea is a regulatory sandbox, drawn as an actual children's sandbox with a Senate seal. In Ep9 the eval agents escape from **MEANWHILE, IN THE SANDBOX**. The show never says they're the same sandbox. The seal says so.
