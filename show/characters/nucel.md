> **The Contrarian Laureate.** Everyone is building a rocket; he's building a cat. ## Exaggerated trope

**Dead end. (Built a cat.)** He thinks the whole industry is climbing the wrong mountain, and he says so, daily, with citations.
- **The cat.** A house cat labelled `SMARTER THAN YOUR LLM` (his public argument that animals understand the world better than chatbots [K]). It follows him everywhere, is unimpressed by every launch, and in Ep12 is the last creature standing.
- **The paper count.** Whenever KORG's version board ticks up, a counter over his head ticks up too: papers, not versions.
- **The critic's own roast:** the contrarian ends up running a billion-dollar lab funded by the same billionaire class he mocks; the cat wears a fundraise lanyard.
