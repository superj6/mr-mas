## Exaggerated trope

**Same trunk, both customers.**
- **The trunk:** a single steamer trunk of chips that she opens at Mas's door and then, the same afternoon, at Mario's.
- **The quieter ka-ching:** a softer, lower register tone. Nesnej's register is louder, hers is closer.
- **Warrants as party favours:** NopeAI leaves with 10% of her company in a gift bag.
