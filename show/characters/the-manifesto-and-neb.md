# THE MANIFESTO & NEB (Z61A)

> **The Accelerationist Hype Duo.** A venture firm run by a manifesto preacher and a rap-quoting operator. One publishes a 5,000-word creed declaring "the lightning works for us" and lists "trust and safety" among its enemies; the other runs the political machine. In 2025 they launch a super PAC with a name that means the opposite of what it does. Their scroll and Mario's scroll unroll at each other: one becomes a runway, the other a speed bump.

## Exaggerated trope

**MANIFESTO vs ESSAY.** The show's most literal ideological duel: THE MANIFESTO's scroll and [MARIO](mario.md)'s scroll unroll toward each other across a runway. His becomes the runway; Mario's becomes a speed bump.
- **The enemies list** is pinned up as a row of wanted posters: `EXISTENTIAL RISK`, `TRUST AND SAFETY`, `TECH ETHICS`, `RISK MANAGEMENT`. In Ep9 a new poster appears: `PACE`.
- **NEB** is the operator: the checks go out with a boombox (he opens his books with rap lyrics [K]), and the lyrics on the boombox's LCD are always [INVENTED] and generic.
- **FOLLOWING THE PAST** vs **PRIVATE LAST**: two PAC blimps over every election scene, both throwing dodgeballs. The show never shows one without the other.
- **The Orb connection:** Z61A funded THE ORB. The show's most loyal familiar was bankrolled by its loudest accelerationists.

## Visual design notes (style-agnostic)

- **THE MANIFESTO:** a tall figure in a dark suit, carrying a rolled manifesto like a torch, with a small lightning bolt on a leash. **No head-shape, baldness or body jokes.**
- **NEB:** a shorter figure in a tracksuit jacket over a shirt, with a boombox on his shoulder and a checkbook.
- **The PAC blimps:** FOLLOWING THE PAST (orange) and PRIVATE LAST (Misanthropic's blue), the same size, always in the same sky.
- **The AI "reporters":** cut-paper figures with press badges and blinking-cursor faces who knock on doors.
- **Never photoreal, never a traced photo, never a cloned voice.**
