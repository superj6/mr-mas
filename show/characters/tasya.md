## Exaggerated trope

**He owns the building the fight is in.**
- **"Below them, above them, around them,"** drawn literally. In the Blip he becomes the floor, the walls and the ceiling of the NopeAI office, and every employee is standing on him.
- **The key ring**: giant, jangling, one key per tenant. It's his only moving part.
- **Serenity is leverage.** He never raises his voice, never runs, and is three moves ahead. Only Mas is calmer, and even that is arguable.
- **Landlord with a second tenant.** He hedges by building [THE HUMANIST](the-humanist.md)'s lab in the basement.

## Visual design notes (style-agnostic)

- **Silhouette:** slim, upright, glasses, a quarter-zip or open-collar shirt. His hands are usually clasped, except when jangling keys.
- **Signature features:**
  1. **The key ring**, the size of a steering wheel.
  2. **The architecture pose**: his body subtly merges into walls and floors when he's "around them."
- **Palette:** slate `#5B6B8C`, window-pane blue, cloud white.
- **Signature prop:** the key ring, with fob microtext (`AGI CLAUSE` in the 2019 key; removed in Ep8).
- **Staging:** he rarely enters; he's already in the architecture. In Ep12 he **refuses to sit at the Last Supper because he owns the floor**.
