## Exaggerated trope

**Chairman (all seats).** He sits on every board that matters, so the show gives him every chair.
- **The nameplates.** At any GATESTAR table, every place card reads `CHAIRMAN`. When the American CEOs sit down, they are all sitting in his chairs.
- **The plug.** Nole, cut out of the UAE deal, is found under the table with a flashlight and a pair of pliers. The deal stays plugged in.
