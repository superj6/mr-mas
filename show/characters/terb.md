## Exaggerated trope

**Wherever a board is on fire, he's already there.**
- **The extinguisher and the term sheet.** He puts out the fire and hands you the paperwork in one motion.
- **Nole's nemesis:** the last time these two were in a room, Terb made Nole buy the bird.
- **Zero drama.** Calm, capable, faintly bored by catastrophe.
