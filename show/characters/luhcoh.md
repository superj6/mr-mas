## Exaggerated trope

**The Other Kill Switch.** The show has two governors of opposite coasts reaching for the same button in the same week, three days apart (California Sep 18, New York Sep 21, 2026).
- **The tangle.** Two kill-switch cords, one from each coast, run into the same data center and knot around each other. Nobody can tell which one is live.
- **The torn pages.** Her first signature comes on a bill with pages removed; the second attempt adds pages back.
