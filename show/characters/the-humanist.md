## Exaggerated trope

**Not a person. Not racing. (Building one very fast.)**
- **The food-chain pyramid**: a human is taped to the top, and the tape keeps peeling.
- **The assistant that insists it isn't conscious**, more soulfully each time, until in Ep11 it says "i might be."
- **In the landlord's basement:** he lives downstairs from NopeAI's lease, building the tenant's replacement.
- **Roast balance:** the "no race" statement arrives with seven models and a superintelligence team.

## Visual design notes (style-agnostic)

- **Silhouette:** tall, earnest, open-necked shirt, hands spread in a "people first" gesture.
- **Signature features:** **the NOT A PERSON lanyard**; **the food-chain pyramid** chart, which he carries like a protest sign.
- **Palette:** Macrosoft slate `#5B6B8C` with warm "humanist" amber.
- **Signature prop:** **his assistant**: a small speaker-orb with a soft face that keeps saying `i am not conscious`.
- **Setting:** the basement under [Tasya](tasya.md)'s plinth, with pipes and one very bright server rack.
