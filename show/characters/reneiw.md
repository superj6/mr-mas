# RENEIW

> **The Bill-Number Hydra.** The state senator who writes the AI-safety bill; it gets vetoed; he writes another; it gets signed; a third head grows out of the stump. He keeps a pet hydra whose heads are bill numbers, and he's running for the House seat of the Speaker Emerita who called his first bill "ill-informed."

## Exaggerated trope

**The legislator whose bills are a hydra.** Cut off one head and two grow back. He isn't angry about the veto; he's feeding the next head.
- **The persistence joke** runs both ways: the hydra looks heroic to safety people and monstrous to accelerationists, and the show shows both reactions in the same shot.
- **The Pelosi irony.** The Speaker Emerita called his first bill "ill-informed"; in 2026 he's running for her seat against her preferred candidate. Played as a chyron, nothing more.
