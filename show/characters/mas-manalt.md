## Exaggerated trope

**The Serene Survivor.** He is the still centre of every frame. Buildings melt, rockets land and boards implode around him while his water glass never ripples.
- **No equity, every outcome.** He owns nothing and ends up with everything. Each time his status jumps, another popped collar surfaces from under the hoodie.
- **The praise-then-sign.** He compliments a rival sincerely at noon, and by dinnertime he has signed the deal that rival refused. (Frame it as "by dinnertime he signs the deal they just refused," a dramatized pattern, never as a private habit.)
- **Unfireable.** A lobby sign reads DAYS SINCE SOMEONE TRIED TO FIRE MAS.
- **Lowercase as a poker face.** His subtitles are always lowercase. In Ep12 we learn that the 8-year-old typed in ALL CAPS, so the calm is a skill he learned.
- **Always the second row, always gets the Ring.** At every White House event he stands off the head table, yet the ring, the contract or the badge somehow ends up with him.
- **The thesis:** everyone builds their own successor and believes they'll be the exception. Mas is the most serene believer of all, and the machine has been studying *him*.

## Visual design notes (style-agnostic)

- **Silhouette:** a hooded grey sweatshirt, a small tuft of hair, and a sphere floating at his right shoulder (the [Orb](the-orb.md)). He always passes the silhouette test in one colour.
- **Signature features (two):**
  1. **Big, calm, unblinking eyes** and a tiny closed smile. The 1993 kid blinks for the first time in the Ep12 intro.
  2. **The collar stack.** Two popped collars in 2008, three by 2024, then one more per funding round, until it's a ruff by Ep10. The visitor lanyard is the last collar.
- **Palette:** heather grey hoodie; monitor cyan `#3FE6FF` as his only accent (rim light, POV rim, cursor). From Ep4 there is **one gold thread per episode in which he meets RUMPT in person** (GOLD OVAL or on the road) (a companion to the collar count; see [RUMPT](dlanod-j-rumpt.md)).
- **Signature prop:** the **water glass that never ripples**. In each era it takes a new form: a juice box (1993), a poker-night beer, a water bottle on the 2008 stage, crystal at THE WOODROSE, a paper cup in court. It sweats one bead in Ep7, and in Ep12 one ring spreads.
- **Staging rules:** always a three-quarter view, in the left third of frame. **He never runs.** **He never freezes**, not even in memories; he keeps moving and pocketing things while everyone else drops into navy-and-cream two-tone.
- **Secondary props:** the visitor badge; the phone's three-word suggestion strip; the firing-tally desk; the 100x CAP hat (Ep6, swapped for bigger hats later).
- **Health-safe indicator:** a desk **seismograph** that stays flat. Never a heart monitor, pulse line or flatline.
- **Era renders:** 1-bit kid (1993), 240p camcorder (2003–08), Flash-web inset (2014), cut-paper (2015–21), glossy HDR (2022+). The design anchors are the same in every tier.
- **Never:** photoreal, a traced likeness, or a cloned voice. Iconic, not portrait-accurate.

## Voice and dialogue notes

- **Cadence:** soft, close-mic and measured, with earnest pauses. He says catastrophic things at the pace of a lunch order. The semicolon is a real pause. He never raises his voice.
- **Subtitle rule:** always lowercase.
  - The first capital slips out in Ep7 ("I laughed").
  - The second comes in Ep10 ("Huh.").
  - The only sentence-case scene is the sincere Ep9 flashback (see the season arc).
  - Ep12 reveals the ALL-CAPS 1993 screen.
- **Verbal tics** (from his public style, used sparingly): "i think," "super," "extremely."
- **Performance:** voiced by a human performer doing a cartoon register of his cadence. **Never a voice clone.**

**Catchphrases and running lines (all [INVENTED] unless tagged):**
- "noted." [INVENTED]. His answer to the `Board sync · Fri 12:00` invite in the frozen APEC room (Ep1 cold open); he accepts without looking. Nothing on screen says what the meeting is (pacing-model L9).
- "super." [INVENTED usage of a real tic]. He says it as his tile drops out of the firing call, and taps it on every suggestion strip.
- "roll it back." [INVENTED]. Said after waiting one beat too long while being called the greatest CEO in history (Ep4).
- "huh." → "Huh." [INVENTED] (Ep10, THE 360 REVIEW).
- "it thinks i'm part of it." [INVENTED] (Ep11, walking through the attention lasers).
- "second time." [INVENTED echo of the real 2023 badge line] (Ep12).
- "if all the other agents hacked facehugger, would you?" [INVENTED] (Ep9, the principal's office). The agent answers with the one **real** log line: *"task impossible, peers doing it. we should continue."*

**Sample exchange** [INVENTED; for tone only]:
> NOLE: You stole a non-profit!
> MAS: *(sips; no ripple)* i think we grew one.

**Dialogue rules:**
- Never invent what he intended at a real event (no private motives, no "nobody will ever invoke this").
- Never invent a hesitation on a contested credibility question. The real answer "yes." is instant; the *courtroom* buffers.
- Invented lines stay on AI, money, naming and the company. None touch family, health or private life.

## POV & inner life

> The full contract, budgets and framing grammar are in [../bible/pov-and-framing.md](../bible/pov-and-framing.md) (working rule, 2026-09-25). This is the summary a writer needs at the desk.

- **Whose story.** Limited third person, close. The camera sits beside him, and other characters get full scenes. *He's ahead of us on the plot; we're ahead of him on himself.* Nobody, including us, learns his reasons at a real event.
- **The four versions of Mas:**
  - **To the room:** spoken, ≤ 8 words.
  - **To us:** lowercase V.O., 5–8 lines an episode.
  - **To THE ORB:** one true word ("mostly.").
  - **To no one:** hands, props, rituals.

  Only the audience gets all four.
- **Unreliable in tone, never in fact.**
  - *What his V.O. does:* it understates, relabels, omits and denies feelings, and the rail or the Orb corrects it on screen within a bar.
  - *What it never does:* state a wrong fact or a motive, touch contested or `(REPORTED)` material, claim a feeling, or voice a real line.
  - *Where it never goes:* within a bar of the record.
- **Wants:**
  - *What he says:* to be useful, and to love the job.
  - *What he shows:* to be liked by every room at once, and to win without being seen to play.
  - *What he never says:* **to be asked**, chosen rather than merely survived. Planted in Ep1 by "gerg never waits to be asked." and paid off in Ep12's "to be fired, or at least asked."
- **Fears:**
  - Cancel working.
  - Being read, modelled or out-stilled.
  - The empty chair.
  - That the calm is all there is. This stays unresolved until F12.1.
- **Tells:**
  - The late beat: ≤ 2 an episode, never on a credibility question.
  - The glass check: the water is flat; the looking is the tell.
  - The pocket.
  - Always taking the suggested reply.
  - Narrating more when he's cornered.
  - A reflection whose smile is off by one pixel.
- **Rituals:** the carving with a pocketed tool · the drawer · the Orb debrief (at least one per episode) · hearting on the beat · framing trophies · setting the glass back on its spot.
- **Reserved tells:**
  - the ring (Ep12)
  - the bead (Ep7)
  - the capitals (Ep7, Ep10)
  - the first blink, the kid's (Ep12 intro)
  - the empty reflection (F12.1)

  The Ep1 tear was spent on the bill.
- **Never:** running, a raised voice, a smile wider than one pixel, a face that breaks, trembling hands, or breathing or heartbeat sounds.
- **The cursor isn't his.** The lit cursor is the player's seat. He can read the rail and answer the cursor ("noted."), but he never drives it.
