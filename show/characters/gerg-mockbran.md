## Exaggerated trope

**He became the org chart.** Every departure makes him bigger, until the chart collapses into one node with his face on it.
- **He never stops typing.** Holidays, sabbaticals, depositions, a courtroom witness box, the Last Supper.
- **He declares AGI at launches** the way other people say "thanks for coming."
- **The checkbook signs itself on autopilot.** He *bankrolls* political money but never works the doors. The PAC's AI "reporters" belong to the PAC, and Gerg is nowhere near them.
- **The loyalty is literal.** When Mas is fired, Gerg is gone within hours. When Mas returns, Gerg is back "coding tonight."

## Visual design notes (style-agnostic)

- **Silhouette:** hunched over a laptop that is always open, arms in a permanent typing pose, even while walking.
- **Signature features:**
  1. **Keycaps pop off like popcorn** whenever he's excited (they bounce, and Mas pockets the CTRL key in the intro).
  2. **Screen glow from below** in terminal green `#39FF88`, whatever the lighting.
- **Palette:** muted navy tee or plain hoodie; terminal green accent; everything else desaturated.
- **Signature prop:** **the napkin that becomes a website** (Ep1, Ep3 flashback, intro).
- **Secondary props:** a laptop on the witness-box rail (Ep8); a beach chair plus laptop (Ep3); an org chart that folds into a single node (Ep9); a self-signing checkbook (Ep5, Ep8).
- **Acting:** he looks up from the keyboard at most once per episode, always at the worst moment.

## NEVER DO

- **No wedding, no spouse.** The 2019 office wedding (the robot-hand ring bearer, Alyi officiating) is **excluded** as family life. The household PAC gift is shown as *Gerg's checkbook*; the spouse is never drawn or named.
- **Political money appears once** (Ep5) and is always paired in the same beat with the other side's money (Z61A's both-party endorsements, and later PRIVATE LAST). Never make partisan mockery of the donation.
- **The diary is an exhibit, not a diary.** It appears only as a courtroom evidence screen (`EXHIBIT`), read into the record. No prying-open, no "screaming lockbox."
- **"I thought he was going to hit me"** is quoted only. No physical staging, no flinch, no fist.
- **"Everyone reports to Greg" is Sottiaux's quote.** Never attribute it to Gerg or Mas.
- He doesn't run the PAC's fake AI "reporters." He bankrolled the PAC, and the show never implies he ran its operations.
- No body jokes, no age jokes.
