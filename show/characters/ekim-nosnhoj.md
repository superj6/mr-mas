# EKIM NOSNHOJ

> **The Self-Adjourning Speaker.** The Speaker of the House holds a gavel that adjourns itself. He sends the House home early, notes that the three AI CEOs have "very different ideas," and agrees to host a "deliberate discussion" about what role, "if any," government should play. His gavel bangs on the word "any."

## Exaggerated trope

**ADJOURNED.** A presiding officer whose instrument of order keeps ending the session.
- **The gavel** bangs by itself on key words: "if any," "deliberate," "home."
- **The empty chamber.** When he sends the House home, the gavel keeps adjourning the empty room.
- The roast is procedural, never personal: Congress's AI posture in 2026 is to schedule a discussion.
