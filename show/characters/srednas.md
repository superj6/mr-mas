## Exaggerated trope

**The far ends meet.** He and NONNAB agree on almost nothing except this: the billionaires building superintelligence should stop. The show draws the agreement as physics.
- **The horseshoe magnet.** Each man holds one end. The GPU in the middle is pulled toward both at once and doesn't move.
- **The oligarch index cards.** He reads from hand-lettered cards; every card says `OLIGARCHS` and one says `ALSO GPUS`.
- **The left-side roast** (he's an independent who caucuses with Democrats) is that his solution is a ban plus a brand-new agency, announced as a bill title before anyone knows what the agency does. The right-side roast in the same shot is NONNAB, the populist right's best-known strategist, holding the other pole and agreeing with him.
