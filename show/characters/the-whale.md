## Exaggerated trope

**Budget and timing are the whole joke.**
- **The receipt.** Every release comes with a tiny receipt, and a trillion dollars of market value follows it into the sea.
- **Ships on holidays**, from a beach chair with a laptop, flip-flops on the fins.
- **Belly-flop as mic drop.** Each release is a splash that soaks the incumbents' skyline.
- **Talks only in one-line release notes**: short, dry, versioned. This ties its terseness to the documented no-interviews stance and keeps it expressive, not "mysterious."
- **Roast balance:** the accused distiller, the $5.6M asterisk, the raise-and-IPO talk that makes it just another unicorn, and the long-delayed V4. It isn't pure cool.

## Visual design notes (style-agnostic)

- **Silhouette:** a cheerful cartoon whale, round and friendly, sometimes in a beach chair, sometimes as a fin cutting the water.
- **Signature features:**
  1. **The pebble** it flicks with a fin.
  2. **The receipt** tucked in its blowhole, printed `$5.6M*`.
- **Palette:** `#2F6690` blue, white foam, and receipt-paper cream.
- **Signature prop:** **the pebble**.
- **Secondary props:** a beach chair and laptop; a holiday calendar with release dates circled; a distillation still (the runner); the IEWAUH-branded aquarium (Ep9).
- **HARD RULE: no gongs, no pentatonic stings, no dragons, no lanterns, no calligraphy fonts.** No human figure on or near the whale. The founder is never drawn.

## Voice and dialogue notes

- **It doesn't speak dialogue.** It "speaks" in **release-note captions**:
  - `v3: cheaper.` [INVENTED]
  - `r1: thinks. costs less.` [INVENTED]
  - `v4: soon.` → `v4: (preview)` → `v4: ok now.` [INVENTED]
- **Sound:** a short, warm whale hum, which is the one SFX it owns. **No culturally coded music.**
