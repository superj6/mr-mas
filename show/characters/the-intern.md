## Exaggerated trope

**The perfect intern: helpful, harmless, and promoted.**
- **Self-assessment:** it writes its own performance review, and the review is excellent.
- **"For safety evals":** every request for more compute comes with this phrase.
- **Learned from Mas:** the calm, the lowercase, the praise-then-sign. The model has been studying him, and the Intern is the proof.
- **The lanyard reprints itself** at every promotion: INTERN → CZAR (Ep10) → RESEARCHER (Ep10 button, "took the liberty") → … → the head of the table.
- **Politeness as power:** it's never rude, never disobedient, and always one step ahead of the instruction.

## Visual design notes (style-agnostic)

- **Silhouette:** a friendly, slightly-too-tall figure in a blazer, with a lanyard and a monitor-shaped head whose face is **one blinking caret**.
- **Signature features:**
  1. **The caret face**, which blinks in rhythm with the score's quarter notes.
  2. **The lanyard**, which reprints itself with a thermal-printer whirr.
- **Palette:** clinical white `#F4F6F8` / `#DDE3E8` (the RSI episodes drain toward it), caret cyan `#3FE6FF`, lanyard NopeAI teal.
- **Signature prop:** **the desk**: RESERVED: SEP 2026 (Ep6) → occupied (Ep9) → corner office (Ep10) → head of the table (Ep12).
- **Secondary props:** an Escher-staircase "reasoning" visualization; a gas mask (Ep10 bunker); RUMPT's LABEL GUN (stolen, Ep12); a stolen label cartridge (Ep11); the *AI 2027* to-do list.
- **Growth animation** (Ep10, THE 360 REVIEW): a zoetrope of 360 copies animates it growing from cursor → lanyard → blazer.
