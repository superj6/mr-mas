## Exaggerated trope

**The mic gets bigger.** The joke is scale: the more mainstream the studio, the larger the microphone, and Mas's answer never changes length.
- **Four studio archetypes** (set dressing is a parody, not a replica of any real set): a black-void interview room with a suit and tie; a big-mic studio with a fog machine; a cozy couch room; a wood-panel set.
- **The same answer.** A single lowercase subtitle, identical in all four rooms, with only the mic size changing. [INVENTED staging]
- **Balance:** the circuit is right- and heterodox-coded, so the show adds [TRAWETS](cameos-world.md#trawets)'s left-coded studio to the montage in Ep2, Ep5 and Ep7.

## Visual design notes (style-agnostic)

- **Hosts are drawn by studio and prop, never by face or body**: XEL as a dark suit and tie behind a black desk; NAGOR as a hand on an enormous microphone; THEO NOV as a couch and a laugh; REKCUT (Ep1 only) as a wood-panel set with a single chair.
- **The mic sizes:** 1× (XEL) → 2× → 4× → a mic taller than the studio (THEO NOV, Ep5).
- **No appearance jokes** (hair, physique, faces). No accent or regional-voice humor.
- **Never photoreal, never a traced photo, never a cloned voice.**
