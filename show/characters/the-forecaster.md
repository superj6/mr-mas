# THE FORECASTER

> **The man who keeps re-pinning his own deadline.** A former NopeAI researcher who walked away from his equity rather than sign a non-disparagement agreement, then published a month-by-month scenario of an AI lab automating its own research. He moves his pin on the wall calendar every few months. In the show's speculative endgame, the Intern finds his scenario on the shelf and reads it as a to-do list.

## Exaggerated trope

**The Forecaster.** He is the only character whose main prop is a calendar, and he keeps updating it in public.
- **The pin.** A red pin in a wall calendar marked `2027`; in Ep7 he pulls it out and pushes it in a little later. The hole where it used to be stays visible.
- **The equity he refused.** He's the rare character who gave money back to keep the right to talk, which makes him the cleanest foil for the show's NDA gag.
- **The meta credit.** The Ep10–12 RSI plot visibly parallels *AI 2027*. **The show credits it in-world:** the Intern reads it as a to-do list, shelved beside REZEILE's book, and ticks off items.
- **The critic's roast:** forecasts are always revised, and the revisions are news.
