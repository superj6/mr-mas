## Exaggerated trope

**Politely On Fire.**
- **Serenity under alarm:** the siren spins above him and he calmly schedules a meeting about it.
- **The comeback kid who never raised his voice.** He is humiliated by a typo, made to dance, and caught with a staged duck. Then three years later the code red is on NopeAI's roof and he smiles.
- **He rents to everyone.** TPUs to NopeAI, TPUs to Misanthropic, and then he rents from Nole himself.
- **Roast balance:** the typo, the duck, the "AI" counter, the dance. Every win is preceded by a pratfall.

## Visual design notes (style-agnostic)

- **Silhouette:** slim, calm, hands folded; soft sweater or open collar. Serene half-smile.
- **Signature features:**
  1. **The code-red siren** on his roof (Ep1–5), then empty after it flies away (Ep6), then back on *every* roof (Ep11).
  2. **The polite smile**, which never changes even when he's literally on fire.
- **Palette:** ELGOOG "skewed primaries" (red, yellow, green and blue, deliberately off-brand and rotated).
- **Signature prop:** **the siren.**
- **Secondary props:** DRAB's telescope, which slowly turns to stare at him (Ep1); a rubber duck on a visible puppet rod (Ep1); a banana (Ep5, the image-app gag); a TPU rent meter.
