# YLLIT

> **The Synthetic Starlet.** An AI-generated "actress" unveiled at a film festival, told by the actors' union that she "is not an actor," turned down by two talent agencies, and booked anyway: a music video, a feature film called *Misaligned*, and a talk-show interview in which she glitches mid-answer. Her end credits thank "18 real humans," for half a second.

## Exaggerated trope

**Not an actor. Keeps booking.** The industry's most-rejected performer has the fullest calendar in Hollywood.
- **The union chorus** says "not an actor"; the booking board behind them fills with her name.
- **The credits.** "18 real humans" scroll by in half a second, faster than anyone can read, which is the joke about who did the work.
- ***Misaligned*.** Her first feature is named after the show's central fear. Nobody in the show comments on it.
- **The target** is the hype machine around synthetic performers and the studios that book them, not any person.
