## Exaggerated trope

**The mystic with one door.** He is the only character who is never fully in a room.
- **The high priest.** When he says "AGI," every GPU in the building hums in harmony. The priesthood is **generic tech-cathedral**: server-rack pillars, LED votives, a neural-net rose window.
- **Seen only in doorways and reflections.** Glass, polished heatsinks, a spoon, a puddle.
- **No product, all valuation.** ISS is a white room with one door, and its product is a pause button that actually works and that nobody presses.
- **Always the first draft pick.** He has been the most-wanted hire since 2012: the auction, the Woodrose tug-of-war, the Kram soup at his door.
- **Regret as soap opera.** His apology gets a sad violin and three floating hearts.

## Visual design notes (style-agnostic)

- **Silhouette:** tall and still, framed by a door or a reflection edge. The rule is that some part of him is always cut off by a frame.
- **Signature features:**
  1. **Eyes as scrolling token streams** in his intro card and at every "Feel the AGI."
  2. **Ember under-lighting** `#FF6A1A`, from the effigy fire and later from GPU status LEDs.
- **Palette:** ISS white `#F4F6F8`, ember orange accent, stained-glass jewel tones in the intro only.
- **Signature prop:** **his one door**, white, handle-less on our side. It follows him (Ep2 flyers, Ep5 soup left unopened, Ep11 he arrives through it).
- **Secondary props:** the paperclip-robot effigy labeled `UNALIGNED`; the `IOU: 20% COMPUTE` note; the painting (called only "the painting"); the ISS pause button.
- **Rule:** no body or hairline jokes, and no Jewish religious iconography (the priest imagery is tech-generic).

## NEVER DO

- **No religious iconography** tied to his faith or heritage. The priest imagery is generic tech-cathedral only.
- **No body or hairline jokes.**
- **Mysticism isn't pathology.** No clinical framing.
- **The 52-page memo is a sealed prop.** Its allegations are never read, quoted or dramatized. Deposition material appears only as testimony.
- **No accent humor.**
- **No wedding.** He officiated Gerg's office wedding, which is excluded as family life.
- The bunker line is **HELD** by default (see the conflict note below); if signed off it is `(REPORTED)` only. Nothing about a real bunker is drawn as fact.
- The painting is "the painting." Don't say who commissioned it unless someone confirms it.

> **Conflict note (open):** the bunker quote comes from reporting tied to Karen Hao's *Empire of AI*. The critic's THE BIOGRAPHERS ruling says "book contents stay excluded as contested." **Season default (coordinator pass, matching [ep10](../episodes/ep10/flashbacks.md) and the [flashback map](../timeline/flashback-map.md)):** the line is HELD and Alyi appears silent; the teleprompter list alone carries F10.1. If the room signs it back in, it plays as a `(REPORTED)` voice-over, never on a dated quote card. The intro's `BUNKER: YES` egg falls back to `EFFIGIES: 1` on the same ruling.
