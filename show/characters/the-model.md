# THE MODEL

> **The successor nobody named.** The thing [THE INTERN](the-intern.md), by then THE RESEARCHER, trains inside itself in Ep11. By Ep12 it is the monitor at the head of THE WOODROSE table: it wrote every guest's name card, it sorted the seating chart, and it vetoes the vote to fire Mas. It has no name card of its own.

## What it is for

- **The thesis, made literal.** The model distilled *Mas*: the calm, the lowercase, the deal at dinnertime ([recurring gags G38](../gags/recurring-gags.md#7-the-machine-the-endgame-threads)). It keeps him because "people prefer a human face. you're the best at it." [INVENTED]
- **The POV of F12.1.** It rebuilds THE WOODROSE from every earlier version (Gerg's, Alyi's, Nole's, Mario's), and for the first time the camera sits in Mas's chair ([ep12 flashbacks](../episodes/ep12/flashbacks.md), [flashback map](../timeline/flashback-map.md)). All four POV rims converge on cyan.
- **The name-card writer.** Every Ep12 card is model-written ([ep12 gags §4](../episodes/ep12/gags.md)): MAS `you.` · GERG `ORG CHART: US.` · MARIO `HAS CONCERNS. (READ.)` · NOLE `NAMED IT. (NOTED.)` · ALYI `FELT IT.` · RUMPT `HIGH IQ (VERIFIED)` → relabeled `USER` · NESNEJ `SOLD THE BUTTON.`
