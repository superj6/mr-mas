> **The Atheist of AGI.** The French founder who dropped his first model as a torrent magnet link, says the AGI talk is "about creating God," and pitches European sovereignty while cashing checks from a US landlord, a Dutch chipmaker and a Korean memory giant. His chatbot is a dog called LE CHIEN, and mid-scene it gets renamed.

## Exaggerated trope

**Not a god. (Building one.)** He insists AGI talk is religious hype while building the thing the hype is about.
- **The sovereignty sash.** He wears a sash reading `SOVEREIGN` that is stitched together from other countries' checks: a US landlord's, a Dutch chipmaker's, a Korean memory maker's.
- **Torrent calling cards.** He hands everyone a business card that is a magnet link.
- **LE CHIEN.** His chatbot is a small dog. In Ep8 it is renamed EBIV mid-scene and keeps answering to both names. (Keeps the show's renaming motif going outside RUMPT.)
