# Industry Cameos

Cameo-tier figures from tech, money and the labs. Each entry gives the real counterpart, tier, faction and episodes, then the verified basis, trope, a quick design note, voice, card, beats, and what it must never do. Major and recurring industry characters have their own files (see [mas-manalt.md](mas-manalt.md) for the tag legend, and the full list in [../bible/naming.md](../bible/naming.md)).

**House rules for cameos:**
- One or two shots and one gag each. Dialogue is [INVENTED] unless tagged.
- Silhouette plus one prop is enough; nobody gets a likeness.
- Title names (THE MATCHER, THE KID…) are used where a reversed name collides or where the non-Western-name rule applies.
- **Epstein guardrail (critic D30):** several people here (e.g. SETAG, DIRE, LEIHT) have Epstein-file coverage. **No island, file, jet or yacht-arrival imagery near them. Ever.**
