## Exaggerated trope

**The house always has a dealer.**
- **He deals every card game**, including the PACE Accord.
- **The tell:** in Ep4's dorm-poker flashback nothing floats over Mas's head. MAON's bot is the first thing that ever reads a tell there: `THINKING… 20s`.
- **The halftime show:** in Ep3, his reasoning model's chain of thought is a stadium halftime show, until the steel shutter drops.
