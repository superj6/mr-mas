## Exaggerated trope

**The Populist Sheriff.** A pro-regulation Republican who treats the AI industry like a cattle-rustling case. He doesn't want a framework; he wants a name on a form.
- **The fairness asset:** he and [SREDNAS](srednas.md) come from opposite ends of politics and pull in the same direction (THE HORSESHOE). The show uses him to prove that "slow down" is not a one-party position.
- **The clock.** His Oct 1 deadline is one of only two real dates the speculative Ep10 is allowed to use.
