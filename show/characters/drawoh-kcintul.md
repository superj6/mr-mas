# DRAWOH KCINTUL

> **The Echo.** The Commerce Secretary who repeats the President's last two words, and carries an eraser that deletes the word SAFETY from every sign he passes. When the President says "Going fine, I think it's going fine," he says "Going fine."

## Exaggerated trope

**The Echo.** He never originates a sentence in the President's presence; he finishes one.
- **The eraser.** His institutional act is subtraction: one word off one sign. The building behind the sign doesn't change, which is the LABEL GUN joke run in reverse.
- **The cut collector.** He turns the key on the CUT DIAL while RUMPT names the percentage.

---

## Visual design notes (style-agnostic)

- **Silhouette:** a sharp suit, a pocket square, and a pink eraser the size of a loaf of bread held like a clipboard.
- **The sign:** a stone lintel reading `THE SAFETY INSTITUTE`; after his eraser pass, `THE CENTER (FORMERLY SAFETY)`, with eraser crumbs on the steps. (Name ruling: CAISI is THE CENTER, not THE INSTITUTE, which collides with MIT.)
- **Staging:** always half a step behind RUMPT, mouthing the last two words a beat early.
- **No gags about his appearance, family or past firm.**
- **Never photoreal, never a traced photo, never a cloned voice.**

## Voice and dialogue notes

- **Register:** salesman's warmth; loud agreement.
- **Verified line** (dated card): "Going fine." (Jun 17, 2026) [P✓].
- **[INVENTED] echo rule:** his invented lines are only ever the last two words of RUMPT's previous line (e.g. *[INVENTED]* …highest IQ. / *Highest IQ.*). He never gets an original invented opinion.
- Voiced by a human performer in a cartoon register. **Never a voice clone.**
