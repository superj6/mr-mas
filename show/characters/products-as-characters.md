# Products as Characters

The models and devices are cast members. This is where the show's "tech as visual spectacle" lives: every chatbot has a body, a tic and an arc. **The writers' own model (CLOD) gets roasted as hard as anyone.**

Tags: see the legend in [mas-manalt.md](mas-manalt.md). Guardrails: [bible/guardrails.md](../bible/guardrails.md). Org and product names: [world/orgs-and-products.md](../world/orgs-and-products.md).

**Contents:** [CHATGTP](#chatgtp) · [CLOD](#clod) · [KORG](#korg) · [SYDNEY](#sydney-of-gnib) · [ZOMBIE 4o](#zombie-4o) · [THE GOBLINS](#the-goblins) · [Q\*](#q-star-q) · [Supporting products bench](#supporting-products-bench)

**Shared rules for all products:**
- Parody names and parody logos only, in off-brand colours. No real UI, OS sounds or trademarked interface elements.
- A product "speaks" only in [INVENTED] lines, except where a real, publicly reported output is quoted and tagged (e.g. SYDNEY's 2023 line).
- Products never stand in for a real person's private life, and "romance" between products is between *companies*.
