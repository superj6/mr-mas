# REZEILE

> **The Prophet whose warnings became a recruiting pitch.** For twenty years he has argued that building superintelligence would end humanity. The labs read him, got excited, and built it faster. His 2025 book sits on every villain's nightstand, dog-eared at the capability chapters, and Mas once suggested he might deserve the Nobel Peace Prize for getting everyone interested.

## Exaggerated trope

**Told you so. (Hardcover.)** He is the most consistent voice in the show, and his consistency has the least effect.
- **The recruiting pitch.** His warnings are drawn as a wanted poster that the labs keep reading as a job ad.
- **The nightstand.** His hardcover is on the bedside table of KRAM, NOLE, MAS and MARIO (Ep6), bookmarked at the capability chapters, never at the conclusions.
- **The Nobel form.** Mas mails him a Peace Prize nomination form (Ep1, [K] tweet); in Ep12 it comes back, filled in, addressed to the machine. [INVENTED]
- **The critic's own roast:** the prophet's book title is also the show's most accurate plot summary, which he'd point out, at length.
