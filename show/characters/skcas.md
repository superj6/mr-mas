# SKCAS (lanyard: THE CZAR)

> **The Podcast Czar.** A venture capitalist with two hats stacked on his head (AI on top, CRYPTO underneath, or the other way round, depending on the day) who runs national AI policy between podcast episodes. His lanyard reprints itself every time his job changes: `CZAR` → `CZAR (FORMER)` → `PODCAST (CURRENT)`. A four-mic table follows him through the season with a ticker that reads `BESTIES TAKE:`.

## Exaggerated trope

**The Podcast Czar and his Greek chorus.** Policy is content and content is policy. Every official act is recorded for a Friday episode, and every podcast take becomes an official act.
- **Two hats, one head.** AI and CRYPTO are stacked like a totem; he swaps them when corrected, and the staff secretary is always correcting him.
- **Fear-mongering, stamped.** He accuses the safety lab of scaring people into regulation, which is itself a line delivered to scare people away from regulation.
- **The bailout double-negative.** He says "no federal bailout" on the same week the CFO says "backstop," and the audience watches the taxpayers in the bleachers try to work out which one to believe.

## Visual design notes (style-agnostic)

- **Silhouette:** a quarter-zip or open-collar shirt (his public uniform), **two stacked hats** (a baseball cap reading `AI` on a second reading `CRYPTO`), and a lanyard that reprints.
- **The lanyard** is a small thermal printer on a cord. It spits out a new badge at each job change.
- **The four-mic table** floats behind him in every scene he's in, with a scrolling `BESTIES TAKE:` ticker. See [THE BESTIES](the-besties.md).
- **The lantern-shadow gag (Ep6):** KCAJ holds up a lantern and it throws a creature's shadow on the wall. SKCAS stamps the shadow `FEAR-MONGERING`, and the shadow turns and takes a selfie with the stamp.
- **No gags about his appearance or personal life.**
- **Never photoreal, never a traced photo, never a cloned voice.**
