## Exaggerated trope

**Polite, archival, relentless.** She never raises her voice. She simply produces the document.
- **The archive.** Behind her, stacks that go back 175 years; every lawsuit exhibit is pulled from the stacks by a librarian's ladder.
- **The privacy irony, both ways.** Mas says she doesn't care about user privacy; she answers by subpoenaing his users' chats. Both are roasted in the same shot.
- **The memo.** Her best witness is the defendant's own executive, who called it "the largest theft of labor in human history."
