## Exaggerated trope

**All in. Again.**
- **The mirror:** he and Mas are drawn as reflections. Same pose, same calm. The reflection winks first. Nobody, including them, is sure which one is real.
- **Mail mix-ups:** Sama Nos gets Mas's mail. "What would Sama do?" makes both heads turn.
- **The bet that funds the bet:** he sells one winning bet (INVIDIA) to buy the next (NopeAI), then issues junk bonds to double down.
- **300-year plans** on a scroll that makes Mario's look like a Post-it.
- **The crown on a timer:** richest in Asia, briefly.

## Visual design notes (style-agnostic)

- **Silhouette:** compact and smiling, in a neat suit, hands open like a dealer showing empty palms.
- **Signature features:**
  1. **The mirror frame.** Whenever he shares a scene with Mas, an ornate mirror frame appears between them.
  2. **Casino chips** that trickle from his cuffs.
- **Palette:** BANKSOFT silver, casino-felt green, banknote cream; the banknote balloon in the intro.
- **Signature prop:** **a slot-machine bucket of casino chips**, which he feeds into the GATESTAR ring (Ep4).
- **Secondary props:** the 300-year plan scroll; a crown on a 72-hour egg timer (Ep8); the $11.1B bond note (Ep9).
- **Mirror rule:** Mas never ripples, so the *reflection's* water glass is the one that sloshes.
