## Exaggerated trope

**The streamer who ran the company for a weekend.**
- **The 72-hour hourglass**, which shatters when Mas returns.
- **A livestream overlay** follows him: chat spamming "F", and a 700-person "raid" (the employee letter).
- **Giant shears** (his name's pun) and a sticky-note nameplate.
- **"Deeply pleased"**: a gracious exit line, played as sincere.
