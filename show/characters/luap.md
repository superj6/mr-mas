## Exaggerated trope

**The first sycophant.** Years before any chatbot flattered Mas, Luap did it in plain HTML.
- **He speaks in essays.** Lines appear as Verdana text overlays with **blue underlined links**; clicking one loads the past.
- **The kingmaker's props:** a parachute (the cannibal line) and a ramen crown (the 2014 coronation).
- **He called it**, in an essay, and he'd like the footnote corrected.
- **Rumpled:** the fleece and the unbothered, faintly amused expression of a man who has explained this before.

---

## Visual design notes (style-agnostic)

- **Silhouette:** a relaxed figure in a zip fleece with a slight stoop over a laptop running a plain text editor.
- **Signature features:**
  1. **HTML overlays**: his speech appears as typed text with blue links; the rest of the frame is Flash-era web.
  2. **The fleece name patch** (the intro egg).
- **Palette:** orange `#FF7F2A` on cream `#F3EEDC`; link blue `#0000EE` for the hyperlink device.
- **Signature prop:** **the ramen crown**, which dissolves into Kram's soup thermos in Ep5.
- **Secondary props:** the parachute strapped to young Mas; a batch photo; a web-player buffering spinner.
- **The "cannibals"** in his essay are *always hoodie founders with forks and laptops*. Never islanders, tribal figures or any real people.
