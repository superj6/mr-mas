# THE MOON

> **The Benchmark Moon.** A crescent moon that is always one decimal point ahead on a leaderboard nobody can reproduce, with a distillation still dangling from its tip. It is the mascot of the lab TOHSNOOM and its model IMIK. Other labs keep discovering that their own products were quietly built on top of it.

## Exaggerated trope

**One decimal ahead.** The moon is always slightly brighter on the leaderboard than whatever it's being compared to, and the leaderboard is always in a language of footnotes.
- **The distillation still.** A copper still hangs from the moon's lower tip, dripping into other labs' products. It is the show's gag #10 ("everyone accuses everyone of copying other AIs' answers") in mascot form.
- **The irony:** the American labs that complain about THE MOON keep turning out to be built on it (THE KID's Composer, RIMA's Inkling), while MISANTHROPIC accuses it of drinking from CLOD. Everybody's glass is full of somebody else's water.

## Visual design notes (style-agnostic)

- **A friendly crescent moon** with sleepy eyes, a small benchmark-scoreboard sash, and a copper distillation still dangling from its tip.
- **Phases:** crescent (Ep5) → gibbous (Ep6–7) → full (Ep9, K3).
- **Banned imagery:** gongs, pentatonic stings, dragons, lanterns, mooncake or festival clichés; no caricature of any person. The **founder is never drawn**.
- It never speaks; it only glows, one decimal brighter.
