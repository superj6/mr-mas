## Exaggerated trope

**The man with the lantern.**
- **The shadow:** his lantern throws a big, slightly unpredictable creature shadow on the wall behind every speech.
- **The stamp:** SKCAS stamps the shadow FEAR-MONGERING, and *the shadow takes a selfie with the stamp*.
- **Balance:** the shadow is roasted too. It grows bigger whenever Misanthropic is raising money.
