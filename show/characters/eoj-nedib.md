## Exaggerated trope

**The Analog Commander.** The first president to regulate AI does it with the most analog tools in the building: a fountain pen, parchment and a handshake. The comedy is the mismatch between the instruments and the thing being regulated.
- **Voluntary everything.** His first AI policy is literally a pinky promise.
- **The longest document in the room.** EO 14110's scroll is long enough to outrun Mario's essays, a runner that later gets topped only by the Pope's encyclical.
- **Voided while wet.** Every prop he owns is voided, one by one, by the next president's marker. The even-handed point: both administrations govern AI by *document*, one by the scroll and one by the receipt.

## Visual design notes (style-agnostic)

- **Silhouette:** a dark suit, aviator sunglasses (his long-standing public accessory) pushed up on his head, a fountain pen held like a baton.
- **Props (his set, which RUMPT voids one by one):**
  1. **The PINKY-PROMISE scroll** (Jul 21, 2023), with seven company pinkies inked in.
  2. **The EO 14110 scroll**, unrolling down the White House colonnade and out the gate.
  3. **THE THREE-TIER MAP** (Jan 13, 2025), a world map in three colours of highlighter.
  4. **The fountain pen**, the counterpart to RUMPT's ever-growing marker.
- **The deepfake NEDIBs:** crude cut-paper copies with visible scissor edges and slightly wrong ties. They heckle in his cadence. Only he and the Orb can tell them apart.
- **Staging:** always seated at a desk or signing. **Never** stumbling, stairs, falls, mix-ups or any gag that reads as age or health.
- **Never photoreal, never a traced photo, never a cloned voice.**
