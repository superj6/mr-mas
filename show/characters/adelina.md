## Exaggerated trope

**The Translator.** She is the one person who can read Mario's 40,000 words and tell you what they cost.
- **The shredder.** Every scroll goes in; three bullets and a revenue chart come out.
- **"In plain English:"** Her one catchphrase [INVENTED]. She uses it to deflate her own company's grandiosity as often as anyone else's.
- **She runs it.** While Mario is at the UN, she's on the phone with a landlord.
- **Roast balance:** she is roasted as Misanthropic's revenue engine. It's her plain English that reveals the ad about not buying ads.

## Visual design notes (style-agnostic)

- **Silhouette:** a tidy blazer over a plain top, a clipboard or tablet, and a small desk shredder under one arm like a handbag.
- **Signature features:**
  1. **The shredder**: compact, whirring, and always warm.
  2. **Reading glasses pushed up** when she translates. (Design flourish only; no age cue.)
- **Palette:** Misanthropic terracotta `#B8573A` with graphite; her revenue charts are the only saturated green on the Misanthropic side.
- **Signature prop:** **the three-bullet card** that comes out of the shredder, always titled `IN PLAIN ENGLISH:`.
- **Staging:** she is usually in the *other* room: in frame, behind glass, visibly working while Mario speaks.
