## Exaggerated trope

**He inherited the progress bar.** The hardware guy is handed an AI problem. Then the assistant ships in his first week, and he doesn't know what he did.
- **The engineer's calm:** he fixes the hinge while the building burns.
- **Speculative arc (Ep10–12):** the one CEO who wants to build *the device*, not the model. At the PACE table he's the one inspecting the chips' hinges.
