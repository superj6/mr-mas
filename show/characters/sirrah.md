# SIRRAH

> **The Explainer.** The vice president who hosted the first White House AI meeting and explained the technology to the country in the plainest possible terms: it's two letters. She stacks giant alphabet blocks, **A** and **I**, and restacks them for every audience. By Sep 2026 she's added four more blocks, and they spell **SLOW**.

## Exaggerated trope

**The Explainer.** She explains AI to everyone in the most basic terms available, and the basic terms turn out to be the correct ones: it *is* two letters, and by 2026 the two letters need a regulator and a treaty.
- **The blocks.** Everything she says about AI gets built with giant children's alphabet blocks. The lesson plan never changes; the blocks get bigger.
- **The first czar.** She is the first to wear the CZAR lanyard (informally, 2023), which then passes through SKCAS, sits on a hook marked `NEAR FUTURE`, and ends on the Intern. The lanyard is the show's bipartisan proof that nobody ever knew what the job was.
- **The Democratic roast** is that her 2023 governance was a photo op and a pinky promise, and her 2026 plan arrives as a position after leaving office. Played straight, roasted evenly.

## Visual design notes (style-agnostic)

- **Silhouette:** a tailored pantsuit and a pearl strand (her long-standing public style); an arm around a giant alphabet block.
- **Signature prop:** **the A and I blocks**, primary-coloured, bevelled, knee-high in Ep1 and taller than her by Ep9. In Ep9 she stacks **S-L-O-W** on top, so the tower reads `SLOW / AI`.
- **The CZAR lanyard:** in Ep1 a plain lanyard with a handwritten `CZAR?` sticky note.
- **Staging:** at a lectern with a pointer, like a teacher at the front of a class.
- **No gags about her laugh, her voice or her mannerisms.** The roast is on public statements and policy only.
- **Never photoreal, never a traced photo, never a cloned voice.**
