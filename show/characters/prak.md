## Exaggerated trope

**The lone holdout.**
- **Standing when everyone sits:** at the PACE table he's on a ski machine in the corner, still moving.
- **The swapped button:** his PAUSE button has been replaced by a crystal ball that shows only generic silhouettes labeled `ADVERSARY`.
- **Balance:** he's the most sincere voice *against* pausing, which gives the PACE Accord a real counter-argument. Nobody else at the table is honest enough to say it.
