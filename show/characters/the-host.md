# THE HOST

> **Host of Hosts.** India's prime minister stages the season's biggest unity photo: every leader and CEO raising joined hands on one stage. Two rival CEOs, side by side, don't hold hands; they each raise a fist. The resulting hole in the chain becomes **THE GAP**, a three-episode runner that someone eventually tries to photoshop shut.

## Exaggerated trope

**Host of Hosts.** He stages unity photo-ops that the tech rivals ruin.
- **THE GAP.** A daisy chain of raised, joined hands across a stage, with one fist-shaped hole in the middle where two CEOs refused to link.
- **The conductor.** He conducts the photo like an orchestra, baton up, and two instruments play a different song.
- **Even-handed politics:** when THE GAP becomes a domestic political row in Ep8, the show runs **both** the ruling party's and the opposition's spin as chyrons, side by side, and roasts both.
- The roast is the stagecraft of summitry, which the show also roasts at Paris (NORCAM), Bletchley (THE INTERVIEWER) and Évian.

## Visual design notes (style-agnostic)

- **Silhouette:** drawn with dignity; a conductor's baton in one hand, the other raised in the linked-hands pose.
- **THE GAP:** a long horizontal chain of paper-cut hands; the hole is exactly two fists wide. It is a recurring prop that hangs in the background of later scenes like a banner.
- **The robot dog:** a generic four-legged robot wearing an `OUR OWN WORK` sticker **over an older barcode sticker**. **No national-origin tag, no university name** (critic D36).
- **No caricature of dress, faith or ethnicity.** No accent humor.
- **Never photoreal, never a traced photo, never a cloned voice.**
