## Exaggerated trope

**He has concerns, and he has GPUs.**
- **The essays become infrastructure.** Each one unrolls into a bridge, a runway or an international standard. The word count grows: 15k → 40k → an IPO filing.
- **"Addendum:"** Every scroll has one, and every Addendum walks back the scroll's premise by exactly one clause.
- **The TOO DANGEROUS vault always leaks.** It started with GTP-2's Valentine's box and carries on through MYTHIC's drafts folder and the government's 18-day lock.
- **The halo is plugged into Nole's data center.** A cord runs from his halo to a COLOSSAL socket, with the rent meter spinning.
- **The red lines are paint.** In Ep7 he paints them on the floor with a roller, and that is the one heroic beat. It's roasted on both sides: the refusal is sincere, and it follows a 2025 Pentagon check.
- **Anxiety is a rhetorical style, never clinical.** It shows up as word counts, footnotes and "Addendum:".

## Visual design notes (style-agnostic)

- **Silhouette:** curly hair, round glasses, a zip fleece, and one raised finger ("one concern").
- **Signature features:**
  1. **The raised finger**, his entrance pose from the intro vault.
  2. **The scroll** that trails from his fleece pocket, however far he walks.
- **Palette:** parchment `#E9DCC0`, ink blue `#1F3A93`, Misanthropic terracotta `#B8573A` for the lighthouse. **Never red, never overalls, never a cap, never a mustache. Nothing Nintendo.**
- **Signature prop:** **the scroll**, with readable fragments: "…country of geniuses in a datacenter…", "…machines of loving grace…", "…we must pace the frontier…", and always a final `Addendum:`.
- **Secondary props:** the TOO DANGEROUS vault (with a `DRAFT — DO NOT PUBLISH` sheet fluttering out); the red-line paint roller; the pedometer (Ep10); the `SUPPLY CHAIN RISK` hoodie he sells; a halo with a plug; the NO ADS neon (which is itself an ad).
- **Non-stick fleece:** the GOLD OVAL's gilding slides right off him (the RUMPT gag).
- **Invisible to RUMPT:** in Ep8 he walks through the GOLD OVAL again and again and only the Orb tracks him.

## NEVER DO

- **Nothing Nintendo:** no red, no cap, no overalls, no mustache, no pipes, no jump sound effect. His name is a parody reversal only. If legal objects, fall back to DAIRO.
- **Anxiety is never clinical.** No pills, therapy couches or panic attacks.
- **Family:** his father's illness and death are excluded. The co-founder relationship with Adelina is shown only professionally (see [adelina.md](adelina.md)).
- **Military:** paperwork only. No weapons or hardware, and **no Venezuela operation, no Minab school strike, no Iran-war context.** The red lines are described in words and painted on a floor, never illustrated.
- **The EA memo's "shrimp over families" line** appears only as a chyron quoting "a Trump political adviser's memo." Never RUMPT's line, and never Mario's.
- **No halo either way.** Every heroic beat is paired with a roast in the same episode (the Pentagon check with the red lines; NAVE's >10% with the sprinting data centers).
- **No unadjudicated claims** about Misanthropic's conduct beyond the public record (distillation accusations are *his* accusations, framed as such).
