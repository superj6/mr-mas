## Exaggerated trope

**The adult with the calculator.**
- **The catcher's mask** comes down on every earnings call.
- **The tape:** calculator tape unspools from Mas's sentences and piles up around her ankles.
- **The BACKSTOP trio** (critic C19): HARAS's backstop vs SKCAS's "no bailout" vs [NERRAW](cameos-world.md#nerraw) holding the taxpayers' bleacher tickets.
- **Sent in his place:** Mas sends her to the King, and she comes back with a printed speech.
