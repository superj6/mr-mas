# NORCAM

> **The Deepfake Impresario.** Europe's host regulates AI by starring in it. He promotes his summit with a stream of deepfakes of himself, tells the country to download the French chatbot instead of CHATGTP, and declares "plug, baby, plug" beside a nuclear reactor with a wall plug. His deepfakes hold his press conferences while he watches from the wings.

## Exaggerated trope

**The Deepfake Impresario.** He is the host of every summit and the star of every promo, and the photo shows only his hands.
- **The deepfakes.** A troupe of NORCAMs, each slightly glossier than the last, handles the press while the real one watches from the wings and mouths along.
- **"Plug, baby, plug."** A nuclear reactor with a household wall plug; he plugs it in and the summit lights up.
- **Europe's paradox:** "Europe is back in the race," he announces, while [NEYEL](neyel.md) hits snooze on the rulebook. Both get the joke in the same scene.

## Visual design notes (style-agnostic)

- **Silhouette:** a slim dark suit, an open hand raised mid-speech, a lapel mic.
- **The deepfakes:** cut-paper duplicates with a faint scanline shimmer and a `DEEPFAKE` watermark on their shoulders; they gesture half a beat out of sync.
- **The reactor:** a cartoon cooling tower with a two-pin plug on a long cord (generic, no real plant).
- **Staging:** always framed as the host, arms open, at the center of a stage he built.
- **No accent humor. No gags about his marriage, age gap or personal life.** No "slap" meme.
- **Never photoreal, never a traced photo, never a cloned voice** (the deepfakes are drawn as obvious cut-outs, never realistic).
