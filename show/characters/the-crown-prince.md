## Exaggerated trope

**Buys by the gigawatt.** He shops for compute the way other patrons commission cathedrals.
- **The mirage.** On the horizon of every Gulf scene, a shimmering data center. When anyone looks straight at it, it becomes solid, with a rent meter already spinning.
- **The lease.** The first tenant's logo is Nole's. The show's "everyone rents from their enemies" runner gets a desert chapter.
- The roast is on **the money**: sovereign wealth as a compute shopping cart, and the American CEOs lining up behind it.

## Visual design notes (style-agnostic)

- **Represented mostly by the patronage, not the person:** the mirage, a gold pen that signs gigawatt contracts, a very long table.
- **When shown**, drawn with dignity in a plain dark business suit or a neutral silhouette. **National and religious dress is never a design joke.**
- **No palace, no weapons, no security detail.**
- **Never photoreal, never a traced photo, never a cloned voice.**
