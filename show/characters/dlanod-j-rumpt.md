# DLANOD J. RUMPT ("President RUMPT")

> **The Renamer-in-Chief.** A dealmaker Sun King for whom everything is a number, a cut, a gold object or a new name. He is the only character in the show who renames things *in-world*, the way the show renames everyone, and the joke is that reality keeps its old shape underneath the label. ## Exaggerated trope

**The Renamer-in-Chief, a dealmaker Sun King.** He grants and revokes: EOs, tariffs, the Stargate stage, the Misanthropic ban, preemption of state laws and the chip cuts. Every tech king courts him, and he wants six things from each of them, **in this order**:

1. **Numbers** (pledges: "What's your number?")
2. **A cut** (10%, 15%, 25%)
3. **The win** ("WHOEVER WINS AI, WINS!")
4. **Obedience** (the ban)
5. **Credit** ("I came up with the idea")
6. **Naming rights** (AI → SI → "super")

He is the only character who can relabel reality, and the running joke is that **reality keeps its old shape underneath the label**. His AI arc is a reversal played dead straight: every phase is his own verified words, in order, with no editorializing. The satire is the sequence.

- **Absolutely no hair, skin-tone, weight, hand-size or age jokes.** The bible already bans body and hairline jokes. His design must pass the silhouette test on suit, tie, folder and podium alone.

- **Render:** gold is his accent. Everything he touches gets gilded by one karat.
- **Never photoreal, never a traced photo, never a cloned voice.**
