## Exaggerated trope

**Called it. (Before launch.)** The heckler who is sometimes right, always early, and never quiet.
- **The stamp.** `CALLED IT`, slammed onto every launch poster. By Ep9 the stamp has been used so much the letters are worn to `CALL D IT`.
- **Stamps the successes too.** When a launch works, he stamps it anyway, with a small `(PARTIALLY)` underneath.
- **The rhyme with LUAP:** LUAP's card is `CALLED IT. (IN AN ESSAY.)`; SUCRAM's is `CALLED IT. (BEFORE LAUNCH.)`. An intentional echo; the two never share a scene.
