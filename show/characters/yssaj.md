## Exaggerated trope

**He rents to both sides.**
- **The van:** one delivery van, one driver, two identical $100B rent invoices at Mas's door and Mario's door.
- **The smile-arrow logo** curls into a rent meter.
- **The bookseller's calm:** a shelf-stocker's efficiency, applied to wars.
