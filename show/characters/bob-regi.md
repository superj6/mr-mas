## Exaggerated trope

**Sue one, buy the other.** A studio chief who fights AI in court on Monday and licenses his castle to it on Thursday, then has to host its funeral.
- **The castle.** A generic storybook castle (never the real Disney castle design). In Ep6 it's shrink-wrapped and wheeled onto the AROS lot with a `LICENSED ×200` tag.
- **The funeral.** In Ep7, [SELBEEP](selbeep.md) officiates AROS's tombstone service; YENSID's characters, generic storybook archetypes, file out in single file, holding their own contract pages.
- **The even-handed edge:** the studio is roasted for its whiplash, and so is the AI company that took the castle and closed the lot.
