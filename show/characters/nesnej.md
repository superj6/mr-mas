## Exaggerated trope

**Sells shovels. Funds diggers.**
- **Circular money:** he invests in his customers so they can buy his chips, and the check goes round and round (THE MONEY-GO-ROUND, Ep6).
- **KA-CHING ends every fight.** Whatever the other characters are arguing about, his register rings and the scene is over. It goes silent in Ep12.
- **The jacket grows a pocket per acquisition**: FACEHUGGER, the unnamed inference chips, even a pocket for the King's speech.
- **The whisperer:** his register and RUMPT's rhyme. He is the one king who haggles the President *down* ("Would you make it 15?").
- **Survivor's calm:** he doesn't flinch at −$589B because he started with $40,000 in a diner booth and "thirty days from going out of business."
- **Balance roast:** he says China will win, tells RUMPT export limits backfired, and ships crates that bounce between two tollbooths: `15% OF ZERO`.

## Visual design notes (style-agnostic)

- **Silhouette:** a sharp black leather jacket with a stage-ready stance, arms spread as if presenting a chip the size of a tray.
- **Signature features:**
  1. **The jacket's pockets**, which multiply visibly across the season.
  2. **The register**: a brass cash register that appears beside him from nowhere. KA-CHING.
- **Palette:** black leather, INVIDIA green `#7CFF4F` glow, gold only for the Ep4 statue (which shatters into 1-bit pixels).
- **Signature prop:** **the cash register.** The drawer is empty and it "clunks" with no bell in 1993; it becomes a fried-chicken bucket in Ep6, sweats in Ep8, and falls silent in Ep12.
- **Secondary props:** GPU crates tossed like newspapers; the 1-XGD box (2016) and the DGX Spark (2025) in matching hand-carry poses; **the golden speakerphone** held up on the Ep9 stage; the SYNNED napkin with `$40,000`.
- **Flashback render:** 1-bit (1993), cut-paper (2016).
