## Exaggerated trope

**The Greek chorus.** In classical drama the chorus comments on the action; here the chorus *books* the action. They react to every episode's A-plot live, and by Ep9 they're the venue.
- **THE SPAC KING:** the macro take, delivered as if from a stock chart. Everything is a "Sputnik moment."
- **THE SULTAN OF SCIENCE:** the only one who reads the paper; always 30 seconds too technical for the other three.
- **THE MODERATOR:** tries to cut to a sponsor mid-apocalypse.
- **SKCAS:** the one with the government lanyard, who has to leave the table every time the phone rings.

## Visual design notes (style-agnostic)

- **The table:** a floating four-mic podcast desk with its own spotlight rig, drawn as a small UFO that hovers in the corner of scenes. A scrolling LED ticker reads `BESTIES TAKE:` followed by one line.
- **Members:** each is drawn by prop, not by face: THE SPAC KING with a stock-ticker scarf, THE SULTAN OF SCIENCE with a lab coat over a hoodie, THE MODERATOR with an oversized gavel-shaped mic, SKCAS with the two hats.
- **No appearance gags**; no jokes about ethnicity, weight or hair for any member.
- **Never photoreal, never a traced photo, never a cloned voice.**
