# OIGNEB

> **The worried professor in the safety car.** The most-cited computer scientist alive drives a pace car with a speed-limit sign bolted to the roof, in front of a Grand Prix of labs. Every time he checks his mirror, everyone behind him floors it. By September 2026 he is briefing the UN Security Council that loss of control is "an unprecedented threat for countries that none can contain alone."

## Exaggerated trope

**The safety car.** In motor racing the safety car slows the field after a crash; here it tries to slow the field *before* one.
- **The mirror gag.** He glances at his rear-view mirror; the whole grid (KORG, CHATGTP, CLOD, INIMEG) floors it past him.
- **The odometer.** Instead of miles, the dashboard counts citations. It rolls past 1,000,000 in Ep6 and nobody slows down.
- **The critic's own roast:** he is always right on schedule and always ignored on schedule; his LawZero lab builds a slower AI, and the grid laps it.
