# ODNOMIAR

> **The Line Painter.** The Commerce Secretary who governs chips by painting a line on the floor and daring anyone to cross it. Her rival's engineers redesign a chip to limbo under it, and she promises to "control it the very next day." Two years later the next administration takes a 15% cut of that same limbo chip, which makes her line the first in the show's bipartisan chain of lines on floors.

## Exaggerated trope

**The Line Painter.** Policy as floor tape.
- **The limbo.** [NESNEJ](nesnej.md) shrinks a chip (the H20) to slide under her line and bows when it makes it through. The show treats it as a dance number.
- **The chain of lines.** Her cut line (2023) → RUMPT's CUT DIAL taking 15% of the same chip (2025) → [MARIO](mario.md)'s two red lines painted with a roller (2026). Three administrations of floor paint; nobody's floor stays clean. This is the show's bipartisan rhyme.
- **The walk-back.** She said it on Dec 2; her department softened it on Dec 12. The line moves an inch.
