# NIVAG MOSWEN

His press office posts in the President's ALL CAPS.

## Exaggerated trope

**Vetoed it. Then out-regulated it.** He is the show's mirror of RUMPT: equally good at the stagecraft of power, pointed the other way.
- **The veto.** In 2024 he kills the safety bill with the same "innovation" argument the accelerationists use.
- **The pivot.** By 2026 he is the most aggressive AI regulator in the country, and he announces it in the President's typography.
- **The campaign button.** Every AI action comes with a lapel pin reading `2028`. Nobody asks him about it; everybody looks at it.
- **The Democratic roast** is his flip and the positioning; it's paired with the Republican roasts of the same episode.
