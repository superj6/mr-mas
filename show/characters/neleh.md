## Exaggerated trope

**She read the charter. Literally.**
- **The paper as grimoire:** her academic paper glows and floats. A single footnote sets off a five-day chain reaction.
- **Footnotes orbit her head** like electrons.
- **Principled, never villainous.** The joke is that she's the only one who treats the mission statement as a binding document, in an industry that treats it as a logo.
- **Balance note:** her merger enthusiasm is on Alyi's whiteboard (`PLAN B: MERGE W/ MISANTHROPIC ✓ (NELEH)`), so she isn't a halo character either.
