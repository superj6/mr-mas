# THE COUNTERPART

> **The Long Game.** China's leader is **never caricatured and never drawn as a person.** He appears only as a presence: a gold podium that mirrors RUMPT's across the water, a banquet table that grows one seat a year, a tollbooth over Nesnej's GPU crates, and a voice heard through an interpreter's earpiece saying only what he has publicly said. The satire is aimed at **policy on both sides of the tollbooth**, never at him personally or at China as a people.

## Exaggerated trope

**The Long Game.** He never raises his voice, and every CEO who flies to meet him gets seated one chair lower than last time.
- **The banquet table grows one seat a year.** 2023: Mas isn't at it. 2026: Mas is seated; Mario's place card was never printed.
- **The mirrored podium.** RUMPT's gold podium, reversed like a reflection. When the two face each other across the water, the show's two superpowers are literally reflections, which is the joke (neither side is the original).
- **The tollbooth (the policy roast).** A tollbooth arch over NESNEJ's crates: `15%` (Aug 2025) → `25%` (Dec 2025). Then Beijing tells its own firms to stop buying the chip, and the crate **bounces between two tollbooths**, the US one and the Chinese one, with a sign reading `15% OF ZERO`. Both governments get roasted in one shot.

## Visual design notes (style-agnostic)

- **No face, no body, no caricature, ever.** He is represented by objects:
  1. **The mirrored podium** (RUMPT's podium, flipped, in a cooler gold).
  2. **The banquet table**, a long, plain table with no cultural motifs, which adds a seat each year.
  3. **The tollbooth** over the crates.
  4. **The interpreter's earpiece**: a close-up of a small earpiece on a table; the audio is a neutral interpreter's voice.
- **Banned imagery:** Winnie-the-Pooh; gongs, pentatonic stings, dragons, lanterns or any "Oriental" cliché; his family; military parades; Taiwan, Tibet, Xinjiang or Hong Kong content (war-adjacent and outside the show's AI remit).
- **Music:** no culturally coded stings. His scenes use the show's neutral dinner strings.

## Voice and dialogue notes

- **No invented dialogue at all.** He is heard only through the interpreter's earpiece, and only in verified public lines:
  - "always under human control" (Sep 24, 2026) [V/H]
  - "partners not rivals" (May 2026) [V]
  - "show their talents" (Feb 17, 2025) [H]
- Everyone else talks *about* him; he answers with the table.

## NEVER DO

- **Never caricature him or draw his face or body.** No impersonation, no invented dialogue.
- **No Winnie-the-Pooh**, no ethnic or cultural clichés (gongs, dragons, lanterns, pentatonic stings, accents).
- **No family** (his wife and daughter never appear or get mentioned).
- **No military, Taiwan, Tibet, Xinjiang, Hong Kong or human-rights material**: it is outside the AI story and brushes the war/casualty exclusions.
- **Even-handedness:** every tollbooth joke hits both governments at once.
