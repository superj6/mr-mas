## Exaggerated trope

**Knighted, Nobeled, Unimpressed.**
- **Simultaneous chess** against the whole cast. He moves on every board without looking up, and he's winning most of them.
- **The only honours in the room** (a sash, a medal, a knighthood), and yet *a banana image app* gets more users than his life's work.
- **Measured contempt for hype.** "This is embarrassing" is his most dangerous weapon.
- **Promoted upstairs:** literally one floor up.
- **Roast balance:** the banana, the promotion into a title, and the empty CEO chair he leaves behind with a chess clock still running.

## Visual design notes (style-agnostic)

- **Silhouette:** compact, alert, leaning slightly forward over a chessboard; one hand hovering over a piece.
- **Signature features:**
  1. **A ring of chessboards** around him, one per rival.
  2. **The unimpressed eyebrow**, a single raised brow that never comes down.
- **Palette:** MINDDEEP blue-grey with ELGOOG's skewed primaries; gold for the Nobel medal.
- **Signature prop:** **a pawn**, used to pop other people's hype balloons.
- **Secondary props:** a chess clock (on the empty CEO chair, Ep9); a banana on a podium (Ep5); the Go board (Ep9 micro, **no human opponent drawn**).
