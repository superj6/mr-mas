## Exaggerated trope

**The Yacht Emperor.**
- **The yacht is a data center.** The hull opens and server racks slide out like a car carrier's ramp.
- **Richest for a day.** He wears a sash with the date on it. It gets handed to the wrong Larry once (in the TOO MANY LARRYS gag, EGAP only; see NEVER DO).
- **"Put it on the ELCARO tab."** [INVENTED] Every GATESTAR gets billed to him, and he adds the debt to the yacht's ballast.
- **CEO of everything**, as RUMPT put it: a nameplate on every seat he isn't sitting in.
