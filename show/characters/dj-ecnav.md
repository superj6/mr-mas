## Exaggerated trope

**The DJ of Deregulation.** He treats AI policy as a set list: drop the OPPORTUNITY track, fade out SAFETY, keep the crowd moving.
- **The scratch.** Whatever record you hand him, he scratches it into OPPORTUNITY.
- **The turn.** By Sep 2026 the same man who said the future won't be won by "hand-wringing about safety" is telling a lab to stop building Frankenstein, *and* telling it not to ask the government for rules. The comedy is holding both at once: stop, but don't regulate.
- **The split.** He is the only member of the administration who, on camera, leaves room for real risk while the President calls it a hoax. That makes him the Republican side of Ep9's cross-cutting (paired with SIRRAH, THE FORMER and SREDNAS the same week).
