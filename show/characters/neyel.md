## Exaggerated trope

**Wrote the rulebook. Hit snooze.** The satire is Europe's two-speed AI posture: first to legislate, first to postpone.
- **THE RULEBOOK** is a hardback the size of a paving slab with a clock-radio **snooze button** set into the spine. Pressing it makes the pages go soft.
- **The framed prediction.** "Human reasoning next year," in a gilt frame on her office wall; next to it, in a cheaper frame, the scientists' retraction letter.
- **NOTERB** is her attack dog in Ep1 only: the man who said "blackmail" and meant it.
- **Even-handed:** the same episode that snoozes THE RULEBOOK has RUMPT's ONE RULEBOOK (Dec 2025) arriving to meet it; two rulebooks, neither read.
