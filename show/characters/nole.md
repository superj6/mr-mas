## Exaggerated trope

**The Rage-Quitting Titan.** Every relationship runs the same loop: fund → storm out → sue → rent to them → agree with them.
- **He named it.** The founding story is his, in his telling, every time.
- **Enters through ceilings.** Never a door. A SPACEZ booster descends, and every glass sloshes except Mas's.
- **The version board.** A split-flap sign under a permanent `KORG 5: NEXT QUARTER` banner creeps up by 0.1, Zeno-style, toward 4.9999.
- **Landlord to his enemies.** Rent meters spin over every COLOSSAL data hall he leases to Mario and Radnus.
- **He agrees last, loudest, and only after someone else has won.** "Mario is right."

## Visual design notes (style-agnostic)

- **Silhouette:** a broad figure leaning out of a rocket hatch, one arm always extended with a phone (mid-post).
- **Signature features:**
  1. **The 1am post lamp.** A single desk lamp that switches on whenever he posts, whatever the time of day.
  2. **The stamp.** Every big entrance ends with a red rubber-stamp slam (`SUED OVER IT.`).
- **Palette:** rocket red `#E0301E` accent; chrome and matte black `#0B0B0D` (zAI). **His renders use a heavy-metal album-cover style**: lens flares, and he is 40% taller in his own version.
- **Signature prop:** **the $1B novelty check** with fine print `received: $133M`.
- **Secondary props:** the version board; the golden key (whose lock keeps getting changed); the painting (always just "the painting"); the EGOD chainsaw (a paper-cutting prop only, gifted by [IELIM](cameos-world.md#ielim)); a campaign decal reading only `SUMMER 2024`.
- **Never draw:** the May 30, 2025 black eye.
