# World Cameos

Cameo-tier figures from government, international politics, culture and media, and the critics' chorus. Each entry gives the real counterpart, tier, faction and episodes, then the verified basis, the trope and gag, a quick design note, the card, the beats, and what it must never do. Major and recurring figures have their own files (see [mas-manalt.md](mas-manalt.md) for the tag legend, and the full name list in [../bible/naming.md](../bible/naming.md)). Tech, money and lab cameos live in [cameos-industry.md](cameos-industry.md).

**House rules for cameos:**
- One or two shots and one gag each. Dialogue is [INVENTED] unless tagged, and never in quotation marks as real speech.
- Silhouette plus one prop is enough; nobody gets a likeness. Never photoreal, never a traced photo, never a cloned voice.
- Title names (THE FORMER, THE INTERVIEWER…) are used where a reversal collides or where the non-Western-name rule applies.
- **Even-handedness:** every political cameo is paired, in the same episode, with a roast of the other side (see each entry's *Pair*).
- **No accent humor, no appearance jokes, no family, no health, no unadjudicated allegations** for anyone on this page.
- **Epstein guardrail (critic D30):** no island, file, jet or yacht-arrival imagery near anyone with Epstein-file coverage (on this page, that includes NONNAB).
