## Exaggerated trope

**Unflappable, and always the one holding the borrowed parts.**
- **The spotlight swings onto her, then away.** It happens in the Ep1 CEO weekend, the Ep2 demo, the Ep3 resignation and the Ep7 boomerang.
- **Borrowed parts:** her model is a **nesting doll**; open it and there's a whale inside, and inside that a small moon (INKBLOT is built on PEEKDEEP and IMIK).
- **Composure as a superpower.** Everything around her melts; she smooths her jacket and signs the next thing.
- **Balance note:** the fairness critic retired "Unflappable Understudy" because it cast the most prominent woman founder as a permanent second. She is a founder with her own lab, roasted for the *no-product $2B* and the *borrowed parts*, not for being someone's deputy.
