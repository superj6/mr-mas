## Exaggerated trope

**The Godfather with Regrets.** He made the family business, and now he warns everyone about it, beautifully lit.
- **Godfather lighting** (generic film-noir styling; never a specific film's IP): he's shot from below, in a warm key light, with a slow push-in.
- **The proud teacher.** The one thing he doesn't regret is his student: at the Nobel lectern he looks toward ALYI's doorway and smiles.
- **The clock.** An old wall clock whose face reads `10–20%`. In Ep9, RUMPT's HOAX BUSTER vacuums it up; two days later he walks into Congress with a new one reading `MAYBE A YEAR`.
