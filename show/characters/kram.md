## Exaggerated trope

**Every pivot is a costume change, and the paint is still wet.**
- **The wardrobe:** the metaverse avatar suit (with no legs), then the "open source" hoodie, then gold chains and MMA sparring gear, then a closed-model tuxedo, then open again.
- **Soup diplomacy:** a thermos in which checks float like croutons. It appears whenever anyone is poached, including at the Last Supper.
- **Draft night:** he buys talent like a sports franchise, with jerseys that list citation counts.
- **"We meant to do that"** after every leak, flop or pivot.

## Visual design notes (style-agnostic)

- **Silhouette:** compact and upright, in a keynote-stage pose with a big grin. One hand always holds a thermos.
- **Signature features:**
  1. **The costume** changes between (or during) scenes, with visible wet-paint drips on the new logo.
  2. **The thermos** with checks floating in it like croutons. His gold chain has a tiny thermos pendant.
- **Palette:** ATEM violet `#6C5CE7`; blue-and-white for the older era; gold chain accents.
- **Signature prop:** **the soup thermos.**
- **Secondary props:** smart glasses that glitch; a stadium draft board of GPU racks; a buzzer he forgot to press (the Ep6 game show); a BOOKMOLT aquarium.
- **Rule:** no hair, haircut or body jokes (the bible bans body and hairline jokes). The costume carries the gag.
