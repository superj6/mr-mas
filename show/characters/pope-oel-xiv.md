# POPE OEL XIV (+ POPE SICNARF)

> **The Pontiff of Plain Speech.** The first American pope takes his name partly because of AI, turns down a virtual-pope avatar of himself, and publishes a 42,300-word encyclical on safeguarding the human person in the age of AI, presented with an AI-lab co-founder on the panel. It is the one scroll in the show that ADELINA cannot shred into three bullets. **Nothing devotional is ever mocked**; the satire points at the tech industry's silence, not at the Church.

## Exaggerated trope

**The Pontiff of Plain Speech.** The one voice in the season who writes longer than Mario and says it more simply.
- **The scroll that can't be shredded.** ADELINA feeds *Magnifica humanitas* into her shredder; it comes out the other end whole, with a bookmark in it.
- **The scroll that outruns every scroll.** EO 14110 (Ep1) outran Mario's essay; the encyclical outruns both.
- **The deepfake refusal.** He is the only public figure in the show who says no to his own AI avatar; NORCAM's deepfakes bow when he enters.
- **The silence gag:** after the encyclical, the chyron reads "Tech titans mostly silent" [H]. Mas sends a fruit basket with no card. The Misanthropic co-founder is the only tech figure at the table.
- **The satire target** is the industry's response (or non-response), never faith.

## Visual design notes (style-agnostic)

- **Silhouette:** simple white cassock and zucchetto, drawn plainly and respectfully; reading glasses; the scroll.
- **The scroll:** parchment that unrolls down the colonnade, through the square, and out of frame; the word count ticks up on a small brass counter.
- **SICNARF** (Ep1–2 only): appears once at the G7 lectern (Ep2) and as the *obviously fake* puffer balloon (Ep1). No other imagery.
- **Never** vestments as costume gags, no liturgical parody, no altar or sacrament imagery, no saints or relics.
- **Never photoreal, never a traced photo, never a cloned voice.**

## Voice and dialogue notes

- **Register:** warm, plain, Midwestern-American directness (no accent humor); short sentences inside a very long document.
- **Verified lines** (dated cards): "an empty, cold shell that will do great damage…" (Sep 18, 2025) [H] · "do not feel joy or pain, do not know from within what love, work, friendship or responsibility mean" (May 2026) [V] · SICNARF, G7: humans must not lose control of AI (Jun 14, 2024) [H].
- **[INVENTED] lines are minimal and never devotional:** *[INVENTED]* It's forty-two thousand words. Please read the whole thing. / *[INVENTED]* (to the avatar) No, thank you.
- Voiced by a human performer. **Never a voice clone**, and never an AI-generated pope voice (that is the joke he refused).

## NEVER DO

- **Nothing devotional is mocked**: no sacraments, liturgy, saints, prayer, relics or vestment gags.
- **Pope Francis's death, funeral and health are never shown or referenced.** The SICNARF→OEL handoff is the name-taking only.
- **No abuse-crisis material** (including items on the France itinerary).
- **No war or weapons imagery** around "disarmed"; the headline is a chyron only, and SICNARF's lethal-weapons line is omitted.
- Never photoreal, never a traced photo, never a cloned voice, never an AI-generated papal voice.
