# KING LARCHES

> **The Gardener-King.** The monarch treats AI the way he treats an invasive species in a walled garden: with polite, relentless concern. He hands every visiting CEO a printed copy of his own speech about the dangers, and in September 2026 he hosts an AI summit, stays about twenty minutes, and asks whether we have "sufficient means of control before it's all too late?"

## Exaggerated trope

**The Gardener-King.** To him AI is a vigorous non-native plant in the national garden. He doesn't ban it; he prunes it, frets about it, and prints the pruning guide.
- **The printed speech.** Every CEO who meets him leaves carrying a copy. By Ep9 the copies have their own filing cabinet at INVIDIA.
- **Twenty minutes.** He arrives, says the most alarming sentence of the week in the gentlest possible voice, and leaves before the coffee.
- **The even-handed edge:** the roast is on royal soft power as AI policy (a speech, a banquet, a summit with no outcome), not on the man.
