## Exaggerated trope

**He has worked for everyone in this room, and everyone uses his words.**
- **The jersey changes mid-shot**: NopeAI → ALSET → NopeAI → his own school → Misanthropic, with the number the same every time.
- **Coinages as currency:** whenever a CEO says "vibe coding" or "English is the new programming language," a tiny `™ JERDNA` tag pops up next to the word.
- **Wholesome to the core.** He's the only character nobody roasts personally. The joke is that every lab keeps losing him, and that he keeps teaching their rivals for free.
