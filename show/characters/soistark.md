## Exaggerated trope

**The Stage Manager.** Every summit is a production and he is the only professional in the building. His catchphrase is a cue ("stand by… go"), and his power is logistical: who's on the stage, who's in the photo, which pen goes to whom.
- **Signs nothing.** The President signs; the stage manager hands him the pen.
- **The "no" is a cue, not a speech.** At the NU he doesn't argue; he flips a clipboard page and stamps it.

## Visual design notes (style-agnostic)

- **Silhouette:** a headset with a boom mic, a clipboard, a lanyard with an ALL ACCESS badge. Dressed in black like stage crew, with a suit jacket over it.
- **The clipboard** always shows the next item of the run-of-show: `CUE: GENIUS` (Ep5), `CUE: ROUND TABLE` (Ep6), `CUE: CLASS PHOTO — MAS OUT` (Ep7), `CUE: NO` (Ep9).
- **Staging:** at the edge of frame, lit by a single work light.
- **Never photoreal, never a traced photo, never a cloned voice.**
