## Exaggerated trope

**The Supply-Chain Diplomat.**
- **The gold plaque**, the loyalty gift, which kicks off RUMPT's GOLD OVAL karat meter.
- **Never says "AI":** only the trademark, with a tiny ™ that floats after him.
- **Two partners, one slide:** CHATGTP and INIMEG on the same keynote slide, neither noticing the other.
- **The 99% progress bar**, which becomes his successor's problem.
- **Roast balance:** "I cannot spend it fast enough" (per RUMPT) sits next to the tariff threat.
