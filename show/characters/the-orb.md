## Exaggerated trope

**The priest of verification.**
- **The only confidant.** Mas tells it things he tells nobody else, and it answers with a chime.
- **Verdicts as a season arc:** it is the show's one objective narrator, and it's slowly losing confidence about its own owner.
- **The fact-checker.** It checks *everyone* even-handedly: RUMPT's claims, MOSWEN's memes, THE 49%'s photoshop.
- **It sees what others can't.** In Ep8 RUMPT can't see Mario, and only the Orb tracks him walking through the GOLD OVAL.
- **It remembers.** From Ep9 it starts replaying things unprompted, which foreshadows the model studying Mas.

## Visual design notes (style-agnostic)

- **Silhouette:** a perfect sphere at Mas's right shoulder, slightly above eye level.
- **Signature features:**
  1. **The iris:** one round lens that swivels (with a servo SFX) and dilates.
  2. **The scan beam:** a thin cyan fan that sweeps and reveals hidden truths for a few frames.
- **Palette:** chrome `#E8F1F5` → `#3A4A55`; iris cyan `#3FE6FF`.
- **Signature prop:** **the toast**, a small system notification that pops up beside it with the verdict.
- **Render:** a pre-rendered chrome sphere with an SVG iris (any style works: chrome ball, flat disc, pixel sprite in 1-bit scenes).
- **Memory device ("the iris replay"):** it can replay only events **after 2019**, once the Orb existed. Earlier memories need another device (the [flashback map](../timeline/flashback-map.md), rule 5).

## NEVER DO

- **It never scans a real private individual on screen** in a way that implies real data capture. Its targets are the cast, crowds as silhouettes, and props.
- **The fact-checks are even-handed.** Every partisan check is mirrored (RUMPT in Ep2 ↔ MOSWEN in Ep5).
- **No real OS sounds or UI.** The toast is a parody notification.
- Don't depict or allege real-world biometric-data controversies about the product; they're not in the research file.
