## Exaggerated trope

**The Stamp.** A man whose only tool is the biggest possible rubber stamp.
- **Stamps first.** He stamps before reading the file.
- **The hand-me-down.** The `SUPPLY CHAIN RISK` label is usually reserved for companies like Huawei. In the show the stamp is literally [IEWAUH](cameos-world.md#the-workaround)'s hand-me-down, with the old owner's name scratched off the handle.
- **The badge and the ban.** He hands KORG a badge in January and stamps CLOD in March. The satire is the six-week gap, not the department.
- **The even-handed edge:** the lab he stamps is the one that insisted on two red lines, and the lab that signs hours later (NopeAI) promises the same lines "with safeguards." Everybody's paperwork gets roasted.
