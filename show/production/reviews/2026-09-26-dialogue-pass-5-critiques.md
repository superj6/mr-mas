## naturalness

# Dialogue-naturalness critique: Ep1 (cold open, Acts One–Three, tag), Ep2 and Ep3

I read the scripts as they are on disk now: Ep1 after its conversation, second, scene-craft and final passes; Ep2 at staff draft 5.3; Ep3 at draft 3.5. They have moved on since the conversation-pass reports in my brief, and several lines those reports quote are already gone. For example, Ep1 sc 10's "what year is it?" is now "it's 2023, by the way.", and Ep2's Forecaster line has been reworded.

This is a page read only. I did not hear, record or time anything, and I edited no files.

**Overall:** the pass mostly worked. The talk scenes now play as exchanges in complete sentences:
- Ep1: launch night, the lobby terms, Gerg's call.
- Ep2: the séance, backstage, the bridge, the watch party.
- Ep3: the writing room, the keycap call, the exit interview, the pledge.

What's left is small: one exchange that still volleys (Ep3 sc 8), a few leftover lead-in and feeder lines, some logic slips, lines repeated across episodes, and a spoken habit that a stock voice will make sound blurted.

Every rewrite below is new invented dialogue. Each needs the `[INVENTED]` tag and the owners' usual guardrails and facts pass.

## Across all three episodes

1. **A name said as its own sentence ("Mas. Did you…").** A stock voice drops its pitch and stops on "Mas.", then starts the next sentence cold, so the line sounds blurted.
   - Where: Ep1 sc 7, 9, 15 (twice), 20; Ep3 sc 18 and 22.
   - Fix: a comma, or move the name to the end of the line. Specific rewrites are listed under each episode.
2. **Lines repeated across episodes.** Tone guide R10 says a line never repeats word for word in the next episode.
   - "Last one…": Ep1 sc 1 (panel host) and Ep2 sc 2 (reporter).
   - "Don't mind me.": Ep1 sc 15 (Sucram) and Ep2 sc 7 (the Humanist).
   - Radnus congratulates, Mas says "thank you.": Ep1 sc 13 and Ep2 sc 19.
   - "That's how a race starts." (Ep1 sc 11), "That's how it starts." (Mario, Ep3 sc 8) and "That's how you get two." (Maon, the same scene).
3. **Four unseen people asking questions so a real line has a trigger.** Ep1 sc 1, Ep2 sc 2 and its tag, Ep3 sc 23. Two of them are "worry" questions ("does it worry you?", "Are you worried…?"). Each works on its own; give each a different speech rhythm so it doesn't sound like the same device every time.
4. **Radnus and Tasya sound alike (Ep1).** Both are calm corporate "we" voices ("We're very pleased." / "We're being thoughtful."; "It's fine." / "It's long-term."). The scripts separate them by what they want; the voice presets and paces should too. Radnus is quick and apologetic, and Tasya is unhurried.

## Ep1: cold open, Acts One–Three, tag

**Medium**

- **Sc 17, Mario: "That's the footnote. The honest answer depends on who else is buying."**
  - Problem: it is left over from the cut feeder question "what does it cost?". "The honest answer" now answers a question nobody asked.
  - Rewrite: "So I'm adding a footnote. What it costs depends on who else is buying."
  - "we'll read it." and "It has an appendix." stay, and Nesnej's real line still answers "who else is buying".
- **Sc 8, Egap: "And whose idea was it?"**
  - Problem: a pure setup question for the punchline.
  - Rewrite: EGAP (peering at the chat): "Someone else built that? It looks like our work." RADNUS (gently): "It is ours. (beat) We published it."
  - Nirb's "Do we still have badges?" stays.
- **Sc 20, Mas: "you're typing."**
  - Problem: it describes the sound back to us (R7), and by ear it's a non sequitur between "forty emails" and "Build's still running."
  - Rewrite: MAS (hearing the keys): "is that the build?" GERG: "Still running. I don't go to bed till it's green. It's bad luck."
  - This keeps typing as the trigger and leaves the late-hour beat to Act Four.
- **Sc 5, Rima: "Then let's keep it one."**
  - Problem: after Gerg's three sentences, "one" (meaning a research preview) is lost. It can be heard as "keep it to one post".
  - Rewrite: "Then let's treat it like one. No press, no keynote. One post, and a banner that says it can be wrong."
- **Sc 5, Alyi: "Six years and eleven months since we started."**
  - Problem: the click is the trigger, but the line doesn't point at it, so it can land as a mystic aside after the rail.
  - Rewrite: "Six years and eleven months. From the day we started to that click." "you counted." / "Someone should." stay.
- **Sc 7, Rima: "Mas. Is that… for the users?"**
  - Problem: the name stops the line, and "for the users" is vague by ear.
  - Rewrite: "Mas… a million people. That's for them, isn't it?" "it's the bill." stays.

**Low**

- **Sc 9, Tasya: "Mas. We're very pleased. It's a partnership."** → "Mas, we're very pleased. It's a partnership." This keeps the partnership / stuck / long-term rhythm.
- **Sc 9, Mas: "that's a lot of servers."**
  - Problem: nothing in Tasya's paragraph gives a quantity, so the line exists for Act Four's "that's a lot of desks."
  - Rewrite Tasya's line to earn it: "…you'll build on our servers, as many as you like, for as long as you like."
- **Sc 20, Gerg: "Mas. Did you just post on Tidder?"** → "Mas, did you just post on Tidder?"
- **Sc 15, the clone: "Mr. Manalt. Are you nervous?"** → "Are you nervous, Mr. Manalt?" Keep the senator's "Mr. Manalt. There's talk…", which suits a hearing.
- **Sc 12, Oigneb: "It's six months, for every lab."**
  - Problem: stiff.
  - Rewrite: "It's not the font. It asks every lab to pause for six months." The facts owner checks "every lab" against the letter. "Next quarter." still answers it.
- **Sc 22, Mas: "so. how's the partnership?"**
  - Problem: the full stop gives a false start before the question.
  - Rewrite: "so, how's the partnership going?"

## Ep2 (draft 5.3)

**High**

- **Sc 19, Radnus: "Lovely garden. Congratulations to them." / Mas: "thank you."**
  - Problem: it repeats Ep1 sc 13's exchange between the same two men. "Congratulations to them" is stiff when said to the man beside you, and "thank you." for a congratulation meant for "them" can read as Mas mishearing.
  - Rewrite: RADNUS: "Lovely garden. I see they let your chatbot in." MAS: "as a guest."
  - The wristband on screen reads `GUEST`, and it recalls the Ep1 sc 8 lanyards. It claims nothing beyond the integration already on screen.
  - The fallback is silence, as in the script.

**Medium**

- **Sc 17, the Forecaster: "I already took it. Seventy percent it's worth not signing."**
  - Problem: "took it", said as the pen withdraws, can be heard as "I took the deal". The second sentence isn't something anyone says.
  - Rewrite: "Already did. I put it at seventy percent that not signing is worth it."
- **Sc 19, Gerg: "One lot trains the next model, and the other lot keeps this one talking."**
  - Problem: "lot" is British, and Gerg isn't.
  - Rewrite: "Compute. Different compute. Some of it trains the next model, and the rest keeps this one talking."
- **Sc 19, Mas: "once. they let me hold the clicker."**
  - Problem: after "You ever miss being up there?", "once." is heard as "I missed it once".
  - Rewrite: "i was up there once. they let me hold the clicker."
- **Sc 2, the reporter: "Last one, then we'll let you get back on the trail. Artificial intelligence: does it worry you?"**
  - Problem: it repeats Ep1's first spoken words, and the colon construction is written, not spoken.
  - Rewrite: "One more and we'll let you get back on the trail. This AI stuff, does it worry you?"
- **Sc 4, Gerg: "We put some of them up today."**
  - Problem: "them" has no referent by ear, just where the newcomer needs to learn what the event is.
  - Rewrite: "It's a blog post. We put some of your old emails up today. The candles were extra."

**Low**

- **Sc 4, Terb: "Before we start: the independent review is back. Here's what it found."**
  - Problem: announcer register.
  - Rewrite: "Before we start, the independent review is back. I'll read you the finding."
- **Sc 7, the Humanist: "Don't mind me. I'm just moving in downstairs."**
  - Problem: it repeats Ep1.
  - Rewrite: "I'm just moving in downstairs. I hope that's all right." Tasya's "Welcome." then answers the question.
- **Sc 18, Mario: "…The long one's holding up the lighthouse. I do have one concern."**
  - Problem: the pivot has no bridge.
  - Rewrite: "…The long one's holding up the lighthouse. And it all comes down to one concern." Then "It's that we might win."

## EP1: cold open, Acts One to Three, tag

**The story as a newcomer would retell it:**
- A calm CEO, Mas Manalt of NopeAI, answers a panel question. A white pellet from a lit window across the bay lands in his water. He accepts a "Board sync · Fri 12:00" invite without looking.
- The show rewinds to Nov 2022. Gerg wants to ship tonight, Rima wants it kept quiet, and Alyi (seen only as a reflection) asks "what if it wakes up?". Mas presses the button. A million users arrive in five days, and he cries over the bill.
- The rival search company panics. The landlord, Macrosoft, pays about $10B, and everything now runs on its servers. Its chatbot Sydney gets put on a five-turn leash.
- Mario's careful rival lab launches the same day, and his careful memo gets shipped as a product. A pause letter and "Shut it all down" arrive, and Mas starts writing PLEASE.
- Washington: the class photo; a fake news clip reposted from the lit window; the Senate, where a clone does the chairman's job while Mas says AI does tasks, not jobs. His wallet shows he owns nothing. He hands over PLEASE REGULATE ME, which a critic has already stamped. At the extinction statement, a chip seller answers the question of cost. A crack runs across the sky and into Mas's reflection.
- The dark room: the Orb arrives and verifies him as human. He posts "AGI achieved internally", then edits it into a joke. Deepfakes of the president appear. His phone suggests "super". The date rolls to Friday.
- Tag: CEO of the Year, the drawer of pocketed things, the framed GUEST lanyard, a newspaper's lawsuit, and "noted."

**Where a newcomer needs outside knowledge:**
1. **Sc 11 pre-beat, Nole's post "…which is why I named it "Open" AI…" (line 516).** It contradicts the name on the door, and the final pass already logged that. The fix it proposes (printing the fuller clause) would tell Ep1 viewers what Ep2's séance gets its laugh from ("It was supposed to be open." / OPEN → NOPE). Cutting the post from Ep1 is the better fix: Ep2 then owns the fact, and Kram's hoodie carries the pre-beat alone.
2. **Acts One to Three have little on-screen threat.** Between the invite (sc 2) and sc 23, Mas is threatened only by Alyi's reflection (sc 5, 12), the two hailstones (sc 1, 14) and the crack (sc 17). A newcomer will probably retell these acts as "his rise, in funny vignettes". That's not a missing fact, but check it in the stick-figure reel. The no-spoiler rule still applies.
3. **The lit window and the hailstone.** Ep1 raises the question twice. Neither Ep1 nor Ep3 answers it at a size a newcomer can read (see Ep3, item 5).
4. **Minor jargon and loose references:**
   - Kram's crate says `MODEL WEIGHTS`; the wet paint carries the joke anyway.
   - Sirrah is never shown to be the vice president or a candidate. Ep2's `…and she 'A.I.'d' it…` and Ep3's `PREV. HOLDER: SIRRAH` rely on that.
5. **Sc 15, the clone and the chairman.** The clone asks "Are you nervous?" and the chairman answers "I am, a little." This only works if the two sound and look different (the difference on screen is a 1-px highlight). If their takes sound alike, it reads as a man answering his own question. Check the reel.

**What drags or over-explains (insider read):**
1. **Sc 9, Tasya's name card.** Its stat `MACROSOFT · OWNS: THE SERVERS` tells his speech ("Everything you build, you'll build on our servers…") about 15 s before he says it. That is the v4.1 "label tells the joke" problem. A stat drawn from his key ring would let the terms land in his own voice. Mas's reply "that's a lot of servers." only echoes Tasya; it's the one weak line in that exchange.
2. **Sc 19, item 4 (line 1044).** The raised hands are on screen before Remuhcs says "Every single person raised their hand.", so the picture gives away the real line. Open on the question plate over the room and raise the hands on the line, using the same drawings.
3. **Sc 17, Mario's "The honest answer depends on who else is buying." (line 922).** It sets up Nesnej's "The more you buy…" so closely that insiders hear the punchline coming. "It doesn't say what it costs." is setup enough.
4. **Small repeats (low priority):**
   - "Health insurance." is heard three times in about 3 s: the insert, the clone, and the chairman's echo.
   - Nole's role appears three times in about 3 minutes: the bio in sc 6, again in sc 11, then his plate in sc 12. Cutting the sc 11 post fixes this.
   - "Five turns each, Sydney." says what the `5` timer already shows.
5. **Long, but earns it; don't trim:** the launch-night argument, Tasya's terms in the lobby, Gerg's call in sc 20, and Mario at the extinction sheet.
6. **A note for the naturalness read:** the White House and the Senate still string together two-line quips:
   - "Just one?" / "It has sub-concerns."
   - "How much longer?" / "Longer than that."
   - "which one am i?" / "Most of them."

   None of these drag, but it is the back-and-forth rhythm the showrunner complained about.

## EP2

**The story as a newcomer would retell it:**
- Nole (he funded NopeAI, left, and is now suing it) crashes a séance where the company's published old emails rebut his case. OPEN becomes NOPE. An independent review puts Mas back on the board.
- A podcast mic grows during the host's long pauses. The landlord moves its own AI team in downstairs.
- Rima's voice demo is stolen by Mas's one-word post, "her" (the engineer explains the film reference). Alyi leaves.
- The search for Alyi: his reflections, his old chair, the safety team's door plate unscrewed, and an IOU for computing power that was never delivered.
- Departing staff are told to stay quiet or lose their shares. One of them refuses. A famous voice objects, and Voice 5 greys out.
- Mas is put on the committee that checks Mas. Elppa's walled garden takes the chatbot in and leaves Mas outside.
- Nole locks his own phone in a cage, then drops his suit "for now". Alyi's new company returns the IOU.
- Tag: the podium candidate uses AI panic and AI fakes, and Nole refiles in federal court.

**Where a newcomer needs outside knowledge:**
1. **Sc 8, the Trawets segment, the Trustbuster and the Elppa memo.** A newcomer can't tell who the host is, who "her" is, or why he looks down the lens at Mas. The script lists it as safe to miss, but it costs about 13 s at the top of Act Two, and only the rulebook swipe plants anything.
2. **TPOOL (sc 15).** The app opens before the show says it was Mas's company. F2.1 only implies it, and Ep3 sc 15 is the first time it's stated. That is withholding by design. If a fix is wanted, it can be in-world: the dead app's own login screen reads `welcome back, mas`.
3. **"Compute" as a noun (low).** The word appears in sc 14 ("was it the compute?" and `IOU: 20% COMPUTE`) about 5 minutes before Gerg explains it in sc 19. The `NEVER DELIVERED` tag carries the broken promise anyway.
4. **Tag, `…and she 'A.I.'d' it…`.** "She" is never set up. The script accepts this as texture, and the Orb's scan carries the beat.

**What drags or over-explains (insider read):**
1. **Sc 7: one fact told three times in about 20 s.** The plate says `MACROSOFT'S NEW AI CHIEF`, the Humanist says "I brought my own team." (line 457), and the rail says `MACROSOFT HIRES ITS OWN AI TEAM` (line 464). Let the rail carry only the date.
2. **Sc 18, Mario has one kind of joke.** He makes three length jokes here: "Four pages.", the "brief document" scroll, and "the long one's holding up the lighthouse". That follows Ep1's memo, addendum, footnote and appendix jokes. Keep "nothing here is shiny" and "It's that we might win." (the one new thing about him), and drop one of the scroll gags.
3. **Sc 4, the unsent-draft ghost.** I agree with the script's own trim list, item 1 (§9).
4. **Sc 19, Radnus's "Congratulations to them." / "thank you."** This repeats Ep1 sc 13's exchange with the same man. Keep it if the callback is meant; otherwise it reads as reuse.
5. **The glass-doesn't-move tell** is used four times here, and Ep3 repeats it at the stadium. Watch that it doesn't become wallpaper (low priority).
6. **Keep the engineer's film explanation** in sc 11 (line 710). Insiders will notice it, but it's said into a live mic, which makes it a consequence, and it's the newcomer's only key to "her".
7. **Long, but earns it; don't trim:** Nole's case, "You kept them." / "we keep everything.", Xel's question about Alyi and Mas's answer, the Forecaster, Haras and Gerg, and Gerg and Mas at the watch party.

## EP3
