## Showrunner guidance on the world and public threads (2026-09-25, binding)

> "We do not necessarily need to incorporate things into every episode. I just wanted to make sure you are taking in consideration and some level of incorporation of these and similar aspects in the total season script. I'll use your judgement for now."

- **Menu, not quota.** Treat `bible/world-stakes.md` (including THE PUBLIC PRESSURE LAYER) as a menu. Pick the strongest items for the season as a whole.
  - Land each one where it naturally serves the story, as a navigable obstacle or a consequence. Some episodes may carry several; many will carry none.
  - The world should be felt across the season without any episode reading as a checklist.
  - Rough target: about 20–30 world and public beats in the season, down from the 77+ proposed, weighted to the episodes where the real record is strongest.
- **DOT: approved by the lead's judgment, light.** About 5–6 beats across the season, at her signature moments: Ep5 night desk, Ep8 "Take two", Ep9 screwdriver, plus the finale lanyard and "second time". Never at the cost of Mas's screen share.
- **THE CHINA CARD: approved, capped at about 3 uses in the season.** Only where the record shows that person citing China. Never as a running gag.
