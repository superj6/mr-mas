# MR. MAS · Season revision plan

> **Season planner, 2026-09-26. Final after the adversarial critic's pass** ([§19](#19-critic-log): every amendment taken, four of them adjusted). This is the season's single allocation. It weighs every menu at once, because they all compete for the same screen time:
> - world stakes: China and geopolitics, jobs, money and energy
> - THE PUBLIC PRESSURE LAYER
> - orbit and lore
> - DOT and THE CHINA CARD
> - candidate style moments
>
> It applies the showrunner's notes in [SHOWRUNNER-NOTES](SHOWRUNNER-NOTES.md) (newest first) and the season editor's [top 10](../reel/SEASON-NOTES.md#top-10-structural-notes).
> - **This pass edits no episode file.** Each episode writer applies their section to `beats.md`, `facts.md` and `outline.md`, plus the teleplay for Eps 1–3. The other owners apply [§17](#17-handoffs).
> - **Names:** everything this plan puts on screen is registered in [naming.md](../bible/naming.md). Two §8 names appear here only as production labels, never on screen ([§16](#16-names-registered-in-this-pass)).
> - **Supersedes:** where this plan and the insertion tables disagree, this plan wins. That covers [world-stakes §6](../bible/world-stakes.md#6-per-episode-insertion-table), [§10.4](../bible/world-stakes.md#104-per-episode-table), [orbit-and-lore §1.2](../bible/orbit-and-lore.md#12-by-episode) and SEASON-NOTES' [world-stakes section](../reel/SEASON-NOTES.md#world-stakes-for-the-season-story-revision). The menus stay the fact bank and the staging detail. On style, [style-range](../bible/style-range.md) (now published) wins; [§14](#14-style-moments-proposed-ranked-for-the-style-range-pass) is this plan's story-side request to it.

**What's firm:**
- the [guardrails](../bible/guardrails.md), and parody names only
- no on-screen "speculative" label, soft ones included (a `?` on a date that reads as a hedge)
- no episode announces its own climax
- Ep1 never states the firing before it happens

**Everything else is a guideline**, including every count in this file. Where the plan goes over or under a menu's guide, it says why in a line ([§13](#13-guidelines-bent-and-why)).

**The test for every beat below** ([flow-and-continuity §5a](../bible/flow-and-continuity.md#5a-clear-to-a-newcomer-the-real-coherence-test)):
- A newcomer can retell the episode from what the show told them.
- An insider never sits through a fact twice.
- Every world, public or lore beat names its job: an obstacle Mas navigates, a mirror of Mas, a consequence, or a laugh that lands with no footnote.
- Every beat enters through a room Mas is in or a screen he's watching.
- **Every phone, rail or monitor insert rides a shot that's already running:** the phone lit in the same frame as his face, or a push-in, never a cutaway. Timings cut on story, never on bars ([flow-and-continuity §1–§2](../bible/flow-and-continuity.md#1-think-in-sequences-not-shots)). Twenty small inserts cut the other way would rebuild the v3 animatic's string of mixed inserts.
- **Text holds long enough to read.** Flow §2's floor is about 0.25 s + 0.05 s per character. A real quote shows only its key clause on screen, and `facts.md` keeps the full wording. The budgets below include the hold.
- Every beat is paid for with a cut in its own episode.

**Refs:**
- `W` is a world-stakes item, `PP` a public-pressure item and `O` an orbit item.
- `J` is a booked jump in [style-jumps](../bible/style-jumps.md), and `A` one of its alternates. [style-range](../bible/style-range.md) keeps both ids.
- `#n` is a beat in `beats.md`, `sc n` a teleplay scene and `rN.NN` a reel beat.
- Tags follow [guardrails §3](../bible/guardrails.md#3-fact-handling-tags).
- **Mas share** is the share of runtime with Mas on screen, or on a screen he's watching with his glass or hand in frame. The guide is 70% or more, with signposted exits at 12% or less and 3 or fewer ([SEASON-NOTES note 1](../reel/SEASON-NOTES.md#1-from-ep5-the-show-stops-being-told-through-mas-eps-5-7-9-11-12)). "Now" is the reel figure; "after" is an estimate from the reel's durations.
